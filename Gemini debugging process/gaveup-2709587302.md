## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Resolved (`Milotic spe 0`) & Why `p2@p1` Gave Up (979.8s)

### A. The `102 == 102` Speed-Tie + `Rock Slide` Flinch Deadlock (`trace_2709587302.log`, lines 18–21)
In Round 5 of `both@spectator`, the inference solver reached line `3.8` with:
- `Tyranitar`: `32/0/2/0/0/17` (`Mega Spe 118`)
- `Milotic`: `32/0/0/0/32/1` (**`spe = 1` $\to$ `Spe = 102`**)
- `Kingambit`: `0/32/0/0/2/32` (**`spe = 32` $\to$ `effectiveSpe = 102`**)
- `Whimsicott`: `0/0/1/0/11/32`

Even though `102 == 102` allows `Kingambit` to move before `Milotic` on a 50/50 speed-tie draw, **`the guess alone` failed to reproduce Turn 3 under `budgets[1]` (`600` probes, `2` backtracks, `4.3s`)** (trace line 18). Why?
1. **`scoreOf` Regression Under `Rock Slide` Flinch (`reconstruct.mjs`, lines 1667–1679)**: `Tyranitar-Mega` moves before `Milotic`/`Kingambit` and fires `Rock Slide`, which carries a `30%` secondary flinch roll on `Kingambit`. Whenever `Rock Slide`'s unpinned PRNG draw rolled a flinch on `Kingambit`, flipping the speed-tie `random(0, 2)` draw after `Rock Slide` changed the line at `3.8` from:
   - `got = |move|p1a: Milotic|Icy Wind|...` (`want = |move|p2a: Kingambit|Low Kick|...` $\to$ **`fields = 2`** matching `["", "move"]`)
   to:
   - `got = |cant|p2a: Kingambit|flinch` (**`fields = 1`** matching `[""]`, since `"cant" !== "move"`).
   Because `[8, 1, 2] < [8, 2, 2]`, `resolveTurn` **rejected** flipping the speed-tie draw alone (`outranks(reach, score) === false`).
2. **`chances` Pair Exclusion (`reconstruct.mjs`, line 1974; `rng-command.js`, line 447)**: `resolveTurn`'s two-draw pair search only pairs draws matching `d.chance || d.hi - d.lo + 1 > 16`. Because `Battle.speedSort` calls `prng.shuffle` $\to$ `random(0, 2)` (so `chance === false` and `span = 2 <= 16`), the speed-tie draw is **excluded from `chances`**, so `resolveTurn` never tries `(speedTie, rockSlideFlinch)` together within small probe budgets.

### B. Why `both@spectator` Passed in 40s
In `both@spectator` (`known = [false, false]`), `Milotic` (`p1:3`) is an inferred Pokémon inside `fallback`'s `cast`.
