## 5. Root-Cause Diagnosis: Why Inference Stalled at Turn 2.10 & Cycled in Fallback

### A. Root Cause 1: `choiceFor(teamPreview)` Discards Lead Disguises Without Putting `Zoroark` Last (`reconstruct.mjs:454-457`)
Because `|replace|p1b: Zoroark|Zoroark-Hisui, L50, F` appears later in Turn 2, `unmaskIllusion()` (`protocol.mjs:81-108`) **correctly** unmasks raw line 63 to `|switch|p1b: Zoroark|Zoroark-Hisui, L50, F|100/100` and records `disguises.p1 = ['Whimsicott']` from Round 1 onward.

However, when `choiceFor()` builds P1's Team Preview order (`reconstruct.mjs:453-482`):
```js
const leads = side.active.length;
const last = (state.disguises?.[sideId] || [])
  .filter(name => !state.reveal[sideId].slice(0, leads).includes(name))
  .map(name => bench.indexOf(name))
  .filter(at => at >= 0);
```
1. Because `'Whimsicott'` was one of P1's two Turn 0 leads (`state.reveal.p1 = ['Glalie', 'Whimsicott', 'Zoroark']`), `.filter(...)` removes `'Whimsicott'` from `last`, leaving `last = []`.
2. Of course, a lead cannot start in Party Position 4 at Team Preview. **How can `Zoroark` ever enter disguised as a lead?** Only if **`Zoroark` itself starts in Party Position 4** (or every Pokémon behind `Zoroark` has fainted), and the lead switches directly into `Zoroark`—swapping the retreating lead into Party Position 4 right before `Illusion.onBeforeSwitchIn` runs!
3. Because `choiceFor` does not place `Zoroark` in `last` when `disguises` names a lead, it treats `seen = [0 (Glalie), 2 (Whimsicott), 1 (Zoroark)]` and picks one unseen 4th member from `pool = [3 (Basculegion), 4 (Ninetales), 5 (Baxcalibur)]`.
4. Variant `0` (`options[0]`) emits **`>p1 team 1, 3, 2, 4` = `[Pos 1: Glalie, Pos 2: Whimsicott, Pos 3: Zoroark-Hisui, Pos 4: Basculegion]`**.
5. When `Whimsicott` (Pos 2) Eject-Buttons into `Zoroark-Hisui` (Pos 3) at Turn `2.10`, Pos 2 and Pos 3 swap (`[Glalie, Zoroark, Whimsicott, Basculegion]`), **`Basculegion` remains in Party Position 4**, and Showdown prints:
   ```
   wanted |switch|p1b: Whimsicott|Whimsicott, L50, F|100/100
   got    |switch|p1b: Basculegion|Basculegion, L50, M|100/100
   ```

### B. Root Cause 2: `watchParty` Does Not Detect `Illusion` Reading the Back of the Party (`reconstruct.mjs:240-255, 2528-2530`)
Turn 0 Team Preview *did* enumerate 6 party-order variants (`run.widths[0] = 6`), and odd variants (`options[1]`: `team 1, 3, 4, 2`; `options[3]`: `team 1, 3, 5, 2`; `options[5]`: `team 1, 3, 6, 2`) all place `Zoroark-Hisui` in Party Position 4—any of which would have immediately reproduced `|switch|p1b: Whimsicott|Whimsicott, L50, F|100/100`!

Yet `reconstruct` **never tested `variants[0] > 0`** when Turn 2 failed at `2.10`:
- Turn 0 succeeded cleanly on its own, locking `variants[0] = 0`.
- On Turn 2 (`t = 2`), `reconstruct` only re-walks `variants[0]` (`reconstruct.mjs:2529`) if `(run.party || []).includes(2)`.
- `watchParty()` (`reconstruct.mjs:240-255`) only pushes `at.k` into `party` inside a property getter on `pokemon.set` guarded by `if (!this.isActive && battle.activeMove && ...)`.
- That hook catches `Beat Up`, which reads `pokemon.set` during `battle.activeMove`, **but `Illusion.onBeforeSwitchIn` reads `pokemon.side.pokemon[i]` directly (never `.set`) while `battle.activeMove` is `null`**.
- Thus `run.party` remained `[]`, `carriers` was `[]`, and `redrawTurn` exhausted `maxProbes` and `maxBacktracks` redrawing RNG dice under `variants[0] = 0`, where `Basculegion` was deterministically trapped in Position 4.
