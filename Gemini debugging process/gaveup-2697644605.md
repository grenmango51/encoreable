## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. `evidencePass` Cuts Off Before `|-damage|...|1/100`, Leaving `knowledge` Unchanged (`read = 1`)

3. Consequently, `settle()` eliminates **zero** spreads (`read = 1`, `moved = false`). Because `knowledge` remains completely unnarrowed (`136,663,185` spreads per Pokémon), `choose()` validates `picks` itself across Turn 0 and lines `1.0–1.3` (`2 turns exact, 1 dice handed on`), making **`unchanged = true`** (`next === picks`).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2:4 Basculegion-F`'s true `HP` and `Def` SPs are fixed ground-truth constants, and Channel 2 sees `Basculegion-F`'s exact HP integer (`1..3 / MaxHP`). `fallback` only needs to lower `Kingambit`'s `Atk` (which avoids Focus Sash cleanly once `Basculegion` isn't stripped to `HP = 195`).
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:4 Kingambit`'s true `Atk` SP is a fixed constant, so `fallback` only probes `Basculegion-F` (`hp` and `def`), and `closestSpread` cannot accidentally hold `Kingambit Atk` at `32` while stripping `Kingambit hp`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/knowledge.mjs`, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Fix `closestSpread` Tie-Breaking So `hp` Is Not Silently Stripped to `0` (`knowledge.mjs` lines 672–680)**:
   `closestSpread` scans `(hp, def, spd)` keys in ascending `k` order and breaks equal-cost ties in favor of the lowest `k` (`hp = 0`). When `fallback` probed `Basculegion def = d` on `2/0/0/32/0/32` or `32/0/0/32/0/2`, `closestSpread` preferred stripping `hp` (`2 -> 0` or `32 -> 0`, dropping `MaxHP` to `195`) over trimming `spe` or `spa`. Similarly, probing `Kingambit def/spa/spd/spe` stripped `Kingambit hp` instead of `atk`. Breaking Manhattan-distance ties to prefer keeping `prev.hp` (or trimming non-participating/already-varied stats first) prevents `closestSpread` from collapsing `MaxHP` onto `HP = 195` (`Focus Sash` boundary) during defensive probes.
2. **Do Not Skip `rebuild()` via `replayWith` `same` Check When Failing Line Is a Roll/Sash Boundary (`infer.mjs` lines 677–679)**:
   On Turn 1 `1.4`, `built.inputLog` held a single unpinned high roll on `Sucker Punch` under which `Basculegion def 32` (`HP = 195, Def = 117`) and `Kingambit atk 0` (`Atk = 170, HP = 227, Def = 85`) both still triggered `Focus Sash`, causing `replayWith` to report `same = true` (`plays out the same`) and skip `rebuild()`. Had `rebuild()` run, `steerDice` would have immediately found the non-lethal damage rolls (`179..194` and `208..226`), marking `Basculegion def` and `Kingambit atk` as `moved` and unlocking the single-stat / pair sweeps!
3. **Allow `evidencePass` or `fallback` to Pair Attacker Offense and Defender Bulk on Focus-Sash / `1/100` Mismatches**:
   When a rebuild fails on `wanted |-damage|...|1/100` vs `got |-enditem|...|Focus Sash`, `evidencePass` currently stops at `cutoffAt = |-enditem|` without recording any `1/100` constraint, and `fallback` only explores pairs among stats that individually changed `outcome !== baseOutcome`. When both guesses are far inside the lethal OHKO region (`249..296` vs `197–227 HP`), single-stat probes can miss the narrow survival window (`[MaxHP - 3, MaxHP - 1]`) unless `(attacker off, defender def)` or `(defender hp, defender def)` pairs are probed directly or `steerDice` checks survival rolls without skipping via `replayWith`.
