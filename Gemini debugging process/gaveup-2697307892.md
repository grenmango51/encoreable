## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking in Champions Format
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Neither side has exact HP numbers.
- In the Champions format, percentage displays follow:
  $$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor \Big|\Big|\, 1$$
- Each percentage integer represents a bucket of roughly $1.7 \dots 2.0$ HP.
- Both sides' max HP values are unobserved.
- Every single damage exchange has 3 to 5 simultaneous unknowns:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lfloor \cdot \rfloor\%$$

### Root Cause 2: The Multi-Turn Recoil-to-Residual-HP Coupling Knot (Turns 3–6)
In Showdown, when a move faints a target, recoil damage is calculated from the **actual HP the target had remaining**, not from the move's full damage roll:
$$\text{Recoil} = \left\lfloor \text{Target Remaining HP} \times \frac{33}{100} \right\rfloor$$

This mechanic creates a tight circular chain across Turns 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6:
1. **Turn 3 Knife-Edge Survival of P2 Rillaboom**:
   - P2 Rillaboom starts Turn 3 at $66/193$ HP (spectator: `34/100`).
   - P1 Rillaboom hits P2 Rillaboom with Grassy Glide, dropping it to $28/193$ HP (spectator: `14/100`).
   - Gardevoir hits Primarina with Hyper Voice, leaving Primarina at $65/176$ HP (spectator: `36/100`).
   - P2 Rillaboom hits Primarina with Wood Hammer, fainting it.
   - P2 Rillaboom takes recoil from Primarina's remaining 65 HP:
     $$\text{Recoil} = \lfloor 65 \times 0.33 \rfloor = 21 \text{ HP}$$
   - P2 Rillaboom is left at $28 - 21 = \mathbf{7/193}$ HP (spectator: `3/100`).
   - **Critical Dependency**: If P2 Rillaboom had taken $> 27$ HP of recoil (or if it had taken 8 more HP damage from Grassy Glide or earlier attacks), **P2 Rillaboom faints on Turn 3**. If it faints on Turn 3, Turns 4, 5, and 6 cannot execute, causing the replay to terminate prematurely.
   - Conversely, if Gardevoir deals more damage to Primarina on Turn 3 (e.g. leaving Primarina at 21/100 instead of 36/100), Primarina has less HP when Wood Hammer hits, producing less recoil and leaving P2 Rillaboom with too much HP (spectator diff: `wanted 3/100, got 4/100` or `wanted 9/100, got 10/100`).
2. **Turn 3 $\rightarrow$ Turn 5 Cross-Turn Recoil Cascade**:
   - On Turn 3, Gardevoir's Hyper Voice also hits P1 Rillaboom down to $32/201$ HP (`15/100`).
   - At end of Turn 3, Grassy Terrain heals P1 Rillaboom to $44/201$ HP (`21/100`).

   - On Turn 5, P2 Rillaboom faints P1 Rillaboom with Wood Hammer.
   - P2 Rillaboom takes recoil from P1 Rillaboom's 44 HP:

   - P2 Rillaboom enters Turn 5 at $31/193$ HP (after Turn 4 Grassy Terrain heal).

### Root Cause 3: Joint Guessing Failure in `choose`
1. In `inferSpreads`, each round attempts to establish an exact HP path using `choose(0, cloneKnowledge(knowledge))`.
2. When evaluating candidate spreads under `channel = 0`:
   - `choose` evaluates `evidencePass({ exact: true })`.
   - `evidencePass` executes `ctx.paths(pre)` to find consistent HP paths matching the spectator percentages.
   - In `paths(raw)`:
     ```js
     const dealt = amounts && e.change.victim ? dealtBy.get(e.change.victim) : null;
     if (dealt && (amounts[j] !== dealt.amount || tag % SPAN !== dealt.v)) continue;
     ```
     Because recoil requires matching `dealt.amount` from the victim's exact remaining HP on the path, independent candidate spreads almost never align on valid recoil amounts.
   - `paths` returns `null`, setting `ctx.exact = null`.
3. `choose` fails to find a valid joint spread within its budget ($32 \times S \times W$ tries).
4. `guessTries` limit is hit, and `choose` returns `null`.
5. As a consequence:
   - `exact` is reset to `null`.
   - `pins` is reset to `null`.
   - No dice are handed on to guide `reconstruct`.

### Root Cause 4: The Fallback Perturbation Loop
Without dice pins, `reconstruct` fails on Turn 1, Turn 2, or Turn 3, triggering `fallback`:
1. In Round 1:
   - Rebuild diffs on Turn 1 Fake Out (`wanted 92/100, got 91/100`).
   - Fallback perturbs Primarina's Def to 32 to absorb Fake Out damage.
2. In Round 2:
   - Rebuild passes Turn 1, but diffs on Turn 2 Grassy Terrain heal for P2 Rillaboom (`wanted 34/100, got 32/100`).
3. In Round 3:
   - Rebuild diffs on Turn 3 Grassy Glide (`wanted 14/100, got 8/100`).
   - Fallback attempts single-stat perturbations on P2 Rillaboom:
     - Perturbing `hp` to 1 stops at Turn 2 Fake Out (`wanted 85/100, got 82/100`).
     - Perturbing `def` to 32 stops at Turn 2 Moonblast (`wanted 27/100, got 17/100`).
     - Perturbing `atk` to 0 does not move the line (`wanted 14/100, got 8/100`).
   - Fallback exhausts its 8 rebuilds: *"nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds"*, hitting `fallbackRebuilds`.
4. In Round 4:
   - Rebuild diffs on Turn 3 Gardevoir Hyper Voice on Primarina (`wanted 36/100, got 21/100`).
   - Primarina's altered Def 32 (with 0 SpD) leaves it vulnerable to Gardevoir's +1 Hyper Voice.
5. Inability of Single-Stat Perturbations:
   - Because the constraint system is coupled circularly across 5 Pokémon, changing one stat on one Pokémon invariably breaks an earlier or later turn in the recoil chain.
   - Fallback repeatedly exhausts its per-round budget (`fallbackRebuilds`) and eventually its total budget (`fallbackTotal`).

### Root Cause 5: Search Stall and Restart Accumulation
1. Each round that fails to advance `deepest` increments `stalled`.
2. After 16 stalled rounds in `default`, the search restarts from `usage`.
3. The `usage` start encounters the same cyclic knot on Turns 1–3 and stalls.
4. The search restarts with `wide` ($4\times$ fallback rebuilds and guess tries), which stalls again.
5. The search restarts with `widest` ($4\times$ all limits), which stalls again.
6. Across all 4 starts, the search executes:
   - **294 rounds**
   - **934 rebuilds**
   - **2,792,702 probes**
   - **13,228.6 seconds (~3.67 hours)**
7. Terminates with `stop = "stalled"`.

### Root Cause 6: Why Gardevoir and Salamence Retained 136.6M Spreads
1. **Salamence (136,663,185 spreads left = 100.0%)**:
   - Switched out on Turn 1 without acting.
   - Re-entered on Turn 4 and fainted from Gardevoir Hyper Voice without acting.
   - Because the scaffold rebuilds never advanced past Turn 3 in a valid state, Turn 4 was never verified by evidence. Zero evidence was recorded against Salamence, leaving its full spread space intact.
2. **Gardevoir (136,663,185 spreads left = 100.0%)**:
   - Used Calm Mind on Turn 1 (stat boost only; 0 damage dealt or received).
   - Used Protect on Turn 2 (blocked moves; 0 damage dealt or received).
   - Its first offensive interaction was Hyper Voice on Turn 3, and its first defensive interaction was taking Zap Cannon on Turn 6.
   - Because the scaffold stalled on Turns 1–3, Gardevoir's Hyper Voice was never verified under an exact HP path, leaving all 136,663,185 spreads uneliminated.
