## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Percentage Truncation Across Both Sides
In Channel 0 (spectator view), neither player's exact HP is visible. All damage and healing events appear only as integer percentages:
$$\text{pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
Because max HP for every Pokémon on both sides is an unobserved variable ($base + SP + 75 \in [base+75, base+107]$), each integer percentage represents a discrete bucket of $1.5 \dots 2.1$ actual HP. Every single damage exchange couples 4 simultaneous unknowns: Attacker offensive stat, Defender defensive stat, Defender max HP, and the RNG damage roll (85% to 100%).

### Root Cause 2: Turn 4 Delphox Heat Wave Primary Target Mismatch Knot
On Turn 4, Delphox was flinched by Rock Slide in the battle protocol (`|cant|p1a: Delphox|flinch`). However, in Showdown's simulation engine and the recorded mimic choice structure:
- Delphox selected `Heat Wave` on Turn 4 before being flinched.
- In ground truth, Delphox targeted `p2b: Sneasler` as its primary target:
  `wanted |move|p1a: Delphox|Heat Wave|p2b: Sneasler|[spread] p2a,p2b`
- When candidate guesses simulate Turn 4, the choice reconstruction heuristic (`MimicPlayer.aim()` or Showdown's automatic target resolution) evaluates P2's side:
  - `p2a: Indeedee-F` is poisoned and sitting on **10/100 HP**.
  - `p2b: Sneasler` is sitting on **66/100 HP**.

### Root Cause 3: Turn 1 Raichu Poison Touch Survival / Switch Order Perturbations in Fallback

1. **Default start**: Stalls on Turn 4/5 after 21 rounds.
