## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The 1-HP Integer Boundary Between `36/161` (`9/100`) and `37/161` (`10/100`)
At `Staraptor`'s pinned `MaxHP = 161` (`hp = 1` SP):
- Burn damage is deterministic: $\lfloor 161 / 16 \rfloor = 10$ HP per turn (`-20` HP across Turn 3 and Turn 4 upkeep).
- Turn 3 `3.12` (`22/100`) admits **two consecutive integers**: $\lfloor 3600 / 161 \rfloor = 22$ and $\lfloor 3700 / 161 \rfloor = 22$.
- Turn 3 upkeep (`-10` HP) maps both to `16/100` (`26/161 = 16.15%`, `27/161 = 16.77%`).
- Turn 4 upkeep (`4.10`, `-10` HP) **splits the two integers**:
  $$16 / 161 = 9.938\% \to \mathbf{9/100} \text{ (wanted)} \qquad\text{vs.}\qquad 17 / 161 = 10.559\% \to \mathbf{10/100} \text{ (got)}$$

Meanwhile, `Staraptor`'s HP after `3.12` is governed by:
$$h_3 = 161 - R_1 - D_{\text{MG}} - R_3$$
where:
- **`R_1` and `R_3` + `D_MG` Coupling**: $R_1 = \text{round}(D_1 \times 0.33)$ is bounded by `1.9` (`72/100` $\implies$ `116` or `117`, so $R_1 \in \{44, 45\}$, verified by `Start 6` line 327 where `hp 0` gave `160 - 45 = 115 = 71/100`), while $R_3 = \text{round}(\text{MaxHP}(\text{Sinistcha}) \times 0.33) \in [48, 59]$ is determined by `Sinistcha`'s `HP` SP (since `3.10` KOed `Sinistcha` from `100/100` HP) and $D_{\text{MG}}$ is determined by `Sinistcha`'s `SpA` SP and `Staraptor`'s `SpD` SP.

Whenever the search guessed a `Sinistcha` spread whose `MaxHP` ($R_3$) and `SpA` ($D_{\text{MG}}$) left `Staraptor` at **`37/161` (`22/100`)** after `3.12` instead of **`36/161` (`22/100`)**, `Staraptor` passed Turn 3 upkeep at **`27/161` (`16/100`)** and missed `4.10` by `+1` HP (**`17/161 = 10/100`**).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Staraptor`'s exact `MaxHP = 161` (`1` SP), `SpD`, and `Atk` are known constants, and Channel 2 prints `Staraptor`'s exact HP integers at every step (`116/161` or `117/161` at `1.9`, exact `h_2/161` at `3.9`, `36/161` at `3.12`, `26/161` after Turn 3 burn, `16/161` at `4.10`). That immediately reveals exact integers $R_1$, $D_{\text{MG}}$, and $R_3 = h_2 - 36$, locking `Sinistcha`'s `MaxHP` bracket and `SpA` in a single evidence pass without cycling.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Sinistcha`'s exact `MaxHP` (fixing $R_3$) and `SpA`, and `Cofagrigus`'s exact `MaxHP` and `Def` (plus Channel 1's exact `Cofagrigus` HP at `1.7`, fixing $R_1$), are known constants. `reconstruct` and `choose()` never need to guess `Sinistcha`, so `Staraptor` cleanly lands on `36/161 -> 16/161 = 9/100`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Include Prior-Turn Recoil Victims and Hit Attackers in `cast` / `lender` for Upkeep Stalls**:
   Turn 4's stall (`4.10: |-damage|p2b: Staraptor|9/100 brn|[from] brn`) depended on Turn 3's recoil victim (`Sinistcha`, fainted at `3.10`) and Turn 3's attacker (`Sinistcha`'s `Matcha Gotcha`), neither of which appeared in Turn 4's protocol segment. Recording each Pokémon's prior HP-moving contributors (recoil targets and prior attackers in `lent` or a turn-agnostic dependency list) would allow `fallback` to perturb `Sinistcha` when `Staraptor`'s HP percentage stalls one HP high on Turn 4.
2. **Order `choose()` (`seq`) by Coupling / Constraint Density Instead of Fixed Slot Order**:
   In `infer.mjs` line 997, `seq` placed unconstrained `Snorlax` (`p1:1`) and Turn-4-arrival `Hawlucha` (`p1:5`) ahead of `Staraptor` (`p2:1`), and placed `Sinistcha` (`p1:3`) before `Staraptor` without early cross-Pokémon pruning until the leaf. Ordering `seq` so tightly coupled or narrowed Pokémon (`Staraptor` with `hp = [1, 1]`, `Cofagrigus` with `hp = [14, 19]`, and `Sinistcha` coupled via `3.12`/`3.9`) are solved together first — and skipping unconstrained Pokémon (`Snorlax`, `Hawlucha`) during the exact-path search — prevents `guessTries` from burning its budget on irrelevant combinations.
3. **Avoid Shifting `picks` Off `MaxHP` Boundaries During `closestSpread` Between Rounds**:
   `Sinistcha` began Usage Start at `HP = 32` (`MaxHP = 178`), and Round 2 moved `Sinistcha` off its `HP` to reproduce `3.10` (`0 fnt`), trapping later rounds with an $R_3$ / $D_{\text{MG}}$ mismatch. Preferring `aimFor` perturbations that preserve `MaxHP` when adjusting bulk (`def`) against a `0 fnt` KO check keeps recoil-coupled targets on the right `Math.round(MaxHP * 0.33)` boundary.
