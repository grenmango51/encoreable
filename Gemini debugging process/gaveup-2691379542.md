## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Cross-Turn Flare Blitz / Recoil / Grassy Terrain Coupling (`1.5` $\leftrightarrow$ `1.7` $\leftrightarrow$ `2.10`)
1. Turn 1 `1.7` (`78/100`) and Turn 1 GT (`84/100`) restrict `Blaziken` (`Atk = 32`, `233`) with `HP = 2..4` (`157..159` HP) such that reaching **`2.10: 89/100`** (`141/157`) **uniquely requires `recoil = 34`** (`157 - 34 = 123/157 = 78.34%` $\to +18 = 141/157 = 89.80\%$). By contrast, `recoil = 33` leaves `124/157 = 78.98%` (`78/100`), which heals to `142/157 = 90.44%` $\to$ **`got 90/100`**.
2. Since Showdown computes recoil as `Math.round(damage * 33 / 100)`, `recoil = 34` requires `Flare Blitz` damage **$d \ge 102$** (`102 * 0.33 = 33.66` $\to$ `34`; `100 * 0.33 = 33`).
3. Meanwhile, Turn 1 `1.5` requires `Farigiraf` to remain at **`55/100`** ($\lfloor 100(M - d)/M \rfloor = 55 \implies d \le 0.45M$). For **any `Farigiraf HP SP <= 31` (`M <= 226`)**, $\lfloor 0.45 \times 226 \rfloor = 101 < 102$, so **every roll at `55/100` produces `recoil = 33` and `2.10 = 90/100`**.
4. **Only `Farigiraf HP SP = 32` (`MaxHP = 227`)** can simultaneously satisfy `1.5 = 55/100` (`227 - 102 = 125/227`), `1.7 = 78/100`, and `2.10 = 89/100`!

### B. Why `evidencePass` & `choose()` Failed on Every `2.10` Round
Whenever a rebuild stopped at `2.10` (`cutoffAt = 2.10`), `evidencePass` read `2.10` (`89/100`) on `Blaziken` (`atCut = true` in `evidence.mjs:2473`), yet `choose()` **exhausted `guessTries` (`32 / 128 / 512`) and returned `exact = null` every single time** (Round 8 in Starts 1, 3, 4, 6; Round 5 in Starts 2, 5; Round 12 in Starts 3, 4, 6):
1. **`totalChange` Backward Propagation Gap (`evidence.mjs:1541, 3881`)**: Because `Blaziken` is unknown (`!S.kn.known`), `totalChange` sets `perA = true` at `1.7` (`recoil`). At that point in the forward pass, `2.10` (`89/100`) has not been read yet, so `after()` sets `Farigiraf`'s `1.5` `dealtOk` to `[99..102]`. When `2.10` later runs and `wholePaths()` walks backward, `Blaziken`'s `alive` map drops `124/157` (`recoil = 33`), **but `wholePaths()` never re-runs `after()` to push `total = 102` back into `Farigiraf`'s `1.5` `dealtOk`**. Consequently, `Farigiraf HP <= 31` is **never eliminated** from `knowledge` even when `Blaziken 2/32` is pinned!

1. **Propagate `totalChange` / `dealtChange` Backward Through `wholePaths()` (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs:1541, 3881`)**:
   When a later display (`2.10: 89/100` on `Blaziken`) eliminates `124/157` (`recoil = 33`) during `wholePaths()`, `after()` is never re-invoked to narrow the source hit's `dealtOk` (`total = 102` on `Farigiraf`'s `1.5`).
