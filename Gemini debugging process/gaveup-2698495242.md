### Root Cause 3: The Grassy Terrain Healing Feedback Loop (Turns 1–10)
Grassy Terrain was active on Turns 1–5 (from P1 Rillaboom) and re-established on Turns 6–10 (from P2 Rillaboom).
In Showdown, Grassy Terrain heals:
$$\text{Heal} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{maxHP}}{16} \right\rfloor$$
At the end of every turn, spectator percentage is:
$$\text{pct}_{\text{end}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{start}} - \text{Damage} + \lfloor \text{maxHP}/16 \rfloor}{\text{maxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
Because the heal is integer-truncated and then the ratio is integer-truncated, a discrepancy of just 1 HP in either incoming damage or max HP alters the percentage:
- Round 2 diff: Turn 1 Grassy Terrain heal on Incineroar (`wanted 91/100, got 90/100`).
- Round 7 diff: Turn 6 Grassy Terrain heal on P2 Rillaboom (`wanted 57/100, got 58/100`).
- Round 10 diff: Turn 4 Grassy Terrain heal on Incineroar (`wanted 98/100, got 97/100`).

### Root Cause 5: Joint Guessing Failure in `choose`
1. In `choose(0, cond)`:

4. As a result:

### Root Cause 6: The Fallback Perturbation Trap
Without dice pins, `reconstruct` runs the unguided guess and diffs on Turn 1, Turn 3, or Turn 4, triggering `fallback(t, diff)`:
1. `fallback` tests single-stat perturbations (0, 32, middle) on unknown Pokémon named by the turn.
2. In Round 5:
   - Rebuild diffs on Turn 4 U-turn on P2 Rillaboom (`wanted 52/100, got 44/100`).
   - Perturbing `Rillaboom hp 18` breaks Turn 3 recoil (`wanted 87/100, got 86/100`).
   - Perturbing `Rillaboom hp 32` breaks Turn 4 (`wanted 52/100, got 45/100`).
   - Perturbing `Rillaboom atk 0` breaks Turn 4 (`wanted 52/100, got 44/100`).
   - Perturbing `Rillaboom atk 32` breaks Turn 3 Wood Hammer (`wanted 56/100, got 51/100`).
   - Fallback exhausts its 8 rebuilds: *"nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds"*, hitting `fallbackRebuilds`.
3. In Round 8:
   - Search drops all the way back to Turn 1 U-turn on Incineroar (`wanted 85/100, got 83/100`).
   - Fallback perturbs Incineroar `def 32` to pass Turn 1.
4. In Round 9:
   - The altered Incineroar Def breaks Turn 4 Grassy Terrain heal (`wanted 98/100, got 97/100`).
5. Because every exchange connects two unknown Pokémon and multiple turns via Grassy Terrain and switches, changing one stat on one Pokémon invariably breaks another turn.

### Root Cause 7: Search Stall and Restart Accumulation
1. Each round that fails to advance `deepest` increments `stalled`.
2. After 16 stalled rounds in `default`, the search restarts from `usage`.
3. `usage` encounters the same cyclic knot on Turns 1–4 and stalls.
4. The search restarts with `wide` ($4\times$ fallback rebuilds and guess tries), which stalls again.
5. The search restarts with `widest` ($4\times$ all limits: maxProbes, backtracks, fallbackTotal, etc.), which stalls again.
6. Across all 4 starts, the search accumulated:

7. The run terminated with `stop = "stalled"` and limits `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "guessTries", "probes", "searches", "stalled"]`.
