1. **Mystical Fire damage** from Hatterene: Takes $D_{\text{Hatt}}$ damage.
   $$\text{HP}_1 = 167 - D_{\text{Hatt}}$$

## 7. Search Limits and Budget Consumption Summary

- **Total Execution Time**: 6,630.3s (~110.5 min) in sweep benchmark; 5,179.0s (~86.3 min) in isolated trace rerun.
- **Rounds**: 78
- **Rebuilds**: 348
- **Probes**: 1,165,334
- **Stop Condition**: `stop = "searches"`
- **Limits Recorded**: `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "guessTries", "probes", "searches"]`
- **Broken Proofs**: `0` (Soundness fully maintained; no true spreads removed).
- **Key Takeaways & Engine Findings**:
  1. **Coupled Multi-Variable Fallback Limitation**:
     - `fallback` in `infer.mjs` operates by varying one stat of one Pokémon along one dimension at a time while holding all other Pokémon's guessed stats constant.
     - When an observed percentage (such as Sandstorm at line 2.23) depends on the composite sum of damage dealt by attacker $A$, damage taken by defender $B$, and drain healed from defender $C$, changing any single parameter in isolation breaks an intermediate threshold (such as line 2.4 Mystical Fire damage or line 2.6 Trick Room turn order).
  2. **Trick Room Sensitivity**:
     - Under Trick Room, slower Pokémon act first. When the engine guesses Tyranitar with Spe SP = 14 (Speed 94) and Sinistcha with Spe SP = 0 (Speed 90), the speed relationship inverts, causing fallback tests to fail at line 2.6 before they can ever evaluate damage at line 2.23.
  3. **Dice Escalation on Deterministic Incompatibilities**:
     - The engine escalated dice search through 4 budget levels and 1.16 million probes. Because stat-driven damage ranges and Trick Room move orders are fundamentally deterministic, spending search budget on dice seeds cannot overcome a structural stat mismatch.
