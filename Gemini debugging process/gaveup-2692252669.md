   - If the observed log shows a -Speed Pokémon moving *after* a faster base-speed opponent under Trick Room (or moving *before* in normal speed), the engine should immediately inject candidate spreads with non-zero Speed SPs ($\ge 10$ SP) into the first round of evidence rather than waiting for search restarts.
2. **Coupled Defense Boost & Leftovers Handling in Fallback**:
   - Fallback currently sweeps one stat at a time (`sps[stat] = val`). For a Pokémon with multiple Coil/Bulk Up boosts and Leftovers recovery, altering Defense without adjusting HP or SpA immediately fails preceding damage thresholds.
   - Fallback should support coupled pairwise probes `(hp, def)` for combatants with active recovery and multi-stage defensive stat boosts.
3. **Cross-View Replay Sharing for Mimic Battles**:
   - In mimic benchmarks where `both@spectator` resolves cleanly in 431.6s, the spectator witness spread can be cross-seeded into single-view solvers (`p1@p2`) as a candidate start, preventing single-view runs from burning 2,300+ seconds in an asymmetric rigidity trap.
