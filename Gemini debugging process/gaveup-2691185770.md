### 1. The 7% Eruption Cliff & Faint Discontinuity

### 2. The Recoil Coupling Seesaw
To prevent Camerupt from fainting, fallback swept through higher Defense and HP for Camerupt (e.g., `Camerupt def 32`, `Camerupt hp 32`):
- When Camerupt's Defense was raised, Camerupt survived Wood Hammer, but took less damage (e.g., `12/100`, `17/100`, or `28/100`).
- However, Rillaboom's recoil is strictly $\lfloor \text{damage dealt to Camerupt} / 3 \rfloor$.
- Reducing damage on Camerupt immediately reduced Rillaboom's recoil:
  `wanted |-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|60/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|61/100|[from] Recoil`
- The engine was trapped in a binary contradiction:
  - Low Camerupt Defense $\implies$ Camerupt dies (`0 fnt`), Eruption lost.
  - High Camerupt Defense $\implies$ Camerupt survives, but Rillaboom recoil is wrong (`61/100` vs `60/100`).

### 3. Hyper-Sensitivity of Eruption's Dynamic Base Power
Because Eruption base power scales as $\lfloor 150 \times \text{currentHP} / \text{maxHP} \rfloor$:
- At the true battle value of 13 HP, $\text{BP} = 11$.
- If Camerupt takes slightly less damage and is left at 20 HP (`12/100`), $\text{BP} = \lfloor 150 \times 20 / 177 \rfloor = 16$ (a **45% increase** in move power!).
- If Camerupt takes slightly more damage and is left at 11 HP, $\text{BP} = 9$ (an **18% decrease** in move power).
- Because Eruption hits both Raichu and Rillaboom simultaneously, any shift in Camerupt's remaining HP drastically alters the damage rolls on BOTH defenders:
  - Raichu diverges: `wanted 31/100, got 30/100` or `got 29/100`.
  - Rillaboom diverges: `wanted 43/100, got 47/100`.
