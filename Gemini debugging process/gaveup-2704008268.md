## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### View Asymmetry
- **`both@spectator` (Channel 0, `PASS` in 118.2s) & `p2@p1` (Channel 1, `PASS`)**: With P2 free to adjust (or P1 fixed to ground truth in `p2@p1`), the inference avoids the Channel 2 rigid wall on `Gholdengo` vs. known `Sneasler` / `Rillaboom` / `Raichu` and finishes cleanly (in `search 2`, Round 13 for `both@spectator`).
- **`p1@p2` (Channel 2, `GAVE UP` in 1,488.3s)**: Fixing P2 to ground truth and exposing exact integer HP on P2 (`Sneasler` `1/maxhp` non-Sash survival, `Rillaboom` Speed) couples all four active P1 stats (`Gholdengo`'s `hp/def/spd/spa/spe`, `Tyranitar`'s `spe`) so tightly that `budgets[0..3]` across all 6 starts fail to reproduce the battle.

### Architectural Takeaways
1. **Trace Dispatch Script Must Target the Failing View(s) from `gave_up_list.json`**:
   `batch_35_next.txt` / the cluster trace dispatcher ran `--views both@spectator` even though `slowest_gave_up.json` explicitly recorded `views: ["p1@p2"]` for `2704008268`. Always passing the failing view(s) from `gave_up_list.json` (`--views p1@p2`) avoids wasted trace runs that pass on the wrong view.
2. **Prevent `fallback` from Blacklisting Late-Turn Diffs on `reach(base)[0] < t` (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`, lines 614–618)**:
   As seen in Round 12 (line 45), when `fallback` evaluates a Turn 8 failure, `base` runs with `maxBacktracks: 0`. If `base` regresses to Turn 5 (`5.12`) and `dice` (`budgets[1]`) does not complete Turn 8, `infer.mjs` executes `return none()` and marks Turn 8 `fruitless` (`spend.fruitless.add(mark)`) without probing a single stat. Allowing `fallback` to fall back to `pins` from the round that reached Turn `t` (or probing when `dice` reaches Turn `t` / before blacklisting `mark`) would keep late-turn stalls searchable.
3. **Multi-Stat 66-SP Coupling Around Non-Lethal `1/maxhp` Windows**:
   When a Channel 2 exact-HP hit forces an attacker (`Gholdengo`) into a narrow `spa` range while `hp`/`def`/`spe` are simultaneously constrained by percentage Life Orb thresholds (`87/100`, `19/100`) and Speed brackets, single-stat `fallback` moves frequently collide with the 66-SP cap. Jointly relaxing coupled stats on the same Pokémon (or prioritizing `spa`/`spe` alongside `(hp, def)` in `choose()`) reduces futile cycling across search tiers.
