### Root Cause 5: Search Algorithm Failure Mode (`choose`, `fallback`, and `stalled`)
1. **Joint Guessing Failure in `choose`**:
   - `choose` attempts to pin candidate spreads sequentially across the 8 active Pokémon (`seq`).
   - After pinning all 8 Pokémon, it evaluates `evidencePass({ exact: true })` over the verified turns.
   - Because the 8 Pokémon are coupled in a circular constraint graph under spectator percentages, picking near-default or independent candidate spreads almost never yields a joint set where every verified turn has an exact HP path.
   - `tries` (budgeted at $32 \times S \times W$) is exhausted in every round, triggering the `guessTries` limit and returning `null`.
2. **Loss of Exact Proof and Dice Pins**:
   - When `choose` returns `null`, `exact` is reset to `null` and `pins` is reset to `null`.
   - Without exact HP pins, `reconstruct` must search the dice space unguided.
   - `reconstruct` encounters a diff on Turn 1, Turn 2, or Turn 3 (such as the Archaludon vs Sinistcha speed inversion on Turn 4).
3. **Fallback Rebuild Exhaustion**:
   - The diff triggers `fallback`.
   - `fallback` tries local perturbations on the Pokémon involved in the diff.
   - However, fixing the diff on Turn 4 (e.g., boosting Sinistcha's Speed to 106) breaks Turn 5 or Turn 6 (due to the drain heal / Sitrus Berry / Dragon Pulse survival knot).
   - `fallback` exhausts its per-invocation budget (`spent >= FALLBACK_REBUILDS * S * W`, hitting `fallbackRebuilds`) and eventually exhausts the global fallback budget (`spend.rebuilds <= 0`, hitting `fallbackTotal`).
4. **Stall Counter Accumulation**:
   - Each round that fails to advance past `deepest` increments `stalled`.
   - After 16 rounds in `default`, the search restarts from `usage`.
   - `usage` encounters the same circular knot and exhausts its budget.
   - The search restarts with `wide` ($4\times$ fallback rebuilds and guess tries), and then `widest` ($4\times$ all limits).
   - None of the restarts find a valid joint spread that passes all 7 turns.
   - Across all restarts, the search accumulates 300 rounds, 924 rebuilds, and 1,178,480 probes before terminating with `stop = "stalled"` after 16,892.4 seconds (4.69 hours).
