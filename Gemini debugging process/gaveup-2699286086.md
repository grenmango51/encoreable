## 8. The Turn 11 Double-Drain Bottleneck, Circular Dependency, and Search Regression Mechanics

### The Turn 11 Multi-Dependency Drain Bottleneck
On Turn 11, Sinistcha executes Matcha Gotcha on Garchomp and Basculegion under Trick Room. In Channel 0, two consecutive drain heal percentages must match simultaneously:
1. `|-heal|p1b: Sinistcha|57/100|[from] drain|[of] p2a: Garchomp`
2. `|-heal|p1b: Sinistcha|58/100|[from] drain|[of] p2b: Basculegion`

In Pokémon Showdown mechanics, draining moves heal $\lfloor \text{damage dealt} / 2 \rfloor$, where damage dealt is capped at the victim's actual remaining HP.
- Garchomp's remaining HP ($HP_G$) is the result of Turn 2 Fake Out, Turn 2 Heat Wave crit, Turn 2 Sitrus Berry, and Turn 11 Kingambit Sucker Punch (7 HP in ground truth).
- Basculegion's remaining HP ($HP_B$) is the result of Turn 2 Heat Wave, Turn 8 Wave Crash recoil (dependent on Delphox remaining HP and Garchomp Dragon Claw), Turn 9 Rocky Helmet (dependent on Basculegion max HP), Turn 9 Wave Crash recoil (dependent on Sinistcha Def), and Turn 10 Wave Crash recoil (dependent on Kingambit Def) (6 HP in ground truth).
- Sinistcha's HP going into Turn 11 ($HP_{S9} = 99/178$) is the result of Turn 3 Rock Slide, Turn 9 Rock Slide, and Turn 9 Wave Crash.

The percentage displayed on Sinistcha after the second drain is:
$$\left\lfloor 100 \times \frac{HP_{S9} + \lfloor HP_G / 2 \rfloor + \lfloor HP_B / 2 \rfloor}{M_S} \right\rfloor$$
In ground truth:
$$\frac{99 + 3 + 3}{178} = \frac{105}{178} \approx 58.988\% \implies \mathbf{58/100}$$

Notice the extreme sensitivity: $105 / 178$ is $58.988\%$, just $0.012\%$ below $59\%$.
- If Basculegion has 8 HP remaining instead of 6 HP (caused by a slight damage roll variation in any of Turns 2, 8, 9, or 10), its drain heal becomes $\lfloor 8 / 2 \rfloor = 4$ HP.
