## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across Active Combatants
In Channel 0:
- All 8 Pokémon brought into the battle engaged in combat exchanges.
- Showdown Champions mode calculates shared spectator percentages via integer truncation:
  $$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- No exact damage, exact HP, or recoil values are visible to the spectator.
- Every exchange presents simultaneous unknown variables on both sides:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown maxHP} \rightarrow \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{HP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk/SpA, Def/SpD, maxHP, roll)` yield identical percentages (e.g. `18/100`, `65/100`, `74/100`), `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads. Metagross retained 121.1M spreads, Sneasler 136.7M, Camerupt 136.7M, Mr. Mime 105.1M, and Milotic 105.3M.

### Root Cause 2: Turn 8 Mirror Coat Reflection Coupling (The Critical Bottleneck)
The fatal pathology of this match occurs on Turn 8:
1. On Turn 8, Sneasler uses Rock Slide on Camerupt (`65/100`), Camerupt uses Earth Power on Milotic (`74/100`), and Milotic uses Mirror Coat on Camerupt (`18/100`).
2. In Showdown's mechanics, Mirror Coat is not a normal damage move with base power and stat ratios; its damage is **strictly deterministic and integer-coupled**:
   $$\text{Damage}_{\text{Mirror Coat}} = 2 \times \text{Actual Integer Damage Taken by User}$$
3. In the ground-truth battle:
   - Milotic had 188 HP after Turn 6 Recover.
   - Earth Power dealt exactly 37 HP damage to Milotic (`188 -> 151/202 = 74.7% -> 74%`).
   - Mirror Coat dealt exactly $2 \times 37 = 74$ HP damage to Mega Camerupt.
   - Mega Camerupt took 32 HP from Rock Slide (`135 -> 103/157 = 65.6% -> 65%`).
   - Mirror Coat then reduced Camerupt to $103 - 74 = 29$ HP (`29/157 = 18.47% -> 18%`).
4. In the spectator inference search:
   - The solver generates joint guesses for Camerupt and Milotic.
   - If a guess produces slightly higher bulk for Milotic (or lower SpA for Camerupt): Earth Power deals, for example, 30 HP instead of 37 HP (`188 -> 158/202 = 78%` or `151/202 = 74%` depending on max HP).
   - Mirror Coat then reflects only $2 \times 30 = 60$ HP!
