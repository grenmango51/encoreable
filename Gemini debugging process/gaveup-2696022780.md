## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why `fallback` Aborted at `2.12`

### A. Turn 2 Speed Coupling Pinned `Archaludon` Too Slow (`Spe = 3`) Against Max-Speed `Garchomp` (`Spe = 32`)
On Turn 2, `p2a: Pelipper` (`Modest`, Base Spe `65`) moved before `p2b: Archaludon` (`Modest`, Base Spe `85`). Since both were under `Tailwind`, `Pelipper`'s Speed SP had to exceed `Archaludon`'s Speed SP by at least `20` (`65 + Spe_Pel + 20 > 85 + Spe_Arc` $\implies$ `Spe_Pel > Spe_Arc + 20`).
- `aimFor()` settled `Pelipper` at `Spe SP = 26` (`111` Spe, `222` in Tailwind) and `Archaludon` at `Spe SP = 3` (`108` Spe, `216` in Tailwind) — valid for Turn 2 (`222 > 216`).
- Meanwhile, nothing on Turns 0–2 constrained `Garchomp`'s Speed against `Archaludon`, so `aimFor()` / `usage` kept `Garchomp` at `Spe SP = 32` (**223 Spe** for `Garchomp-Mega-Z`).
- On Turn 3, `Archaludon` needed `2 * (105 + Spe_Arc) >= floor((171 + Spe_Gar) * 1.1)` (e.g., `Pelipper Spe = 32` [`234`] with `Archaludon Spe = 7..11` [`224..232 > 223`], or `Garchomp Spe <= 25` [`<= 215 < 216`]).
- Because Turn 3 diverged at `|cant|p2b: Archaludon|flinch` BEFORE `speed.mjs` could observe a Turn 3 speed sort (`prefix.cutoffAt` stopped right at the divergence), `evidencePass` could not read the `Archaludon > Garchomp` speed constraint, leaving `picks` frozen at `Garchomp Spe = 32` and `Archaludon Spe = 3`.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p1@p2` and `p2@p1` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2:2 Archaludon`'s exact `Spe` and `SpA`, `p2:0 Pelipper`'s exact `Spe` and `SpA`, and `p2:1 Basculegion`'s exact `Atk`/`Spe` are known constants, and Channel 2 prints P2's exact `hp/maxhp` integers. Knowing `Archaludon`'s true `Spe` immediately lets `Pelipper` (`Spe >= Archaludon + 20`) and `Garchomp` (`<= Archaludon * 2` under Tailwind) satisfy Turn 3 without needing `fallback` to recover the Turn 3 speed order.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:2 Rillaboom`'s exact `HP` and `SpD` (`Sitrus Berry` + `Grassy Terrain` exact integers) and `p1:0 Garchomp`'s exact `Spe` are known constants, and Channel 1 prints `Rillaboom`'s exact HP after `Weather Ball` and `Electro Shot` on Turn 2 (`no 2.12 percentage ambiguity`), while `Garchomp`'s known `Spe` directly prevents `fallback` from getting stuck at `2.12`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Discard `exact` / `proof.rolls` in `fallback`'s `rebuild()` (`infer.mjs` lines 492, 499)**:
   `fallback` sets `exact: null` and `pins: handOn(built, null)` when rebuilding `start` through Turn `t`. Here, both Default Round 7 and Usage Round 2 **had already reproduced Turn 2** under `exact` + `proof.rolls` and reached Turn 3 (`|cant|p2b: Archaludon|flinch`), yet `fallback`'s `base` rebuild with `exact: null` and `handOn(built, null)` got stuck at `2.12` (`45/100` vs `48/100`). Passing `exact` through `t - 1` (or preserving the verified `t - 1` `proof.rolls` / snapshot from `built`) prevents `fallback` from aborting on an already-verified earlier turn and blacklisting `t`'s stall line in `spend.fruitless` before testing a single `spe` perturbation.
2. **Allow Turn-3 Speed Perturbation When `reach(base)[0] < t` Was Already Bypassed by `built`**:
   In `infer.mjs` line 614, `if (reach(base)[0] < t) return none();` assumes `start` cannot reach turn `t` — even though `built` in the immediately preceding round *did* reach turn `t` (`built.report.diffs[0].turn === t`) with the exact same spreads (`start === picks`). When `built` already reached `t`, `fallback` should never treat a pre-`t` failure in an unpinned/non-`exact` `rebuild` as grounds to abandon `t` and skip perturbing `Archaludon spe` / `Garchomp spe`.
