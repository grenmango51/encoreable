## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. How `evidencePass` (`sync()` + `provenLines()`) Failed to Rule Out Focus Sash at `1.19`
In Rounds 1–4 of `default`/`wide`/`widest` starts (and Rounds 1–3 of `usage` starts), `evidencePass` and `choose()` resolved lines `1.0`, `1.8`, `1.11`, `1.14`, `1.15`, and `1.18`, landing on the same stable plateau at Turn 1 `1.19`:
- **Default / Wide Guess**: `p2:3 Kingambit 32/0/0/0/0/1`, `p1:1 Charizard 12/0/19/23/0/12`, `p2:5 Floette 0/7/0/29/4/26`, `p1:3 Kingambit 17/29/0/0/13/2`
- **Usage Guess**: `p2:3 Kingambit 32/0/0/0/2/0`, `p1:1 Charizard 12/0/19/23/0/12`, `p2:5 Floette 0/7/0/29/4/26`, `p1:3 Kingambit 17/29/0/0/13/0`

When Round 5 (`default`) and Round 4 (`usage`) reached line `1.19`, the rebuild printed `|-enditem|p2b: Kingambit|Focus Sash` at `prefix.cutoffAt`, followed by `|-damage|p2b: Kingambit|1/100`, whereas the replay printed only `|-damage|p2b: Kingambit|1/100`. Two interacting behaviors in `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` prevented `evidencePass` from rejecting Focus Sash:
1. **`provenLines()` Returned `null` at Cutoff (lines 1105–1118)**: Because `|-enditem|p2b: Kingambit|Focus Sash` falls inside `Damage` (`seen.from <= prefix.cutoffAt < seen.to`), `for (...; view[i].at < seen.to; i++) if (view[i].at >= prefix.cutoffAt) return null` disabled `shown`, bypassing `sameLines(ev.lines, shown)` in `moveChange()` (line 1182).
2. **`sync()` `awaited` Accepted `1/100` After `|-enditem|` with `survived = false` (lines 2469, 2502–2507)**: At `atCut`, `sync()` matched `survivor('|-enditem|p2b: Kingambit|Focus Sash') === 'p2:Kingambit'` to `want` (`|-damage|p2b: Kingambit|1/100`), set `awaited = 'p2:Kingambit'`, and on the very next entry called `observe(mine, '1/100', entry.at)` with **`survived = false`**.

Because `1` HP left by Focus Sash satisfies `interval(rec, M, '1/100') = [1, 3]` when `shown === null` and `survived === false`, `evidencePass` removed zero spreads from `(Charizard spa = 23, p2b: Kingambit hp = 32, spd = 0/2)` and handed back `2 turns exact, 6 dice handed on` for the **unchanged** guess (`unchanged = true`), freezing rounds 6–8 in an identical loop (`search 2`, `search 3`, `search 4` $\to$ `stop = 'searches'`).

### B. Why `fallback` Failed to Escape `1.19` Across 8, 32, and 128 Rebuilds

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2b: Kingambit`'s exact bulk (`207 HP, 118 SpD`) and `Floette`'s exact stats (`151 HP, 87/107 Def, 227 SpA, 144 Spe`) are fixed ground truth, and Channel 2 reports P2's exact integers (`3/207` at `1.19` without Focus Sash!). Knowing `p2b: Kingambit` took `204` damage cleanly forces `Charizard`'s `spa` down near `1` SP on the very first pass.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1b: Charizard`'s exact `SpA = 198` (`spa = 1` SP) and `Spe = 141` are fixed ground truth, and Channel 1 prints exact integers on `p1a: Kingambit` (`99/207`) and `p1b: Charizard` (`82/173` $\to$ `42/173`). With `Charizard SpA = 198` fixed, `p2b: Kingambit` cannot survive Heat Wave at `spd = 0` (`228..270` damage), immediately forcing `p2b: Kingambit`'s `hp` and `spd` up into the `[204, 206]` non-Focus-Sash survival bracket (`spd >= 13` at `hp = 32`).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` & `infer.mjs`)
1. **Treat Missing Survival Announcements at `prefix.cutoffAt` as Lethal/Non-Survival Evidence in `evidencePass` (`evidence.mjs` lines 1105–1118, 2469, 2502–2507)**:
   When the replay prints a direct HP line (`|-damage|p2b: Kingambit|1/100`) at `prefix.cutoffAt` while the rebuild prints a survival item/ability announcement (`|-enditem|p2b: Kingambit|Focus Sash`) first, `provenLines()` returns `null` and `sync()` sets `awaited` and calls `observe(..., survived = false)`. This leaves the scaffold's own Focus-Sash-triggering spreads uneliminated, stalling `inferSpreads` in an `unchanged` loop. When `prefix.observedLine` is a plain `|-damage|` line and the rebuild emitted a `SURVIVAL` line (`Focus Sash`, `Sturdy`, `Endure`) immediately before its HP line, `moveChange` knows the real `Damage` event did **not** emit that announcement and can safely reject candidates whose `Damage` event triggers it (or require `raw < h`).
2. **Prevent `replayWith` `same` Check from Skipping `rebuild()` on `SURVIVAL` Mismatches (`infer.mjs` line 678)**:
   Just as in parity-sensitive percentage boundaries, `replayWith` tested `p2:3 Kingambit spd 32` using Round 5's single unpinned Heat Wave roll (`r >= 5`), which still triggered Focus Sash and returned `same = true`, skipping `rebuild()` even though rolls `r = 3, 4` (`204`, `206`) survive cleanly at `1/100`! Disabling the `replayWith` shortcut on the failing hit's turn (or whenever `diff.actual` / `diff.expected` involves a `SURVIVAL` announcement or percentage HP threshold on the failing turn `t`) would let `fallback` immediately rebuild `Kingambit spd 32` and solve `1.19` in 1 rebuild instead of exhausting 240 rebuilds across 6 starts.
