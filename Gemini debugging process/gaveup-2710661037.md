## 6. Spectator Failure Analysis (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Coarse Percentage Rounding Across Cascading Stat Interactions
In spectator view (`Channel 0`), neither player's set is known (`known = [false, false]`). All HP updates are reported strictly as rounded percentages:
$$\text{pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{current\_hp}}{\text{max\_hp}} \right\rfloor$$

Across Turns 1 through 3, multiple interlocked damage and recovery equations compound simultaneously:
1. **Turn 1 Fake Out**: P1 Sneasler Atk vs P2 Sneasler Def (`73/100` wanted vs `78/100` got).
2. **Turn 1 Flare Blitz & Recoil**: P2 Incineroar Atk (-1) vs P1 Corviknight Def (+1) (`64/100` damage), with Incineroar taking $\lfloor \text{max\_hp} / 6 \rfloor$ Rocky Helmet and $\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor$ recoil (`72/100` wanted vs `71/100` got).
3. **Turn 1 & 2 Burn**: Corviknight taking $\lfloor \text{max\_hp} / 16 \rfloor$ burn damage across consecutive turns.
4. **Turn 3 Hospitality**: Sinistcha restoring $\lfloor \text{max\_hp} / 4 \rfloor$ HP to Corviknight.
5. **Turn 3 Double-Hit**: Sneasler Rock Slide spread (0.75x) + Gengar Shadow Ball into Corviknight.
6. **Turn 3 Brave Bird & Recoil**: Corviknight (+1 Atk, burned 0.5x) into Gengar (Base 60 HP, Base 60 Def), with Corviknight taking $\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor$ recoil.

Because every operation truncates independently, a 1-point difference in max HP or Defense flips display percentages across multiple combatants.

### Root Cause 2: Turn 3 PRNG Dice Flinch / Speed Order Entanglement
Turn 3 presents an acute PRNG ordering bottleneck:
1. **Speed Order Ambiguity**:
   - P2 Sneasler (Base Spe 120) and P2 Gengar (Base Spe 110) are on the same side.
   - If Gengar's Speed Stat Points are guessed higher than Sneasler's, Gengar executes Shadow Ball before Sneasler executes Rock Slide (`wanted |move|p2a: Sneasler|Rock Slide..., got |move|p2b: Gengar|Shadow Ball...`), breaking line 3.2.
2. **Rock Slide Spread Flinch Roll**:
   - When Sneasler correctly moves first, Rock Slide consumes accuracy, critical hit, damage, and secondary flinch rolls (30% probability) for both targets.
   - In the target log, Corviknight does NOT flinch.
   - When the search runs with 5 or 6 forced dice handed on, the flinch roll is unconstrained and resolves to a flinch (`|cant|p1b: Corviknight|flinch`), prematurely aborting Turn 3 before Corviknight can execute Brave Bird.

### Root Cause 3: The 5-Round Cyclical Attractor (Flinch vs Gengar 0 fnt)
