## 7. View Contrast (`both@spectator` vs. `p2@p1`) & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `both@spectator` Passed While `p2@p1` Gave Up
- **In `both@spectator`**: Both `p1:Raichu` and `p2:Incineroar` are free variables. Even though `Incineroar` was guessed slow (`spe = 2`, Tailwind `164`), `Raichu` was in `disputed` (named by `|cant|p1a: Raichu|flinch`), so `fallback` rebuilt `Raichu spe 0` (`143 < 164`) in Start 3 (`wide`) and resolved Turn 3's `+3` priority `Fake Out` race without ever raising `Incineroar`'s Speed.
- **In `p2@p1`**: `p1:Raichu` is fixed at its true `Spe = 178`. The **only** way to satisfy Turn 3 `3.3` is to raise `p2:Incineroar` (`spe >= 9`, Tailwind $\ge 180$). However, `Incineroar` was excluded from `disputed`, causing `replayWith` to falsely skip every `Incineroar spe` probe (`same = true`) against `built.inputLog`'s `Volt Tackle` (`0` priority) placeholder.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Treat Any Speed-Race Candidate on a `hidden` Turn as `disputed` in `replayWith` (`infer.mjs` lines 671, 677)**:
   `disputed` currently includes only `[actorOf(diff.expected), actorOf(diff.actual)]`. On Turn 3 (`|cant|p1a: Raichu|flinch`), the Pokémon whose priority move *caused* the flinch (`p2b: Incineroar` using `+3` `Fake Out`) is an active participant in the `+3` priority speed race with `Raichu`'s hidden move (`Fake Out`), yet `Incineroar` is absent from line `3.3` and thus excluded from `disputed`. Whenever `(built.report.hidden || []).includes(t)` and `order` is true (or `diff.expected` is `|cant|...|flinch` / paralysis), **no Speed (`spe`) probe of any Pokémon active in turn `t` should be skipped via `replayWith`** — changing their Speed can make a previously rejected invisible-choice variant (like `+3` `Fake Out`) legal where `built.inputLog` only recorded the `0`-priority fallback (`Volt Tackle`).
2. **Prioritize Hidden-Flinch / Priority-Bracket Causes in `fallback` `cast` (`infer.mjs` lines 576–580)**:
   When `diff.expected` is `|cant|<slot>|flinch`, the Pokémon that used `Fake Out` (or another flinch move) earlier in the turn (`|move|<flincher>|...|` targeting `<slot>`) is directly coupled to `<slot>`'s move bracket and should be placed in `near` (`rank = 0` or `1`) ahead of uninvolved Turn-3 actors.
3. **Trace-to-Sweep Alignment Note**:
   When investigating single-view failures from `slowest_gave_up.json`, pass `--views <failing_view>` (here `--views p2@p1`) to `npm run score -- --trace` rather than `--views both@spectator`, since `both@spectator` can mask single-view deadlocks by shifting the opponent's free Stat Points (`Raichu spe 0`).
