## 4. Search Limits Hit & Stopping Reason

### Stopping Reason: `stop = "stalled"` (and `stop = "searches"`)
In `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`:
- Across all inference rounds, candidate spreads are evaluated by re-simulating the battle using `reconstruct()`.
- The deepest verified turn never advanced past Turn 3 / early Turn 4 (`deepest = [3, ...]`).
- Each round that failed to advance `deepest` incremented `stalled++`.
- When `stalled >= maxRounds` (default 16, scaled by budget), the search terminated with `stop = "stalled"`.
- When candidate bounds ceased tightening across search budget levels, `level` escalated through `budgets[0, 1, 2, 3]`, hitting the `searches` limit.

### Root Engine Defect: Single-Turn Weather Execution of Electro Shot & Poisoned Variant Rewalking
Detailed investigation revealed that **even with 100% ground-truth spreads**, `reconstruct()` fails completely on Turn 4 with `stop: 'stuck'`. This failure stems from a cascading interaction in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`:

1. **Protocol Target Suppression for Weather-Boosted Charge Moves**:
   - In Pokémon Showdown, when `Electro Shot` executes under Rain, the charge turn is bypassed and the move executes in a single turn.
   - Showdown logs the move line without a target:
     ```
     |move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]
     |-prepare|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot
     |-boost|p2b: Archaludon|spa|1
     |-anim|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot|p1b: Basculegion
     ```
   - The target is omitted from `|move|` (`parts[4] === ''`) and is only declared in the subsequent `|-anim|` line.

2. **Charge Target Recovery Failure (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` line 208)**:
   - `planSegment()` parses the move line and sets `action.target = null`.
   - `recoverChargeTargets()` attempts to resolve missing targets on charge moves, but only looks forward to future turns:
     ```javascript
     for (const later of plans.slice(k + 1)) { ... }
     ```
   - Because `recoverChargeTargets` assumes a 2-turn charging sequence, it never checks `|-anim|` on the *same turn* (`k`). Consequently, `action.target` remains `null`.

3. **Suboptimal Target Variant Defaulting**:
   - With `action.target = null`, `reconstruct.mjs` calls `targetOptions(side, i, moveTarget, preferred = null)`.
   - In doubles, `targetOptions` defaults to `[' +1', ' +2', ' -1']`.
   - Variant 0 selects index 0: `+1` (aiming at `p1a: Archaludon`), whereas the ground-truth choice was `+2` (aiming at `p1b: Basculegion`).

4. **Destructive Fallback Die Commitment**:
   - Under Variant 0 (`+1`), `steerTurn` correctly settles earlier dice (Dire Claw damage die 21=5, Muddy Water miss die 25=99, Muddy Water crit die 27=0, and damage die 28=5).
   - However, Variant 0 cannot advance past index 15 because Archaludon attacks `p1a` instead of `p1b`.
   - Stalled at index 15, `steerTurn` executes fallback logic on remaining unsettled dice. It grabs die 29 (the 30% secondary effect accuracy check of Muddy Water: `random(100) < 30`) and commits candidate 0 (`29=0`).
   - Pinning die 29 to 0 forces Muddy Water to trigger its 30% accuracy reduction (`|-unboost|p2b: Archaludon|accuracy|1`), introducing an artificial mismatch at index 10.

5. **Variant Rewalk Contamination (`rewalk` at line 2454)**:
   - `reconstruct.mjs` saves `partial.subs` (which now contains poisoned die `29=0`) and re-evaluates the other target variants using `rewalk`.
   - When Variant 3 (`move 1 +2, move 1 +2`, the actual ground-truth choice) is evaluated, it inherits `29=0`.
   - As a result, Muddy Water unboosts Archaludon's accuracy in Variant 3 as well, immediately producing:
     ```
     expected: '|-ability|p2b: Archaludon|Stamina|boost', actual: '|-unboost|p2b: Archaludon|accuracy|1'
     ```
   - `rewalk` judges Variant 3 as failing at index 10 (inferior to Variant 0's index 15) and discards it without ever searching it.
   - `reconstruct()` completely fails on Turn 4 across every possible candidate spread.
