## 6. Mutual Coupling, Bottlenecks, and Search Failure in `both@spectator`

1. P2a Rillaboom attacks P1b Annihilape with Wood Hammer:

### The Extreme Boundary Sensitivity on Line 5.10 (`74/100` vs `75/100`)
- Rillaboom's post-recoil HP is at `69/100`.
- Grassy Terrain restores `floor(MaxHP_rilla / 16)`, which represents $6.25\%$ of Max HP.
- Nominally: $69\% + 6.25\% = 75.25\%$, which floored to an integer percentage naturally evaluates to **`75/100`**.
- For the post-heal percentage to be exactly **`74/100`**, the combination of:
  - Rillaboom's Max HP,
  - Annihilape's Max HP,
  - Annihilape's Defense,
  - Rillaboom's Attack,
  must place Rillaboom's integer HP after recoil at the exact lower boundary of $69\%$ (e.g. $69.0\%$) such that adding `floor(MaxHP / 16)` remains strictly below $75.0\%$.
- Any spread that causes $D$ to be even 1 HP lower, or Rillaboom's Max HP to differ by 1 SP, produces:
  `wanted |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|74/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|75/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`.
- This exact mismatch (`74/100 vs 75/100`) caused the inference search to stall in every single search stage (Rounds 8, 13, 16, 17, 18, 19).

2. The recursive function `choose(n, cond)` in `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` has a total try budget of `tries = 32 * S * W = 64` (and up to 256 in wide stages).

4. `choose()` exhausts all tries (`limits.add('guessTries')`) and returns `null`.
