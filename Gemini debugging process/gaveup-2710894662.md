## 6. The Anatomy of Search Failure in `both@spectator`


### Trap 2: The Turn 4 Last Respects Overdamage Collision (`10/100` vs `1/100`)

$$\text{Damage} \propto \frac{\text{Atk}_{\text{Basculegion 1}}}{\text{Def}_{\text{Basculegion 2}}} \times \text{BasePower}(50 + 50 \times 1) \times \text{Adaptability}(2.0) \times \text{Type}(2.0)$$

  - P2 Basculegion Def = 96, Max HP = 195
