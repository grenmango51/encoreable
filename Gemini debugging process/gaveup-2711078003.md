## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

This case exposes two distinct failure modes on Turn 1's 4-hit **Beat Up** sequence (`1.4`–`1.16`), one killing the **`usage` starts (Starts 2 & 5)** and the other killing the **`default` / `wide` starts (Starts 1, 3, 4, 6)**.

### A. Why Evidence Skips Beat Up Hits 3 & 4 (`evidence.mjs` lines 894–900, 966, 972)
In `evidencePass` (`analyseHit`), `evidence.mjs` deliberately watches `p.set` on every non-active party member (`!p.isActive`) during `dryRow`:
```js
// Beat Up's power is read off the set of each Pokemon it calls on, and
// who stands in the back of the party, and where, is a choice the
// replay never shows: the rebuild's is one guess among others.
...
supported: supported && !carried && !back.size,
```
On Turn 1, `Lucario` and `Weavile` are active, while `Primarina` and `Rillaboom` sit in the back of P2's party. Thus:
- **Hits 1 & 2 (`1.4` and `1.8`)** have `back.size === 0` (`supported: true`) and are read exactly via `moveChange`.
- **Hits 3 & 4 (`1.12` and `1.16`)** read `Primarina.set` and `Rillaboom.set` (`hit.back === true`, `supported: false`), so **`evidencePass` discards their damage calculations and models both hits as opaque `band('down')` transitions** (allowing any `h_after <= h_before` that prints `89/100` and `84/100`).

### B. How Starts 2 & 5 (`usage`) Reached Turn 3 in Round 1, Then Deadlocked Forever on Turn 1
1. **Round 1 (`line 34`)**: Starting from #1 usage spreads put **all four P2 Pokémon at their exact ground-truth spreads** (`2/32/0/0/0/32`, `2/32/0/0/0/32`, `2/32/0/0/0/32`, `32/0/0/32/2/0`), reproducing Turns 0, 1, and 2 cleanly and stopping on Turn 3 (`Gholdengo` Life Orb `81/100` vs `80/100`).
2. **66-SP Shedding + `band('down')` Unreachable `exact` Line (`130/145` at `1.12`, line 36)**:
   - After Round 1's `evidencePass` (which saw Turn 3 `Hyper Voice` on `Lucario-Mega`), `choose()` aimed `Lucario`'s `spd` above `0` (e.g., `2`) while keeping `atk = 32` and `spe = 32`. Because `2 + 32 + 0 + 0 + 2 + 32 = 68 > 66`, `closestSpread` shed `2` SP from `hp` (`2 -> 0`, lowest key index first), picking **`Lucario 0/32/0/0/2/32` (`MaxHP = 145`, `Def = 90`)**.
   - At `MaxHP = 145`, `89/100` at Hit 3 (`1.12`) holds **only for `h_3 = 130`** (`129/145 = 88%`, `131/145 = 90%`), requiring total 3-hit damage of **`145 - 130 = 15`**. However, with `Weavile` (`Atk = 172`, `-1` = `114`) vs `Lucario` (`Def = 90`), the minimum possible 3-hit sum is **`6 (Lucario BP 16) + 6 (Weavile BP 17) + 4 (Primarina BP 12) = 16`** (`145 - 16 = 129`, typically `128`).
   - Because Hit 3 was a `band('down')`, `evidencePass` never checked `getDamage` for Hit 3, accepted `Lucario 0/32/0/0/2/32`, and `paths()` wrote **`130/145`** into `exact` (`cutAt` set at `1.12` per `evidence.mjs:4037`, so `brokenProof` did not fire). Round 2 (`line 36`) then failed at `1.12` (`wanted 130/145, got 128/145`).
3. **`held` Pollution (`Lucario 29/5/0/0/0/32`) & Within-Turn Multi-Hit Greedy Sampling (`lines 38–66, 354–464`)**:
   - In Round 2's fallback on `1.12`, the first probe `Lucario hp 29` (`MaxHP = 174`) yielded `closestSpread` = **`29/5/0/0/0/32`** (dropping `atk` from `32` to `5` to fit `29 + 5 + 32 = 66`), which got past `1.12` and stopped at `1.16` (`wanted 84/100, got 86/100`). `infer.mjs:1093` stored `held.set('p2:1', 29/5/0/0/0/32)`, anchoring `Lucario` at `MaxHP = 174` (`hp = 29`) for Rounds 3–7.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Lucario` (`147 HP, 90 Def`), `Weavile` (`172 Atk`), `Primarina`, and `Rillaboom` are known constants, and Channel 2 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers (`140/147`, `134/147`, `130/147` or `131/147`, `124/147`) on Beat Up. Turn 1 reproduces immediately with zero Stat Point ambiguity on P2, allowing `Gholdengo` (`17/0/0/17/0/32`) and `Salamence` (`16/0/0/20/0/30`) to be inferred cleanly from Turns 1–3.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Salamence` (`186 HP, 176 SpA`) and `Gholdengo` (`179 HP, 187 SpA`) are fixed constants, so Start 2 (`usage`) never triggers a Turn 3 `Gholdengo` Life Orb mismatch (`81/100` vs `80/100` never occurs!), and Round 1 of `usage` reproduces the entire 4-turn battle on the first try (`Lucario` and `Weavile` are already at their exact `#1` usage spreads).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/`)
1. **Beat Up Reads `baseStats.atk` Only, Not Stat Points (`evidence.mjs` lines 891–900, 972)**:
   `Beat Up`'s `basePowerCallback` reads `this.dex.species.get(move.allies.shift().set.species).baseStats.atk`, which depends *only* on which species are in the back of the party (and their order), **not** on any Stat Points. Moreover, if all remaining unrevealed team-sheet members' base-Attack BPs are bounded (or if `evidencePass` evaluates the union of `moveChange` tables across valid back-row party permutations when `hit.back` triggers, or checks whether `130/145` is impossible under *all* party orders), `evidencePass` would never hand an impossible `130/145` `exact` target into Round 2, and in `default` starts (`Lucario 2/0/10/0/0/32`, `Weavile 32/0/2/0/0/32`) it would immediately rule out `(Lucario def=10, Weavile atk=0)` from `1.12` + `1.16`.
2. **Guard `paths()` Against Choosing an Unchecked `band('down')` Exact HP on a Multi-Hit Turn (`evidence.mjs:4037`)**:
   In Start 2 Round 2, `Lucario 0/32/0/0/2/32` (`MaxHP = 145`) was handed `exact = 130/145` on `1.12` even though `130/145` is unreachable by `Weavile` (`min = 16 > 145 - 130 = 15`) under any back-row Pokémon. And when Round 2's fallback tested `Lucario hp 29`, `held` locked `29/5/0/0/0/32` (`atk = 5`, `66/66` SP) permanently.
3. **Intra-Turn Multi-Hit Roll Selection (`reconstruct.mjs:1939–1952`)**:
   `resolveTurn` commits `subs[draw]` greedily hit-by-hit (`sample.pick` among rolls sharing the same displayed percentage) and never backtracks within the same turn when a later hit in that turn (`1.16` `147/174`) proves an earlier hit's ambiguity (`159` vs `160` at `1.8`, `155` vs `156` at `1.12`) picked the wrong side of the percentage bucket.
4. **Filter Out Cap-Overflow Artifacts in `fallback`'s `moved` Loop (`infer.mjs:825–832`)**:
   On `Lucario 2/0/10/0/0/32` (`44` SP), probing `Lucario atk 1..31`, `spa 23..31`, and `spd 23..31` only changed the Beat Up output because `44 + 23 > 66` forced `closestSpread` to strip `def` and `hp`. Those 55 wasted rebuilds exhausted `Start 6`'s entire `128`-rebuild budget before the 2-stat pair search (`infer.mjs:833`) could test `(Lucario def 0, Weavile atk 32)`.
