When line 6.9 fails repeatedly, `infer.mjs` invokes `fallback(seen, start, ...)` on Turn 6:
1. Fallback perturbs single stats of active Pokémon in isolation (`Rillaboom hp 32`, `Rillaboom atk 10`, `Rillaboom atk 32`, `Rillaboom def 32`, `Rillaboom spa 32`).
2. If fallback adjusts P1 Rillaboom's `hp` to 32, it alters the Turn 5 Wood Hammer recoil percentage on Tyranitar, causing the simulation to fail earlier:
   `fallback on turn 6: Rillaboom hp 32 - stops at 5.9: wanted |-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|75/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|76/100|[from] Recoil`.
3. If fallback adjusts P1 Rillaboom's `atk`, it fails because the damage ratio on Turn 6 depends jointly on P1 Rillaboom's Atk, P2 Rillaboom's Def, and P2 Tyranitar's HP/Def from Turn 5.
4. Fallback exhausts its per-line rebuild allocation (`fallbackRebuilds = 8`), concluding:
   `fallback on turn 6: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`.

### Joint Guess Failure and Stat Loss
When fallback fails, `infer.mjs` calls `choose(0, ...)` to explore joint candidate combinations. Because 6 active Pokémon have entered the field by Turn 6, `choose` must solve a combinatorial space of depth 6 within `tries = 32 * S * W = 64` attempts. `tries` quickly hits 0, logging `guessTries`, and returns `proof = null`.

With `proof = null`, **all exact turns and dice pins are erased**.

### Turn 0 Grassy Surge Speed Priority Regression
In the subsequent round, without pinned exact turns, `infer.mjs` samples unconstrained candidate spreads via `closestSpread`.
- In ground truth, P1 Rillaboom has Spe 113 (8 SP) and P2 Rillaboom has Spe 105 (0 SP).
- Whenever `closestSpread` samples a candidate for P2 Rillaboom with $\ge 8$ SP in Spe, or a candidate for P1 Rillaboom with $< 8$ SP in Spe, **P2 Rillaboom becomes faster than P1 Rillaboom**.
- On Turn 0, Showdown resolves lead ability activations in Speed order. If P2 Rillaboom is faster, P2's Grassy Surge fires first:
  `round 14, 0.0s: reproduces through turn 0 - wanted |-fieldstart|move: Grassy Terrain|[from] ability: Grassy Surge|[of] p1a: Rillaboom, got |-fieldstart|move: Grassy Terrain|[from] ability: Grassy Surge|[of] p2b: Rillaboom`.
- The search instantly regresses from Turn 5 all the way back to **Turn 0**.

### The Infinite Stalling Loop
The search was trapped in a destructive oscillation:
1. Re-climb from Turn 0 through Turn 4 (burning fallback rebuilds on Turn 3 Volcarona damage and Turn 4 Sneasler GT heal `6/100` vs `7/100`).
2. Reach Turn 5/6, where Wood Hammer recoil and Life Orb fail on `39/100` vs `38/100`.
3. Fallback burns its rebuild budget on Turn 6 without finding a single-stat fix.
4. Joint `choose` runs out of `guessTries`, losing all pins.
5. Candidate re-sampling causes P2 Rillaboom to outspeed P1 Rillaboom, regressing back to Turn 0.
6. The deepest turn never advanced past Turn 5/6. After `stalled >= maxRounds` (16) across `default`, `usage`, `wide`, and `widest` restarts, the search terminated with `stop = "stalled"`.
