## 7. Summary & Root Cause Synthesis

1. **Replay Divergence Due to Mimic Setup**:
   - In the human Showdown match, integer damage percentages produced subtle rounding differences (e.g. Turn 1 Rough Skin dealing `87/100` vs `88/100` in mimic).
   - In the mimic battle, planted Stat Points from usage statistics generated exact percentage thresholds on Turn 4 (`Incineroar 1/100`), Turn 5 (`Gholdengo 56/100`), and Turn 6 (`Gholdengo 36/100`).

2. **Why Single-View Inference Succeeded**:
   - In `p2@p1` and `p1@p2`, fixing one side's exact stats decoupled the damage equations and Speed order into independent 1-variable linear bounds.
   - Gholdengo's exact Speed was known relative to Rillaboom, and exact HP values locked the PRNG state, allowing both single views to solve cleanly in under 30 seconds with 0 fallback rebuilds.

3. **Why Spectator View Stalled and Gave Up**:
   - Spectator Channel 0 provides only rounded integer percentages out of 100 without known stats or seeds.
   - On Turn 6, Gholdengo (base 84 Speed) must strictly outspeed ally Rillaboom (base 85 Speed) without Trick Room or priority, requiring $\text{SP}_{\text{spe}}^{\text{Gholdengo}} \ge \text{SP}_{\text{spe}}^{\text{Rillaboom}} + 2$.

   - Any single-stat perturbation that adjusted Turn 6 speed order or damage immediately destroyed Turn 4 Wave Crash damage or Turn 5 Sucker Punch damage.
   - The search became locked in a circular oscillation across 6 phases and 240 fallback rebuilds (8, 32, and 128 rebuilds per tier), ultimately terminating at 68 rounds, 314,153 probes, and 433.7s (2,978.8s in cluster sweep) with `stop = "searches"`.
