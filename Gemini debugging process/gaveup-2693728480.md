   - Channel 1 displays exact HP integers for all P1 Pokémon (e.g., Incineroar at `150/202`, `82/202`, `132/202`, `125/202`).
   - On Turn 4, Archaludon's +1 SpA rain-boosted Electro Shot deals $162 - 82 = 80$ exact damage to Incineroar's known 124 SpD. This directly isolates Archaludon's SpA stat.
   - On Turn 4, Incineroar takes 7 HP recoil from Flare Blitz, proving the exact damage dealt was between 21 and 24 HP, bounding Archaludon's Defense immediately.

### B. Decoupled Linear Search Without Combinatorial Feedback
In single-view mode, exactly 4 Pokémon are unknown rather than 8. Because defending stats or attacking stats are invariant constants, every verified damage roll produces an exact proof path (`proof.exact`), allowing RNG state and choices to hand on smoothly across rounds without triggering fallback deadlocks.

## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up (Detailed Breakdown)

In `both@spectator`, inference consumed **94 rounds, 575 rebuilds, and 1,820,359 probes** over **6,698.7 seconds** (1.86 hours in the sweep; 4,341.2s in trace reproduction) before halting with `stop: "searches"`.

The failure was driven by four interconnected structural bottlenecks:

### A. The Spectator Channel (Channel 0) Dual-Sided Quantization Trap
Under `both@spectator`, both teams are completely unknown (`known = [false, false]`).
Channel 0 never provides exact HP numbers. Every reported health value is quantized:
$$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Current HP} - \text{Damage}}{\text{Max HP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
Where damage is governed by:
$$\text{Damage} = \left\lfloor \left( \left\lfloor \frac{2 \times 50}{5} + 2 \right\rfloor \times \text{Move BP} \times \frac{\text{Attacker Stat}}{\text{Defender Stat}} / 50 + 2 \right) \times \text{Roll} \right\rfloor$$
Because 8 Pokémon participate across 11 turns with 4 distinct field/weather shifts (Grassy Terrain T0–T5, Rain Dance T4–T7, Grassy Terrain T8–T11, Sunny Day T9–T11), the simultaneous floating of Max HPs, Attack stats, Defense stats, and 16-step discrete rolls creates vast coupled manifolds where multiple candidate combinations match early turns but diverge later.

### B. Dense Compound Healing and Recoil Multi-Tracks
This battle features one of the highest densities of simultaneous non-combat health adjustments:
- **Grassy Terrain**: Restores $\lfloor \text{Max HP} / 16 \rfloor$ at end-of-turn for all grounded combatants. Active on Turns 0–5 and Turns 8–11.
- **Leftovers**: Restores $\lfloor \text{Max HP} / 16 \rfloor$ for Archaludon on every single turn (Turns 2–11).
- **Sitrus Berry**: Restores $\lfloor \text{Max HP} / 4 \rfloor$ on Incineroar (Turn 4).
- **Recoil**: Flare Blitz on Incineroar (Turn 4: 7 HP; Turn 5: 34 HP) and Wood Hammer on Rillaboom (Turn 11: 4 HP) inflict $\lfloor \text{damage} \times 0.33 \rfloor$.
- **Stamina**: Boosts Archaludon's Defense by +1 stage on Turn 2, Turn 4, Turn 8, and Turn 11.

While Archaludon's repeated Leftovers and Grassy Terrain recoveries tightly bounded its Max HP to pin exact `hp` at SP 32 (197 Max HP, reducing spreads to 2,997), its attacking, defending, and speed stats remained floating alongside all stats of Incineroar, Rillaboom, Charizard, and Basculegion.

### C. Specific Stalled Turns & Deadlock Points

#### 1. Turn 3 Sitrus Berry & Flare Blitz Recoil Premature Trigger
In Starts 1–3, the search repeatedly failed on Turn 2/3:

Because Incineroar's Max HP and Archaludon's SpA were floating, candidate rolls either caused Sitrus Berry to trigger too early on Turn 3 or altered the recoil percentage from Flare Blitz between `47/100`, `48/100`, and `50/100`.

#### 2. Turn 8 End-of-Turn Healing Order & Speed-Tier Deadlock

In Pokémon Showdown, end-of-turn residual effects (Grassy Terrain healing, Leftovers) execute in strict order of Pokémon Speed:
- Ground truth Level 50 Speeds: Archaludon has Speed **112**, Rillaboom has Speed **108**.

  `got |-heal|p1b: Rillaboom|67/100|[from] Grassy Terrain` instead of Archaludon's Leftovers!

### D. The Fallback Rebuild Systematic Failure
When recurring protocol diffs occurred, `infer.mjs` invoked `fallback()`:
1. **Single-Stat Mutation Assumption**:
   - `fallback()` modifies **one stat of one Pokémon** at a time (`changes = [{ ...d, value }]`).
   - However, the Turn 8 healing order and damage values depend on the joint relationship between Rillaboom's Speed, Archaludon's Speed, Rillaboom's Defense, and Archaludon's SpA. Modifying one stat in isolation cannot correct a coupled discrepancy.
2. **Early Divergence Discarding Sound Spreads**:
   - When `fallback()` modified an early stat (such as Incineroar's HP or Archaludon's Def/SpD) to fix Turn 8, the rebuild failed earlier in the log:
     ```
     fallback on turn 8: Incineroar hp 32 - stops at 3.12: wanted |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|48/100|[from] Recoil, got 50/100
     fallback on turn 8: Incineroar atk 19 - stops at 4.8: wanted |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|34/100, got 30/100
     fallback on turn 8: Charizard atk 32 - stops at 6.7: wanted |-damage|p2b: Charizard|55/100, got 47/100

     ```
   - Because `reach(b)[0] < t` (the candidate stops before Turn 8), `fallback()` immediately discards it.
   - Consequently, `fallback()` exhausted up to **128 rebuilds** per invocation without discovering a candidate that passed Turn 8.
