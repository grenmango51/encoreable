## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up

### Pathologies Observed in the Search:

#### 1. Total Information Asymmetry and Percentage Quantization
- In Channel 0, neither player's stats are known.
- All 8 participating Pokémon have unknown base stats, natures, and EVs across all 6 dimensions.
- All damage, recoil, and healing events are reported exclusively as integer percentages ($\lfloor \text{HP} \times 100 / \text{MaxHP} \rfloor$). Max HPs are completely unknown.

#### 2. The Failure of Joint Guessing in `choose`
In `infer.mjs`, `choose(n, cond)` performs depth-first branch-and-bound search across the sequence of seen Pokémon (`seq.length = 8`):
- Per-round budget: `tries = 32 * S * W` (at `S=1, W=1`, exactly **32 tries**).
- At each node, `tries` is decremented:
  - 1 try for pinning HP/Def/SpD (`keyed`)
  - 1 try for pinning the full spread (`narrowed`)
  - 1 try at the leaf to evaluate `evidencePass({ exact: true })`
- Traversal of a single depth-8 branch from root to leaf consumes $8 \times 2 + 1 = 17$ tries.
- With only 32 tries available, `choose` can evaluate at most **1 full branch** and begin a 2nd branch before exhausting all tries, triggering limit `guessTries`, and returning `null`.
- When `choose` returns `null`:
  - `exact = null` and `pins = null`.
  - No exact proofs or dice pins are handed to the next round.
  - The subsequent round falls back to picking each Pokémon's spread **independently** via `closestSpread(kn)`.

#### 3. Cross-Turn State Entanglement on Turn 5

## 7. Turn 5 Mutual Dependency Mathematical Breakdown

The failure to resolve `both@spectator` centers on the non-linear mathematical coupling spanning Turns 1, 4, and 5:

### 1. Cumulative HP Chain of P2 Rillaboom
Rillaboom's HP entering Turn 5 governs the exact recoil taken by Incineroar:
$$\text{HP}_{\text{Rillaboom, T5}} = \text{MaxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}} - \text{Dmg}_{\text{FakeOut, T1}} + \text{Heal}_{\text{GT, T1}} - \text{Dmg}_{\text{ZapCannon, T4}} + \text{Heal}_{\text{GT, T4}}$$

In the ground truth:
- $\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}} = 203$
- $\text{Dmg}_{\text{FakeOut, T1}} = 14 \implies \text{HP} = 189 \implies \lfloor 189 \times 100 / 203 \rfloor = 93/100$
- $\text{Heal}_{\text{GT, T1}} = \lfloor 203 / 16 \rfloor = 12 \implies \text{HP} = 201 \implies \lfloor 201 \times 100 / 203 \rfloor = 99/100$
- $\text{Dmg}_{\text{ZapCannon, T4}} = 74 \implies \text{HP} = 127 \implies \lfloor 127 \times 100 / 203 \rfloor = 62/100$
- $\text{Heal}_{\text{GT, T4}} = \lfloor 203 / 16 \rfloor = 12 \implies \text{HP} = 139 \implies \lfloor 139 \times 100 / 203 \rfloor = 68/100$

### 2. Recoil and Healing Coupling on P1 Incineroar in Turn 5
Incineroar enters Turn 5 at max HP ($184/184$ HP, $100/100$):
1. **Flare Blitz Lethal Damage**:
   - Flare Blitz deals overkill damage ($> 139$), capping damage dealt at $\text{HP}_{\text{Rillaboom, T5}} = 139$.
2. **Recoil Equation**:
   $$\text{Recoil} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{Rillaboom, T5}}}{3} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{139}{3} \right\rfloor = 46$$
   $$\text{HP}_{\text{Incineroar, post-recoil}} = 184 - 46 = 138$$
   $$\text{Spectator Demand: } \left\lfloor \frac{138 \times 100}{184} \right\rfloor = 75/100 \text{ (exact)}$$
3. **Grassy Terrain Healing Equation**:
   $$\text{Heal}_{\text{GT}} = \left\lfloor \frac{184}{16} \right\rfloor = 11$$
   $$\text{HP}_{\text{Incineroar, upkeep}} = 138 + 11 = 149$$
   $$\text{Spectator Demand: } \left\lfloor \frac{149 \times 100}{184} \right\rfloor = 80/100 \text{ (exact)}$$

If $\text{HP}_{\text{Rillaboom, T5}}$ shifts by even $\pm 2$ HP (e.g., $137$ or $141$ HP due to different Raichu SpA or Rillaboom SpD on Turn 4):

### 4. Circular Incompatibility of Single-Stat Perturbations
