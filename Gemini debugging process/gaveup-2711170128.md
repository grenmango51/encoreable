## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

In `both@spectator`, the search ran for **3,455.6 seconds (57.59 min)**, executing **52 rounds, 292 rebuilds, and 346,047 probes**, before surrendering with `stop: "searches"`.

### Root Cause 1: Blizzard Secondary Effect Roll Divergence (`|-status|p2b: Golisopod|frz`)

  4. Damage roll on Golisopod.

- In the ground-truth battle, this 10% freeze check rolled false (no status).
- However, during inference reconstruction, the candidate spreads and dice assignments handed down from Turns 1-5 caused the PRNG state at step 5 to roll $\le 10$, inflicting freeze:

### Root Cause 2: Fallback Misdiagnosis as a Turn-Order / Speed Mismatch
In `infer.mjs`, when a rebuild reproduces up to line $L$ and stops with a line diff, `fallback(seen, start, ...)` analyzes the mismatch:
- The diff reported:
  - Wanted: `|move|p2a: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]`
  - Got: `|-status|p2b: Golisopod|frz`
- Because the expected line is a `|move|` action and the received line is an effect on another active Pokémon, `fallback` interprets this as an action-ordering failure (i.e. Archaludon was supposed to act, but Golisopod or another event took precedence, or speed tiers were inverted).
- Consequently, `fallback` invokes its speed perturbation routine (`probe(changes)`):
  ```text
  fallback on turn 6: Archaludon spe 32, Golisopod spe 0
  fallback on turn 6: Archaludon spe 0, Golisopod spe 32
  fallback on turn 6: Archaludon spe 32, Rillaboom spe 0
  fallback on turn 6: Archaludon spe 0, Rillaboom spe 32
  ```

### Root Cause 3: Fruitless Speed and Bulk Sweeps in Fallback Rebuilds
Speed modifications are completely inert against secondary effect rolls:
1. Froslass has a base Speed of 120 (Mega) and Timid nature, reaching 170 Speed.

### Root Cause 4: PRNG Dice Coupling and Seed Alignment Across Turns 1-5
Why was the freeze roll so persistent across rounds?
- In `reconstruct.mjs`, when reconstructing a battle, dice outcomes are settled sequentially.
- If a spread replicates the exact percentage damage of:
  - Turn 2: Rillaboom Fake Out (10 damage -> `94/100`), Froslass Shadow Ball (54 damage -> `64/100`, -1 SpD drop)
  - Turn 4: Volcarona Overheat (61 damage -> `71/100`), Pelipper Hurricane (82 damage -> `53/100`)
  - Turn 5: Basculegion Wave Crash crit (101 damage -> `47/100`, 33 recoil -> `62/100`), Froslass Shadow Ball lethal
- Then every such spread consumes the **exact same number of PRNG calls** across Turns 1-5.
- Because the PRNG state entering Turn 6 is identical for all candidate spreads that successfully reproduced Turns 1-5, Froslass's Blizzard is fed the exact same sequence of pseudo-random numbers.
- Unless the backtracking search can explore alternate dice paths in Turn 6 (specifically forcing the secondary effect die to settle on a non-freeze value $> 10$), the forward simulation will deterministically roll a freeze every single time.
