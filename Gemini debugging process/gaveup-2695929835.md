## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up (Detailed Breakdown)

In `both@spectator`, inference exhausted **115 rounds, 679 rebuilds, and 1,059,474 probes** over **5,692.9 seconds** (1.58 hours in the sweep run; 1,664.4s in trace reproduction) before terminating with `stop: "searches"`.

The root causes of the search giving up are detailed below:

### A. The Spectator Channel (Channel 0) Information Deprivation
On Channel 0, neither player's exact HP is revealed; every value is quantized to an integer percentage.
Under `both@spectator`, both teams are completely unknown (`known = [false, false]`).
Every observed value is the integer quotient:
$$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Current HP} - \text{Damage}}{\text{Max HP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
where damage is determined by integer division and discrete rolls:
$$\text{Damage} = \left\lfloor \left( \left\lfloor \frac{2 \times 50}{5} + 2 \right\rfloor \times \text{Move BP} \times \frac{\text{Attacker Stat}}{\text{Defender Stat}} / 50 + 2 \right) \times \text{Roll} \right\rfloor$$
With 8 active Pokémon across 7 turns, floating Attacker Stats, Defender Stats, Max HPs, and 16-step damage rolls create wide, coupled solution manifolds.

### B. High Density of Multi-Source Recoil, Recovery, and Helmet Interactions
Across 7 turns, P1 Incineroar participates in **7 distinct non-combat HP modifications**:
1. Turn 2: Leftovers recovery (`+1/16` max HP).

4. Turn 5: Leftovers recovery (`+1/16` max HP).
5. Turn 6: Rocky Helmet damage from hitting Volcarona (`-1/6` max HP).
6. Turn 6: Flare Blitz recoil (`-33%` of damage dealt to Volcarona).
7. Turn 6: Leftovers recovery (`+1/16` max HP).
8. Turn 7: Rocky Helmet damage from hitting Volcarona (`-1/6` max HP).
9. Turn 7: Flare Blitz recoil (`-33%` of damage dealt to Volcarona).

- P2 Incineroar triggers Sitrus Berry (`+25%` max HP) on Turn 5 and takes Flare Blitz recoil on Turn 5.

### D. The Fallback Rebuild Systematic Failure
When a rebuild repeats a diff, `infer.mjs` triggers `fallback()` on Turn 6 or 7.
`fallback()` systematically fails here because:
1. **Single-Stat Mutation Assumption**:
