## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Starts Stalled at Turn 2 (`2.9`) & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Unresolved Turn 2 `Rock Slide` Flinch (`2.9: got |cant|p2b: Grimmsnarl|flinch`)

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` & `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Classify Same-Actor `move` vs `cant|flinch` as a Speed-Order Divergence (`infer.mjs` lines 624–625)**:
   When `diff.expected` is `|move|p2b: Grimmsnarl|...` and `diff.actual` is `|cant|p2b: Grimmsnarl|flinch`, `who(diff.expected) === who(diff.actual)` (`p2b: Grimmsnarl`). `infer.mjs` lines 624–625 still set `order = true` because `kind(diff.actual) !== kind(diff.expected)` (`move` vs `cant` in `ACTION_START`), causing `fallback` to sort `spe` dimensions first and waste its entire `8`-rebuild quota in Starts 1 and 2 bisecting `Grimmsnarl spe` (each breaking Turn 1 `1.11` and triggering a `fresh` rebuild). Requiring `who(diff.expected) !== who(diff.actual)` (or checking whether `Grimmsnarl` can even outspeed the flinch source `Aerodactyl` at `spe = 32`) prevents wasted fallback cycles on non-causal Speed stats.
