### 1. Loss of Exact HP Anchors in Spectator Channel

### 2. Multi-Body Recoil & Threshold Couplings
The battle contains two multi-body dependency networks where variables across 4 to 6 Pokémon are interdependent:

- **Turn 4 Coupling (Volcarona + Mega Golisopod + Farigiraf + Rillaboom)**:
  1. Volcarona's spread `Heat Wave` in Rain simultaneously damages Mega Golisopod (resisted by rain, 2x weak) and Farigiraf. Both damage values depend on Volcarona's SpA, Rain modifier (0.5x), spread modifier (0.75x), and the respective target's SpD and max HP.

'g' : 'y'`) requires Volcarona's post-damage HP to be strictly `<= 50.0%` of its max HP while rounding to 50/100. Any guess with an odd max HP or damage differing by 1 HP emits `50/100g`, causing divergence.
  3. Rillaboom's `Wood Hammer` KOs Farigiraf. The recoil damage dealt to Rillaboom is `Math.round(remainingHP / 3)`. The remaining HP of Farigiraf is `maxHP - Turn 3 Heat Wave + Grassy Terrain heal - Turn 4 Heat Wave`.

    - Rillaboom's max HP (p1)
    - Farigiraf's max HP (p2)
    - Farigiraf's SpD (p2)
    - Volcarona's SpA (p1)
    - Turn 3 and Turn 4 Heat Wave damage rolls.

### 3. Failure of Joint Guess Proofs (`guessTries` Exhaustion)
In `infer.mjs`, when multiple Pokémon are inferred, `choose()` attempts to pin candidate spreads sequentially for all active combatants and call `evidencePass(..., { exact: true })` to find an exact HP path (`ctx.paths(raw)`).
- When 6 combatants are simultaneously unconstrained, `choose()` tests combinations generated from `closestSpread(kn, aim)`.
- For `ctx.paths()` to succeed, the chosen spreads must admit a single set of discrete 16-step damage rolls and exact integer recoil values that simultaneously satisfy all 9 damage lines and health tokens across all turns.
- In Round 8, `choose()` executed 277 passes, exhausted `guessTries`, and returned `proof.exact = null`.

### 4. Reversion, De-synchronization, and the Fallback Loop
When `proof.exact` is null:
- No exact turns are marked (`exact = null`), and no verified dice rolls are handed on (`pins = null`).
- In Round 9, `reconstruct` was forced to re-simulate from Turn 1 using default dice and unpinned guesses with a low probe budget.
- Without the pinned dice, the simulation failed at Turn 4 line 4.3 (`|-damage|p2b: Farigiraf|60/100`).
- Because line 4.3 had failed previously, `again` evaluated to true, triggering `fallback()`.
- `fallback()` tested single-stat perturbations of individual Pokémon. It mutated Farigiraf's SpD to 32, which allowed line 4.3 to pass but shifted the failure to line 4.7 (`|-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|78/100|[from] Recoil`).
