## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Gave Up on Turn 8

Three mechanisms combined to trap every one of the 6 starts at Turn 8:

### A. `evidence.mjs` Cannot Read Recoil Off a Substitute (`band('down')` + False `exact`)
In `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` (lines 1798–1803, 2618–2626):
```js
recoilCall = battle.effect?.id === 'substitute' ? null : { pokemon, move, total: damageDealt };
```
Because `p1a: Incineroar`'s `Flare Blitz` broke `p2a: Gengar`'s `Substitute` rather than damaging `Gengar`'s HP directly, the Substitute absorbed an unprinted amount ($\lfloor \text{MaxHP}_{\text{Gengar}} / 4 \rfloor$), so `recoilCall` is `null` and `effectChange` returns `band('down', what)`.
1. **Zero Spreads Eliminated**: `evidence` removes zero spreads of `p1:2 Incineroar` or `p2:0 Gengar` from Turn 8's Recoil line.
2. **Guess Frozen (`unchanged = true`) at `HP = 13` / `11` (`MaxHP = 183` / `181`)**: Because `p1:2 Incineroar` had taken no HP changes in Turns 1–7, its `HP` remained unconstrained; when `evidence` narrowed `Atk` (`9`) and `Spe` (`11`), `closestSpread` lowered `HP` from `32` to `13` (Default) or `11` (Usage) to stay within the 66-SP cap. Under `Gengar MaxHP = 167` (`32` HP SP $\to$ `41`-HP Substitute), `Flare Blitz` recoil is $\text{round}(41 \times 33 / 100) = \mathbf{14}$ HP:
   - Default (`MaxHP = 183`): $183 - 14 = 169/183 = \mathbf{92/100}$ (wanted `93/100`, i.e., `171..172/183`, recoil $\le 12$).
   - Usage (`MaxHP = 181`): $181 - 14 = 167/181 = \mathbf{92/100}$ (wanted `93/100`, i.e., `169..170/181`, recoil $\le 12$).
   Since Substitute HP and recoil involve **zero RNG rolls**, no `budgets[level]` search (`search 1..4`, even `16,000` probes and `resample: 1`) can change `169/183` to `171/183`.
3. **Spurious `exact` Rewrite**: In `evidencePass({ exact: true })`, `paths()` (`evidence.mjs:3965-3968, 4031`) lets the `band('down')` change on the Turn 8 cutoff step from `183` to `171` (`171/183 = 93/100`) without checking whether `Gengar`'s Substitute can deal `12` recoil, causing Round 10+ to report `wanted 171/183, got 169/183` (`169/181` in Usage).

### B. Why `fallback` Never Probed `Incineroar hp` or `Gengar hp`: Early Abort at `5.15` (`68/100`)
Normally, `fallback` (`infer.mjs:478`) exists precisely to perturb spreads on lines no evidence hook reads—testing `p1:2 Incineroar hp 32` (`202 - 14 = 188/202 = 93/100`) or `p2:0 Gengar hp 0..16` (`subHp <= 37`, recoil $\le 12$) would have reproduced Turn 8 immediately.

Instead, `fallback` aborted at `infer.mjs:614-618` **before entering `dims`** because `base` (`exact: null`, `maxBacktracks: 0`) stalled earlier at **`5.15: wanted |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|70/100, got |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|68/100`**:

- Reaching `70/100` (`138..139 / 196`) requires **both** `Heat Wave -> 156` **and** `Darkest Lariat = 18` ($156 - 18 = 138/196 = 70.4\% \to 70/100$).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2:0 Gengar`'s true `MaxHP` (and `Substitute` HP) is known, and Channel 2 prints `p2a: Incineroar`'s exact integer HP on Turn 5 (`79/100` and `5.15` `70/100`), completely preventing the `5.15` (`exact: null`) `fallback` stall! Thus `fallback` can freely perturb `p1:2 Incineroar hp` if its guess drops too low for Turn 8's `93/100` recoil.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:2 Incineroar`'s real `MaxHP` and exact recoil HP on Turn 8 (`hp/maxhp`, e.g., `188/202`) are printed directly in Channel 1, and `p1:2 Incineroar` never gets squeezed by `closestSpread` into `HP = 13` or `11`, while `fallback` can adjust `p2:0 Gengar hp` on Turn 8 if `Gengar`'s `Substitute` HP needs to match the exact recoil integer.

### Architectural Takeaways
1. **Substitute-Recoil Coupling in `evidence.mjs` (`lines 1798–1803, 2621`)**:
   When a move's recoil comes from breaking a Substitute (`battle.effect?.id === 'substitute'`), `sub[STORED]?.hp` (`subHp = Math.floor(MaxHP / 4)`) is already tracked per candidate in `evidence.mjs:2558`! Evaluating `actions.applyRecoilDamage(Math.min(dealt, subHp), move, S)` for each surviving `Gengar` `hp_SP` (when the hit broke the Substitute, `dealt >= subHp`, so damage to the Substitute is *exactly* `subHp`!) would let `evidence` directly link `p2:0 Gengar`'s `HP` to `p1:2 Incineroar`'s `HP` on Turn 8 instead of falling back to `band('down')`.
2. **Avoid Hard-Aborting `fallback` When `base` Hits an Earlier Two-Roll Stall (`infer.mjs:614-618`)**:
   In `fallback`, `exact` is wiped to `null`, stripping the earlier turn's exact HP pins (`156/196` at Turn 5 `Heat Wave`) that `Round 9` and `Round 10` needed to get past `5.15`. Preserving `exact` up through `t - 1` during `fallback`'s `base` check (or allowing `resolveTurn` to retry `dice` / pairs of damage rolls on a multi-hit percentage squeeze) would prevent `fallback` from prematurely returning `none()` and blacklisting Turn 8 in `spend.fruitless`.
