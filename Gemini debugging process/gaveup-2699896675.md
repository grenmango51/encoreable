## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled at Turn 2 `|upkeep`

### A. The `Dire Claw` + `Poison Touch` Ordinal-Shift Trap in `resolveTurn()` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
In `[Gen 9 Champions]` (`node_modules/pokemon-showdown/data/mods/champions/moves.ts` line 215, `data/abilities.ts` line 3370, and `sim/battle-actions.ts` lines 1099 & 1121), when `Sneasler` hits `Milotic` with `Dire Claw`, Showdown executes two sequential status-inflicting checks in `moveHit`:

1. **Step 5 — `secondaries()` (`battle-actions.ts` line 1343, `Dire Claw` secondary)**:
   - Draws `secondaryRoll = this.battle.random(100)` (`lo=0, hi=99`, **`chance: false`**).
   - If `secondaryRoll < 30`: calls `this.sample(['psn', 'par', 'slp'])`, consuming a second draw **`battle.random(3)`** (`lo=0, hi=2`, **`chance: false`**) at ordinal **`i + 1`**, and sets that status on `Milotic`.
2. **Step `DamagingHit` — `Poison Touch` (`abilities.ts` line 3375)**:
   - Draws **`this.randomChance(3, 10)`** (`lo=0, hi=9`, **`chance: true`**).
   - If `< 3` (and `Milotic` has no status yet), inflicts **`'psn'`**.

Now examine what occurs in `resolveTurn()` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` lines 1917–2024) when `Dire Claw` rolls `< 30` at ordinal `i` and `sample(['psn', 'par', 'slp'])` rolls **`0` (`'psn'`)** at ordinal `i + 1`:
- **Why `PRNG` Correlation Makes `Poison Touch` Fire on `i + 1` Too**:
  In Showdown's `PRNG.random(m)` (`sim/prng.ts` line 101), a 32-bit integer `result` is mapped to `Math.floor(result * m / 2**32)`. Thus `sample(3) === 0` (`'psn'`) holds whenever `result / 2**32` $\in [0, 1/3)$, and `randomChance(3, 10)` (`Poison Touch`) holds whenever `result / 2**32` $\in [0, 0.30)$. **90% of the time (`0.30 / (1/3)`) that `sample(3)` at ordinal `i + 1` produces `'psn'`, that exact same unpinned PRNG output at ordinal `i + 1` ALSO triggers `Poison Touch` if `Dire Claw`'s `sample(3)` is skipped!**
- **Why Every Scan in `resolveTurn()` Fails to Clear `|-status|p2b: Milotic|psn`**:
  1. **Single-die scan (lines 1917–1952)**:
     - Flipping `i` (`Dire Claw` `random(100)`) to `99` (`>= 30`) removes `sample(3)` at `i + 1`. Consequently, `Poison Touch` shifts forward from ordinal `i + 2` into ordinal **`i + 1`**, reads the unpinned PRNG output (`< 0.30`), and **still emits `|-status|p2b: Milotic|psn`** (zero progress in `scoreOf`).
     - Flipping `i + 1` (`sample(3)`) to `1` (`par`) or `2` (`slp`) merely changes `psn` to `par`/`slp`, leaving `scoreOf` tied (`[index, 0, 0]`, never outranking `[index, 0, 0]`).
     - Flipping `i + 2` (`Poison Touch`) does nothing because `Dire Claw` already poisoned `Milotic` in Step 5 before `DamagingHit` ran.
  2. **Two-chances `pairs` scan (lines 1973–1994)**:
     - `chances` only lists draws that existed in the baseline run (`a = i` and `b = i + 2`, since `i + 1` is `0..2`, not `chance || span > 16`).
     - When trying `{ [i]: 99, [i + 2]: 9 }`, removing `sample(3)` shifts `Poison Touch` to **`i + 1`**, so pinning `i + 2` misses `Poison Touch` completely!
  3. **`brought` scan (lines 2000–2024)**:
     - Line 2000–2024 was specifically written for a chance draw `a` that brings a new die `b` when flipped (`const b = drawsOf(flipped.trace, t).find(d => d.i > a.i)`). When `a = i` (`Dire Claw` `random(100)`) is flipped to `99`, **`b` IS `Poison Touch` at `i + 1`** (whose range changed from `0-2` to `0-9`)!
     - **Fatal Filter Bug (`reconstruct.mjs` line 2010)**:
       ```js
       if (!b || b.chance || b.hi - b.lo + 1 > 16 || before.get(b.i) === `${b.lo}-${b.hi}`) continue;
       ```
       Because `brought` was designed only for turning `Dire Claw`'s secondary *on* (`sample(3)`, which has `chance === false`), line 2010 explicitly checks **`if (... || b.chance || ...)`** and **skips `Poison Touch` (`b.chance === true`)**! Had line 2010 permitted `b.chance` (trying `va = 99, vb = 9`), `resolveTurn` would have disabled both `Dire Claw` and `Poison Touch` in two probes and solved Turn 2 immediately.

### B. Why All 27 Target Variants, Backtracking, and `resample: 1` Repeated the Same Failure
- **Target Variants (`width = 27`)**: Turn 2 has three single-target moves (`Shadow Ball`, `Zap Cannon`, `Dire Claw`), giving $3^3 = 27$ target combinations (`maxProbes = 4,000` / `16,000`). Reordering targets never changes `Sneasler`'s `Dire Claw` $\to$ `Milotic` ordinal alignment inside `resolveTurn`, so `search 3` spent thousands of probes walking all 27 variants and hitting `stuck` (`S=4`) or `probes` (`S=1`).
- **Backtracking (`hpFigure === false`)**: Because `|upkeep` is not an HP line, `reconstruct` ran `redrawTurn(1)` with `strict = true` (`reconstruct.mjs` line 2597). No Turn 1 roll could change `Dire Claw` / `Poison Touch` on Turn 2, so `redrawTurn` returned `false` (`stop = 'stuck'`) without hitting `dieBacktracks`. (Earlier Turn 2 HP mismatches in Rounds 1–3 of Starts 1, 3, 4, 6 with `maxBacktracks = 0` added `backtracks` to `limits`.)
- **`search 4` (`resample: 1`)**: Although `resample: 1` dropped `pins` and `seed`, the fresh unpinned `seed` (`sampleSeed + 1`) still landed on a Turn 2 state where `Dire Claw` and `Poison Touch` collided at `i` / `i + 1` (`8 dice handed on` instead of `6`), failing identically on `|upkeep`.
- **Why `fallback` Did Not Cycle (`fallbackRebuilds` Absent)**: Unlike Turn 7 stalls caused by bad Stat Points, `fallback` never entered its cast-perturbation loop (`infer.mjs` line 610+) and emitted zero `fallback on turn 2:` messages across all 50 rounds. Because `Dire Claw` + `Poison Touch` poisoning `Milotic` is purely an RNG-search failure in `resolveTurn()` (affecting even `usage` Starts 2 & 5), **no Stat Point spread on any Pokémon in `cast` can prevent `Dire Claw` + `Poison Touch` from firing under an unfixed PRNG sequence** — the round loop simply repeated `search 1` until `searched >= 2` and then escalated `level` through `search 2 → 3 → 4 → stop = 'searches'`.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **Different Unpinned Dice Alignment Entering Turn 2**: In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 known) and `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 known), one side's Stat Points are known from Turn 0 and its exact `hp/maxhp` fractions are visible on Channel 1/2. Once past Turn 2 `|upkeep`, both single-player views reconstructed the rest of the 9-turn battle cleanly.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Allow `b.chance` (and Downstream Shifted Chance Dice) in `resolveTurn()`'s `brought` Scan (`reconstruct.mjs` line 2010)**:
   In `resolveTurn()`, the `brought` loop (lines 2000–2024) checks:
   ```js
   if (!b || b.chance || b.hi - b.lo + 1 > 16 || before.get(b.i) === `${b.lo}-${b.hi}`) continue;
   ```
   When flipping a chance die `a` (like `Dire Claw`'s `random(100) < 30`) **off** eliminates an intermediate draw (`sample(['psn', 'par', 'slp'])`), a *different* downstream chance die (`Poison Touch`'s `randomChance(3, 10)`) shifts into ordinal `a.i + 1` with `b.chance === true` and `before.get(b.i) !== `${b.lo}-${b.hi}``. Because line 2010 rejects `b.chance`, `resolveTurn` cannot jointly turn off a move's secondary effect and an ability's `DamagingHit` proc (`Poison Touch`, `Static`, `Flame Body`, `Effect Spore`, etc.) when eliminating the secondary shifts the ability's ordinal. Removing `!b.chance` (or searching the shifted `chance` draws in `flipped.trace` when `flipped.trace` has a different draw-range signature after `a.i`) directly fixes this entire class of secondary + contact-ability stalls.
2. **Treat `random(100)` Secondary Rolls as Chance-Shifting Draws**:
   Even in `pairs` (line 1974), `Dire Claw`'s `random(100)` (`hi - lo + 1 === 100 > 16`) is included in `chances`, so removing `b.chance` from line 2010 (or checking all `b` in `flipped.trace` whose `(lo, hi)` differs from `before.get(b.i)` at `b.i > a.i`, testing `candidates(b)` for both small-span and chance dice) handles both directions: turning a secondary **on** (`sample`) and turning a secondary **off** (`Poison Touch` shifting forward).
