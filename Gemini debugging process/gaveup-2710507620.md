### B. The Mathematical Coupling
The damage, recoil, healing, and percentage quantization equations are tightly interdependent:
$$\text{damage}_{\text{Hammer}} = \left\lfloor \left( \frac{22 \times 120 \times \text{Atk}_{\text{Rillaboom}}}{50 \times \text{Def}_{\text{Farigiraf}}} + 2 \right) \times 1.5 \times 1.2 \times 1.3 \times \frac{\text{Roll}_1}{100} \right\rfloor$$
$$\text{recoil}_{\text{Rillaboom}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{damage}_{\text{Hammer}}}{3} \right\rfloor$$
$$\text{HP}_{\text{post-recoil}} = \text{HP}_{\text{start, Rillaboom}} - \text{recoil}_{\text{Rillaboom}}$$
$$\text{heal}_{\text{Grassy}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}}}{16} \right\rfloor$$
$$\text{HP}_{\text{end, Rillaboom}} = \text{HP}_{\text{post-recoil}} + \text{heal}_{\text{Grassy}}$$
$$\text{percent}_{\text{display}} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{end, Rillaboom}}}{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}}} \times 100 \right\rceil$$
