1. **Turn 6 Move Order: `p1a: Garchomp` vs `p2b: Garchomp`**:

### C. The Stalling Mechanism Across 127 Rounds

1. **Failure to Advance `deepest`**:
   - In `infer.mjs`, the variable `stalled` increments whenever a rebuild fails to advance past `deepest = [turn, index]`.
   - Fixing Turn 6 speed (`Garchomp spe 32`) immediately causes Turn 6 Rough Skin or Grassy Terrain percentage mismatches.
   - Adjusting Garchomp's HP to satisfy Turn 6 Grassy Terrain causes Turn 7 Wood Hammer / U-turn speed discrepancies or Turn 7 Grassy Terrain healing order discrepancies.
   - Adjusting Glimmora's speed to fix Turn 7 healing order causes Turn 8 Mega-Z vs Choice Scarf speed inversions.
   - Adjusting stats on either side alters the exact damage rolls required on Turns 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5.
2. **Exhaustion of `guessTries` and `fallbackRebuilds`**:
   - During candidate selection in `choose()`, each joint pin attempt is verified by `settle()`. Because the multi-pokemon constraints cannot be jointly satisfied along the exact HP path, `guessTries` (limit of $32 \times S \times W$) is repeatedly exhausted.
   - Fallback search attempts are capped by `FALLBACK_REBUILDS` ($12 \times S \times W$) and `FALLBACK_TOTAL` ($60 \times S$).
3. **Restart Stages**:
   - The search sequentially attempted all 4 stages: `default`, `usage`, `wide`, and `widest`.
   - In each stage, the search reached Turn 5 or Turn 6, oscillated across the same set of mutually conflicting speed and percentage constraints, and failed to advance `deepest`.
   - Once `stalled >= maxRounds` was reached on the final `widest` pass, the process terminated with:
     `stop: "stalled"`.
