## 6. Why `both@spectator` Failed (128 Rounds, 495 Rebuilds, 180,846 Probes, Searches Limit Hit)

### A. The Mathematical Deadlock of Leftovers Rounding
At Level 50 in Champions format:
$$\text{HP} = \text{BaseHP} + \text{StatPoints} + 75$$
For Araquanid (Base HP = 68):
- Minimum HP (0 SP): $68 + 0 + 75 = 143$
- Maximum HP (32 SP): $68 + 32 + 75 = 175$
- Planted True HP (29 SP): $68 + 29 + 75 = 172$

Showdown's Leftovers recovery is defined as:
$$\text{HealAmount} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{MaxHP}}{16} \right\rfloor$$
- For Max HP = 175 (32 SP): $\lfloor 175 / 16 \rfloor = 10$ HP ($10 / 175 \approx 5.714\%$)
- For Max HP = 172 (29 SP): $\lfloor 172 / 16 \rfloor = 10$ HP ($10 / 172 \approx 5.814\%$)

Although both HP values heal exactly 10 raw HP per turn, the fractional percentage difference ($0.100\%$ per heal) compounds over multiple turns of chip damage, Leftovers recovery, and Leech Life draining:
- **Turn 2**: Araquanid damaged to 52%. Leftovers heals (+10 HP) $\rightarrow$ 57%.
- **Turn 3**: Araquanid Leech Life drains Kingambit (+8–10 HP) $\rightarrow$ 62%.
- **Turn 3 End**: Leftovers heals (+10 HP) $\rightarrow$ 68%.
- **Turn 4**: Araquanid takes chip damage $\rightarrow$ 68%.
- **Turn 4 End (Line 4.11)**:
  - If Max HP = 175 (32 SP): Current HP becomes 128. $\frac{128}{175} \approx 73.14\% \rightarrow \mathbf{73/100}$.
  - If Max HP = 172 (29 SP): Current HP becomes 127. $\frac{127}{172} \approx 73.837\% \rightarrow \mathbf{74/100}$.

Because the ground truth battle had 172 Max HP, Showdown logged `74/100`. Every guess with 175 Max HP produced `73/100`.

## 7. Conclusion & Engine Insights

Match `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2708784758` gave up in `both@spectator` after **3,566.6s** (and 748.7s in dedicated trace) due to an irreconcilable sub-percentage rounding mismatch in **Leftovers recovery on Turn 4**.

On Turn 4, P1 Araquanid recovered health via Leftovers. In the ground truth battle, Araquanid's planted stats corresponded to 172 Max HP (HP SP = 29), where 10 HP of recovery evaluated to $73.837\%$, rounded by Showdown to `74/100` at line 4.11 (`|-heal|p1b: Araquanid|74/100|[from] item: Leftovers`). However, because Araquanid is a premier defensive tank, the joint inference generator's usage priors consistently assigned it 32 HP SP (175 Max HP). With 175 Max HP, 10 HP of recovery evaluates to $73.14\%$, which Showdown rounds to `73/100`.

When line 4.11 failed, the fallback rebuild grid attempted to rescue the simulation by perturbing Atk, Def, SpA, SpD, and Spe across active Pokémon. Because fallback grid sweeps deliberately exclude HP SP perturbations to prevent invalidating preceding damage proofs, the engine cycled through 367 fallback rebuilds without ever altering Araquanid's HP SP from 32 to 29. Even when search budgets were widened 4-fold across 6 search phases, the mathematical grid deadlock persisted, ultimately exhausting the `searches` budget.

In contrast, single-player views (`p2@p1` and `p1@p2`) passed in under 30 seconds because knowing either player's exact team sheet eliminated the multi-variable ambiguity, fixing Araquanid's true HP at 172 and resolving line 4.11 on the first attempt.

### Actionable Engine Recommendations:
1. **Periodic Healing Feedback on Max HP**:
   - Lines with `|-heal|...|[from] item: Leftovers` (as well as Grassy Terrain and Black Sludge) provide strict arithmetic constraints on Max HP.
   - When a 1% mismatch occurs on a Leftovers heal (`wanted 74/100, got 73/100`), the engine should compute the allowable Max HP values that round to the target percentage and immediately filter out incompatible HP SP values.
2. **Targeted HP Perturbation in Fallback Rebuilds**:
   - If a simulation halts specifically on a `|-heal|` line rather than an attack or speed line, fallback rebuilds should permit small local perturbations ($\pm 1, \pm 2, \pm 3$) on the healing Pokémon's HP Stat Points.
3. **Multi-Turn Percentage Drift Compensation**:
   - When a Pokémon undergoes 3 or more consecutive percentage changes (damage $\rightarrow$ Leftovers $\rightarrow$ drain $\rightarrow$ Leftovers), sub-percentage errors accumulate. The inference generator should recognize this sequence and relax boundary pinning on HP SP = 32.
