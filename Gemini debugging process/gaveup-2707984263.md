## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. `Guard Split` (`2.0`) Erased All Special-Bulk & `Pelipper SpA` Evidence on `Snorlax`
On Turn 2 (`2.0`), `p1a: Runerigus` used `Guard Split` on `p1b: Snorlax`. In `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` (`settleWrites`, lines 3147–3174), a handler that writes `storedStats` is only mapped when at most **one** candidate value moves the written stat (`moving.length <= 1`). Because both `Runerigus` (`p1:5`) and `Snorlax` (`p1:1`) are unknown in `both@spectator`, `askWrites` sees two moving dependencies (`p1:1|spd` and `p1:5|spd`) and marks `Snorlax.def` and `Snorlax.spd` as **`unmapped`**:
> `Guard Split on p1: Snorlax's spd: no one candidate value says what it wrote, so what reads it is set aside`

Consequently:
- Every hit on `Snorlax` after Turn 2 (`2.11` `Hyper Voice`, Turn 3 `Hyper Voice`, and Turn 4 `4.6` `Hurricane`) becomes `supported = false` and degrades to an opaque `band('down')` change.
- `Snorlax` keeps virtually its entire spread space (`0` exact stats across the board), `choose()` trivially emits `5 turns exact` every round (so `read < 2` / `unchanged` immediately hands Turn 4 to `fallback`), and **`Pelipper`'s `SpA` receives zero narrowing from `4.6`**, leaving only Turn 3 `3.5` (`Indeedee 17/100`) to constrain `Pelipper`'s `SpA`.

### B. Both Default and Usage Starts Overshot `Pelipper`'s True `SpA = 5` (`132`)
Because `Pelipper`'s planted spread (`31/0/0/5/20/10`, Usage #15) runs unusually low Special Attack (`SpA = 5`, `132`), both start families guessed `Pelipper SpA` far too high for `4.6`:
1. **Default / Wide Starts (`1, 3, 4, 6`) $\to$ `Pelipper SpA = 24` (`152`)**:
   `choose()` orders `seq` with `p1` before `p2`, pinning `p1:2 Indeedee` first at `16/0/0/32/0/2` (`MaxHP = 151`). Against `151` HP `Indeedee`, Turn 3 `3.5` (`17/100`) forces `Pelipper` to high `SpA`, so `aimFor` picks `Pelipper 32/0/0/24/0/2` (`SpA = 24`, `152` SpA). At `4.6`, CRIT `Hurricane` from `SpA 152` deals **`[112..132]`** damage; even the minimum roll (`112`) from `202` HP leaves `202 - 112 =` **`90 / 267` (`33/100`)**, **overshooting `102/267` (`38/100`) by 12 HP**. Because no roll in `[0..15]` reaches `38/100`, `steerDice` returns `none(true)` and `reconstruct` halts at `4.6`.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Pelipper`'s exact `SpA = 5` (`132` SpA) and `Salamence`'s exact `SpA = 32` (`172` SpA) are known constants, and Channel 2 prints P2's exact HP integers (`Pelipper` untouched, `Salamence` untouched). - **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Snorlax` (`267 HP`, `132 SpD`), `Runerigus` (`165 HP`, `125 SpD`), `Indeedee` (`136 HP`, `115 SpD`), `Sinistcha`, and Channel 1's exact integers (`24/136` on `Indeedee`, `102/267` on `Snorlax`, `203/267` on Turn 2) are all known. ### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Include `['early', 'other']` Endpoints in `halve` (`infer.mjs` line 813)**:
   On an HP damage line (`4.6`), moving the true offensive stat (`Pelipper spa 32`) changes the printed HP percentage (`33/100 -> 31/100`, producing `'other'`), while the opposite end (`Pelipper spa 0`) breaks an earlier hit (`3.5`, producing `'early'`). Requiring `'same'` (`!ends.includes('early') || !ends.includes('same')`) excluded `Pelipper spa` from bisection while letting 13 irrelevant `66`-SP-budget artifacts (`'early'` + `'same'`) consume all 128 rebuilds. Treating `'other'` symmetrically with `'same'` (or bisecting any dimension with one `'early'` end and one `>= t` end) would bisect directly to `Pelipper spa = 5..13`.
2. **Test `middle` / Non-Extreme Values Before Exhausting Budget on Off-Stats When Both Ends Are `'early'` (`infer.mjs` lines 812–821)**:
   In Start 5 (`usage`), `Pelipper spa 0` and `Pelipper spa 32` both stopped `'early'` at `3.5` (because `3.5` bounded `Pelipper spa` from both above and below around `Indeedee`'s `138` HP), leaving `Pelipper spa` with `d.moved = false` and completely skipped by `halve` and `moved`. For the attacker/defender of the failing line `t`, testing `middle` (or `spots` / `16`) immediately after `['early', 'early']` avoids missing an interior solution window.
3. **Prioritize Attackers/Defenders of `diff` Ahead of `66`-SP Budget Artifacts in `halve`**:
   In Start 6, `Runerigus`, `Indeedee`, and `Salamence` burned ~95 rebuilds bisecting `66`-SP budget saturation thresholds on stats unrelated to `4.6` (`Runerigus atk/spa/spe`, `Indeedee atk/def`, `Salamence hp/atk/def/spd`). Ordering `halve` by `cast` tier (and/or `d.moved` first) ensures the failing action's own `Pelipper spa` is resolved before peripheral Turn 1–3 budget edges drain the fallback quota.
