## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Percentage Quantization Across 8 Active Combatants
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Neither side's exact integer HP is disclosed. Every HP change is rounded to integer percentages: $\lceil \text{currentHP} \times 100 / \text{maxHP} \rceil$.
- 8 Pokémon were brought into combat (4 per side), and all 8 engaged in multi-turn exchanges with multiple damage events per turn (Hurricane, Thunder, Ice Spinner, Swords Dance boosts, Leech Life drains, Leftovers ticks, status chip).
- Every combat formula involves coupled unknowns:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \longrightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \longrightarrow \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because many combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` produce identical spectator percentages on Turns 1–2, the initial rounds cannot eliminate candidate spreads.

### Root Cause 2: The Turn 3/4 Gardevoir Confusion vs. Full Paralysis Deadlock
The primary stalling bottleneck in spectator search occurred across Turns 3 and 4:
- On Turn 2, Gardevoir is hit by Pelipper's Hurricane (confusing it) and Goodra's Thunder (paralyzing it).
- On Turn 3, Gardevoir is at `20/100y par`. It rolls confusion: hits itself, dealing damage down to `11/100 par`.

  - In the ground-truth battle: Gardevoir passes the confusion check (does not hit self), then rolls the 25% paralysis check: **Full Paralysis!** (`|cant|p2a: Gardevoir|par`).
- **The Search Dilemma**:
  - In candidate guesses across **dozens of rounds** (Rounds 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, 27, 29, 30, 31, 32, 34, 35, 36, 37, 39, 40, etc.):
    ```text
    wanted |cant|p2a: Gardevoir|par
    got    |-damage|p2a: Gardevoir|1/100 par|[from] confusion
    ```
    or `1/144 par|[from] confusion`.
  - **Why did this happen?**
    1. If Gardevoir's candidate maxHP or Def/Atk ratio is even slightly different from ground truth, the confusion self-hit damage formula $(((2 \times 50 / 5 + 2) \times 40 \times \text{Atk} / \text{Def}) / 50 + 2)$ results in different integer damage.
    2. More critically, the PRNG sequence advances through:
       - Damage rolls on Turn 1 (Hurricane, Ice Spinner)
       - Damage rolls on Turn 2 (Hurricane, Thunder, confusion inflict, paralysis inflict)
       - Confusion damage roll on Turn 3
       - Hurricane/Thunder damage rolls on Turn 3
       - Leech Life damage and drain rolls on Turn 3
    3. If any preceding turn required an extra backtrack or alternate dice roll to match a spectator percentage (e.g. Golisopod drain healing `81/100` vs `82/100`, or Goodra Leftovers `17/100` vs `16/100`), the PRNG seed position entering Turn 4 is offset.
    4. An offset PRNG produces a confusion self-hit roll instead of a full paralysis roll, causing the simulator to emit `|-damage|p2a: Gardevoir|1/100 par|[from] confusion` instead of `|cant|p2a: Gardevoir|par`.
    5. Because Gardevoir was at 11% HP, hitting itself in confusion dropped it to 1% HP (or outright fainted it), immediately terminating the rebuild.
