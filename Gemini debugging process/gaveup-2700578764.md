### 5.3 The Turn 4 Reconstruct Diff: Untargeted Spread Move and `randomFoe()`
On Turn 4, the input log recorded:
```
>p1 move suckerpunch +2, move heatwave
```
Notice that for `heatwave`, no target slot was specified.
In Pokémon Showdown doubles (`activePerHalf === 2`), when a move with `target: "allAdjacentFoes"` is used without an explicit target:
1. `pokemon.getMoveTargets(move, target)` is evaluated.
2. In `battle.getTarget()`, if no target location was given, Showdown calls:
   ```ts
   return pokemon.side.randomFoe() || pokemon.side.foe.active[0];
   ```
3. `randomFoe()` calls `this.battle.sample(actives)`, which draws a pseudo-random integer (`random(2)`):
   - 0 selects `p2a: Kommo-o`
   - 1 selects `p2b: Absol`
4. The simulator prints `|move|p1b: Salamence|Heat Wave|<selectedFoe>|[spread] p2a,p2b`.
   In the mimic recording (Sodium RNG), this die was rolled as `1`, producing:
   ```
   |move|p1b: Salamence|Heat Wave|p2b: Absol|[spread] p2a,p2b
   ```
5. In `reconstruct.mjs`, `steerDice` intercepts:
   - `hitStepAccuracy` (accuracy rolls)
   - `getDamage` (damage rolls and crits)
   - `hitStepMoveHitLoop` (multihit counts)
   
   **`steerDice` does NOT intercept `randomFoe()` or `getTarget()`.**
6. Consequently, when reconstruct simulates Turn 4 from the seed, the Sodium PRNG draw at that position produces `0` (`p2a: Kommo-o`), emitting:
   ```
   |move|p1b: Salamence|Heat Wave|p2a: Kommo-o|[spread] p2a,p2b
   ```
   Because `p2a: Kommo-o` does not match the observed `p2b: Absol`, `standing()` in `reconstruct.mjs` fails, and reconstruct flags a mismatch at `turn: 4, index: 8`.

Because Kommo-o's true level 50 HP is 160 (Leftovers heals 10 HP, exactly 6.25%), any candidate spread with a different max HP (e.g. 150 HP heals 9 HP = 6.0%; 182 HP heals 11 HP = 6.04%) produces a 1% discrepancy in the rounded spectator percentage after damage or Clangorous Soul recoil.

### 5.7 The Stalling Mechanism
Under the core design of the engine:
1. "A line a rebuild stops on proves nothing" (`AGENTS.md`): Evidence hooks cannot extract constraints from turns that were never successfully reached and verified.
2. Because rebuilds repeatedly halted on Turn 4 (or 5), turns 5, 6, 7, and 8 were never settled.
3. Volcarona (which only acts on Turn 8) and Kommo-o's late-game turns (Turns 6-7) received virtually no evidence narrowing.
4. In `infer.mjs`, when a round does not advance past `deepest` (the furthest `[turn, index]` reached so far), it executes:
   ```javascript
   if (!deepest || past(at, deepest)) deepest = at;
   else stalled++;
   ```
5. Because every round hit the same Turn 4 diff or Turn 5 heal diff, `stalled` accumulated monotonically.
6. The search attempted candidate spread mixing (`mixOf`), trying combinations of previously visited spreads. These mixes suffered the exact same Turn 1–4 failures.
7. After 581 rounds, 1,747 rebuilds, and 353,189 probes, `stalled >= maxRounds` was reached, terminating the search with `stop: "stalled"`.
