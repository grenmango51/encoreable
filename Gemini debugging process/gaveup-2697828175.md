### The Mathematics of the Grassy Terrain Healing Discrepancy
- Grassy Terrain heals exactly $\lfloor \frac{\text{maxHP}}{16} \rfloor$ HP.
- In spectator channel 0, HP display percentages are computed as:
  $$\text{display\_pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- Rillaboom base HP is 100. In Level 50 Champions format:
  $$\text{maxHP} = 100 + \text{SP}_{\text{HP}} + 75 = 175 + \text{SP}_{\text{HP}}, \quad \text{where } \text{SP}_{\text{HP}} \in [0, 32]$$
  Thus $\text{maxHP} \in [175, 207]$.
- For $\text{maxHP} \in [175, 191]$: $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor = 11$ HP ($\approx 5.76\% - 6.28\%$).
- For $\text{maxHP} \in [192, 207]$: $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor = 12$ HP ($\approx 5.80\% - 6.25\%$).
- At near-zero residual HP (after suffering both Wood Hammer recoil and two instances of Rocky Helmet damage), Rillaboom's integer HP was extremely low (e.g. 1 to 2 HP, corresponding to `Math.floor(100 * 1 / 176) = 0%` or `Math.floor(100 * 2 / 176) = 1%`, displayed as rounded percentages).
