## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Milotic`'s exact `Spe` (and exact `MaxHP` / `Def` / `SpD`) and `Gholdengo`'s exact `MaxHP` are known constants, and Channel 2 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers for `Milotic` (`73%` $\to$ `45%` $\to$ Sitrus `69%` $\to$ `33%` $\to$ `1%` exact!) and `Gholdengo` (`0 fnt`). - **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Delphox`'s exact `Spe` (`32` SP $\to$ `102` paralyzed when Mega) and `Kingambit`'s exact `Atk` and `MaxHP` are fixed constants, and Channel 1 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers for `Delphox` (`20/100y par`) and `Kingambit` (`6.5`). ### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`, `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Discard `exact` and `variants` Inside `fallback` (`infer.mjs` line 499)**:
   `Round 13` / `Round 6` had *already* solved Turns 0–6 using `exact` and `built.variants`, yet `fallback`'s `rebuild()` passed `exact: null` and `variants = 0`. That forced `base` (`maxBacktracks: 0`) to re-discover Turn 4 (`4.9`) and Turn 6 (`6.5`) blind without exact HP guidance, causing `base` to regress before Turn 7. Passing `built`'s `variants` (or running `fallback`'s `base` with `proven?.exact` when `base` without `exact` stops before `t`) would keep `fallback` at Turn 7.
2. **Do Not Bail Out of `fallback` on `reach(base)[0] < t` Before Checking `dice` or Testing Stat Probes (`infer.mjs` lines 614–617)**:
   `infer.mjs` already pays for `dice = await rebuild(start, budgets[1])` (`maxBacktracks: 2 * S`) on line 610, yet line 614 checks `reach(base)[0] < t` (the `maxBacktracks: 0` run!) and returns `none()`. Worse, `none()` adds the Turn 7 mark to `spend.fruitless`, permanently disabling `fallback` for `search 2..4` (and `wide` / `widest`). When `base` stops before `t` solely because `maxBacktracks: 0` and `exact: null` failed on an earlier turn (`4.9` / `6.5`), `fallback` should fall forward to `dice` (or `proven.exact`) and **must not** add `t`'s line to `spend.fruitless` before testing a single Stat Point!
3. **Allow `order`-Line `fallback` to Probe `dims` (`Delphox spe` / `Milotic spe`) When `exact` Reaches `t`**:
   On Turn 7, `|cant|p1a: Delphox|par` vs `|move|p2a: Milotic|Ice Beam|p1a: Delphox` is a textbook `order` diff (`infer.mjs` line 624). Had `fallback` not exited early at line 614, `dims.sort((a, b) => (a.stat !== 'spe') - (b.stat !== 'spe'))` would have immediately probed `Delphox spe 32` (`102` > `102`/`101`) and `Milotic spe 0` (`101` < `102`), resolving Turn 7 in a handful of probes and letting `speed.mjs` lock `Delphox > Milotic` permanently.
