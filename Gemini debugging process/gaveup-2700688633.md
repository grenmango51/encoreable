## 5. Root Cause Analysis

### A. Why Start 1 (`default`) Gave Up at Turn 2 (`2.8`) in `trace_2700688633.log` (`both@spectator`)
On Turn 2 (`2.8`), `p1b: Sneasler` (`Rock Slide`) flinches `p2b: Dragonite`, and **`Dragonite` (`304` in Tailwind) must be sorted before `p2a: Sneasler` (`284` in Tailwind)** — otherwise, if `p2a: Sneasler` is faster than `Dragonite` (`spe 32` vs `spe 11`), `reconstruct` either prints `|cant|p2a: Sneasler|flinch` or prints `p2a: Sneasler`'s `Dire Claw` before `|cant|p2b: Dragonite|flinch`:
1. **Default Start Inverted the Turn 2 Speed Order**: `defaultSpread` gave `p2b: Dragonite` (`Modest`) `32/0/0/32/0/2` (`Spe 122`) and `p2a: Sneasler` (`Adamant`) `32/32/0/0/0/2` (`Spe 142`). 2. **66-SP Budget Collision in Turn 2 Fallback (Lines 14–18)**: Because `Dragonite` (`10/0/13/32/0/11`) had already spent all `66` SP, probing **`Dragonite spe 32`** (`+21` Speed SP) stripped `21` SP from `Dragonite`'s bulk/SpA, flipping Turn 1's `Dragonite` vs `p2a: Sneasler` speed order (`152 > 140`) and shifting Turn 1 RNG ordinals/damage so `fallback` regressed to **`1.12: wanted |-heal|p2a: Sneasler|90/100, got 91/100`** (even with `given: null` dice re-search). Meanwhile, `Dragonite spe 0, Sneasler spe 32` inverted `p2a: Sneasler`'s Turn 2 bulk/order (`2.2: got 61/100`), exhausting all **`8` fallback rebuilds** (`fallbackRebuilds`).
