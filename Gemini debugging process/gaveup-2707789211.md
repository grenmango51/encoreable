## 5. Diagnostic Investigation: Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up

### A. Non-Linear Recoil and Percentage Quantization Coupling
On Turn 2 and Turn 3, the battle features four massive recoil attacks and three distinct end-of-turn 1/16th Max HP rounding events:
1. **Recoil Formula**:
   $$\text{Recoil} = \max\left(1, \left\lfloor\frac{\min(\text{DamageDealt}, \text{DefenderRemainingHP})}{3}\right\rfloor\right)$$
2. When Mega Staraptor KOs Scovillain from full HP, the recoil taken is strictly proportional to Scovillain's integer Max HP.
3. When Rillaboom KOs Primarina on Turn 3, the recoil taken is strictly proportional to Primarina's integer Max HP.
4. **Spectator Channel 0 Quantization**:
   Spectator logs only display integer percentages:
   $$\text{DisplayPct} = \text{round}\left(100 \times \frac{\text{CurrentHP}}{\text{MaxHP}}\right)$$
5. On Turn 3, Staraptor's HP after Turn 2 Brave Bird recoil, Turn 2 Burn tick, and Turn 3 Burn tick lands right on the rounding threshold between `57.5%` and `58.0%`. A shift of just 1 HP in Scovillain's planted bulk or Staraptor's planted Max HP flips the displayed spectator line between `57/100` and `58/100`.

### B. The Turn 0 Entry Ability Speed-Tier Trap
In `both@spectator`, the engine attempts univariate coordinate descent in `fallback` on candidate Pokémon.
When fallback tries adjusting Staraptor's stat points on Turn 3 to fix the `57/100` burn tick:
- Staraptor has base Speed 100; Vanilluxe has base Speed 79; Rillaboom has base Speed 85.
- Stat points in Champions format are constrained to 66 SP total across stats.
- When fallback reallocates points into Attack or Defense, it pulls points from Speed (e.g., `Staraptor spe 0` or `spe < 12`).
- As soon as Staraptor's Speed drops below Vanilluxe's Speed (139 vs 140+):
  ```
  fallback on turn 3: Staraptor atk 32 - stops at 0.22: wanted |-ability|p2a: Staraptor|Intimidate|boost, got |-weather|Snowscape|[from] ability: Snow Warning|[of] p1a: Vanilluxe
  ```
- Because Showdown triggers entry abilities strictly in descending Speed order, flipping the speed tie causes Vanilluxe's `Snow Warning` to execute before Staraptor's `Intimidate`.
- The rebuild immediately fails at line `0.22`, before Turn 1 even begins!
- Out of 32 fallback rebuilds for Staraptor, **7 distinct parameter assignments failed at Turn 0.22**.

### C. Multi-Target Cross-Turn Coupling Gridlock
To satisfy all spectator percentage assertions up to Turn 3, the engine must simultaneously balance:
1. `p2a: Staraptor`: Max HP (governs burn tick) and Speed (must stay > Vanilluxe).
2. `p1b: Scovillain`: Max HP and Defense (governs Staraptor's Turn 2 recoil).
3. `p1a: Vanilluxe`: Defense and Max HP (governs Rillaboom's Turn 2 recoil and Vanilluxe's Turn 3 survival).
4. `p2b: Rillaboom`: Attack, Defense, and Max HP (governs Turn 2 & Turn 3 Wood Hammer recoil and Grassy Terrain healing ticks).
5. `p1b: Primarina`: Max HP and SpD (governs Rillaboom's Turn 3 recoil).

When fallback modifies any single Pokémon's candidate spread in isolation:
- Modifying `Rillaboom atk` breaks Turn 2 Vanilluxe damage (`stops at 2.9: wanted 25/100, got 15/100`).
- Modifying `Rillaboom def/spa/spd/spe` leaves Turn 3 Grassy Terrain healing unchanged (`wanted 62/100, got 63/100`).
- Modifying `Primarina hp` pushes past line 3.10 to 3.11, but immediately collides with Vanilluxe's heal (`wanted 36/100, got 37/100`).

### D. Dice Search Escalation on a Deterministic Bulk Wall
When fallback fails to find a single-variable path forward:
1. `infer.mjs` invokes the dice search ladder (budgets 1, 2, 3, 4).
2. Search levels 2, 3, and 4 scale `maxProbes` and `maxBacktracks`, testing whether different damage rolls in Turns 1–2 can alter the HP totals entering Turn 3.
3. However, Wood Hammer and Brave Bird against frail targets dealing >100% overkill damage produce recoil capped strictly by target Max HP, which is a deterministic constant unaffected by damage rolls.
4. Furthermore, Burn ticks (1/16th Max HP) and Grassy Terrain healing (1/16th Max HP) are 100% deterministic functions of Max HP with zero dice involved.
5. Probing 1.57 million RNG seeds on a deterministic bulk discrepancy accomplishes nothing.
