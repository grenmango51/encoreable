## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Turn 1 Recoil-to-Rocky-Helmet Arithmetic Deadlock (`r = 17` vs. `r = 18`)
Let `p1a: Basculegion`'s MaxHP be $M = 195 + \text{SP}_{\text{hp}}$ ($195 \le M \le 227$), with `Life Orb` damage $L = \lfloor M / 10 \rfloor$, `Rocky Helmet` damage $H = \lfloor M / 6 \rfloor$, and Turn 1 `Wave Crash` recoil $r = \text{round}(\text{dealt} \times 33 / 100)$.
Because `Staraptor` fainted on Turn 1, `Wave Crash` damage is capped at `Staraptor`'s remaining HP $h_{\text{Star}}$ after `Sucker Punch` (`28/100`).

In **every search start**, Turn 1 rebuilt with `Staraptor` guessed at **`hp = 18` (`MaxHP = 178`, Starts 1, 3, 4, 6)** or **`hp = 17` (`MaxHP = 177`, Start 2, 5)**:
- For `MaxHP = 178` (or `177`), $\lfloor 100 \cdot h_{\text{Star}} / \text{MaxHP} \rfloor = 28$ holds **only for $h_{\text{Star}} \in \{50, 51\}$**.
- Both $50$ and $51$ produce identical Turn 1 recoil:
  $$r = \text{round}(50 \times 0.33) = \text{round}(16.5) = 17 \quad \text{and} \quad r = \text{round}(51 \times 0.33) = \text{round}(16.83) = 17$$

Now examine `p1a: Basculegion` across **all 33 legal `hp` SP values (`0..32`, $M = 195..227$)** when **$r = 17$**:
1. **For $M \in [195, 211]$ (`hp = 0..16`)**:
   - Turn 1 passes (`91/100` after `-17`, `81/100` after $-L$), **but Turn 2 `2.7` always prints `65/100` (never `64/100`)**:
     - `hp = 0` ($M=195$): $195 - 17 - 19 - 32 = 127$ ($\lfloor 12700 / 195 \rfloor = 65/100$) — verified by line 490.
     - `hp = 5` ($M=200$): $200 - 17 - 20 - 33 = 130$ ($\lfloor 13000 / 200 \rfloor = 65/100$) — verified by lines 36, 67, 306.
     - `hp = 9` ($M=204$): $204 - 17 - 20 - 34 = 133$ ($\lfloor 13300 / 204 \rfloor = 65/100$) — verified by lines 149, 225, 382.
     - `hp = 16` ($M=211$): $211 - 17 - 21 - 35 = 138$ ($\lfloor 13800 / 211 \rfloor = 65/100$) — verified by lines 37, 151, 307, 384.
2. **For $M \in [212, 227]$ (`hp = 17..32`)**:
   - Turn 1 itself breaks immediately:

**Under $r = 17$, zero out of 33 `Basculegion hp` spreads can satisfy Turn 1 (`91/100`, `81/100`) and Turn 2 (`64/100`) simultaneously!**

By contrast, when **`Staraptor` has `hp >= 27` (`MaxHP` `187..192`)**, `28/100` allows **$h_{\text{Star}} \in \{54, 55\}$**, yielding recoil **$r = \text{round}(54 \times 0.33) = 18$** (note $r = 19$ requires $h_{\text{Star}} \ge 57$, which exceeds $0.29 \times 192 = 55.68$). Under **$r = 18$**, `Basculegion` cleanly reproduces `91/100 -> 81/100 -> 64/100` for all **`hp` $\in \{5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 15, 16\}$** — including both `hp = 5` (`200 - 18 - 20 - 33 = 129/200 = 64/100`) and `hp = 9` (`204 - 18 - 20 - 34 = 132/204 = 64/100`) that `Basculegion` was already sitting on!

### B. Why `evidencePass` & `choose()` Never Escaped `Staraptor hp = 18` / `17`
1. **`totalChange` Disengaged Victim-HP Coupling (`V = null`) and Never Re-Ran `after()` (`evidence.mjs`, lines 1540–1633)**:
   - Because `Wave Crash` read `Basculegion`'s unknown `atk`, `perA = true` in `totalChange` (line 1541), which set `const V = !perA && ... = null` (line 1542).
   - During the forward pass at `1.13`, `Basculegion` had not yet observed `2.7` (`64/100`), so both `r = 17` and `r = 18` survived `1.13` and `after()` (`narrowDealt`) left `Staraptor` with all `h0` values (`45..55`) and all `33` `hp` SP values `[0..32]`.

Thus `Staraptor`'s `hp` stayed `[0..32]` (`33` values) even when `Basculegion` was pinned!
2. **`choose()` Ordering + `aimFor()` Kept `Staraptor` Stuck Below `hp = 27` (`infer.mjs`, lines 1014–1071; `knowledge.mjs`, line 806)**:

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:5 Basculegion`'s exact `MaxHP = M` and `Atk` are fixed constants, and Channel 1 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers (`M - 18/M`, `M - 18 - L/M`, `M - 18 - L - H/M`). Knowing `Wave Crash` recoil was **exactly `18`** immediately forces `Staraptor` to `54..55` HP (`hp >= 27`) on Turn 1, completely bypassing the `r = 17` trap.
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2:2 Staraptor`'s exact `MaxHP >= 187` and `p2:1 Indeedee-F`'s exact `MaxHP` and `Def` are fixed constants, and Channel 2 prints `Staraptor`'s exact post-Sucker-Punch HP (`54/MaxHP` or `55/MaxHP`). ### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` & `infer.mjs`)
1. **Couple Recoil / Total-Damage Effects Back to Fainting Victims After Later HP Cuts (`evidence.mjs`)**:
   In `totalChange` (line 1542), `perA = true` disabled `span`, and `after()` (`narrowDealt`) only ran forward at Turn 1 `1.13`. When Turn 2 `2.7` (`64/100`) later eliminated `r = 17` from `Basculegion`'s HP chain, `Staraptor`'s `hp` never received the backward constraint `h0 >= 54` (`hp >= 27`). Re-propagating `dealtOk` back to `V` during `backward()` (or checking `totalChange` victim chains in `wholePaths()`) would immediately trim `Staraptor`'s `hp` to `[27, 32]`, letting `aimFor()` pick `hp = 29/30` cleanly.
2. **Avoid `aimFor()` Returning `prev` Unchanged When `values.length === SPAN` Inside `choose()`**:
   Because `Staraptor.hp` had all 33 values alive (`values.length === 33`), `aimFor(kn, prev, q)` returned `prev.hp` (`18` or `17`) for all `q` (`0.5`, `0.25`, `0.75`), causing `choose()` to test the exact same `Staraptor hp` three times instead of sampling `0`, `16`, and `32` when a Pokémon's HP is coupled to an upstream recoil/drain failure.
3. **Include Upstream KO Victims in `lent` / `cast` When a Recoil User's HP Stalls (`infer.mjs`, line 577)**:
   Just as fainted drain targets govern the drainer's later HP (`gaveup-2706101187`), a fainted recoil victim (`Staraptor` KOed by `Wave Crash` on Turn 1) governs the recoil user's (`Basculegion`) starting Turn 2 HP. Recording `victim -> recoilUser` in `lent` would include `Staraptor` in Turn 2's `cast` so `fallback` can probe `Staraptor hp = 32` when `Basculegion` stalls on `64/100`.
