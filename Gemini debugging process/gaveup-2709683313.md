Consequently, **any** round in `p1@p2` or `both@spectator` that fails to solve Turn 1 (`Round 1`) sets `common.limits.add('variants')` (`reconstruct.mjs:2502`).

## 7. View Asymmetry & Architectural Takeaways

1. **Always Match `--views` to `slowest_gave_up.json` When Dispatching Cluster Traces**:
   `slowest_gave_up.json` records `"views": ["p1@p2"]` for `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2709683313`, whereas the trace runner invoked `--views both@spectator`, which solved cleanly in `52.9s` (`6` rounds, `0` fallbacks). Dispatching traces using each entry's failing `views` field avoids wasted runs on passing views and directly captures the `p1@p2` stall line.
2. **Avoid Unnecessary Turn 1 `variants > 256` Explosions from Ally Slots on `'normal'` Moves (`reconstruct.mjs:423`)**:
   On Turn 1, a single flinched Pokémon (`Golisopod`, `10` options) combined with three `'normal'`-target moves (`Fake Out`, `Shadow Claw`, `Shadow Ball`, each expanding to `3` options via `doubles && moveTarget !== 'adjacentFoe'`) inflated Turn 1 to `270 > MAX_VARIANTS = 256`, tripping `'variants'` on Round 1. Ordering foe targets ahead of ally targets and capping/deferring ally-target expansion on `'normal'` moves when no ability/item/redirect cue suggests ally targeting keeps single-flinch turns well below `256`.
3. **Allow `fallback` Probes to Adjust Non-Failing Turns / Multiple Coupled Stats When Exact-HP (`channel = 2`) Constraints Bind (`infer.mjs:604, 654, 679`)**:
   In `p1@p2`, `fallback` runs perturbed spreads with `maxBacktracks: 0` (`budgets[0]`) and holds the remaining 5 stats near a `66/66`-SP guess via `closestSpread`. When exact `hp/maxhp` lines on `p2` (`channel = 2`) tightly lock one stat (`Gholdengo` `spa`/`hp`), single-stat `fallback` perturbations strip SP from a tight stat or fail on an earlier turn's unredrawn roll (`backtracks`), cycling through all `240` fallback rebuilds (`782.4s`) without advancing.
