## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `2.10` Trapped Starts 1–2 (`both@spectator`) and Why `p1@p2` / `p2@p1` Gave Up

### A. The `Dire Claw` vs. `Poison Touch` Local-Maximum & Ordinal-Shift Trap at `2.10`
In Champions (`node_modules/pokemon-showdown/data/mods/champions/moves.ts:215–225`), `Dire Claw` has a **`30%` secondary effect** (`onHit: const status = this.sample(['psn', 'par', 'slp']); target.trySetStatus(status, source)`), which runs in Step 5 (`this.secondaries`, `sim/battle-actions.ts:1099`). Immediately afterward, `Poison Touch` (`data/abilities.ts:3370–3379`) runs in Step 7 (`DamagingHit`, `sim/battle-actions.ts:1121`) with `this.randomChance(3, 10)` (`30%`) and `target.trySetStatus('psn', source)`.

Whenever `reconstruct()` settles Turn 2 (`Heat Wave`, `Dire Claw` damage) into a PRNG state where `Dire Claw`'s `randomChance(30, 100)` (draw ordinal $K$) rolls `< 30` and `sample(3)` (ordinal $K+1$) rolls `0` (`psn`), **`resolveTurn()` in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` becomes mathematically trapped at `2.10`** (`scoreOf = [10, 4, 4]`):

1. **Poison Touch Is Masked & `|-status|...|psn` Holds a Higher `fields` Score (`4 > 0`)**:
   - Expected `2.10`: `|-status|p2b: Volcarona|psn|[from] ability: Poison Touch|[of] p1b: Sneasler` (5 fields after leading `|`).
   - Actual `got`: `|-status|p2b: Volcarona|psn` (matches the first 4 pipe-separated tokens $\implies$ **`fields = 4`**).
   - Because `Volcarona` already has status `psn` from `Dire Claw`, `Poison Touch` at draw $K+2$ fails silently (`trySetStatus` returns `false`), so flipping $K+2 \to 0$ changes nothing (`score = [10, 4, 4]`).
   - Flipping `Dire Claw` ($K$) alone to `99` (miss) removes `sample(3)` ($K+1$); on the 70% of unpinned draws where `Poison Touch` misses, `got` becomes the next move line (`|move|p1a: Floette|...`), dropping `fields` from `4` down to `0` (`[10, 0, 0] < [10, 4, 4]`), so it is rejected.
2. **Two-Chance Pair Search (`reconstruct.mjs:1973–1994`) Misses the Shifted Ordinal**:
   - The pair loop tries `(a = K, b = K + 2)` with `subs = { [K]: 99, [K + 2]: 0 }`.
   - Because flipping $K$ to `99` **deletes** `sample(3)` ($K+1$), `Poison Touch` shifts from draw ordinal $K+2$ down to **$K+1$**. Thus `subs[K+2] = 0` pins a *later* draw (such as `Flame Body` or `Dazzling Gleam` crit), leaving `Poison Touch` at $K+1$ unpinned and still missing 70% of the time!
3. **`brought` Loop (`reconstruct.mjs:2000–2024`) Explicitly Rejects `Poison Touch` on Line 2010**:
   - Lines 1995–2024 were written specifically for `Dire Claw` spawning an extra die when its chance flips from miss to hit (`// a secondary that hits picks its status with a die of its own - Dire Claw's poison, paralysis or sleep`).
   - In the **reverse** direction (`Dire Claw` hit $\to$ miss, exposing `Poison Touch`), `flipped` (`a = K = 99`) finds the shifted draw `b = K + 1` (`Poison Touch`, `randomChance(3, 10)`), **but line 2010 explicitly discards it**:
     ```js
     if (!b || b.chance || b.hi - b.lo + 1 > 16 || before.get(b.i) === `${b.lo}-${b.hi}`) continue;
     ```
     Because `Poison Touch` has `b.chance === true`, `brought` skips it every time.
4. **Backtracking to Turn 1 Cannot Move Turn 2's PRNG State**:
   - Even in `search 2`, `search 3` (`maxBacktracks = 6`), and `search 4` (`resample: 1`), `redrawTurn(1, ...)` only replaces Turn 1's `Close Combat` damage/crit roll in `subs`, which intercepts the return value without shifting `battle.prng`'s sequence on Turn 2 (`3.4s`–`4.0s` per round before failing at `2.10` again).

2. **Fix the `brought` Chance-Die Filter in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` (Line 2010) for Masking Secondaries (`Dire Claw` + `Poison Touch`)**:
   In `resolveTurn`, when a move's own status secondary (`Dire Claw` `randomChance(30, 100)` + `sample(3)`) hits `psn` first, it prevents a subsequent contact ability (`Poison Touch` `randomChance(3, 10)`) from emitting `|-status|...|psn|[from] ability: Poison Touch` while holding a local-maximum `fields = 4` score. Flipping `Dire Claw` to miss deletes `sample(3)`, shifting `Poison Touch`'s draw ordinal down by `1` (breaking the `(a, b)` pair loop) and turning `Poison Touch` into a newly exposed `chance` die — which line 2010 (`if (!b || b.chance || b.hi - b.lo + 1 > 16 ...) continue;`) explicitly refuses to probe. Allowing `brought` to test newly shifted/exposed `chance` draws (`b.chance` at its `0` / `hi` ends) when `scoreOf` is stuck on a `|-status|` / `[from]` mismatch would let `resolveTurn` solve `2.10` directly on the first search (`budgets[0]`) in all views without needing a lucky `154 == 154` speed tie in `wide`.
