## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Coupled 3-Way Speed + Budget Inequality at Turn 2 (`2.2` & `2.6`)
To reproduce Turn 1 (`1.0`, `1.10`) and Turn 2 (`2.2`–`2.6`), the spreads of `p2:1 Floette`, `p1:4 Rillaboom`, and `p1:2 Dragonite` must satisfy **all** of the following simultaneously:
1. **Turn 1 Mega-Evolution Order (`1.0`)**: `Floette-Eternal` (`92 + Floette.spe`) $\ge$ `Dragonite` (`80 + Dragonite.spe`), i.e., `Dragonite.spe - Floette.spe <= 12`.
2. **Turn 1 Recoil (`1.10`: `Floette 55/100`)**: `Floette` must have **`hp >= 25` SP** (`MaxHP >= 174`) beside `Sneasler hp in [0, 7]` (`78..81` recoil), leaving at most **`66 - 25 = 41` SP** (at most `39` SP when `hp = 27`) for `Floette`'s remaining stats (`def`, `spa`, `spe`).
3. **Turn 2 First Speed Order (`2.2`: `Floette-Mega` before `Rillaboom`)**:
   $$102 + \text{Floette.spe} \ge 85 + \text{Rillaboom.spe} \implies \text{Floette.spe} \ge \text{Rillaboom.spe} - 17$$
4. **Turn 2 Second Speed Order (`2.6`: `Rillaboom` before `Dragonite-Mega`)**:
   $$85 + \text{Rillaboom.spe} \ge 100 + \text{Dragonite.spe} \implies \text{Rillaboom.spe} - \text{Dragonite.spe} \ge 15$$
5. **Turn 2 `Dazzling Gleam` Bulk (`2.4`: `Dragonite 57/100`; `2.5`: `Rillaboom 51/100`)**: Both `Dragonite` and `Rillaboom` must preserve their `(hp, spd)` bulk against `Floette`'s guessed `spa`.

Because `speed.mjs` cannot read an order from a line where the rebuild stops *before* the expected Pokémon acts (`2.2` or `2.6`), `evidencePass` could not constrain `Rillaboom.spe`, `Dragonite.spe`, or `Floette.spe` relative to one another. Thus, `picks` stayed unchanged after Round 2/3, handing the entire 3-way speed resolution to `fallback()` (`infer.mjs` line 478).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Dragonite` (`0/0/0/0/0/0`, `Mega Spe = 120`), `Rillaboom` (`3/14/10/1/13/25`, `Spe = 130`), and `Sneasler` (`2/32/0/0/0/32`, `HP 157`) are fixed ground-truth constants. Turn 2 `2.6` (`Rillaboom 130 > Dragonite-Mega 120`) never fails, Turn 1 `1.10` (`79` recoil $\to$ `55/100`) immediately restricts `Floette` to `hp >= 27`, and `2.2` (`Floette-Mega` before `Rillaboom 130`) forces `Floette.spe >= 8`, resolving cleanly.
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Floette` (`27/0/2/5/0/32`, `HP 176`, `SpA 198`, `Spe 154`) and `Whimsicott` (`2/0/0/32/0/32`) are known constants, and Channel 2 publishes `Floette`'s exact `97/176` HP on Turn 1. Because `Floette-Mega` (`Spe 154`) is faster than *every* possible `Rillaboom` (`max 137`) and `Dragonite-Mega` (`max 132`), **`2.2` can never fail** when `fallback` probes `Rillaboom spe 32`, and knowing `Floette SpA = 198` (not `227`) leaves plenty of 66-SP budget headroom for `Rillaboom spe 32` without breaking `2.5` (`51/100`).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/knowledge.mjs`, `infer.mjs`)
1. **Break L1-Distance Ties in `closestSpread` Against Unused / Flat Stats, Not Bulk (`knowledge.mjs` line 679)**:
   When a saturated (`66/66` SP) Pokémon needs `+k` SP in `spe` (like `Rillaboom 11/31/0/0/22/2` or `32/32/0/0/2/0` with `atk = 31..32` unused before `2.6`, or `Floette 29/2/0/32/0/3` with `atk = 2`), shedding `k` SP from unused `atk` has the exact same L1 cost as shedding `k` SP from `(hp, def, spd)`. Because `for (let k = 0; k < KEYS; k++)` scans from `hp = 0` upward and uses strict `<`, `closestSpread` systematically steals `hp` first — breaking earlier verified damage/recoil lines (`1.10`, `2.5`) and inverting `halving`! Preferring keys that match `prev`'s `(hp, def, spd)` on L1 ties (or penalizing changes to `hp`/`def`/`spd` / stats already read by evidence) would let `Rillaboom` shed unused `atk` instead of `hp` and immediately pass `2.6`.
2. **Record `d.sides` When `disputed` Probes a Single-Stat Endpoint (`infer.mjs` line 779)**:
   In Start 5 Round 3, `disputed` probed `[Rillaboom spe 32, Dragonite spe 0]` (line 131), which was identical to changing only `Rillaboom spe` to `32` because `Dragonite.spe` was already `0`. Because `disputed` added `chosen` to `done` without populating `d.sides` for `Rillaboom spe`, the subsequent `for (const d of dims)` loop got `null` from `probe()` and skipped binary halving on `Rillaboom spe` completely. Recording single-changed-dimension outcomes into `d.sides` (or seeding `d.sides` when `chosen` differs in one stat) would prevent `disputed` from silently disabling `halving`.
3. **Avoid Letting `halving` on Non-`order` Stats Starve `mid` Probes of `spe` on Order Stalls (`infer.mjs` lines 817–821)**:
   On an order mismatch (`order === true`), `infer.mjs` currently pushes non-`spe` stats whose ends straddle an earlier damage line (`Dragonite hp`, `Dragonite spd`, `Rillaboom hp`) into `halve[]` and runs all of their binary searches at line 817 *before* testing `mid` (`d.alive[(n-1)>>1]`) on any `spe` dimension at line 818. In Start 5 Round 3, those non-`spe` damage halvings burned the rest of the 32-rebuild quota before `Rillaboom spe 16` (`mid`) was ever tried. Running `spe` `mid` probes before non-`spe` `halve[]` on order stalls avoids wasting order-recovery budgets on unrelated damage thresholds.
