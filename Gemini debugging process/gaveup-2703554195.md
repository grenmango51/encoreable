### Root Cause 4: Oscillating Deadlock and Stalling Counter Escalation
Following the failed fallback in Round 8:
1. Round 9 tried to adjust for Turn 2 damage, but produced an over-healing failure on Turn 2 Grassy Terrain:
   ```
   wanted |-heal|p2b: Rillaboom|32/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2b: Rillaboom|33/100|[from] Grassy Terrain
   ```
2. Fallback on line 2.15 also exhausted its 8 rebuilds without finding any stat that advanced the line.
3. The inference entered a cyclic two-state limit cycle:
   - State A: Guesses satisfy Rillaboom's heal (`32/100`), but fail Annihilape damage (`wanted 59/100, got 62/100`).
   - State B: Guesses satisfy Annihilape damage, but fail Rillaboom's heal (`wanted 32/100, got 33/100`).
4. In `infer.mjs`:
   ```javascript
   if (!deepest || past(at, deepest)) deepest = at;
   else stalled++;
   ```
5. Because `deepest` was at Turn 2 (`at = [2, 15]`), but every round failed at either line 2.7 or line 2.15, `past(at, deepest)` was `false` on every single iteration.
6. `stalled` incremented continuously:
   - Rounds 10 to 22 (13 consecutive rounds) oscillated identically with zero progress.
   - Upon reaching 16 stalled rounds, Phase 1 terminated with `the search gave up; searching again from the spreads people run`.
7. Every subsequent phase (Start 2 `usage`, Start 3 `wide`, Start 4 `widest-default`, Start 5 `widest-usage`, Start 6 `widest-wide`) hit the exact same cycle.
8. In Phase 6, rounds 44 through 70 alternated back and forth between line 2.7 and line 2.15 for 27 consecutive rounds before exhausting all restart budgets and terminating with `GAVE UP` (`stop = 'stalled'`).
