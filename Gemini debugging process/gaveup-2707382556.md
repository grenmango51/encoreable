1. **With `Sneasler spd = 0` (`SpD 100`)**, **no `Floette spa` (`0..32`) can ever avoid triggering `Focus Sash`**, because even `0 SpA` deals $\ge 202$ damage against `Sneasler`'s maximum `187 HP`.

### B. Why `evidencePass` Removed Zero Spreads (`evidence.mjs:2466-2507`, `1105-1118`)
Every round reported `evidence and new guesses, 1.8s, 2 turns exact, 1 dice handed on` without eliminating a single spread:
1. **Focus Sash Cutoff Misaligned `provenLines()`**: When `prefix.cutoffAt` landed on `|-enditem|p1a: Sneasler|Focus Sash` (the rebuild's line at `1.8`), `prefix.observedLine` was `|-damage|p1a: Sneasler|1/100`. In `provenLines()` (`evidence.mjs:1105-1118`), `view[i]` at the cutoff was `|-enditem|...`, so `hpLine(next.line)` returned `null` and **`shown` became `null`**. This disabled the `sameLines(ev.lines, shown)` check (`evidence.mjs:1182`) that normally discards candidates whose `Damage` event emitted `Focus Sash` when the replay did not!
2. **`sync()` Lost the `survived = false` Constraint Across the Awaited Line**: At `evidence.mjs:2502-2507`, `sync()` recognized `survivor(entry.line) === 'p1:Sneasler'`, set `awaited = 'p1:Sneasler'`, and stepped to the very next entry (`|-damage|p1a: Sneasler|1/100` at `entry.at > prefix.cutoffAt`), calling `observe(mine, want.token, entry.at)` (`evidence.mjs:2469`) with default `survived = false`.
3. **`1` HP Matched `[1, 3]`**: Because `allowed(rec, M, '1/100', false)` returns `[1, 3]` (which includes `1`) and `damageEvent` clamped every lethal roll to `left = 1` via Focus Sash, **every lethal `(Sneasler, Floette)` spread pair survived `apply()`**!
4. Consequently, `knowledge` stayed untouched (`136,663,185` spreads on every Pokémon), `choose()` accepted `next === picks` (`unchanged = true`), and `exact` handed Turn 0 + partial Turn 1 back into the next round.

### C. Why `fallback` Failed Without Trying Joint Bulk/SpA Perturbations

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` Passed While `p1@p2` and `both@spectator` Both Gave Up
- **In `p2@p1` (`PASS`)**: `p1:4 Sneasler`'s true random spread (with high `HP` and `SpD`) is **known** from the start, and Channel 1 displays `Sneasler`'s exact `hp/maxhp` integers. Thus `Sneasler` never sits at `155 HP / 100 SpD`, and even if `Floette`'s initial guess (`SpA 227`) initially triggers Focus Sash against known `Sneasler`, moving `Floette spa` alone in `fallback` immediately brings `Light of Ruin` into natural `1/100` survival range.
- **In `p1@p2` (`GAVE UP`)**: Although `p2:3 Floette-Eternal`'s planted `SpA` is known, `p1:4 Sneasler` is unknown and Channel 2 sees only `1/100`. Under both `default` (`32/0/2/0/0/32`) and `usage` (`2/32/0/0/0/32`), `Sneasler` starts with `spe = 32` (`SpD = 100`), so `Sneasler hp 32` leaves `SpD = 100` (fatal even against `0 SpA`), while `Sneasler spd 32` causes `closestSpread` to strip `Sneasler hp` down to `0` (`155 HP`, fatal even against #3 Modest `Floette` `spa = 5`, `157..186` damage). Because `evidencePass` accepts Focus Sash's `left = 1` for `1/100` and `fallback` finds `moved = []`, **`p1@p2` stalls at the exact same Turn 1 `1.8` Focus Sash line as `both@spectator`**.

### Architectural Takeaways
1. **Enforce `!sameLines` / Non-Survival When a Rebuild Emits a `SURVIVAL` Line at the Cutoff (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs:1105-1118, 2466-2507`)**:
   When `sync()` catches `survivor(entry.line)` at `prefix.cutoffAt` (`|-enditem|...|Focus Sash`, `Sturdy`, `Endure`, `Focus Band`) where `prefix.observedLine` is the `|-damage|` line directly (no survival announcement in the replay), `moveChange()`'s `provenLines()` returns `null` and `apply()` treats `1` HP from a Focus Sash proc as indistinguishable from natural `1/100` survival. Passing a flag (e.g., `noSurvival` / `shown = []`) when the replay omits the survival announcement would immediately rule out all `(Sneasler, Floette)` spread combinations that require Focus Sash, narrowing `Sneasler` (`hp`, `spd`) and `Floette` (`spa`) on Round 1 instead of leaving `100%` of spreads untouched and handing on a fake `Focus Sash` exact path!
2. **Allow `fallback` to Perturb `(hp, spd)` / `(attacker_stat, defender_bulk)` Pairs When `moved` Is Empty on a Survival/Damage Cutoff (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs:825-845`)**:
   When a single-stat probe (`Sneasler spd 32`) is clipped by the 66-SP cap (`closestSpread` dropping `Sneasler hp` to `0` to keep `spe = 32`), every single-stat probe leaves `outcome(b)` unchanged (`got Focus Sash` at `1.8`), so `moved` is empty and the 2-stat pair search never runs—wasting `106` unused rebuilds in Start 6. Seeding pairs across the direct attacker/defender stats in `near[0]` / `near[1]` (here `Sneasler`'s `hp, spd` and `Floette`'s `spa`) when `moved.length === 0` would resolve 2-stat survival thresholds directly.
3. **Prefer Keeping `hp` Over `spe` on Equal-Cost L1 Ties in Defensive Fallbacks, or Probe `closestSpread` Under Neutral `aim` for Unused Speed/Attack Stats (`scripts/lib/inference/knowledge.mjs:672-680`)**:
   On `Sneasler 32/0/2/0/0/32`, `Sneasler spd 32` dropped `hp` from `32` to `0` only because `closestSpread` iterates `k` (`hp` ascending) before `f` (`spe`). Even worse, `usage` start puts `32` in `atk` and `32` in `spe`, leaving only `2` SP total for `hp + spd` unless two stats are moved or `aim` relaxes untouched offensive stats.
