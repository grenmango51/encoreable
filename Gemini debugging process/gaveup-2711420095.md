## 6. Why `both@spectator` Failed (95 Rounds, 284 Rebuilds, 642,778 Probes, Stalled)

### A. The Coupled Sitrus Berry, Recoil, and Grassy Terrain Quantization Trap
In `both@spectator`, neither player's stats are known, and all observed HP transitions are integer percentages ($0$–$100\%$). On Turns 2, 3, and 4, Incineroar is entangled in a 4-way non-linear numerical feedback loop:

1. **Turn 2 Flare Blitz Damage vs Sitrus Threshold**:
   - Incineroar's Atk deals damage to Gholdengo's Def.
   - Incineroar suffers recoil equal to $\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor$.
   - Sitrus Berry triggers if and only if:
     $$\text{HP}_{\text{current}} - \text{recoil} \le \lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 2 \rfloor$$
   - In spectator view, this post-recoil HP was displayed as `32/100`.
   - Sitrus Berry restores $\lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 4 \rfloor$, which was displayed as `57/100`:
     $$\left\lfloor \frac{(\text{HP} - \text{recoil}) + \lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 4 \rfloor}{\text{MaxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor = 57\%$$
   - In the search, guesses for Incineroar HP and Gholdengo Def regularly produced `56/100` instead of `57/100` (e.g. Rounds 4, 6 in trace: `wanted |-heal|p1a: Incineroar|57/100|[from] item: Sitrus Berry, got |-heal|p1a: Incineroar|56/100`).

2. **Turn 3 Make It Rain Chip + Fatal Flare Blitz Recoil**:
   - Incineroar takes spread damage from Gholdengo's Make It Rain, bringing it to an intermediate percentage.
   - Incineroar then uses Flare Blitz to finish off Gholdengo. Under Showdown mechanics, recoil on a fatal hit is capped by the target's remaining HP:
     $$\text{recoil} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{CurrentHP}_{\text{Gholdengo}}}{3} \right\rfloor$$
   - The resulting post-recoil HP was displayed as spectator `44/100`.
   - Any 1-point difference in Gholdengo's initial HP, Gholdengo's Def, Incineroar's Atk, or Incineroar's Max HP shifted this recoil from `44/100` to `45/100` (repeated across Rounds 7, 10, 13, 14, 17, 19, 21, 23).

3. **Turn 3/4 Grassy Terrain Healing Quantization**:
   - At the end of Turn 3, Grassy Terrain heals Incineroar by $\lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 16 \rfloor$.
   - The spectator protocol displayed `51/100`:
     $$\left\lfloor \frac{\text{CurrentHP} + \lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 16 \rfloor}{\text{MaxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor = 51\%$$
   - Across almost all rebuilds, the unpinned guesses landed on `52/100` or `50/100g` (green tag at exactly 50%):
     `wanted |-heal|p1a: Incineroar|51/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p1a: Incineroar|52/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`.

Because Max HP, damage dealt, recoil taken, berry healing, and terrain healing are all distinct integer roundings of the same underlying stat parameters, shifting any single Stat Point by $+1$ or $-1$ to fix Turn 3 recoil (`45/100 -> 44/100`) immediately broke Turn 2 Sitrus Berry (`57/100 -> 56/100`) or Turn 4 Grassy Terrain (`51/100 -> 52/100`).

### B. Failure of `paths()` in `evidencePass` and Pin Collapse
When the simulator reaches Turn 4/5:
- In `infer.mjs`, `choose()` attempts to pin spreads sequentially across the active cast (`Salamence`, `Sneasler`, `Incineroar`, `Gholdengo`, `Dragonite`, `Rillaboom`, `Basculegion`).
- For each combination, `choose()` invokes `evidencePass({ exact: true })`, which calls `paths(raw)`.
- Inside `paths()` (lines 3975–4007 of `evidence.mjs`), recoil and drain transitions must match the exact damage dealt by the path's own hits:
  ```js
  if (dealt && (amounts[j] !== dealt.amount || tag % SPAN !== dealt.v)) continue;
  ```
- Because of the tight mutual quantization across the Sitrus Berry, recoil, and Grassy Terrain steps, `options.length` drops to 0 for the Turn 4/5 options.
- When `options.length === 0`, `paths()` returns `null`, causing `ctx.exact` and `proof.exact` to be `null`.
- After exhausting its budget of passes, `tries` is exhausted (`limits.add('guessTries')`).
- Without exact pins, no exact dice or turn boundaries are handed to subsequent rounds (`exact = null`, `pins = null`).

### C. Desynchronization and Fallback Cascade
Because no exact pins were handed on:
