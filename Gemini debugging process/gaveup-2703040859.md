## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

- Raichu-Mega-X's HP on Turn 6 represents the accumulated sum of 7 distinct health modifications over 5 turns:

  3. Turn 4: Volt Tackle recoil ($-\lfloor \text{dmg}_2/3 \rfloor$)

### Technical Takeaways & Engine Implications
1. **Multi-Turn Recoil and Terrain Accumulation as Precision Killers**:
   When a combatant like Raichu-Mega-X stays active across 6 turns, participating in multiple recoil moves (Volt Tackle on Turn 4 and Turn 6) interspersed with repeated end-of-turn recovery (Grassy Terrain ticks on Turns 2, 4, 5, and 6), its remaining HP is the sum of many discrete integer operations. Under spectator view, where each intermediate step is only checked through coarse ceiling percentages, the set of candidate spreads that satisfies all intermediate percentage checks is non-empty, but individual candidate paths drift by $\pm 1$ or $\pm 2$ integer HP. When that drift coincides with a ceiling percentage transition (such as $28/137 = 20.43\% \rightarrow 21\%$ vs $29/137 = 21.16\% \rightarrow 22\%$), the candidate rebuild fails abruptly.
2. **The Fragility of 1-D Coordinate Fallback Perturbations**:
   The fallback heuristic in `infer.mjs` perturbs single statistics of participating Pokémon (e.g., `Raichu atk 1`, `Rillaboom def 16`, `Incineroar def 3`). However, Pokémon like Rillaboom and Incineroar are coupled across multiple turns: Rillaboom attacked on Turn 2, took damage on Turn 3, attacked on Turn 4, and took damage on Turn 6. Shifting Rillaboom's Defense to fix Turn 6 Volt Tackle recoil inevitably invalidates Turn 3 Muddy Water damage. A single-coordinate descent cannot escape this Pareto trap because the feasible solution requires simultaneous coordinate adjustments along a narrow diagonal manifold.
3. **Early Branch Elimination vs. Search Budget Exhaustion**:
   In `both@spectator`, when all starts in the initial search phase stall at the same turn (Turn 6.16), the search escalates through searches 1, 2, 3, and 4 with wider limits. However, widening the search budget does not solve the underlying manifold problem: it merely causes the engine to spend more time testing thousands of 1-D perturbations that are doomed to break earlier prefix turns. Recognizing when all fallback candidates fail due to multi-turn coupled recoil/terrain rounding could trigger joint multi-parameter relaxation or interval pruning rather than brute-force search exhaustion.
