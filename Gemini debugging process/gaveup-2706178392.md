## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

$$\text{Damage}(\text{Beat Up hit} \to \text{Indeedee}) = 2 \times \left\lfloor \text{BaseDamage} \times \frac{r}{100} \right\rfloor \equiv 0 \pmod 2$$
**Every possible damage roll of every `Beat Up` hit — whether non-crit (`8, 10, 12, 14`) or critical (`10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20`) — is strictly an EVEN integer (`2k`).**

Now trace what happened under the inference's guess **`Indeedee 29/0/26/0/11/0` (`MaxHP = 174`, `Def = 122`, `SpD = 136`)** paired with **`Primarina 32/0/0/32/0/2` (`SpA = 195`)**:

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Primarina`'s true random `SpA = 1` (`161`) and `Whimsicott`'s true random `Atk = 7` (`94`) are fixed ground-truth constants, and Channel 1 sees `Primarina`'s exact HP. With `Primarina SpA = 161` fixed, `Indeedee`'s `Usage` and `Default` guesses never get forced up to `SpD = 11` / `HP = 29`; `Indeedee`'s `32/0/32/0/2/0` (`MaxHP = 177`) immediately satisfies both Turn 1 `81/100` (`145/177`) and Turn 2 `61/100` (`109/177`).
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Indeedee`'s exact `MaxHP = 177` (`32` SP), `Def = 128`, `SpD = 127` and exact HP integers (`145/177` on Turn 1, `137/177` $\to$ `129/177` $\to$ `109/177` on Turn 2) are directly visible on Channel 2. Knowing `Indeedee` took `32` damage on Turn 1 immediately pins `Primarina` to low `SpA` (`1` SP) instead of `32` SP, and `MaxHP = 177` avoids the `174` parity trap entirely.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Normalize `markOf(diff)` by Stripping Exact-HP Numerator/Denominator When Comparing Against `spend.fruitless`**:
   In `infer.mjs` line 426 (`markOf = d => (d ? `${d.turn}|${d.index}|${d.expected}` : null)`), Round 5 failed on `|-damage|p2b: Indeedee|61/100` and added that mark to `spend.fruitless`, yet Round 6 (`search 2`) re-entered `fallback` on the exact same line (`2.9`) because `exact` turned `61/100` into `107/174`. Keying `spend.fruitless` on the normalized percentage line (or `turn|index`) would eliminate the duplicate fallback pass (`240` of `480` fallback rebuilds here).
2. **Avoid `held` / 66-SP Saturation Traps Between Attacker and Defender (`Primarina SpA` vs. `Indeedee HP/Def/SpD`)**:
   Whenever a hit between two unknown Pokémon is off by damage, `choose()` (`aimFor`) can lock the attacker at `32` offensive SP (`Primarina SpA = 32`) while forcing the defender onto a smaller `MaxHP` (`Indeedee 29/0/26/0/11/0` at `66/66` SP) to match Turn 1, creating an unbreakable even-damage parity deadlock on Turn 2 (`107/174`) where single-stat fallback probes on `Indeedee` cannot raise `hp` (`29 → 32`) without breaking `def` (`26 → 23`) or `spd` (`11 → 8`). Pairing offensive stats of prior attackers on `Indeedee` (`Primarina spa`) with defender `hp` in `fallback`'s 2-stat search — even when `Primarina spa` alone returned the same failing percentage `63/100` — would escape this class of 2-Pokémon coupled parity traps.
3. **Do Not Mark Off-Stats (`atk`, `spa`, `spe` on Non-Users) as `d.moved` Solely Because 66-SP Trimming Moved an Earlier Turn/Line**:
   Testing `Indeedee atk 12` or `spa 12` shifted `Indeedee`'s failing line from `2.9` (`63/100`) to `2.4` (`75/100`) only because `closestSpread` had to cannibalize `def` to stay within 66 SP. Filtering `d.moved` (or restricting `fallback`'s exhaustive sweep at lines 825–832) so non-participating stats are not probed over every integer when `closestSpread` displaced another stat would prevent wasting dozens of rebuilds per start.
4. **Prevent `evidence.mjs` `paths()` from Pinning an Impossible Exact HP Through a `band('down')` Cutoff Line**:
   Because `Beat Up` Hit 3 read a back-party Pokémon (`hit.back = true`) and degraded to `band('down')`, `paths()` wrote `107/174` into `exact` Turn 2 (`2.9`) even though `107/174` was never checked by `moveChange` and was unreachable under `MaxHP = 174`.
