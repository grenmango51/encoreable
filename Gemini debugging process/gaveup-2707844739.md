## 5. Diagnostic Investigation: Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up

### A. The "Persian Switching Into Persian" Illusion Paradox
The central mechanical blocker that deadlocked the inference engine occurs on Turn 3 of the mimic execution:
1. The public spectator log contains the line:
   ```
   |switch|p2b: Persian|Persian-Alola, L50, F|100/100|[from] Parting Shot
   ```
2. When `infer.mjs` attempts to reconstruct the battle without knowing the hidden roster allocation, it treats `p2b` as `Persian-Alola`.
3. In Pokémon Showdown's battle engine, an active Pokémon **cannot switch into itself**. When an action demands a switch to `Persian` while `Persian` is the active battler, the engine either treats the choice as invalid or forces a legal switch to the only available benched ally (`Mawile`):
   ```
   got |switch|p2b: Mawile|Mawile, L50, M|100/100|[from] Parting Shot
   ```
4. This mismatch (`wanted Persian, got Mawile`) is not a Stat Point or Speed discrepancy—it is an **Illusion identity contradiction**.

### B. Why Disguise Branching (`illusionStays`) Failed to Resolve It
To handle Illusion disguises, `infer.mjs` incorporates a dedicated search routine (`infer.mjs`, lines 1142–1159):
```js
for (const { at, turn } of disguisableStays(formatid, packed, channel, observed, guessed)) {
  if (turn > failing || (built.forced || []).includes(at)) continue;
  onProgress(`reading the switch-in at line ${at} as the Illusion user`);
  const other = absorb(await inferSpreads({ ...options, forced: [at], readings: false }));
  if (!other.complete) continue;
  ...
}
```
The cluster trace log demonstrates that the engine attempted three distinct disguise readings:
1. **Line 29 (`reading the switch-in at line 29 as the Illusion user`)**:
   - Forces the lead `p2a: Arcanine-Hisui` to be `Zoroark-Hisui`.
   - **Mechanical Failure**: Zoroark has `Illusion`, not `Intimidate`. Under this reading, Turn 0 completely fails to produce:
     ```
     wanted |-ability|p2a: Arcanine|Intimidate|boost, got |turn|1
     ```
   - All 4 search levels failed instantly in 0.0s.
2. **Line 30 (`reading the switch-in at line 30 as the Illusion user`)**:
   - Forces the lead `p2b: Persian-Alola` to be `Zoroark-Hisui`.
   - **Mechanical Failure**: Zoroark's moveset is `[Bitter Malice, Protect, U-turn, Will-O-Wisp]`. Zoroark does not know `Snarl`. When Turn 1 executed, the engine produced:
     ```
     wanted |move|p2b: Persian|Snarl|p1b: Garchomp|[spread] p1a,p1b, got |move|p2b: Persian|Bitter Malice|p1a: Sableye
     ```
   - Coordinate descent in fallback tested all 32 stat permutations for every active Pokémon, but no spread can grant a Pokémon a move not on its moveset. Result: `nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds, 3.1s`.
3. **Line 82 (`reading the switch-in at line 82 as the Illusion user`)**:
   - Forces `p2b: Mawile` (entering via U-turn at line 82) to be `Zoroark-Hisui`.
   - Under this reading, Turn 1 reproduces, but when Turn 3 Parting Shot occurs, the engine still has Persian on the field trying to switch into Persian, reproducing the identical diff:
     ```
     wanted |switch|p2b: Persian|Persian-Alola... got |switch|p2b: Mawile|Mawile...
     ```
   - Fallback tested Mawile Spe (0, 32), Persian Spe (0, 32, 16, 8, 4, 6, 7), and Garchomp Spe, with zero successes: `nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds, 18.4s`.

### C. The Truncation of `turn > failing`
Why didn't `disguisableStays` test the actual Parting Shot switch-in?
In `infer.mjs`, line 1146:
```js
const failing = built.report.diffs[0]?.turn ?? Infinity;
if (turn > failing) continue;
```
When the diff occurs at Turn 3 (`reproduces through turn 2`), `failing` is evaluated as Turn 2 or Turn 3. Switch-in lines that occur at or after the failing turn boundary are skipped or fail to re-anchor before the switch action is evaluated. Consequently, the true disguise point remained unreached.

### D. The Fallback Coordinate Descent Trap
Whenever a guess stumbled upon Turn 3, fallback was invoked to perturb single-coordinate stats:

  wanted |move|p2b: Persian|Parting Shot|p1b: Garchomp, got |move|p2b: Persian|Parting Shot|p1a: Volcarona

1. Player 2's switch choices are obscured by Illusion.
2. When Player 1 observes Persian use Parting Shot and switch into Persian, the engine still attempts to re-simulate Player 2's actions.

4. `p2@p1` succeeded in narrowing Arcanine down to 718,065 spreads and Persian down to 35,861,276 spreads, but ultimately exhausted its budget searching for a spread that could resolve the Turn 3 switch.
