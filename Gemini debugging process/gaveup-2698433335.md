### Root Cause 2: The Turn 5 Heat Wave Burn Desynchronization Knot
The central failure point that halted the search across all 4 restart phases was Turn 5:
```
round 5: reproduces through turn 4 - wanted |move|p2b: Milotic|Recover|p2b: Milotic, got |-status|p2b: Milotic|brn
round 6: reproduces through turn 4 - wanted |move|p2b: Milotic|Recover|p2b: Milotic, got |-status|p2b: Milotic|brn
```
1. On Turn 5, Charizard-Mega-Y uses Heat Wave, which targets both `p2a` Umbreon and `p2b` Milotic.
2. In Showdown's simulator, Heat Wave executes the following PRNG sequence:
   - Target 1 (Umbreon): Accuracy check (90%), Crit check, Damage roll (16 discrete rolls), Secondary burn check (10%).
   - Target 2 (Milotic): Accuracy check (90%), Crit check, Damage roll, Secondary burn check (10%).
3. In the recorded battle, neither target was burned, and Milotic cleanly executed Recover:
   `|move|p2b: Milotic|Recover|p2b: Milotic`
4. However, during spectator search, whenever candidate spreads on Turns 1–4 diverged from ground truth (even by 1 HP), or whenever dice pins were incomplete, the RNG counter entering Turn 5 was misaligned.
5. Consequently, the PRNG call for Milotic's secondary effect hit the $\le 10\%$ burn threshold, causing Showdown to emit:
   `|-status|p2b: Milotic|brn`
6. The reconstructor protocol comparator immediately rejected the simulation:
   `wanted |move|p2b: Milotic|Recover|p2b: Milotic, got |-status|p2b: Milotic|brn`
7. This completely walled the search on Turn 5.

### Root Cause 3: The Turn 4 / Turn 5 Milotic Double-Heal / Recover Rounding Trap
On Turn 4, Milotic takes Dragon Claw from Garchomp and heals twice at end of turn:
- Grassy Terrain heal: $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$
- Leftovers heal: $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$

In the trace log, fallback rebuilds repeatedly diffed on these intermediate heal lines:
- **Round 3**: `wanted |-heal|p2b: Milotic|49/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got 48/100`
- **Round 4**: `wanted |-heal|p2b: Milotic|54/100|[from] item: Leftovers, got 55/100`
- **Round 20**: `wanted |-heal|p2b: Milotic|77/100, got 78/100`

Because Milotic's max HP ranges from 170 to 202 depending on Stat Points, $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$ fluctuates between 10, 11, and 12 HP. A shift of just 1 HP in Milotic's max HP or Garchomp's damage roll pushes the resulting percentage across integer floors, causing an alternating diff cycle between 48% vs 49% and 54% vs 55%.

### Root Cause 4: Turn 6 Fallback Collapse Due to the Turn 5 Barrier
In the few search attempts where Turn 5 narrowly slipped past the burn check (Rounds 10 and 15):
```
round 10 (search 4): reproduces through turn 5 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Charizard|91/100, got |-damage|p1b: Charizard|89/100
...
fallback on turn 6: |-damage|p1b: Charizard|91/100 - the guess alone does not
fallback on turn 6: the guess stops before the turn, at 5.7: wanted |move|p2b: Milotic|Recover|p2b: Milotic, got |-status|p2b: Milotic|brn
```
When `fallback` attempted to perturb candidate spreads on Charizard, Farigiraf, Milotic, or Garchomp to match the Turn 6 Icy Wind damage (`91/100`), every perturbed spread altered the Turn 5 dice sequence and triggered the Turn 5 burn bug (`wanted Recover, got brn`). The search could not even evaluate Turn 6 candidates because Turn 5 acted as an impenetrable upstream barrier.

### Root Cause 5: Cyclic Fallback Exhaustion and Search Budget Depletion
Across all search restarts, fallback perturbations systematically tested individual stats:
- Perturbing `Milotic hp 1 ... 32`: diffs on Turn 4 Dragon Claw damage (`wanted 43/100, got 34/100` or `38/100`).
- Perturbing `Milotic def 0 ... 32`: diffs on Turn 5 Heat Wave damage (`wanted 27/100, got 23/100` or `21/100`).
- Perturbing `Charizard spa 0 ... 32`: either diffs on Turn 2 Weather Ball into Umbreon (`wanted 2/100, got 0 fnt`) or triggers the Turn 5 burn (`wanted Recover, got brn`).
- Perturbing `Garchomp atk 0 ... 32`: diffs on Turn 2 Dragon Claw into Umbreon (`wanted 46/100, got 50/100` or `52/100`).
- Perturbing `Umbreon hp / def / spd`: causes Umbreon to faint early on Turn 2 (`0 fnt`) or fail Turn 3 Moonlight healing (`wanted 75/100, got 74/100`).

`fallback` reported:
`fallback on turn 5: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds` (and later in 32 and 128 rebuilds).

The search progressed through all 4 restart tiers:
1. **Default start**: Stalls on Turn 5 (9 rounds).
2. **Usage start**: Stalls on Turn 5 (20 rounds).
3. **Wide start**: Reruns with $4\times$ fallback rebuilds; stalls on Turn 5 (9 rounds).
4. **Widest start**: Reruns from all starts with $4\times$ all limits; stalls again (38 rounds).

Total work accumulated:
- **76 rounds**
- **362 rebuilds**
- **611,088 probes**
- **4,422.2s (~1.23 hrs)** in cluster sweep (625.2s in local trace)
- Final stop: `stop = "searches"`, with limits `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches", "variants"]`.
