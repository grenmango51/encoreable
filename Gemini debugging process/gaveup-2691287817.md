   - For Archaludon's Leftovers (`subOrder: 4`) to execute **before** Rillaboom's Grassy Terrain (`subOrder: 2`), Archaludon MUST be strictly faster than Rillaboom:
     $$S_{\text{Arch}} > S_{\text{Rilla}}$$
   - If Archaludon and Rillaboom have the **same speed** (for example, if both are guessed at 0 SP on their neutral/negative nature, giving identical Speed 105), the speed comparison ties.
   - On a speed tie, `comparePriority` falls through to `subOrder`. Since $2 < 4$, Rillaboom's Grassy Terrain executes before Archaludon's Leftovers:
     ```text
     |-heal|p2b: Archaludon|...|[from] Grassy Terrain
     |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|...|[from] Grassy Terrain
     |-heal|p2b: Archaludon|...|[from] item: Leftovers
     ```
   - This produces the exact diff observed in Round 7 and Round 8:
     `wanted |-heal|p2b: Archaludon|56/100|[from] item: Leftovers, got |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|96/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`

### Root Cause 3: The Multi-Turn Speed Sandwich on Archaludon
To satisfy all turns in the battle simultaneously, Archaludon's Speed is constrained by three conflicting move and residual orders:
1. **Turn 4 Residuals**: Archaludon must be faster than P2 Rillaboom ($S_{\text{Arch}} > S_{\text{p2\_Rilla}} \ge 105 \implies S_{\text{Arch}} \ge 106$).
2. **Turn 4 Move Order**: Gholdengo (Speed 118) must move before Archaludon to cast Shadow Ball. If Archaludon moves first, it KOes Gholdengo with Electro Shot before Gholdengo acts:
   $$S_{\text{Arch}} < S_{\text{Gholdengo}} = 118$$
3. **Turn 2 Move Order**: P1 Rillaboom (Speed 127) must move before Archaludon to use Wood Hammer before Archaludon's Electro Shot:
   $$S_{\text{Arch}} < S_{\text{p1\_Rilla}} = 127$$

For Archaludon (base Speed 85, Modest nature, level 50 formula $\lfloor (85 + \text{SP} + 20) \times 1.0 \rfloor = 105 + \text{SP}$), this establishes a strict integer corridor:
$$106 \le 105 + \text{SP} \le 117 \iff 1 \le \text{SP} \le 12$$

### Root Cause 4: Failure of Fallback Probes in the Multi-Dimensional Local Minimum
When the rebuild halts at Turn 4 on the Leftovers ordering diff:
1. `fallback` (infer.mjs:732) probes candidate stats by testing their **extreme alive ends** (`alive[0]` and `alive[alive.length - 1]`).
2. For Archaludon's Speed, the surviving domain is $[0, 31]$. Fallback tests `Archaludon spe 31` (Speed 136):
   - At Speed 136, Archaludon becomes faster than P1 Rillaboom (127) on Turn 2.
   - The rebuild stops early at Turn 2 index 3:
     `fallback on turn 4: Archaludon spe 31, Rillaboom spe 0 - stops at 2.3: wanted |move|p1b: Rillaboom|Wood Hammer|p2b: Archaludon, got |move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]`
3. `escort` (infer.mjs:726) detects that `spe 31` broke the Turn 2 order against P1 Rillaboom, and attempts to restore order by pushing Rillaboom's Speed to its upper extreme (`Rillaboom spe 32`):
   - Under the 66 Stat Point cap, forcing Rillaboom to `spe 32` takes points away from Rillaboom's Attack (16 SP) and HP/Defense.
   - With reduced Attack, Rillaboom's Wood Hammer damage on Archaludon drops from 35 HP to lower values.
   - The rebuild stops at Turn 2 index 5 on a damage diff:
     `fallback on turn 4: Archaludon spe 31, Rillaboom spe 0, Rillaboom spe 32 - stops at 2.5: wanted |-damage|p2b: Archaludon|82/100, got |-damage|p2b: Archaludon|80/100`
4. Fallback tests `Rillaboom spe 0`, which stops at 2.3 again.
5. Because intermediate speed values ($1 \le \text{SP} \le 12$) are not tested by the extreme-ends probe, fallback concludes:
   `fallback on turn 4: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`
6. Fallback exhausts its per-line rebuild limit (`FALLBACK_REBUILDS = 8`) and returns `null`.

### Root Cause 5: The Turn 4 Health Bar Color Tag Boundary (`50/100g` vs `50/100y`)
Even when move order is satisfied on Turn 4, the HP state after Shadow Ball and Grassy Terrain healing encounters a color tag boundary:
- In Showdown, current HP $> 50\%$ displays in green (`g`); current HP $\le 50\%$ displays in yellow (`y`).
- In the replay, Archaludon has max HP 197 ($M/2 = 98.5$).
- Shadow Ball deals 110 damage ($197 - 110 = 87$ HP).
- Grassy Terrain heals $\lfloor 197 / 16 \rfloor = 12$ HP ($87 + 12 = 99$ HP).
- Because $99 > 98.5$, HP is green: `50/100g`.
- In Round 6 of inference:
  `reproduces through turn 3 - wanted |-heal|p2b: Archaludon|50/100g|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2b: Archaludon|50/100y|[from] Grassy Terrain`
- Guesses where Archaludon has slightly less max HP or takes slightly more damage result in HP $\le 50\%$, yielding `50/100y` and triggering a diff.

### Root Cause 6: Search Escalation, Restarts Cascade, and Limit Exhaustion
1. When fallback returns `null`, `infer.mjs` increments `level` through the budget progression (`[150, 600, 4000, 4000 resample]`).
2. The initial `default` start gives up with `stop = 'searches'`.
3. `restarts()` initiates sequential restarts:

   - `wide`: runs with $4\times$ fallback rebuilds and joint guess tries.
   - `widest`: scales all search limits $4\times$ wider (`budgetScale = 4`, probes = 16,000, maxRounds = 64, fallbackTotal = 128 rebuilds).
4. Across all restarts, every branch encounters the exact same coupled Turn 4 speed and color-tag failure.
5. Because no search branch ever reproduces past Turn 4 to reach Turn 6, turns 5 and 6 are never reached, and no evidence from them can be recorded.
6. The search finishes all restarts, accumulating 62 rounds, 314 rebuilds, and 3,751,815 probes over 17,856.5s (4.96 hours) before terminating with:
   - `stop = "searches"`
   - `limits = ["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches"]`
   - Massive unresolved pools across all 7 active Pokémon.

## 7. Diagnostic Facts Summary
1. Single-view inferences (`p2@p1` in 18.9s, `p1@p2` in 33.2s) resolved quickly because the observer's exact HP is printed in full integers, exact recoil from Turn 2 Wood Hammer immediately decouples attacker and defender physical stats, and observer speeds are fixed constants.
2. In `both@spectator`, all HP values are rounded percentages, requiring simultaneous inference of attacker stat, defender stat, damage roll, and defender max HP across 7 active Pokémon.
3. Turn 4 end-of-turn residual order in Showdown strictly sorts effects by holder speed before item subOrder. Archaludon's Leftovers can only precede P2 Rillaboom's Grassy Terrain if $S_{\text{Arch}} > S_{\text{p2\_Rilla}}$.
4. Archaludon's speed is restricted to a narrow 12-point integer corridor ($106 \le \text{Speed} \le 117$, or $1 \le \text{SP} \le 12$) by Turn 4 residuals ($> 105$), Turn 4 Shadow Ball move order ($< 118$), and Turn 2 Wood Hammer move order ($< 127$).
5. Fallback probes only test the extreme alive ends (`spe 0` and `spe 31`). Testing `spe 31` breaks Turn 2 move order (2.3), and the resulting `escort` probe on Rillaboom's speed (`spe 32`) deforms Rillaboom's Stat Point budget, breaking Turn 2 Wood Hammer damage (2.5).
6. As a result, fallback exhausts its 8-rebuild budget without passing Turn 4, triggering sequential restarts (`usage`, `wide`, `widest`) that cycle through 62 rounds, 314 rebuilds, and 3.75M probes over 4.96 hours before giving up on search limits.
