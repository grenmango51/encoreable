## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Spread Move Compounding and Percentage Floor Truncation
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Showdown calculates spectator HP percentages using integer floor division:
  $$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- Both `p1b: Floette` (Dazzling Gleam) and `p1a: Sinistcha` (Matcha Gotcha) execute **spread moves** targeting both opposing slots on Turns 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6.
- A spread move deals $0.75\times$ base power, and its damage is evaluated against two completely separate defensive targets simultaneously.
- Spectator mode observes only the rounded percentages on both targets. Because multiple combinations of `(SpA, SpD_a, SpD_b, HP_a, HP_b, roll_a, roll_b)` generate identical rounded percentages (`71/100`, `75/100`, `66/100`, `57/100`, `28/100`), the candidate spread domain cannot be constrained, leaving over 15 million surviving spreads per combatant.

### Root Cause 2: Recursive Dual-Drain & Rocky Helmet Feedback Loop
The primary mechanical failure in spectator mode stems from the coupled drain mechanics:
1. **Sinistcha's Matcha Gotcha Draining**:
   - Deals damage $D_a$ to slot A and $D_b$ to slot B.
   - Restores HP to Sinistcha equal to:
     $$\text{Heal}_{\text{Sinistcha}} = \left\lfloor \frac{D_a}{2} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{D_b}{2} \right\rfloor$$
   - Sinistcha's resulting percentage is $\lfloor 100 \times (\text{HP}_{\text{curr}} + \text{Heal}) / \text{maxHP}_{\text{Sinistcha}} \rfloor$.
2. **Golisopod's Leech Life Draining**:
   - Deals damage $D_{\text{LL}}$ to Sinistcha (or Kingambit).
   - Restores HP to Golisopod equal to $\lfloor D_{\text{LL}} / 2 \rfloor$.
3. **Sinistcha's Rocky Helmet Recoil**:
   - Inflicts recoil damage on Golisopod on contact:
     $$\text{Recoil}_{\text{Helmet}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{maxHP}_{\text{Golisopod}}}{6} \right\rfloor$$
4. **The Coupled Circular Dependency**:

     $$\text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod}} = \text{HP}_{\text{T3}} - \text{Damage}(\text{Kowtow Cleave}) - \text{Damage}(\text{Matcha Gotcha}) + \left\lfloor \frac{D_{\text{LL}}}{2} \right\rfloor - \left\lfloor \frac{\text{maxHP}_{\text{Golisopod}}}{6} \right\rfloor$$
