## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Combatants
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Neither player's exact integer HP is revealed. Every damage event, drain, Leftovers tick, and Sitrus Berry recovery is rounded to integer percentages: $\lceil \text{currentHP} \times 100 / \text{maxHP} \rceil$.
- 8 Pokémon were brought into the battle (4 per side), and all 8 engaged in active combat exchanges with multiple damage events per turn.
- Every interaction contains simultaneous coupled unknowns:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \longrightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \longrightarrow \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` yield identical spectator percentage logs on early turns, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- 5 Pokémon retained over 29 million candidate spreads (Grimmsnarl: 80.3M, Salamence: 57.7M, Tyranitar: 29.7M, Archaludon: 29.6M).

### Root Cause 2: The Turn 6/7 Sinistcha Sitrus Berry Off-by-1 Percentage Dilemma

- In **104 separate rounds**, the simulator successfully reproduced all choices and events through Turn 6, but failed on Turn 7:

  ```text
  got    |-heal|p1b: Sinistcha|63/100|[from] item: Sitrus Berry
  ```
- **The Precise Mathematical Boundary**:
  - Sinistcha's planted maxHP is 178 ($71 + 32 + 75$).
  - Sitrus Berry restores:
    $$\text{heal} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{maxHP}}{4} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{178}{4} \right\rfloor = 44 \text{ HP}$$
  - In the ground-truth battle:
    - Sinistcha enters Turn 7 at 132 HP.
    - Archaludon's Dragon Pulse deals 61 HP damage.
    - Current HP drops to $132 - 61 = 71 \text{ HP}$.
    - Since $71 \le \lfloor 178 / 2 \rfloor = 89$, Sitrus Berry triggers:
      $$\text{HP}_{\text{post-berry}} = 71 + 44 = 115 \text{ HP}$$
    - Spectator percentage log:
      $$\left\lceil \frac{115 \times 100}{178} \right\rceil = \lceil 64.6067\% \rceil = \mathbf{64\%} \longrightarrow \text{`64/100`}$$

    - If Sinistcha enters Turn 7 with just **1 or 2 less HP** (e.g. 130 instead of 132, caused by Archaludon's Turn 5 Snarl dealing 38 HP instead of 36, or Venusaur's Earth Power dealing 24 instead of 23):

### Root Cause 3: The Turn 4/5 Secondary Stalling Cycle on Sinistcha Damage
When the search attempted to branch away from the Turn 7 Sitrus Berry deadlock, it fell into a secondary stall on Turn 4/5:
- In **127 rounds**, the search stopped on Turn 4 or Turn 5:
  - 96 rounds: `wanted |-damage|p1b: Sinistcha|60/100, got |-damage|p1b: Sinistcha|53/100`
  - 31 rounds: `wanted |-damage|p1b: Sinistcha|60/100, got |-damage|p1b: Sinistcha|55/100`
- **Why Turn 4/5 Stalled**:
  - Turn 5 features Venusaur's Earth Power into Sinistcha, followed by Tyranitar KOing Venusaur, followed by Archaludon's spread Snarl hitting both Tyranitar and Sinistcha.
  - To match Turn 5's `60/100` on Sinistcha, the solver attempts to modify Sinistcha's SpD or HP.
  - However, changing Sinistcha's HP or SpD immediately shifts Turn 3's Sandstorm damage percentage or Turn 4's Hospitality healing calculation.
  - Furthermore, patching Sinistcha's SpD higher to survive Turn 5 reduces the damage taken on Turn 7 from Archaludon's Dragon Pulse ($132 - 58 = 74 \text{ HP} > 89 \text{ HP}$), causing Sitrus Berry **not to trigger at all** on Turn 7!

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Turn Coupling Defeating Local Fallback Probes
The engine's fallback mechanism (`fallback(seen, start, ...)` in `infer.mjs`) is designed to probe single-stat variations on the Pokémon active during the failing turn:
1. When Turn 7 fails with the Sitrus Berry mismatch (`64/100` vs `63/100`), fallback probes Sinistcha and Archaludon stats on Turn 7.
2. In all Turn 7 fallback attempts in the trace log, the result was:
   ```text
   fallback on turn 7: Sinistcha hp 16 - stops at 5.13: wanted |-damage|p1b: Sinistcha|60/100, got |-damage|p1b: Sinistcha|58/100
   fallback on turn 7: Sinistcha hp 32 - stops at 6.12: wanted |-heal|p1b: Sinistcha|74/100, got |-heal|p1b: Sinistcha|73/100
   ```

3. Because Sinistcha's entering HP on Turn 7 is the accumulated sum of:
