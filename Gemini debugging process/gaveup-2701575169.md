### A. Uncoupled `Metal Burst` (`carried = true`) + Two-Pokémon HP Deadlock at `5.6`
In `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` (lines 959–966):
```js
const carried = !!clone.damageCallback && !reads.length && !targetHp && !sourceHp;
```
Because `Bastiodon`'s `Metal Burst` returns `1.5 × lastDamagedBy.damage` via `damageCallback` without reading any stat or HP directly, `evidence.mjs` marks `Metal Burst` as `carried = true` (`supported = false`) and treats `5.6` (`|-damage|p1b: Meganium|28/100 brn`) only as an uncoupled downward HP band. Thus, **`evidence.mjs` never links `Meganium`'s `hp`/`spa` to `Bastiodon`'s `hp`/`spd` across `5.2` $\to$ `5.6`**.

Meanwhile, in Round 5 of every start, the guess sent to Turn 5 had **`Meganium 16/0/0/32/0/2`** (`MaxHP = 171`, `141` HP entering Turn 5) and **`Bastiodon 16/0/2/0/32/0`** (`MaxHP = 151`, `SpD = 209`):
1. Under guessed `Bastiodon` (`hp = 16`, `MaxHP = 151`, `SpD = 209`), Turn 4 `Solar Beam` (`46/100`) left `Bastiodon` at `70/151`, plus `+9` Leftovers = `79/151` entering Turn 5. To hit `5.2: |-damage|p2a: Bastiodon|5/100` (`8` or `9` / `151`), `reconstruct` was **forced to pick the max roll `70` damage** (`79 - 70 = 9/151 = 5.96%`).
2. `Metal Burst` therefore returned $\lfloor 70 \times 1.5 \rfloor = \mathbf{105}$ damage! On guessed `Meganium` (`hp = 16`, `141/171` HP), `141 - 105 = 36 / 171 =` **`21/100 brn`** (lines 15, 24: `wanted 28/100 brn, got 21/100 brn` / `36/171 brn`).
3. **Why NO single-stat change in `fallback` could ever pass `5.6`**:
   - **Changing `Meganium hp 32` alone** (`MaxHP = 187`, `154` HP entering Turn 5; line 19): `Bastiodon` stayed at `16/0/2/0/32/0`, so `5.2` still forced `Weather Ball = 70` $\to$ `Metal Burst = 105`, leaving `154 - 105 = 49 / 187 =` **`26/100 brn`** (`< 28/100`).
   - **Changing `Bastiodon hp 0` alone** (`MaxHP = 143`, `74/143` entering Turn 5; line 88): `5.2` forced `Weather Ball = 65` or `66` (`Metal Burst = 96` or `99`), **but** `Meganium` stayed at `hp = 16` (`141/171` HP), leaving `141 - 96 = 45 / 171 =` **`26/100 brn`** (`< 28/100`).
   - **Changing `Bastiodon spd 16` or `8` alone** (lines 388–389): `Bastiodon` still had `hp = 16` (`MaxHP = 151`), so `5.2` still forced `Weather Ball = 70` (`Metal Burst = 105` $\to$ `21/100 brn`).
   - **Changing `Meganium spa` down** (`16, 24, 28, 30, 31`; lines 383–387): Broke `4.7` (`Bastiodon 56/100` or `54/100`) or `5.2` (`Bastiodon 11/100`).
   - Reproducing `5.6: 28/100 brn` required **simultaneously raising `Meganium hp` (`16 -> 32`) AND lowering `Bastiodon hp` (`16 -> <= 11`, e.g. `8`)** (or `Bastiodon` + `Meganium` together).

### B. How `aimFor` Throw-Away Erased the Exact Usage Spreads in Starts 2 & 5 (`usage`)
Even though `Start 2` (`usage`) and `Start 5` (`widest usage`) **started with `Meganium` (`32/0/0/32/0/2`) and `Scovillain` (`31/0/0/3/32/0`) at their exact planted spreads**, `aimFor(kn, prev, 0.5)` (`scripts/lib/inference/knowledge.mjs` line 806) checks:
```js
aim[s] = values.length && values.length < SPAN ? values[Math.floor((values.length - 1) * q + 0.5)] : prev[s];
```
As soon as Turn 2–4 burn ticks (`82/100`) eliminated `Meganium`'s `hp` values `{4..11, 21..28}` (`17 < 33` values surviving) and `Weather Ball` + `Leech Seed` narrowed `Scovillain`'s `hp` to `[18, 32]` (`15 < 33` values surviving), `choose()` picked `aimFor` **ahead of `picks[s][i]`** (`infer.mjs` line 1026), discarding `Meganium`'s exact `#1` usage `hp = 32` in favor of the range midpoint `hp = 16`, and moving `Scovillain` to `27/7/0/13/15/4`. By Round 5 (line 48), both `usage` starts had converged onto the exact same `Meganium 16/0/0/32/0/2` and `Bastiodon 16/0/2/0/32/0` deadlock as `default`.

### C. Why Fallback Cycled Through 8, 32, and 128 Rebuilds Without Reaching the `(Meganium hp, Bastiodon hp)` Pair — And Ran Twice Per Start
1. **66-SP Budget Cannibalization Falsely Marked `Meganium atk/def/spd/spe` as `moved` (Lines 463–472)**:
   - On `Meganium 16/0/0/32/0/2` (50/66 SP), probing `Meganium atk 32` (a special attacker!) exceeded the 66-SP cap, so `closestSpread` stripped `Meganium hp` down to `2` (`2/32/0/32/0/0`), causing `5.6` to print `14/100 brn` instead of `21/100 brn` (line 82).
   - That falsely set `d.moved = true` on **`Meganium atk`, `def`, `spd`, and `spe`**.
   - In Start 6 (`128` rebuilds, lines 338–473), after spending ~93 rebuilds on `dims` ends, `escort` (`Scovillain spe 32, Gholdengo spe 32/0`), `halving`, and `mids`, the `moved` single-stat loop swept `Meganium hp` (14 rebuilds, lines 449–462) and then **`Meganium atk 31..22` (10 rebuilds, lines 463–472)**, exhausting the **128-rebuild quota** at `Meganium atk 22` **before ever reaching `Bastiodon hp`'s single-stat loop or the 2-at-a-time pair loop (`infer.mjs` line 833)** where `(Meganium hp, Bastiodon hp)` lived!
2. **Duplicate Fallback Invocation Per Start (`28/100 brn` vs `48/171 brn`)**:
   - In Round 5, `diff.expected` was `|-damage|p1b: Meganium|28/100 brn` (`mark = "5|6||-damage|p1b: Meganium|28/100 brn"`). Round 5's fallback failed and added that mark to `spend.fruitless`.
   - Round 5's `choose()` then handed Turn 5 `exact` (`6 turns exact`), pinning `Meganium` at `MaxHP = 171`.
   - In Round 6 (`search 2`), `reconstruct` compared against exact HP, changing `diff.expected` to `|-damage|p1b: Meganium|48/171 brn` — a **distinct `markOf(diff)` string** (`"5|6||-damage|p1b: Meganium|48/171 brn"`). Consequently, Round 6 bypassed `spend.fruitless` and re-ran the **exact same fruitless fallback** a second time on Turn 5 in all 6 starts (doubling fallback rebuilds from `240` to `480`)!

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Bastiodon`'s exact `MaxHP = 143` (`hp = 8`) and `SpD = 191` (`spd = 16`), `Scovillain`'s exact `MaxHP = 171` and `SpD = 150`, and `Gholdengo`'s `SpA = 177` are fixed constants, and Channel 2 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers (`67/143`, `75/143`, `8/143` at `5.2`). Because `Bastiodon`'s HP and SpD are known, `Weather Ball` damage at `5.2` is pinned to `67` (`Metal Burst = 100`), leaving `Meganium`'s `hp` as the single free parameter to satisfy `5.6: 28/100 brn`.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Meganium`'s exact `32/0/0/32/0/2` (`MaxHP = 187`, `SpA = 214`, `154` HP entering Turn 5) and `Sableye`'s `MaxHP = 139` (`-17` Leech Seed) are fixed constants, and Channel 1 prints **`54/187 brn`** at `5.6` — immediately telling the solver to the single HP that `Metal Burst` dealt `100` (`Weather Ball = 67` at `5.2`). With `Meganium` fixed, `Bastiodon`'s `hp` alone resolves `5.2` and `5.6`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` & `knowledge.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Discard Still-Valid `prev` Spreads in `aimFor` When `prev` Already Fits (`knowledge.mjs` line 806)**:
   In `Start 2` (`usage`), `Meganium` started at its exact `#1` usage spread `32/0/0/32/0/2`, and `32` remained valid after Turn 2–4 burn (`{0..3, 12..20, 29..32}`). Because `aimFor` switches to the range midpoint whenever `values.length < 33`, `choose()` moved `Meganium` away from `32` to `16` (`16/0/0/32/0/2`), creating the `MaxHP = 171` deadlock at Turn 5. Keeping `prev[s]` when `prev[s]` is still alive (or preferring `prev` ahead of `aimFor` in `choose()` on `usage` starts) would preserve true usage priors.
2. **Prevent Unused Off-Stats From Triggering `d.moved` via 66-SP Cap Cannibalization (`infer.mjs` line 631 / 825)**:
   Testing `Meganium atk 32..22` on a special attacker cannibalized `hp` (`16 -> 2`) and `spa` (`32 -> 28`) under the 66-SP cap, falsely marking `Meganium atk/def/spd/spe` as `moved`. In Start 6, those spurious `moved` dims burned the remaining 128-rebuild budget before `fallback` ever reached `Bastiodon hp` or the 2-stat pair search (`(Meganium hp, Bastiodon hp)`).
3. **Normalize `markOf(diff)` Across Exact-HP vs. Percentage Format (`infer.mjs` line 426)**:
   Round 5 searched `fallback` on `5|6||-damage|p1b: Meganium|28/100 brn` and marked it fruitless, then `choose()` proved Turn 5 `exact` (`MaxHP = 171`), turning Round 6's `diff.expected` into `|-damage|p1b: Meganium|48/171 brn`. Because `markOf` hashes the literal `diff.expected` string, Round 6 failed to recognize the line as already searched and spent a second full `8`/`32`/`128`-rebuild fallback on the exact same line in all 6 starts (wasting 240 rebuilds).
4. **Couple `damageCallback` (`Metal Burst` / `Counter` / `Mirror Coat`) or Include Last-Damager Dependencies**:
   Because `evidence.mjs` skips `Metal Burst` (`carried = true`), `5.6` never constrains `(Meganium hp, Bastiodon hp, Bastiodon spd)` jointly, forcing `fallback` to search blind across 2-stat combinations.
