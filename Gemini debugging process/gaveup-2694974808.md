## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The `teamPreview` Illusion Ordering Bug (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, lines 447–483)
The entire failure across all 3 views (`p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, `both@spectator`), all 6 starts, and all 242 rounds is caused by a single deterministic bug in `choiceFor()` when constructing P1's `teamPreview` choice:

1. **Unmasking Records `disguises.p1 = ['Rillaboom']`**:
   Later in the mimic battle, `Zoroark-Hisui` is unmasked (either by taking a hit `|replace|`, by using a Zoroark-only move in `unmaskBySheet`, or in `p2@p1` immediately at Turn 3 by showing `Zoroark-Hisui`'s exact MaxHP `[130..162]`, which is disjoint from `Rillaboom`'s `[175..207]`). `unmaskIllusion()` therefore adds `'Rillaboom'` to `state0.disguises.p1` and reveals `['Garchomp', 'Volcarona', 'Rillaboom', 'Zoroark']` in `state0.reveal.p1`.
2. **`choiceFor()` Forces the Disguise Species Into the Last Initial Party Slot**:
   In `reconstruct.mjs` lines 453–482:
   ```js
   const last = (state.disguises?.[sideId] || [])
     .filter(name => !state.reveal[sideId].slice(0, leads).includes(name))
     .map(name => bench.indexOf(name))
     .filter(at => at >= 0);
   ```
   Because `'Rillaboom'` is not one of the two leads (`['Garchomp', 'Volcarona']`), `choiceFor()` puts `'Rillaboom'` into `ends` (Slot 4) and `'Zoroark'` into `known` (Slot 3), generating **`options.length === 1`**:
   - **Reconstructed Team Preview**: `[1: Garchomp, 2: Volcarona, 3: Zoroark-Hisui, 4: Rillaboom]` (`>p1 team 5, 2, 4, 6`)
   - **True Team Preview**: `[1: Garchomp, 2: Volcarona, 3: Rillaboom, 4: Zoroark-Hisui]` (`>p1 team 5, 2, 6, 4`)
3. **Turn 1's Switch Moves `Garchomp` Into Slot 4**:
   When Turn 1 executes `p1a: Garchomp` $\to$ `Rillaboom` (Slot 4), Showdown swaps Slot 1 and Slot 4, transforming the party into `[1: Rillaboom, 2: Volcarona, 3: Zoroark-Hisui, 4: Garchomp]`.
4. **Turn 3 Always Copies `Garchomp` Instead of `Rillaboom`**:
   When Turn 3 switches `p1a: Rillaboom` (Slot 1) to `Zoroark-Hisui` (Slot 3), Slot 4 still holds **`Garchomp`**, so `Illusion` copies `Garchomp` and `reconstruct` inevitably prints `|switch|p1a: Garchomp|Garchomp, L50, M|100/100` (`got`) instead of `|switch|p1a: Rillaboom|Rillaboom, L50, M|100/100` (`wanted`)!
5. **No Variant Search Can Escape**:
   Even though the comment at `reconstruct.mjs` line 451 acknowledges *"or a switch moved them"*, `choiceFor()` never includes `Rillaboom` in `known`/`back` permutations alongside `Zoroark-Hisui` (`options.length` is hardcoded to `1`!). Furthermore, `watchParty()` (`reconstruct.mjs` line 248) only marks `party` turns when `battle.activeMove` is truthy (e.g., Beat Up), not when `Illusion` reads `side.pokemon` during a switch, so `carriers` on Turn 3 doesn't even re-check Turn 0 — though with only 1 option in `choiceFor()`, re-checking Turn 0 would still fail.

   - Landed on the exact same `3.1: wanted |switch|p1a: Rillaboom, got |switch|p1a: Garchomp`, or

## 7. Multi-View Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why All Three Views (`p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, `both@spectator`) Gave Up Together
Unlike stat-parity or damage-cutoff failures where one player's exact HP view passes, `choiceFor()`'s `teamPreview` bug broke **every** view simultaneously:
- **`both@spectator` (Channel 0) & `p1@p2` (Channel 2)**: Both observe `|switch|p1a: Rillaboom|Rillaboom, L50, M|100/100` at Turn 3, unmask `Rillaboom` into `disguises.p1`, and get locked by `choiceFor()` into `[Garchomp, Volcarona, Zoroark-Hisui, Rillaboom]`, printing `|switch|p1a: Garchomp` at `3.1`.
- **`p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 known)**: Even on P1's own private channel, Showdown's `battle.add('switch', pokemon, pokemon.getFullDetails)` emits `pokemon.toString()` = **`p1a: Rillaboom`** with `Zoroark-Hisui`'s exact HP/MaxHP (`sim/pokemon.js` lines 49–55, 273–276). Because `Zoroark-Hisui`'s L50 MaxHP (`130..162`) never overlaps `Rillaboom`'s (`175..207`), `unmaskBySheet()` still unmasks Turn 3 and populates `disguises.p1 = ['Rillaboom']` — triggering the exact same single-option `choiceFor()` bug and stalling at `3.1`!

### Architectural Takeaways
1. **Allow `teamPreview` Disguise Targets in All Bench Permutations When Switches Occur (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, lines 449–483)**:
   `choiceFor()` assumes any species in `disguises[sideId]` (that did not lead) must sit at the end (`ends`) of the **initial** Team Preview order. In reality, any switch before the Illusion user enters swaps the active Pokémon into the bench slot the Illusion user vacated — so a species in `disguises` (like `Rillaboom`) can start in Slot 3 and be moved into Slot 4 by Turn 1 (`p1a` $\to$ `Rillaboom`) and Turn 3 (`p1a` $\to$ `Zoroark-Hisui`). Placing `last` species in `known` (or including all permutations of `[...known, ...last, ...chosen]` when the Illusion user enters after a prior switch) immediately unlocks `[Garchomp, Volcarona, Rillaboom, Zoroark-Hisui]`.
2. **Track `Illusion` Party Reads in `watchParty()` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, line 248)**:
   `watchParty()` only records turn `t` in `run.party` when `!this.isActive && battle.activeMove` (for moves like Beat Up). When `Illusion.onBeforeSwitchIn` inspects `side.pokemon` during a switch (`!battle.activeMove`), Turn 0 (`teamPreview`) is never added to `carriers` at line 2529 (`if ((run.party || []).includes(t) && !carriers.includes(0)) carriers.push(0)`), so `reconstruct` cannot re-walk Turn 0 variants to match an Illusion disguise on Turn `t`. Recording `side.pokemon` reads during `Illusion` (`BeforeSwitchIn`) into `party` ensures `reconstruct` tries alternative initial bench orders whenever a later Illusion switch-in disagrees.
3. **Filter `disguisableStays` by `unsettledStays` and Skip Redundant Nested `usage` Restarts (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`, lines 1145, 1265)**:
   - `disguisableStays()` currently calls `illusionStays()` on raw `observed` lines, forcing `inferSpreads()` to waste full multi-start searches and fallback rebuilds on switch-ins already proved not to be Illusion (lines 28 and 34) and on switch-ins already unmasked in `plain` (line 72). Filtering `disguisableStays()` to unsettled switch-ins (`unsettledStays()`) eliminates 3 of the 4 wasted Illusion loops here.
   - Additionally, `inferSpreads()` sub-calls (`readings: false`) currently run nested `usage` restarts inside `default`/`widest default` (Starts 1 and 4), doubling work on readings that are already re-run under Start 2 (`usage`) and Start 5 (`widest usage`).
