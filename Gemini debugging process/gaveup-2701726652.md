### 4. Why Fallback Rebuilds Failed and Looped
When a rebuild stops on Turn 6 (`|-heal|p2b: Volcarona|7/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`), `infer.mjs` triggers `fallback`:
- Fallback perturbs Stat Points of a single Pokémon (Volcarona) in isolation.
- If fallback tests `Volcarona def 7`, `def 32`, or `hp 21`, changing Volcarona's defense or HP immediately breaks Turn 1 Fake Out (`wanted |-damage|p2b: Volcarona|86/100, got 83/100`).
- Fallback logs:
  `fallback on turn 6: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`.
- If fallback runs on Turn 1 and finds a spread (e.g. `Volcarona def 32`) that reproduces Turn 1, that spread breaks Turn 3 Heat Wave (`wanted |-damage|p2b: Volcarona|55/100, got 50/100g`).
- When Turn 3 fails, fallback on Turn 3 cannot fix Turn 3 without breaking Turn 1.
- No single-Pokémon perturbation can satisfy Turns 1, 3, 5, 6, and 8 simultaneously.

### 5. The Mix Cycle and Stall Mechanism
Because fallback cannot reconcile the coupled constraints:
1. Evidence passes produce candidate guesses that have already been evaluated in earlier rounds (`visited.has(signature(next))`).
2. This invokes `mixOf(next, picks, order)`, which splices stats between the current and previous guesses:
   - `back to a guess already tried; mixing it with the last one: Volcarona's spd 16 from the last`
   - `back to a guess already tried; mixing it with the last one: Volcarona's def 30 from the last`
3. Each spliced mix still fails on one of the coupled damage/heal lines (Turn 1 Fake Out, Turn 3 Heat Wave, Turn 5 Basculegion Earth Power, Turn 6 Volcarona Grassy Terrain heal, or Turn 8 Kowtow Cleave).
4. The search frontier never advances beyond Turn 7/8.
5. `stalled` increments on every round where no new frontier is reached.
6. The search executes 255 rounds, 960 rebuilds, and 550,692 probes over 18,136.7 seconds (~5.04 hours).
7. Having exhausted `fallbackRebuilds`, `fallbackTotal`, and reached `maxRounds` stalled rounds, the engine terminates with `stop="stalled"`.
