## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` & `p1@p2` Stalled on Turn 1

### A. Showdown's `flinch` (Priority 8) > `par` (Priority 1) Short-Circuit
In `data/conditions.ts` (`dist/data/conditions.js`), Showdown executes `onBeforeMove` handlers in descending priority order:
1. **`flinch`** (`onBeforeMovePriority: 8`, lines 218–227): unconditionally prints `|cant|<pokemon>|flinch` and returns `false`, **short-circuiting all lower-priority `onBeforeMove` handlers**.
2. **`par`** (`onBeforeMovePriority: 1`, lines 61–67): only runs if the Pokémon did not flinch, calling `this.randomChance(1, 4)` (`random(4) < 1`) and printing `|cant|<pokemon>|par` on `0`.

In the mimic battle, `p1a: Metagross` was both paralyzed (`Zap Cannon`) and targeted by `Rock Slide` (`30%` flinch). To emit **`|cant|p1a: Metagross|par`**, two dice must align simultaneously:
- **Die A** (`Rock Slide` flinch check on `p1a: Metagross`, `randomChance(30, 100)`): must roll **`>= 30`** (no flinch).
- **Die B** (`par` full-paralysis check on `p1a: Metagross`, `randomChance(1, 4)`, **thrown only if Die A did not flinch**): must roll **`0`** (`< 1`, 25% chance).

Under the reconstruction RNG seed (`sampleSeed = 1` $\to$ `seed = sampler(1 ^ 0x5eed).seed()`), Die A rolled `< 30` (`flinch`), so Showdown printed `|cant|p1a: Metagross|flinch` and **never even consumed Die B** (`par`).

### B. Why `resolveTurn` in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` Cannot Solve `(flinch=false, par=true)`
When `resolveTurn(1)` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 1786–2045) faces `wanted |cant|p1a: Metagross|par, got |cant|p1a: Metagross|flinch`:
1. **Baseline Score**: `scoreOf(run, 1)` returns `[idx, 3, 3]` because `|cant|p1a: Metagross|flinch` matches 3 leading pipe-fields (`""`, `"cant"`, `"p1a: Metagross"`) of `|cant|p1a: Metagross|par`.
2. **Single-Draw Search (`lines 1917–1952`) Rejects Un-Flinching**: When `resolveTurn` probes Die A (`Rock Slide` flinch on `p1a`) at `99` (no flinch), Showdown proceeds to Die B (`par`, `randomChance(1, 4)`). Under the unpinned seed, Die B rolls `>= 1` (not paralyzed, 75% probability), so `Metagross` executes its substituted invisible move (`|move|p1a: Metagross|Psychic Fangs|...` or `Protect`, etc.). Its score drops from `[idx, 3, 3]` to **`[idx, 1, 1]`** (only the leading `""` field matches). Because `outranks([idx, 1, 1], [idx, 3, 3])` is `false`, `resolveTurn` rejects turning flinch off!
3. **Two-Chance `pairs` Loop (`lines 1973–1994`) Misses Die B**: The `pairs` loop pairs draws `a, b` from `all = drawsOf(run.trace, 1)`. Because `flinch` short-circuited `par`'s `onBeforeMove`, Die B was **never thrown** in `run.trace` and is absent from `all`.

## 7. Single-View Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` Passed While `p1@p2` and `both@spectator` Gave Up
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known) and `both@spectator` (Channel 0)**: `p1a: Metagross` is unknown (`channel !== 1`), so its exact HP is hidden (`28/100`, `22/100 par`). `resolveTurn(1)` must settle damage rolls and reaches `|cant|p1a: Metagross|par` vs. `|cant|p1a: Metagross|flinch` under the exact same `sampleSeed = 1` PRNG stream where Die A (`flinch`) fires and Die B (`par`) is never drawn, causing both views to give up with `stop = "searches"`, `limits = ["probes", "searches"]`.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1a: Metagross` (`157` HP, `130` SpD, `170` Def, `178` Spe) and `p1b: Incineroar` (`202` HP, `124` Def) are known constants with exact HP (`45/157`, `35/157 par`, `150/202`), allowing `p2@p1` to resolve the battle (`PASS`).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Allow `brought` Two-Die Search (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 1995–2024) to Handle Newly Revealed `chance` Draws**:
   When a higher-priority status/condition die (`flinch`, `slp`, `frz`, `confusion`, `attract`) short-circuits a lower-priority `onBeforeMove` `chance` die (`par`'s `randomChance(1, 4)`), flipping the blocking chance die off reveals `b` with **`b.chance === true`**. Currently, line 2010 rejects `b` whenever `b.chance` is true (`if (!b || b.chance || ...)`), AND `.find(d => d.i > a.i)` only inspects the very first draw after `a.i` (missing `par` when a spread move's second-target flinch roll sits in between!).
2. **Search All Newly Introduced Draws After Flipping a Chance Die**:
   In the `brought` loop (`reconstruct.mjs` line 2009), filtering `drawsOf(flipped.trace, t)` for draws whose ordinal/range was *not* already present at the same signature in `before` (including `chance` draws like `randomChance(1, 4)` when an `onBeforeMove` blocker is cleared on a `|cant|` divergence) would allow `resolveTurn` to find `(Rock Slide no-flinch, Metagross full-par)` in a handful of probes instead of failing Turn 1 across all 6 search starts.
