## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Froslass`'s `spe = 12` (`156`) and `Sneasler`'s `atk = 32, spe = 32` (`182 Atk, 189 Spe`) are fixed ground-truth constants. In fallback, `Raichu spe 0` (`143 < 156`, stops at `1.0`) and `Raichu spe 32` (`178 > 156`, stops at `1.7`) immediately straddle `1.0`, so `halving` steps directly to `Raichu spe 16` (`160 > 156` pre-Mega $\to$ passes `1.0`; `182 < 189` post-Mega $\to$ `Sneasler` outspeeds `Raichu` in `+3` `Fake Out` and passes `1.7`), breaking the deadlock on a single probe!
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Raichu`'s `7/2/20/11/6/20` (`MaxHP 142`, `Def 85`, pre-Mega `165`, post-Mega `187`) is known and Channel 1 prints `Raichu`'s exact HP (`109/142` or `108/142`). `Sneasler`'s guess (`spe = 32`, `189 > 187`) already outspeeds `Raichu-Mega-Y`, and `Froslass spe 0` (`143 < 165`) immediately passes `1.0` and `1.7`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Allow `escort()` on Same-Turn Earlier-Index Stops (`infer.mjs` line 728)**:
   `escort()` currently aborts whenever `!(stop.turn < t)`, ignoring earlier order failures **within the same turn** (`stop.turn === t && stop.index < diff.index`), such as Mega Evolution order (`1.0`) failing before move order (`1.7`). Allowing `stop.turn < t || (stop.turn === t && stop.index < diff.index)` lets `escort([Raichu spe 0], got)` drop `Froslass spe` to `0` immediately after `1.0` fails, resolving chained same-turn speed inequalities in 1–2 rebuilds.
2. **Prevent Disputed Pair Probes from Shadowing Single-Stat Ends & Halving (Lines 775 & 812)**:
   When `disputed` probed `[Raichu spe 0, Froslass spe 32]` where `Froslass` was already at `32`, `done.has(signature(chosen))` caused `tryValue(Raichu spe, 0)` to return `null` without recording `0 -> 'early'` in `d.sides`, suppressing halving of `Raichu spe`. Recording single-stat outcomes when `changes.length === 1` (or caching `probe` outcomes by signature instead of returning `null`) avoids silently dropping binary halving on `spe`.
3. **Include Same-Direction Slow/Slow Pairs in `disputed` Order Probing (Line 775)**:
   Line 775 only probes `[top(da), db.alive[0]]` and `[da.alive[0], top(db)]`, assuming the two actors need opposite speed changes. When an invisible-choice Pokémon (`da`) must go *slower* than an unseen third actor (`Sneasler`) while still remaining *at least as fast* as `db` (`Froslass`) earlier in the same turn, `[da.alive[0], db.alive[0]]` (`[0, 0]`) breaks the mutual ceiling cleanly.
