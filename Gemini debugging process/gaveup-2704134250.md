1. **Known Defender Defenses Directly Isolate Opponent Attack Stats**:
   - In `p1@p2`, P2's exact SpD and HP are known:
     - Turn 1: Sylveon's Hyper Voice hits P2 Venusaur (known SpD 120) and P2 Grimmsnarl (known SpD 121 under Light Screen). Because exact integer damage is visible to P2, Sylveon's exact SpA is bounded immediately.
     - Turn 4: Venusaur's Sludge Bomb hits P2 Pelipper (known SpD 90), pinning Venusaur's SpA.
     - Turn 10: Garchomp's Stomping Tantrum hits Archaludon (known Def 158 under Reflect), directly pinning Garchomp's Atk.
2. **Known Attacker Stats Directly Isolate Opponent Bulk**:
   - In `p2@p1`, P1's exact stats are known:
     - Turn 3: Venusaur takes Sludge Bomb from P2 Venusaur (known SpA 167) and Spirit Break crit from P2 Grimmsnarl (known Atk 140). The exact damage received pins P1 Venusaur's HP, Def, and SpD.
     - Turn 4: Charizard-Mega-Y takes Electro Shot from Archaludon (known SpA 149 at +1 = 223), directly bounding Charizard's SpD and HP.
     - Turn 7: Sylveon takes Electro Shot at +2 SpA and Garchomp takes Weather Ball from Pelipper (known SpA 117), pinning Sylveon and Garchomp bulk.
3. Because equations in single-view have known values on one side and exact numbers on the other, the solver does not have to search over coupled combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)`. Single views converge in 6–7 rounds without fallback rebuild cycling.

## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Showdown truncates all HP displays to percentages:
  $$\text{pct} = \lfloor 100 \times \text{hp} / \text{maxhp} \rfloor \parallel 1$$
- All 8 Pokémon brought to the match engaged in active combat, taking damage, healing, or applying status.
- Every damage exchange contains simultaneous unknowns on both sides:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lfloor \cdot \rfloor\%$$
- Because many combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` yield identical rounded percentages, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- Consequently, median surviving spreads across Pokémon remained at **$10^{7.92}$ (~83.2 million)**, with 0 exact stats resolved.

### Root Cause 2: The Intra-Battle Turn 4 vs Turn 10 Speed Tie Razor
The core search block that caused the engine to stall on Turn 10 is an intra-battle speed tie between P1 Venusaur and P2 Archaludon:
1. **The Ground Truth Stats**:
   - P1 Venusaur: Modest nature (neutral Spe), base 80, SP = 17:
     $$\text{Spe}_{\text{Venusaur}} = 80 + 17 + 20 = 117$$
   - P2 Archaludon: Calm nature (neutral Spe), base 85, SP = 12:
     $$\text{Spe}_{\text{Archaludon}} = 85 + 12 + 20 = 117$$
   - Both Pokémon possess an identical Speed stat of **117**.
2. **Speed Tie Behavior in Showdown**:
   - On Turn 4 (Rain active, Sun inactive): Venusaur and Archaludon speed-tie. The simulator's PRNG resolved the speed tie in favor of **Venusaur**, allowing Venusaur to use Sludge Bomb *before* Archaludon prepared Electro Shot.
   - On Turn 10 (Sunny Day ended or neutral weather): Venusaur and Archaludon speed-tie again. The simulator's PRNG resolved this speed tie in favor of **Archaludon**, allowing Archaludon to execute its sleep check (`|cant|p2a: Archaludon|slp`) *before* Venusaur used Sludge Bomb.
3. **The Deterministic Guess Trap**:
   - The inference engine assigns candidate Stat Points deterministically.
   - In Rounds 8–12: Guess assigned Venusaur Spe SP = 14 ($\text{Spe} = 114$) and Archaludon Spe SP = 5 ($\text{Spe} = 110$).
   - Because Venusaur was strictly faster ($114 > 110$), Turn 4 passed cleanly, but Turn 10 generated:
     - `wanted |cant|p2a: Archaludon|slp, got |move|p1a: Venusaur|Sludge Bomb|p2b: Grimmsnarl`
   - Venusaur moved first in the rebuild, causing the replay diff to diverge on Turn 10!

### Root Cause 3: Inefficacy and Invalidation of Fallback Rebuilds
When the engine detects a difference on Turn 10, `fallback(seen, start, ...)` attempts 1-stat perturbations on the active Pokémon to fix the turn order:
1. **Perturbation Attempt 1: Making Archaludon faster than Venusaur**:
   - Trace line 45 & 121: `Archaludon spe 27, Venusaur spe 5` ($\text{Spe}_{\text{Archaludon}} = 132$, $\text{Spe}_{\text{Venusaur}} = 105$).
   - This fixes Turn 10, but **immediately breaks Turn 4**:
     - `stops at 4.5: wanted |move|p1a: Venusaur|Sludge Bomb|p2a: Archaludon, got |move|p2a: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]`
     - On Turn 4, Archaludon is now faster and acts before Venusaur!
2. **Perturbation Attempt 2: Re-allocating Stat Points from Bulk to Speed**:
   - Trace line 46 & 122: `Archaludon spe 0, Venusaur spe 32` ($\text{Spe}_{\text{Venusaur}} = 132$).
   - Diverts SP from bulk into Speed, which alters Venusaur's HP/SpD:
     - `stops at 3.9: wanted |-damage|p1a: Venusaur|38/100, got |-damage|p1a: Venusaur|35/100`
     - Fails on Turn 3 damage rounding!
3. **Perturbation Attempt 3: Perturbing Pelipper or Grimmsnarl Speed/Bulk**:
   - Trace line 126: `Archaludon spe 0, Pelipper spe 27` $\rightarrow$ `stops at 4.4: wanted |-damage|p2b: Pelipper|69/100, got |-damage|p2b: Pelipper|65/100`
   - Trace line 134-135: `Grimmsnarl spe 32` / `Grimmsnarl spe 16` $\rightarrow$ `stops at 1.13: wanted |-damage|p2b: Grimmsnarl|43/100, got |-damage|p2b: Grimmsnarl|41/100`
   - Trace line 130: `Archaludon spe 0` $\rightarrow$ `stops at 8.3: wanted |move|p2a: Archaludon|Dragon Pulse|p1a: Garchomp, got |move|p1a: Garchomp|Rock Slide`
4. **Summary of Fallback Exhaustion**:
   - Trace line 48: `nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`
   - Trace line 137: `nothing gets past the line in 32 rebuilds`
   - Because no deterministic integer speed assignment can satisfy both Turn 4 (Venusaur first) and Turn 10 (Archaludon first) without breaking earlier turns, fallback is completely deadlocked.

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Stage Search Escalation and Giving Up
1. In `infer.mjs`, when a round fails to make progress past the deepest point reached (Turn 10), `stalled` increments.
2. When fallback exhausts its rebuild budget and `stalled >= maxRounds`, the search escalates:
   - **Stage 1 (Default Start)**: Runs rounds 1–12 (searches 1–4). Stalls on Turn 10; hits `stop = 'searches'`.
   - **Stage 2 (Usage Spreads Start)**: Re-initializes from Smogon usage spreads. Runs rounds 1–8 (searches 1–4). Stalls on Turn 10; fallback fails in 8 rebuilds; hits `stop = 'searches'`.
   - **Stage 3 (Wide Fallback Start)**: Escalates with $4\times$ fallback rebuilds. Runs rounds 1–12 (searches 1–4). Stalls on Turn 10; hits `stop = 'searches'`.
   - **Stage 4 (Widest Restart - Default)**: Escalates all budgets $4\times$ (`budgetScale: 4`). Runs rounds 1–12 (searches 1–4). Stalls on Turn 10; hits `stop = 'searches'`.
   - **Stage 5 (Widest Restart - Usage)**: Runs rounds 1–8 (searches 1–4). Stalls on Turn 10; fallback fails in 32 rebuilds; hits `stop = 'searches'`.
   - **Stage 6 (Widest Restart - Wide Fallback)**: Runs rounds 1–12 (searches 1–4). Stalls on Turn 10; hits `stop = 'searches'`.
3. Having exhausted all starts across both narrow and $4\times$ wide budget levels, the search gives up with:
   - `stop = 'searches'`
   - `limits = ["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "fallbackTotal", "guessTries", "probes", "searches", "stalled"]`
   - Probes: **139,136**
   - Time: **306.8s** (trace) / **2,306.7s** (cluster sweep).

## 7. Summary of Diagnosis

1. **Why Single-View Resolved Rapidly**:
   - In `p1@p2` and `p2@p1`, exact integer HP numbers and known opposing defenses/attackers independently decoupled damage formulas, allowing immediate isolation of stat points without search cycling.
2. **The Spectator Search Deadlock**:
   - **Universal Percentage Masking**: Truncated integer percentages across all 8 active combatants left over 83 million median spreads per Pokémon, preventing `evidencePass` from eliminating false candidates.
   - **The Interlocking Speed Tie**:
     - P1 Venusaur (117 Spe) and P2 Archaludon (117 Spe) are tied in raw Speed.
     - In ground truth, Venusaur won the speed tie on Turn 4 (moving before Archaludon), while Archaludon won the speed tie on Turn 10 (executing its sleep check before Venusaur).
     - Any deterministic speed assignment where $\text{Spe}_{\text{Venusaur}} > \text{Spe}_{\text{Archaludon}}$ reproduces Turn 4 but fails on Turn 10 with `wanted |cant|p2a: Archaludon|slp, got |move|p1a: Venusaur|Sludge Bomb|p2b: Grimmsnarl`.
     - Any deterministic speed assignment where $\text{Spe}_{\text{Archaludon}} > \text{Spe}_{\text{Venusaur}}$ reproduces Turn 10 but immediately breaks Turn 4 (`wanted Sludge Bomb, got Electro Shot`).
     - Any attempt by `fallback` to divert Stat Points from HP/defenses into Speed breaks percentage rounding on Turn 1 (Grimmsnarl `43/100`), Turn 3 (Venusaur `38/100`), or Turn 4 (Pelipper `69/100`).
3. **Outcome**:
   - The engine cycled through all 4 fallback search budgets across 6 sequential restart stages (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, and $4\times$ wide restarts).
   - Rebuilds could not advance past Turn 10, collecting all 7 search limits (`backtracks`, `fallbackRebuilds`, `fallbackTotal`, `guessTries`, `probes`, `searches`, `stalled`) and giving up with `stop = 'searches'` after 139,136 probes.
