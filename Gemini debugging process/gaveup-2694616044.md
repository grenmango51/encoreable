## 7. Architectural Analysis & Mathematical Coupling

### 1. The Priority Inversion Proof
Let $\text{Spe}(P)$ be the effective Speed of Pokémon $P$ at Level 50 under Champions mechanics:
$$\text{Spe} = \lfloor (\text{Base} + \text{SP} + 20) \times \text{NatureMultiplier} \rfloor$$
where $\text{SP} \in [0, 32]$ and $\text{NatureMultiplier} \in \{0.9, 1.0, 1.1\}$.

For Volcarona (Base Speed 100, Timid $\times 1.1$):
$$\max(\text{Spe}_{\text{Volcarona}}) = \lfloor (100 + 32 + 20) \times 1.1 \rfloor = 167$$

For Garchomp-Mega-Z (Base Speed 151, Modest $\times 1.0$):
$$\min(\text{Spe}_{\text{Garchomp-Mega-Z}}) = \lfloor (151 + 0 + 20) \times 1.0 \rfloor = 171$$

Because $\max(\text{Spe}_{\text{Volcarona}}) < \min(\text{Spe}_{\text{Garchomp-Mega-Z}})$, for any two actions $A_{\text{Volcarona}}$ and $A_{\text{Garchomp}}$ in the same priority bracket $p$:
$$\text{Order}(A_{\text{Garchomp}}, A_{\text{Volcarona}}) = [A_{\text{Garchomp}}, A_{\text{Volcarona}}]$$

Therefore, the condition:
$$\text{Order}(A_{\text{Volcarona}}, A_{\text{Garchomp}}) = [A_{\text{Volcarona}}, A_{\text{Garchomp}}]$$
holds if and only if:
$$\text{Priority}(A_{\text{Volcarona}}) > \text{Priority}(A_{\text{Garchomp}})$$

Since Garchomp's move is Dragon Pulse ($\text{Priority} = 0$), Volcarona's move MUST have $\text{Priority} \ge +1$.
Among Volcarona's four known moves from the open team sheet:
- `Struggle Bug`: Priority 0
- `Tailwind`: Priority 0
- `Overheat`: Priority 0
- `Rage Powder`: Priority +2

Thus, `Rage Powder` is the **unique** legal choice that can satisfy Turn 1.
The inference engine's fallback mechanism had no domain awareness of priority brackets versus speed stats, causing it to burn all its rebuild budget attempting impossible Speed SP variations instead of recognizing that only a priority variant could resolve line 1.5.

### 2. Multi-Damage Coupling in Turn 2
In Turn 2, four damage formulas interact concurrently:
1. **Mega-Garchomp Earth Power on Incineroar**:
   $$\text{Damage}_{\text{EP}} = \left\lfloor \left( \left\lfloor \frac{22 \times 90 \times \text{SpA}_{\text{Garchomp}}}{50 \times \text{SpD}_{\text{Incineroar}}} \right\rfloor + 2 \right) \times \frac{\text{Roll}_1}{100} \times 2 \right\rfloor$$
2. **P1 Garchomp Power Gem on Volcarona**:
   $$\text{Damage}_{\text{PG}} = \left\lfloor \left( \left\lfloor \frac{22 \times 80 \times \text{SpA}_{\text{P1\_Garchomp}}}{50 \times \text{SpD}_{\text{Volcarona}}} \right\rfloor + 2 \right) \times \frac{\text{Roll}_2}{100} \times 4 \right\rfloor$$
3. **Incineroar Flare Blitz on Mega-Garchomp**:
   $$\text{Damage}_{\text{FB}} = \left\lfloor \left( \left\lfloor \frac{22 \times 120 \times \text{Atk}_{\text{Incineroar}}}{50 \times \text{Def}_{\text{Mega\_Garchomp}}} \right\rfloor + 2 \right) \times \frac{\text{Roll}_3}{100} \times 1.5 \times 0.5 \right\rfloor$$
   $$\text{Recoil} = \lfloor \text{Damage}_{\text{FB}} / 3 \rfloor$$

In channel 0, every resulting HP value is mapped through integer percentage truncation:
$$P_i = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{curr}} \times 100}{\text{HP}_{\text{max}}} \right\rfloor$$

Because Volcarona's remaining HP from Turn 1 is already quantized, and Power Gem deals $4\times$ super-effective damage, small 1-point changes in SpA or SpD cause wild swings in Volcarona's resulting percentage (jumping directly from surviving at 12/100 to fainting at 0 fnt). When fallback attempted single-stat changes on Incineroar's Attack or Defense, it broke Turn 1's Fake Out damage or Leftovers recovery percentage.
