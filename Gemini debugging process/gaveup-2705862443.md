## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Views Stalled at `3.10` & Why Fallback Cycled

### A. The `encored` + `[from] lockedmove` Blind Spot in `planSegment` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
In `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `planSegment` (lines 170–194) tracks Pokémon hit by `Encore` so that moves forced by `Encore` are not mistaken for the player's own move choice:
```js
    if (kind === '-start' && /Encore$/.test(parts[3] || '')) {
      encored.add(parts[2]);
      continue;
    }

    if (kind === 'move') {
      if (toID(parts[3]) === 'revivalblessing') reviver = { slot: identSlot(parts[2]) };
      if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;
      const from = tagsOf(line).from;
      // ... `lockedmove` is the exception ...
      if (from && from !== 'lockedmove') continue;
```
On Turn 3, `p2b: Whimsicott` inflicted `Encore` on `p1a: Archaludon` at line `3.3` **before** `Archaludon` fired its Turn-2-charged `Electro Shot` at line `3.10` (`|move|p1a: Archaludon|Electro Shot|p2b: Whimsicott|[from] lockedmove`):
1. Line `3.3` added `'p1a: Archaludon'` to `encored`.
2. When `planSegment` reached line `3.10`, `if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;` ran **before** checking `from === 'lockedmove'` and **discarded line `3.10` completely**, leaving `plans[3].actions.p1[0]` as `undefined`.
3. Next, `recoverChargeTargets(plans)` (lines 208–225) scanned `plans[3..]` to recover the hidden target of Turn 2's `plans[2].actions.p1[0]` (`{ moveid: 'electroshot', target: null, needsTarget: true }`):
   ```js
   const match = later.actions[side][slot];
   if (match?.moveid === action.moveid && match.target) {
     action.target = match.target;
     action.recovered = true;
     action.landsOn = plans.indexOf(later);
     break;
   }
   ```
   Because `plans[3].actions.p1[0]` was `undefined`, `recoverChargeTargets` failed to recover `'p2b: Whimsicott'`, leaving **`plans[2].actions.p1[0].target = null`** and **`plans[2].actions.p1[0].landsOn = undefined`**.
4. In Turn 2's `choiceFor()`, `preferred` was `null`, so `targetOptions(side, 0, 'normal', null)` returned `[' +1', ' +2', ' -2']` — defaulting at `variants[2] = 0` to **`+1` (`p2a: Charizard`)**. Because Turn 2 prints `||[still]` with no target, Turn 2 verified cleanly with the wrong stored target.
5. On Turn 3, `resolveTurn(3)` could not change a target already locked on Turn 2 (hitting `probes` / `backtracks`). `reconstructOnce` then called `carriedInto(plans, 3)` (line 228) specifically designed for two-turn charging moves (`a?.landsOn === 3`) — **but because `landsOn` was never set, `carriedInto(plans, 3)` returned `[]`**, so `reconstructOnce` **never** searched `variants[2]`!

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, and `both@spectator` All Gave Up Identically
Unlike damage-parity or multi-unknown stalls where knowing one side (`p2@p1` or `p1@p2`) breaks the deadlock, **none of the views could pass Turn 3 `3.10`** (`slowest_gave_up.json` lists `views: ["p2@p1", "p1@p2", "both@spectator"]` with `stop: "searches"`). Even with all 6 Stat Points of `Archaludon` (or `Charizard` and `Whimsicott`) fixed to ground truth, `planSegment` still dropped `3.10` because `Archaludon` was Encored at `3.3`, `recoverChargeTargets` still left Turn 2's `Electro Shot` target `null` with `landsOn = undefined`, `choiceFor` still picked `+1` (`Charizard`) on Turn 2, and `carriedInto(plans, 3)` still returned `[]`.

### Architectural Takeaways
1. **Do Not Discard `[from] lockedmove` Lines Under `encored` in `planSegment` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, lines 177–193)**:
   When a Pokémon starts a two-turn charging move (`Electro Shot`, `Solar Beam`, `Meteor Beam`, `Sky Attack`, `Fly`, etc.) on Turn $k$ and is hit by `Encore` on Turn $k+1$ before executing `|move|...|[from] lockedmove`, the `[from] lockedmove` execution was locked on Turn $k$ (not overridden by `Encore` on Turn $k+1$), and its target field `parts[4]` is the **only** record of Turn $k$'s chosen target. Moving `const from = tagsOf(line).from;` above `if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;` and guarding with `if (encored.has(parts[2]) && from !== 'lockedmove') continue;` (or recording the target for `recoverChargeTargets` while leaving `choiceFor` free) immediately recovers `target = 'p2b: Whimsicott'` and `landsOn = 3`, fixing both Turn 2's initial target pick and `carriedInto(plans, 3)` in all three views.
2. **Recover `|-anim|` Targets in `readIntents` for Mimic Fidelity (`scripts/lib/mimic.mjs`, lines 99–110)** *(secondary mimic observation)*:
   Charging moves print `|move|...|Solar Beam||[still]` with an empty `parts[4]` followed by `|-anim|...|Solar Beam|<target>`. Reading `|-anim|` targets into `acts.get(actor.base)` when `move` had `target: null` would keep mimic replays faithful to the original player's intended target on Turn 1 (though the `reconstruct.mjs` `encored` + `lockedmove` bug above is a real general protocol bug that affects genuine battles whenever a charging move is Encored before firing).
