### A. Why `both@spectator` Passed in 272.2s (Despite Turn 5 & Turn 7 Stalls)
In `both@spectator` (Channel 0), both sides are unknown and all HP values are reported as percentages (`xx/100`), providing two degrees of freedom that saved Start 1 (`default`):
1. **Turn 5 Escape via `Volcarona spe 4` (Impossible in `p1@p2`)**: In Round 9 (`budgets[0]`: `150` probes, `0` backtracks), `reconstruct` rolled `|-status|p2a: Volcarona|par` instead of `|-status|p1b: Sneasler|brn`. During Turn 5 fallback (lines 32–38), `Volcarona spe 32/16/8` and `Sneasler spe 0` all skipped rebuilding via `replayWith`'s `"plays out the same"` check. Only **`Volcarona spe 4`** (`+1` Spe = `204`, tying `Delphox spe 32 = 204` at `3.5`) diverged in `replayWith`, forcing a full rebuild that happened to draw `Flame Body` `brn` instead of `Dire Claw` `par`.
2. **Turn 7 Escalation to `search 2`**: In Round 13 (`budgets[0]`), Turn 7 stalled at `7.7` (`wanted |-damage|p2b: Garchomp|65/100, got 66/100`) and fallback exhausted `8/8` rebuilds (`unchanged = true`), advancing `level` to `1` (`search 2`: `maxProbes: 600, maxBacktracks: 2`). ### B. Why `p1@p2` Gave Up (`stop: "searches"`, 626.2s) in the Sweep

3. **Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled (`limits: ["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches"]`)**:
   - Notice that in `slowest_gave_up.json`, `p1@p2` hit **`backtracks`, `fallbackRebuilds`, `probes`, `searches`** — and **did NOT hit `guessTries`, `fallbackTotal`, or `stalled`**.

   - Whenever `fallback` hit its per-line `FALLBACK_REBUILDS` cap (`8 * S * W`) without finding a witness past the stalling line and `choose()`'s `picks` remained `unchanged` (no surviving spread move changed the failing line's outcome), `infer.mjs` incremented `level` (`0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4`), exhausting `budgets[0..3]` across every start (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, and `widest`) and terminating with `stop: "searches"` in **626.2s**.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

1. **Cluster Trace View Dispatch Gap**:
   `slowest_gave_up.json` explicitly flagged `views: ["p1@p2"]` for `2697369705`, yet the cluster trace ran `--views both@spectator`. Always matching `--views` to `slowest_gave_up.json[i].views` ensures the trace inspects the actual failing view (`p1@p2`) rather than a passing view (`both@spectator`).
2. **`p1@p2` Rigidity vs. `both@spectator` Slack**:
   In `both@spectator`, percentage HP on both sides plus mutable P2 spreads (`Volcarona spe 4`, `p2:0 Garchomp 3/0/0/32/5/17`) allowed `search 2` to bridge Turn 5 and Turn 7 in `272.2s`. In `p1@p2`, fixed P2 spreads + exact P2 integer HP brackets eliminated P2 slack while P1's active `p1:4 Delphox` held a non-usage random spread (`12/7/7/12/2/26`), causing `fallback` and `choose()` to lock into `unchanged` guesses and hit `stop: "searches"` in `626.2s`.
3. **66-SP Budget Stripping in Single-Stat `fallback` Probes (`infer.mjs` lines 642–659)**:
   Even in the `both@spectator` trace (lines 51–52, 59–60), probing `Garchomp atk 32` or `Garchomp def 23` via `closestSpread(kn, aim)` collided with existing SP allocations (`spa 32`, `spe 17`, etc.), silently stripping `hp`/`def` and breaking Turn 2 (`2.6`) or Turn 3 (`3.12`) instead of cleanly isolating the probed stat.
4. **`replayWith` `"plays out the same"` Skipping RNG-Dependent Speed Perturbations (`infer.mjs` lines 677–679)**:
   During Turn 5 (`Flame Body` `brn` vs `Dire Claw` `par`, lines 32–37), `Volcarona spe 32/16/8` and `Sneasler spe 0` were all skipped as `"plays out the same"` because `replayWith` reused the earlier unpinned speed-tie/RNG sequence. Only `Volcarona spe 4` accidentally forced a tie at `3.5` and triggered a rebuild — a workaround unavailable in `p1@p2` where `Volcarona` is known at `spe 32`.
