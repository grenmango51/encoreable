## 6. Why `both@spectator` Failed (135 Rounds, 749 Rebuilds, 1,586,493 Probes, Stalled)

### A. The Cascading Grassy Terrain & Wood Hammer Recoil Dependency
In `both@spectator`, HP values are quantized into integer percentages ($0$–$100\%$). Rillaboom executed Wood Hammer **three separate times** across the battle:
- **Turn 6**: Wood Hammer on Incineroar (deals 44 damage, recoil 14 HP: `93/100`, terrain heals to `99/100`).
- **Turn 8**: Wood Hammer on Incineroar (deals 42 damage, recoil 14 HP: `93/100`, terrain heals to `99/100`).
- **Turn 11**: Wood Hammer on Sableye (deals 155 damage, recoil 51 HP: `74/100` / `73/100`, terrain heals to `80/100`).

In doubles mechanics, recoil damage is calculated from the **actual damage dealt** to the target:
$$\text{damage} = \left\lfloor \left( \frac{2 \times 50 / 5 + 2}{50} \times 120 \times \frac{\text{Atk}_{\text{Rillaboom}}}{\text{Def}_{\text{Target}}} + 2 \right) \times \text{Modifiers} \times \frac{\text{Roll}}{100} \right\rfloor$$
$$\text{recoil} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{damage}}{3} \right\rfloor$$
$$\text{spectator\_percent} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{currentHP} - \text{recoil}}{\text{MaxHP}} \times 100 \right\rceil$$

This introduces an intractable four-way coupling:
1. Recoil depends on target defense ($\text{Def}_{\text{Sableye}}$) and attacker attack ($\text{Atk}_{\text{Rillaboom}}$).
2. The post-recoil spectator percentage depends on $\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}}$.
3. Target damage must not KO Sableye (Sableye must survive at exact `2/100`, which heals to `8/100` under Grassy Terrain).
4. Grassy Terrain residual healing ($\lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 16 \rfloor$) occurs at the end of every turn, continuously accumulating non-linear roundoff discrepancies across Turns 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11.
