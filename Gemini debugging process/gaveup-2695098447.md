## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled / Exhausted Searches (`stop="searches"`)

### A. Total Absence of Exact HP Denominators and Extreme Dimension
In `both@spectator`, only Channel 0 protocol is available:

- Channel 0 reports only coarse integer percentages rounded down via $\lfloor 100 \times \text{hp} / \text{maxhp} \rfloor$.
- Every percentage change is a multi-parameter product of:
  $$\lfloor 100 \times (\text{Defender\_HP} - \text{Damage}(\text{Attacker\_Atk/SpA}, \text{Defender\_Def/SpD})) / \text{Defender\_MaxHP} \rfloor$$
- Without a single known denominator, candidate pools remain vast (~$10^{7.85}$ combinations per Pokémon).
