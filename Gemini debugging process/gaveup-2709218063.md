## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Unpinned `allAdjacentFoes` Display Target (`Hyper Voice`) on Turn 3 (`3.9`)
Unlike single-target moves, `Hyper Voice` has `target: "allAdjacentFoes"` (`moves.js:9250`), which is excluded from `CHOOSABLE_TARGETS` (`reconstruct.mjs:47`). Both `MimicPlayer` and `reconstruct.mjs`'s `choiceFor()` therefore submit `move 1` with no target (`targetLoc = 0`), hitting both `p2a: Tyranitar` and `p2b: Sinistcha` identically. However, Showdown's protocol line `|move|p1a: Primarina|Hyper Voice|<target>|[spread] p2a,p2b` still prints a single display target `<target>` (`battle-actions.js:386`):
1. At turn start, `resolveAction()` (`battle-queue.js:167`) sees `!action.targetLoc`, calls `getRandomTarget()` $\to$ `randomFoe()` $\to$ `battle.sample([Tyranitar, Gholdengo])` (`random(2)`), and sets `action.targetLoc = +1` or `+2`.
2. At execution, `runMove()` (`battle-actions.js:209`) calls `getTarget(pokemon, move, targetLoc)`. Because `"allAdjacentFoes"` is absent from the `switch (targetType)` inside `validTargetLoc()` (`battle.js:1994–2008`), `validTargetLoc()` returns `false`, causing `getTarget()` (`battle.js:2045`) to invoke `getRandomTarget()` (`battle.sample([Tyranitar, Sinistcha])` $\to$ `random(2)`) **again**.
3. Because `Sinistcha` survived `Flare Blitz` at `7/100`, both `p2a: Tyranitar` (index `0`) and `p2b: Sinistcha` (index `1`) were alive when `Primarina` moved. In `reconstruct.mjs`, every non-tie search across Starts 1–6 (Rounds 4–7 and 12–16, including `search 2..4` up to `maxProbes: 4000 / 16000` and `resample: 1`) exited `resolveTurn` / `redrawTurn` at `3.9` with `got |move|p1a: Primarina|Hyper Voice|p2b: Sinistcha|[spread] p2a,p2b` and failed to flip the target display past `3.9`.

### B. Why `fallback` Failed in Starts 1–5 and Needed Speed-Tie PRNG Shifts in Start 6
1. **`order === false` Deferred Speed Halving Behind All 30 Endpoint Checks**:
   In `infer.mjs:624`, `fallback` classifies a mismatch as a Speed-first `order` bug only if `who(diff.actual) !== who(diff.expected)` or the action `kind` differs. At `3.9`, both `expected` and `actual` name `|move|p1a: Primarina|Hyper Voice|...` (`who === 'p1a: Primarina'`), so `order = false`. Consequently:
   - `dims` was **not** sorted to test `spe` first (`hp, atk, def, spa, spd, spe` per Pokémon across `[Primarina, Tyranitar, Sinistcha, Arcanine, Gholdengo]`).
   - Any dimension whose `[0, 32]` ends bracketed `['same', 'early']` had its binary search (`halving`) **deferred** (`halve.push({ d, ends })`, `infer.mjs:815`) until **all 30 dimensions** finished their endpoint probes (`~44` rebuilds).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
Unlike `2706101187` (an HP-parity trap on a percentage display), both `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` had much tighter initial spreads (half the teams fixed to their exact ground-truth stats, and exact integer HP on one side's Pokémon, preventing Turn 2/3 PRNG drift or letting `reconstruct` / `fallback` land on a consistent PRNG path past Turn 3 without cycling `Primarina`'s speed ties against shifting `Tyranitar` and `Gholdengo` guesses).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Treat Cosmetic Spread-Move Display Targets (`allAdjacentFoes` / `allAdjacent`) Cleanly in Reconstruction**:
   For moves with `target: "allAdjacentFoes"` (like `Hyper Voice`), `|move|...|p2a: Tyranitar|[spread] p2a,p2b` vs. `|move|...|p2b: Sinistcha|[spread] p2a,p2b` has **zero mechanical effect**—the move hits the exact same `adjacentFoes()` set and deals identical damage regardless of which foe `getRandomTarget()` picked for the `|move|` display slot (`battle-actions.js:386`). Failing reconstruction and burning 270 rebuilds / 85,424 probes over an `allAdjacentFoes` display target—or relying on accidental Speed-tie PRNG perturbations (`Primarina spe 21` and `spe 26`) to shift it—causes fragile round regressions whenever another Pokémon's Speed updates.
2. **Investigate Why `resolveTurn` Did Not Settle `getRandomTarget` (`random(2)`) at `3.9`**:
   In `reconstruct.mjs`, `resolveTurn` exhausted all probes and hit `stop = 'stuck'` in `~1.3–1.4s` across `maxProbes = 600`, `4000`, and `16000` (and `resample: 1`) without advancing past `3.9`, even though `getRandomTarget()` draws `random(2)` right before `addMove` (`battle-actions.js:209, 386`) and `Primarina spe 21` / `spe 26` proved a single PRNG shift flips `p2b: Sinistcha` to `p2a: Tyranitar`. Either ensuring `resolveTurn` settles that `getRandomTarget` draw directly, or normalizing/wildcarding the non-mechanical display target field of `allAdjacent*` `[spread]` move lines in `compareTurn`, would eliminate this entire class of `GAVE UP` failures immediately.
3. **Avoid Treating Speed-Tie PRNG Luck as a Spread Constraint in `held` (`infer.mjs:1093`)**:
   When `fallback` succeeded in Start 6 Rounds 4 and 10 via `Primarina spe 21` and `spe 26`, it did **not** actually find a Speed constraint required by Turn 3—it only stumbled into a transient speed tie (`101 === 101` with `Tyranitar`, then `106 === 106` with `Gholdengo`) that shifted the PRNG state. Recording `Primarina 14/0/11/20/0/21` into `held` anchored `Primarina` to a brittle spread that immediately broke as soon as `Tyranitar` or `Gholdengo` moved in Round 10 and Round 12, burning both `searched.get(mark) < 2` fallback slots on the exact same line.
