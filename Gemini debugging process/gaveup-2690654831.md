### The Core Failure Mechanisms:

#### 1. The Speed Inversion Dilemma between Volcarona and Mega Salamence
The central pathology that stalled every search round from Round 4 onwards was an irreconcilable turn-order contradiction between Turn 3 and Turn 4:
- **Turn 3 Order**:
  - `p1a` Volcarona uses `Flamethrower` at line 3.6.
  - `p2b` Salamence uses `Hyper Voice` at line 3.7.
  - Ground truth demand: **Volcarona moves BEFORE Salamence**.

  - At priority 0, Salamence's flinch check executes at line 4.3: `|cant|p2b: Salamence|flinch`.
  - `p1a` Volcarona uses `Quiver Dance` at line 4.4: `|move|p1a: Volcarona|Quiver Dance|p1a: Volcarona`.
  - Ground truth demand: **Salamence moves BEFORE Volcarona**.

#### 2. Why a Static Speed Relationship Cannot Satisfy Both Turns
Between Turn 3 and Turn 4:
- Volcarona has +1 Speed stage from its Turn 1 Quiver Dance on both turns.
- Mega Salamence has 0 Speed stage modifiers on both turns.
- Neither Pokémon experienced paralysis, Tailwind, Trick Room, or Speed-altering items.
- If $Spe_{\text{Volcarona}} > Spe_{\text{Salamence}}$:
  - Turn 3 executes correctly (Volcarona moves before Salamence).
  - Turn 4 **fails**: Volcarona moves before Salamence, executing Quiver Dance at line 4.3 instead of line 4.4 (`wanted |cant|p2b: Salamence|flinch, got |move|p1a: Volcarona|Quiver Dance`).
- If $Spe_{\text{Salamence}} > Spe_{\text{Volcarona}}$:
  - Turn 4 executes correctly (Salamence flinches at line 4.3 before Volcarona moves).
  - Turn 3 **fails**: Salamence executes Hyper Voice at line 3.6 before Volcarona can use Flamethrower (`wanted |move|p1a: Volcarona|Flamethrower|p2a: Raichu, got |move|p2b: Salamence|Hyper Voice`).

#### 3. The Mathematical Implication: An Exact Speed Tie
In Pokémon Showdown mechanics, the **only** way for Pokémon A to move before Pokémon B on Turn 3 and Pokémon B to move before Pokémon A on Turn 4 with identical stage multipliers is an **exact Speed tie**, where an RNG roll breaks the tie on each turn independently:
- Turn 3: 50% RNG tie-break favors Volcarona.
- Turn 4: 50% RNG tie-break favors Salamence.

#### 4. The Blind Spot of Single-Stat Coordinate Fallback
In `infer.mjs`, fallback operates by coordinate descent: holding all candidate spreads constant, it perturbs **one stat of one Pokémon** across candidate values ($0, 32, 16, \dots$):
- In Round 4, the candidate guess had `Volcarona 2/0/0/32/0/32` (Spe SP 32 $\rightarrow$ Speed 228) and `Salamence 10/0/12/0/2/32` (Spe SP 32 $\rightarrow$ Speed 189). Volcarona was strictly faster (228 > 189), failing at Turn 4 line 4.3.
- When fallback perturbed `Salamence spe 32, Volcarona spe 0`:
  - Volcarona (+1 Spe) had Speed $\lfloor 120 \times 1.5 \rfloor = 180$.
  - Mega Salamence had Speed $\lfloor 172 \times 1.1 \rfloor = 189$.
  - Salamence became strictly faster (189 > 180). This cleared Turn 4 line 4.3, but immediately **crashed on Turn 3 line 3.6** (`Hyper Voice` before `Flamethrower`), logged as:
    `fallback on turn 4: Salamence spe 32, Volcarona spe 0 - stops at 3.6: wanted |move|p1a: Volcarona|Flamethrower|p2a: Raichu, got |move|p2b: Salamence|Hyper Voice|p1b: Incineroar`.
- When fallback perturbed `Salamence spe 0, Volcarona spe 32`:
  - Volcarona had Speed 228, Salamence had Speed 154 (228 > 154).
