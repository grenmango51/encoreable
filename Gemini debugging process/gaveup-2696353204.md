## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `both@spectator` Passed While `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Gave Up
- **In `both@spectator` (Channel 0)**: Neither side is locked (`known = [false, false]`) and both sides report percentage HP (`xx/100`). Although Starts 1, 2, and 3 all gave up on Turn 1 (`1.12`: `|cant|p2a: Indeedee|flinch` vs. `|move|p2a: Indeedee|Trick Room`), Start 4 (`widest` default, `budgetScale = 4`) was able to perturb **`p1` (`Rillaboom spe 5` in Round 3, `Tyranitar def 32` in Round 7)** to escape the Turn 1 stall and complete the full 7-turn battle on Round 14 (`197.4s`, `122,783` probes).
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1) and `p1@p2` (Channel 2)**: One side is locked to its true Stat Points (`known[0]` or `known[1] = true`) and its exact integer HPs are enforced. That prevented fallback from perturbing the locked side's spreads (blocking the exact `p1` perturbations that saved `both@spectator`), leaving both `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` unable to clear their stalls within all 6 search starts (`464.7s`, `stop = "searches"`, `limits = ["fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches"]`).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Isolate `spent[t]` Probe Budgets Across Invisible-Choice Variants (`reconstruct.mjs` line 2414 / 2495)**:
   When a Pokémon flinches before acting (`|cant|p2a: Indeedee|flinch`), its real move (`Trick Room`, Priority `-7`) leaves no `|move|` line and lands at the end of `choiceFor()`'s variant list (`v = 5`). Searching `v = 0..4` (`Psychic`, `Follow Me`, `Healing Wish`) against a shared `spent[1]` counter burned `Search 1`'s 150-probe budget before `v = 5` could be searched at all (`1.7` stalls in Starts 1 & 2). Moreover, in `v = 5`, failing to prioritize or resolve the `Rock Slide` flinch secondary (`randomChance(30, 100)`) kept `reconstruct` stalling at `1.12` across `Search 2` and `Search 3` until a lucky `resample = 1` seed or `p1` perturbation (`Rillaboom spe 5`, `Tyranitar def 32`) shifted Turn 1.
2. **Trace the Single-Player Views (`p2@p1,p1@p2`) Specifically When Following Up**:
   Because `trace_2696353204.log` was run with `--views both@spectator` (which passed in the sweep and in the trace) rather than `--views p2@p1,p1@p2` (the views listed in `slowest_gave_up.json`), any future cluster trace targeting the `464.7s` give-up should pass `--views p2@p1,p1@p2` directly.
