## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Percentage Truncation Across All Combatants
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Showdown calculates spectator HP percentages using integer floor division:
  $$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- Neither side's exact current HP, maximum HP, nor exact damage numbers are visible.
- Every damage interaction contains coupled unknowns:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk}, \text{unknown Def}, \text{roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown maxHP} \rightarrow \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{HP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- Dozens of candidate `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` combinations produce identical percentages (`71/100`, `39/100`, `11/100`, `17/100`, `84/100`). Consequently, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads, leaving the median candidate pool at $10^{7.76} \approx 5.75 \times 10^7$ combinations per Pokémon.

### Root Cause 2: The Turn 5 Luxray Double-Edge Recoil & Life Orb Oscillation (The Fatal Bottleneck)
The primary search failure occurs on Turn 5:
1. On Turn 5, Luxray uses Double-Edge on Incineroar while holding Life Orb:
   - Double-Edge deals damage $D$ to Incineroar.
   - Incineroar's HP drops to:
     $$\text{Incineroar HP} \rightarrow \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{HP}}{\text{maxHP}_{\text{Incineroar}}} \right\rfloor = \mathbf{38/100}$$
   - Luxray takes recoil damage: $R = \lfloor D / 3 \rfloor$.
   - Luxray's HP drops to:
     $$\text{Luxray HP}_{\text{recoil}} \rightarrow \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times (\text{HP}_{\text{initial}} - R)}{\text{maxHP}_{\text{Luxray}}} \right\rfloor = \mathbf{75/100}$$
   - Luxray takes Life Orb recoil: $L = \lfloor \text{maxHP}_{\text{Luxray}} / 10 \rfloor$.
   - Luxray's HP drops to:
     $$\text{Luxray HP}_{\text{LO}} \rightarrow \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times (\text{HP}_{\text{initial}} - R - L)}{\text{maxHP}_{\text{Luxray}}} \right\rfloor = \mathbf{65/100}$$
2. In the reconstructed simulation:
   - When the engine guesses spreads for Luxray and Incineroar, it repeatedly encounters a 1% mismatch:
     ```
     wanted |-damage|p2b: Luxray|75/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2b: Luxray|76/100|[from] Recoil
     ```
     OR in the subsequent round:
     ```
     wanted |-damage|p2b: Luxray|65/100|[from] item: Life Orb, got |-damage|p2b: Luxray|66/100|[from] item: Life Orb
     ```
3. Mathematical cause of the 75/76 and 65/66 oscillation:
   - Luxray's base HP is 80 ($155 \le \text{maxHP} \le 187$). At Turn 4, Luxray entered and was reduced to `84/100` ($131 \le \text{HP}_4 \le 157$).
   - Life Orb damage is $L = \lfloor \text{maxHP} / 10 \rfloor = 15$ or $16$ HP.
   - For recoil $R = 13$ HP (from $D \approx 39$–$41$ damage into Incineroar):
     $$\frac{100 \times (131 - 13)}{155} = \frac{11800}{155} = 76.12\% \rightarrow \mathbf{76/100}$$
     $$\frac{100 \times (118 - 15)}{155} = \frac{10300}{155} = 66.45\% \rightarrow \mathbf{66/100}$$
   - For recoil $R = 14$ HP (from $D \approx 42$–$44$ damage into Incineroar):
     $$\frac{100 \times (131 - 14)}{155} = \frac{11700}{155} = 75.48\% \rightarrow \mathbf{75/100}$$
     $$\frac{100 \times (117 - 15)}{155} = \frac{10200}{155} = 65.80\% \rightarrow \mathbf{65/100}$$
   - A difference of just **1 HP of recoil** ($13$ vs $14$ HP, corresponding to a $1$–$2$ HP difference in Double-Edge damage) flips both percentages simultaneously between $(76\%, 66\%)$ and $(75\%, 65\%)$!

### Root Cause 3: Fallback Single-Coordinate Mutation Trap
When `infer.mjs` invokes fallback to resolve the Turn 5 divergence, it tests single-coordinate stat changes:
1. **Mutating Luxray Attack breaks Incineroar damage or Turn 4 damage**:
   - `Luxray atk 32`: deals too much damage, stopping at `5.7: wanted 38/100, got 31/100` (or `30/100`).
   - `Luxray atk 7..20`: deals too little damage, stopping at `5.7: wanted 38/100, got 33/100..35/100`.
   - `Luxray def 32`: reduces damage taken in Turn 4, stopping at `4.1: wanted 84/100, got 88/100`.
2. **Mutating Incineroar Defense breaks earlier turns**:
   - `Incineroar def 23..32`: reduces Double-Edge damage, stopping at `5.7: wanted 38/100, got 40/100..41/100`.
   - `Incineroar def 32`: also breaks Turn 4 Rock Slide damage, stopping at `4.4: wanted 79/100, got 73/100`.
3. **Mutating Incineroar Speed breaks turn order**:
   - `Incineroar spe 20..32`: Incineroar outspeeds Luxray, stopping at `5.6: wanted |move|p2b: Luxray|Double-Edge|p1a: Incineroar, got |move|p1a: Incineroar|Throat Chop|p2b: Luxray`.
4. **The Multi-Variable Deadlock**:
   - To hit $38/100$ on Incineroar, $75/100$ on Luxray recoil, and $65/100$ on Luxray Life Orb without violating Turn 4's $84/100$ and $79/100$ boundaries, the solver must **simultaneously co-adjust**:
     - Luxray Atk
     - Incineroar Def
     - Luxray HP
     - Incineroar HP
     - The RNG roll seed
   - Because single-coordinate fallback mutates only one stat of one Pokémon at a time, it cannot discover the coupled solution. Over 650 fallback rebuilds were executed across all 6 phases, and not a single one escaped Turn 5.
