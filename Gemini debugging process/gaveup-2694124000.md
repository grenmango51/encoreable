## 6. Comparison: Why Player Views Differed / Passed

### Single View Dispatched in Cluster Sweep
In the cluster benchmark sweep, this match was evaluated specifically in the `both@spectator` channel (`"views": ["both@spectator"]`), representing the hardest inference perspective where neither side's exact HP or Stat Points are known.

### Why Player Views Decouple While `both@spectator` Fails
1. **Integer HP Visibility vs Percentage Quantization**:
   In `both@spectator` (channel 0), all HP values are broadcast as integer percentages (`100/100`, `81/100`, `62/100`). The 1% discrepancy (`62/100` wanted vs `63/100` got) arises purely from the ceiling/floor rounding of integer damage fractions against an unknown HP denominator.
   In a player perspective (`p1@p2` or `p2@p1`), the observer's exact HP is known as an exact fraction (e.g. `133/197`), completely eliminating percentage rounding jitter on that player's side.

3. **The Spectator Trap**:
   Only in `both@spectator` must the engine simultaneously guess the attacker's offensive stats, the defender's bulk stats, the attacker's Max HP, and the resulting recoil and status damage across four consecutive turns.
