## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The `|cant|...|flinch` Speed-Order Blind Spot in `scripts/lib/inference/speed.mjs`
Turn 3 and Turn 6 impose opposite speed inequalities on the two `Primarina` (`Modest`, base Speed `60`):
- **Turn 3 (Trick Room)** proves **`Spe(p1:2 Primarina) <= Spe(p2:4 Primarina)`** because both `|move|` lines (`Moonblast` and `Calm Mind`) are printed, so `speed.mjs` emits the speed rule.
- **Turn 6 (Normal Speed)** proves **`Spe(p1:2 Primarina) >= Spe(p2:4 Primarina)`** because `p1b: Primarina` flinches at `6.4` before `p2a: Primarina` uses `Moonblast`. However, **`speed.mjs` fails to emit this rule in BOTH cases**:
  1. **When Turn 6 diverges at `6.4`** (Rounds stalling at Turn 6): `opens()` parses `|cant|p1b: Primarina|flinch` as `{ kind: 'move' }`, `chosen()` returns `null` (`unseen = true`) because flinching suppressed the move line, and `between(item)` (`speed.mjs:744`) requires the immediately preceding executed action `a` to be in the same `(order, priority)` bracket (`a.priority === x.priority`). Because `a` is `p2b: Rillaboom`'s **`Fake Out` (priority `+3`)** while `x` (`p2a: Primarina`'s `Moonblast`) is **priority `0`**, `between` evaluates to `false` (and `Primarina`'s `Protect` has priority `+4`), emitting **no speed rule**.
  2. **Even when Turn 6 succeeds** (Start 2 Round 8; Start 5 Round 10): `sorted(ex)` (`speed.mjs:552–563`) requires `ownMove(...)` (a `|move|` line) for every `choice === 'move'` action. Because `p1b: Primarina` printed `|cant|p1b: Primarina|flinch` instead of a `|move|` line, `sorted(ex)` returns `false`, so line 593 skips `p1b: Primarina` and **still emits no Turn 6 speed rule**!

Thus, `orderSpeeds` (`knowledge.mjs:421`) knows `Spe(p1:2 Primarina) <= Spe(p2:4 Primarina)` and **never learns the reverse bound** that would lock their Speed SPs equal.

### B. How `aimFor()` Repeatedly Broke the Speed Tie — Even After Start 5 Found It
Because `orderSpeeds` only enforces `p1:2 spe <= p2:4 spe`, `p2:4 Primarina`'s `spe` domain is narrowed to `< 33` values (`[2, 32]` when `p1:2 spe = 2`, `[0, 26]` when `p1:2 spe = 0`, `[13, 26]` when `p1:2 spe = 13`):
1. **In `default` / `wide` starts (`Starts 1, 3, 4, 6`)**: `p1:2 Primarina` sits at `spe = 2`, so `orderSpeeds` trims `p2:4 Primarina` to `[2, 32]` (`31 < SPAN`). `aimFor(kn, prev, 0.5)` (`knowledge.mjs:806`) jumps `p2:4 Primarina`'s `spe` to the midpoint **`17`** (`Primarina 17/0/0/14/0/17`, line 39). With `2 < 17`, Turn 3 passes and Turn 6 `6.4` always fails.
2. **In `usage` starts (`Starts 2, 5`)**: Both `Primarina` begin at Modest #1 usage `32/0/0/32/2/0` (**`spe = 0`**), which **ties** in Speed and lets Round 8 pass Turn 6 and reach Turn 7 (`p2b: Raichu 16/100`). ## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- In **`p1@p2`** (Channel 2, P2 known) and **`p2@p1`** (Channel 1, P1 known), one side's `Primarina` Speed SP is a **fixed ground-truth constant** (`p2:4` in `p1@p2`; `p1:2` in `p2@p1`), and the known side's exact `hp/maxhp` integers on Turns 1–6 pin damage rolls directly without `4.11`/`4.13` single-backtrack drift. Even without a Turn 6 speed rule from `speed.mjs`, pinning the unknown `Primarina`'s `spe` via `fallback` (as Start 5 did on Turn 6) stays fixed or avoids the two-sided midpoint drift (`13 -> 19`) that broke Turn 6 in `both@spectator`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/speed.mjs`, `knowledge.mjs`, `infer.mjs`)
1. **Read Speed Order from `Fake Out` Flinches When Only `+4` Protect Is Higher Priority (`speed.mjs`)**:
   On Turn 6, `p1b: Primarina` was flinched by `+3` `Fake Out` (`6.3`) and printed `|cant|p1b: Primarina|flinch` (`6.4`) before `p2a: Primarina` used `Moonblast` (`0`). Because `Protect` (`+4`) outspeeds `Fake Out` (`+3`), a Pokémon flinched by `Fake Out` could not have chosen `Protect`; every other move on `Primarina`'s sheet (`Calm Mind`, `Moonblast`, `Hyper Voice`) is priority `0`. Teaching `sorted()` / `between()` in `speed.mjs` to exclude priority brackets higher than the flinching move (rather than requiring a `|move|` line or `a.priority === x.priority` against `Fake Out`) would emit `Spe(p1:2 Primarina) >= Spe(p2:4 Primarina)` immediately, locking the Speed tie alongside Turn 3's `<=` rule.
2. **Do Not Move a Tied Speed Away from a Surviving Interval in `aimFor()` (`knowledge.mjs:806`)**:
   In Start 2 Round 8 and Start 5 Round 10, `p1:2 Primarina` and `p2:4 Primarina` were already at an equal Speed (`0 === 0` and `13 === 13`) that reproduced Turn 6. Because `orderSpeeds` narrowed `p2:4`'s `spe` to `< 33` values (`[0, 26]` or `[13, 26]`), `aimFor` moved `p2:4`'s `spe` to the midpoint (`13` or `19`), breaking the tie on the very next round. Keeping `prev.spe` when `prev.spe` still survives (or only recentering `spe` when `prev.spe` was eliminated) would preserve working Speed ties across rounds.
3. **Do Not Permanently Blacklist Turn `t` as `fruitless` When `base` Fails Before Turn `t` (`infer.mjs:614–617`)**:
   In Starts 3, 4, 5 (Round 11), and 6, Turn 6 fallback aborted immediately (`return none()`) because `base` (`budgets[0]`, `maxBacktracks = 0`) failed at `4.11` or `4.13`, permanently marking Turn 6 (`6.4`) in `spend.fruitless` without ever probing a single stat on Turn 6. When `reach(base)[0] < t`, `fallback` should either escalate `base` to `budgets[1]` (with `backtracks > 0`) or return `null` without calling `none()`, so an earlier-turn budget miss does not blacklist the stall turn across all remaining starts.
