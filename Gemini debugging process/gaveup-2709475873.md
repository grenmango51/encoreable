## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Neither player's exact HP is revealed. Every damage event, Leech Seed drain, Leftovers recovery, and burn tick is rounded to integer percentages via `Math.ceil(currentHP * 100 / maxHP)`.
- 8 Pokémon were brought into the match (4 per side), and 7 engaged in complex combat exchanges.
- Every combat interaction involves multiple simultaneous unknowns:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk}, \text{unknown Def}, \text{unknown Dice Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because hundreds of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` yield the identical rounded percentage in spectator logs, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- 5 Pokémon retained over 38 million candidate spreads, with Scovillain retaining 206,564 spreads and Golisopod retaining 1,982,748 spreads.

### Root Cause 2: The Turn 7/8 Mega Golisopod Leech Life Drain Off-by-1 Percentage Dilemma
The dominant stalling pattern across the entire cluster run was the Leech Life drain on Turn 8:
- In **112 separate rounds**, the simulator verified every turn through Turn 7, only to fail on Turn 8:
  ```text
  wanted |-heal|p2b: Golisopod|60/100 brn|[from] drain|[of] p1b: Archaludon
  got    |-heal|p2b: Golisopod|61/100 brn|[from] drain|[of] p1b: Archaludon
  ```
- **The Precise Mathematical Boundary**:
  - Golisopod's planted maxHP is 182.
  - Leech Life drain recovery is:
    $$\text{drain} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{damage dealt to Archaludon}}{2} \right\rfloor$$
  - Golisopod's percentage after drain is:
    $$\left\lceil \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{entering T8}} + \text{drain}}{\text{maxHP}_{\text{Golisopod}}} \times 100 \right\rceil\%$$
  - In the mimic battle:
    - $\text{HP}_{\text{entering T8}} = 108$
    - Burned Golisopod (effective Atk 111) attacks Archaludon (Def 152 + Stamina). Damage dealt is 20 HP.
    - Drain = $\lfloor 20 / 2 \rfloor = 10 \text{ HP}$.
    - Golisopod HP after drain = $108 + 10 = 118 \text{ HP}$.
    - Percentage = $\lceil 118 \times 100 / 182 \rceil = \lceil 64.83 \rceil\% = 65\%$ (or under mimic damage rolls, $109 / 182 = 59.89\% \rightarrow 60\%$).
  - If Golisopod enters Turn 8 with **just 1 more HP** (e.g. 110 instead of 109, caused by Scovillain's Overheat dealing 1 less damage on Turn 7, or Golisopod's Turn 7 Leech Life dealing 2 more damage):
    $$\frac{110 + 10}{182} = \frac{120}{182} = 60.44\% \longrightarrow \mathbf{61\%}$$
  - A shift of a single hit point anywhere in Turn 7 pushes Golisopod across the integer percentage boundary from $60\%$ to $61\%$.

### Root Cause 3: The Turn 2/3 Early-Game Stalling Cycle
When the search attempted to branch away from the Turn 7/8 deadlock, it fell into a secondary stall on Turn 2 / Turn 3:
- In **131 rounds**, the search reproduced through Turn 2 and stopped on Turn 3:
  - 100 rounds: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|45/100, got |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|42/100`
  - 10 rounds: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|45/100, got |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|43/100`
  - 6 rounds: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|45/100, got |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|44/100`
- **Why Turn 2/3 Stalled**:
  - Turn 2 features Incineroar's Fake Out into Scovillain (`87/100` or `89/100`).
  - Turn 3 features Annihilape's Close Combat into Scovillain through Reflect, followed by Incineroar's Snarl through Light Screen, followed by Leech Seed drain and Burn damage.
  - To match Turn 3's `45/100`, the solver attempts to modify Scovillain's Defense or HP.
  - However, changing Scovillain's Defense immediately breaks Turn 2's Fake Out:
    `stops at 2.2: wanted |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|89/100, got |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|86/100`.
  - When fallback tries patching Scovillain's Defense to 32 to pass Turn 2, the Close Combat damage on Turn 3 shifts to `51/100` or `59/100`, failing Turn 3 again.

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Turn Coupling Defeating Local Fallback Probes
The engine's fallback mechanism (`fallback(seen, start, ...)` in `infer.mjs`) is designed to probe single-stat variations on the Pokémon active during the failing turn:
1. When Turn 8 fails with the Golisopod drain mismatch (`60/100` vs `61/100`), fallback probes Archaludon and Golisopod stats on Turn 8.
2. In all 24 Turn 8 fallback attempts in the trace log, the result was:
   ```text
   fallback on turn 8: the guess stops before the turn, at 6.8: wanted |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|63/100, got |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|62/100
   ```
   or:
   ```text
   fallback on turn 8: the guess stops before the turn, at 3.11: wanted |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|45/100, got |-damage|p1a: Scovillain|44/100
   ```
3. Because Golisopod's entering HP on Turn 8 depends entirely on the Overheat roll and Leech Life drain roll from Turn 7, adjusting Golisopod's or Archaludon's stats on Turn 8 cannot fix the offset without altering Turn 7 or earlier turns.
4. Consequently, fallback exhausts its rebuild budget (`fallbackRebuilds`), fails to advance past Turn 8, and returns `null`.
