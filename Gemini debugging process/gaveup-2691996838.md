## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p1@p2` and `p2@p1` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Floette-Mega` is fixed at its true `Spe 141` (`spe = 19`) and `MaxHP 176, Def 122`. In Round 2's `fallback` at `1.12`, probing `Volcarona spe = 0` (`Spe 132`) immediately drops `Volcarona` below known `Floette-Mega` (`132 < 141`) without needing to touch `Floette` at all, clearing `1.12` on the first speed probe (`PASS`).
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Volcarona` is fixed at its true `Spe 132` (`spe = 0`), and `Raichu` is fixed at `Atk 109` (`atk = 2`). In `usage` start, `Floette` starts at `2/0/0/32/0/32` (`Spe 154 > 132`), and in `default` start, `Floette` only needs `spe >= 11` (`133 > 132`) rather than `spe = 32`, leaving enough bulk slack for `1.11` so `Floette` clears `1.12` cleanly (`PASS`).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Let Non-Participating Flat Stats Steal Bulk Budget During Speed Pushes (`build()` / `closestSpread`)**:
   In `fallback` (`infer.mjs` line 654), when `disputed` pushed `[Floette spe 32, Volcarona spe 0]` (`+23` SP to `Floette spe`), `closestSpread(kn, aim)` preserved `Floette`'s unused `spa = 9` (never referenced on Turn 0–1) and instead stripped `23` SP from `Floette`'s `(hp=26, def=22)` bulk (`48 -> 25`), breaking Turn 1 `1.11` (`86/100` vs `88/100`). When `build()` probes a `spe` change on an `order` stall, freeing SP from unverified/unused flat stats (`spa`, `atk`) before cutting `(hp, def, spd)` keys would allow `[Floette spe 32, Volcarona spe 0]` to pass both `1.11` and `1.12` on its very first probe.
2. **Allow Joint Speed Pairs on `order` Divergences Regardless of Single-Stat `d.moved`**:
   Because `Volcarona spe = 0` (`132`) remained faster than `Floette spe = 9` (`131`), `Volcarona spe` had `d.moved = false` (`infer.mjs` line 704) and was excluded from the two-stat loop (`lines 833–845`), while off-field `Rillaboom spe = 16` entered `moved` via a Turn 0 speed-tie RNG shift and consumed 29 probes. On an `order` failure, pairing the two disputed Pokémon's Speeds (and if that fails `'early'`, also shedding unused flat SP on the faster-pushed Pokémon) is far more direct than single-stat exhaustive sweeps over unrelated cast members.
