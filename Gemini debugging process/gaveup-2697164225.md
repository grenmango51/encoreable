## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Arithmetic Feasibility of Turn 2 `Innards Out` (`9/100`)
Because `p1b: Victreebel` took no damage before `2.9` and was OHKOed by `Heat Wave`, `Innards Out` dealt exact damage equal to `Victreebel.maxhp` ($M_V = 155 + \text{hp}_V \in [155, 187]$). For `p2a: Delphox` ($M_D = 150 + \text{hp}_D \in [150, 182]$) to land on `9/100`:
$$\left\lfloor \frac{100 \times (M_D - M_V)}{M_D} \right\rfloor = 9 \iff 0.09 \le \frac{M_D - M_V}{M_D} < 0.10 \iff 0.90 < \frac{M_V}{M_D} \le 0.91$$
Since $M_V \ge 155$, this **strictly requires $M_D \ge \lceil 155 / 0.91 \rceil = 171$ ($\text{Delphox hp SP} \ge 21$)**! For example:
- If $M_V = 155$ ($\text{Victreebel hp} = 0$), `Delphox` needs $M_D \in \{171, 172\}$ ($\text{hp}_D \in \{21, 22\}$).
- If $M_D = 182$ ($\text{Delphox hp} = 32$), `Victreebel` needs $M_V \in \{164, 165\}$ ($\text{hp}_V \in \{9, 10\}$).

### B. Primary Bug: `relayChange` (`evidence.mjs` Line 1922) Refused `Innards Out` Because `Heat Wave` Was a Spread Move
When `Delphox`'s `Heat Wave` hit `[Garchomp, Victreebel]` in `both@spectator` (Channel 0):
1. `onChange` recorded `Delphox.moveHits.all = [{ victim: Garchomp, ... }, { victim: Victreebel, ... }]`.
2. At `2.10` (`Innards Out`), `effectChange` called `relayChange` (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` lines 1907–1934).
3. Line 1922 checks:
   ```js
   if (last.hitsOn !== 1 || log.slice(0, -1).some(x => !x.victim.exact)) {
     return 'it carries what more than one hit dealt, and not every one of them is shown exactly';
   }
   ```
   Because `log[0]` was `p1a: Garchomp` and in `both@spectator` `!Garchomp.exact` is `true`, **`relayChange` aborted and returned `null`**.
   (Mechanically, for a 1-hit spread move like `Heat Wave`, `move.totalDamage` is still `0` during `spreadMoveHit`'s `DamagingHit` at `battle-actions.js` line 954 because `move.totalDamage += damage[i]` is only added at line 838 after `spreadMoveHit` returns — so `Innards Out` on `Victreebel` carried **zero** damage from `Garchomp`, yet `relayChange` treated any multi-entry `moveHits.all` the same as a multi-hit move!)
4. After `relayChange` returned `null`, `framesChange` saw `read.moves === false` (since `Innards Out` takes `relay` as a fixed argument rather than reading `Delphox`'s own `maxhp`), so `effectChange` downgraded `2.10` to `band('down', 'Innards Out on Delphox')`.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1a: Garchomp` and `p1b: Victreebel` (`MaxHP`, `SpD`, and exact HP dealt by `Heat Wave`) are both known constants and `exact === true`. Thus `relayChange` hits line 1932 (`if (V.exact) return amountChange(...)`) and directly hands `Delphox` the exact `Innards Out` damage (`Victreebel.maxhp`), immediately restricting `Delphox` to `hp >= 21` in Round 1/2.
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2a: Delphox`'s true `hp` ($\ge 21$), `spa`, and exact HP (`hp/maxhp` in Channel 2) are known ground-truth constants from the start, so `Delphox` neither drops `hp` to `1` nor stalls `Innards Out`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs`, `infer.mjs`, `knowledge.mjs`)
1. **Fix `relayChange` Rejecting Spread Moves (`evidence.mjs` Line 1922)**:
   `relayChange` rejects `Innards Out` whenever `log.slice(0, -1).some(x => !x.victim.exact)`. That guard is only needed when `move.totalDamage` actually accumulates prior hits into `Innards Out`'s `damage` parameter — which in Showdown (`data/abilities.js` line 2129, `sim/battle-actions.js` lines 838, 954) only happens on **multi-hit moves** (`hit > 1`), **not** on single-hit spread moves like `Heat Wave` (where `move.totalDamage === 0` during `spreadMoveHit`'s `DamagingHit`!). Checking `frame.relay === log.at(-1).dealt` (or `!(move.multihit)` / `!traced(..., { relay: d })` sensitivity to `totalDamage`) would allow `Innards Out` on spread moves in `both@spectator` to narrow `(Delphox hp, Victreebel hp)` via `dealtChange` and `narrowDealt` instead of silently falling back to `band('down')`.
2. **Avoid Tie-Breaking Budget Reductions Strictly Against `spa` in `closestSpread` (`knowledge.mjs` Line 656–662)**:
   Because `closestSpread` iterates `a`, `s`, `e` ascending and uses strict `<` (`cost < best[sum].cost`), any budget reduction when raising `hp` on a `0/0/2/31/0/32` spread preserves `spe = 32` and strips `spa` first, even though `Delphox`'s `spe = 32` was unconstrained on Turn 2 while `spa >= 30` was required for `2.9`.
3. **Prioritize Same-Line Movers (`'other'`) Over Early-Boundary Bisections in `fallback` (`infer.mjs` Lines 813–832)**:
   In Start 5 (`usage`), `Delphox hp` produced `2.10: 0 fnt` at `0` and `2.10: 14/100` at `32`, bracketing `9/100` directly at the failing line `2.10`, yet `fallback` spent all remaining rebuilds bisecting `2.8`/`2.9` boundaries on `Victreebel` and `Garchomp` before testing `Delphox hp` in `middles`/`moved`. Running `middles` (or bisecting `'same'` vs `'other'` outcomes on the failing line) before spending half the rebuild budget on unrelated earlier-turn boundaries would resolve `2.10` immediately.
