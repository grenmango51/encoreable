## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `both@spectator` Passed While `p1@p2` Gave Up
- **Why `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known) Passed Cleanly**: With `p1:3 Excadrill` fixed at its exact planted spread (`8/17/10/2/22/7`: `193 HP, 189 Atk, 107 SpD, 115 Spe`) and Channel 1 printing exact HP (`1/193` on `Excadrill`, `96/187` on `Annihilape`), the inference immediately knows `Basculegion-F` dealt **192 damage** to `Excadrill` (`SpA = 165`), `Basculegion` has `Spe >= 115`, and `Excadrill`'s `Earthquake` deals `186` to `Basculegion` (`6/100`), cleanly resolving Turn 2 and finishing the 9-turn log.
- **Why `both@spectator` Passed Only on Start 2 (`usage`)**: In `both@spectator`, Start 1 (`default`) gave up at `2.8` with `Basculegion SpA = 32` (`167`). Start 2 (`usage`) escaped `2.8` only because `Basculegion-F` was unknown and drifted to `SpA = 25` (`159`), temporarily lowering `Muddy Water` below `Excadrill`'s `185` HP floor at `0/32/0/0/32/2`.
- **Why `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known) Was Harder Than `both@spectator`**: Paradoxically, knowing P2's true `Basculegion-F` (`SpA = 165`, `199 HP`, `85 Def`) removed the spectator search's accidental `SpA = 159` slack while `Excadrill`'s guesses remained anchored at `atk = 32, spd = 0` (`23/32/0/0/0/2` and `2/32/0/0/0/2`), causing all 6 starts to stall at Turn 2 `2.8`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Single-Stat `fallback` Probes Blind to 2D `(hp, spd)` / `(atk, spd)` Tradeoffs Under the 66-SP Cap**:
   Because `Excadrill` started at `atk = 32, spe = 2` (`default` and `usage`), probing `Excadrill spd 32` forced `closestSpread` to dump `hp` down to `0` (`0/32/0/0/32/2`, `185 HP`), while probing `Excadrill hp 32` kept `spd = 0` (`85 SpD`). Since neither single-stat probe changed `outcome(got.b)` away from `Focus Sash`, `d.moved` stayed `false` and `fallback` never entered its pair loop (`infer.mjs` line 833). Allowing `fallback` to test a balanced bulk configuration (e.g., `(hp 16, spd 32)` or reducing an unverified offensive stat `atk`/`spa` when `66`-SP saturation drops `hp` to `0` on a Focus Sash / survival mismatch) would unlock `Excadrill`'s `1/100` non-Sash survival even against `p1@p2`'s known `SpA = 165` `Basculegion-F`.
2. **Trace Target Verification**:
   In future diagnostic sweeps, `--views` for `--trace` should be pulled directly from each `slowest_gave_up.json` entry's `views` array (`["p1@p2"]` for `2696815410`), rather than defaulting to `both@spectator`.
