## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why Spectator Inference Gave Up

### 5.1 The Dual Matcha Gotcha Spread Drain Rounding Dependency
1. On Turn 2, `p1a` Sinistcha uses `Matcha Gotcha`, a multi-target draining move that hits both `p2a` Sylveon and `p2b` Basculegion.
2. In Showdown's battle engine, draining mechanics compute:
   $$\text{Heal HP} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Damage Dealt}}{2} \right\rfloor$$
   When executed against two targets simultaneously in doubles:
   - Target A (`p2a` Sylveon) takes damage $D_A$, healing Sinistcha by $\lfloor D_A / 2 \rfloor$.
   - Target B (`p2b` Basculegion) takes damage $D_B$, healing Sinistcha by $\lfloor D_B / 2 \rfloor$.
   - Sinistcha's HP is updated sequentially after each target is struck.
3. In spectator view (`both@spectator`, Channel 0), exact HP values are masked and converted to integer percentages out of 100:
   $$\text{Protocol \%} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Current HP}}{\text{Max HP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor \text{ (with boundary adjustments)}$$
4. On Turn 2, Sinistcha's health line required:
   ```
   |-damage|p1a: Sinistcha|30/100
   |-damage|p2a: Sylveon|83/100
   |-heal|p1a: Sinistcha|36/100|[from] drain|[of] p2a: Sylveon
   |-damage|p2b: Basculegion|30/100
   |-heal|p1a: Sinistcha|68/100|[from] drain|[of] p2b: Basculegion
   ```
5. A change of even 1-2 points in Sinistcha's SpA, Sylveon's SpD/HP, or Basculegion's SpD/HP shifts the integer percentage:
   - If Sinistcha has slightly higher SpA, the drain from Sylveon yields `37/100` or `38/100` instead of `36/100`.
   - If Basculegion takes slightly more damage, the second drain yields `70/100` instead of `68/100`.

### 5.2 Sylveon's Non-Usage Random Spread ($4/1/14/10/16/21$)
1. In competitive VGC, Sylveon almost universally runs standard spreads:
   - High HP/SpA investment (e.g. `164 HP / 252 SpA` or `252 HP / 252 SpA`).
2. In this mimic battle, the mimic generator assigned P2's Sylveon an anomalous **random spread**:
   $$\text{Sylveon SP}: \{ \text{hp: 4, atk: 1, def: 14, spa: 10, spd: 16, spe: 21} \}$$
   - Level 50 Stats: HP 174, Atk 77, Def 99, SpA 154, SpD 166, Spe 101.
3. Because this spread is completely absent from the 47 usage spreads in `usage-spreads.json`, Phase 2 usage restarts drew candidate spreads that drastically mismatched Sylveon's bulk and speed.

### 5.3 The 16/100 Burn Tick Integer Rounding Gate on Turn 4
1. On Turn 2, Matcha Gotcha applied a secondary burn to Sylveon.
2. In Pokémon Showdown, burn damage at the end of each turn deals $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$.
   - For Sylveon with 4 HP SP (HP = 174):
     $$\text{Burn Damage} = \lfloor 174 / 16 \rfloor = 10\text{ HP}$$
     $$\frac{10}{174} \approx 5.747\%$$
3. By the end of Turn 4, Sylveon had absorbed:
   - Turn 2: Matcha Gotcha spread damage.
   - Turn 3: Primarina Hyper Voice spread damage.
   - Turn 4: Burn chip and secondary attacks.
4. Sylveon entered the Turn 4 residual phase with exactly 27 HP remaining ($27 / 174 \approx 15.52\%$, displaying as `16/100 brn`).
5. In candidate inference guesses:
   - If Sylveon is modeled with 0 HP SP (HP = 170): Burn damage is $\lfloor 170 / 16 \rfloor = 10$ HP. Residual percentage rounds to `15/100`.
   - If Sylveon takes even 1 HP more damage across Turns 2–3, its residual HP drops below 27 HP, resulting in `15/100 brn`.
   - Reconstruct halts at Turn 4 line 11:
     ```
     wanted |-damage|p2a: Sylveon|16/100 brn|[from] brn, got |-damage|p2a: Sylveon|15/100 brn|[from] brn
     ```

### 5.4 The Unresolvable Single-Stat Fallback Oscillation Loop
The engine's fallback mechanism mutates **only one stat dimension at a time**:
- **Attempting to fix Turn 4 line 11 (`Sylveon 16/100 brn`)**:
  - Mutating `Sylveon spa` or `Sylveon def/spd`: shifts Turn 2 damage on Sylveon (`wanted 83/100, got 80/100`), failing at Turn 2 line 10.
  - Mutating `Sylveon spe`: alters speed order on Turn 4 between Sylveon and Sinistcha (`wanted Hyper Voice, got Matcha Gotcha`), failing at Turn 4 line 3.
  - Mutating `Sinistcha hp/def/spa`: shifts Turn 2 drain heal (`wanted 68/100, got 70/100`), failing at Turn 2 line 13.
  - Mutating `Raichu hp/def/spa`: shifts Turn 4 drain heal on Raichu (`wanted 55/100, got 53/100` or `54/100`).
- **Attempting to fix Turn 2 line 13 (`Sinistcha 68/100 drain`)**:
  - Mutating `Sinistcha spd`: on line 812 of the trace log, testing `Sinistcha spd 9` passed Turn 2.
  - But with `Sinistcha spd 9`, incoming Hyper Voice damage on Turn 4 jumped, immediately breaking Turn 4 line 4 (`wanted 31/100, got 21/100`).
- The engine was trapped in an exact cycle between Turn 2 (Matcha Gotcha dual-target drain) and Turn 4 (Hyper Voice damage + Raichu drain + Sylveon burn tick).
