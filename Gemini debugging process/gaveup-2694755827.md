## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

The spectator search (`both@spectator`) completely stalled, executing **307 rounds**, **668 rebuilds**, and **607,317 probes** before exhausting all escalation phases and timing out after **4,211.4s** (sweep) / **1,276.2s** (trace).

### Root Cause 1: Spread Move Target Slot Protocol Desynchronization
The primary engine stall was caused by Showdown's protocol representation of spread move targets:
- In Pokémon Showdown doubles, when a Pokémon executes a spread move that hits multiple opponents (such as `Muddy Water`), the protocol line records the primary target specified in the player's choice request:
  ```text
  |move|<User>|<Move>|<PrimaryTarget>|[spread] <TargetA>,<TargetB>
  ```
- In the ground-truth mimic battle:
  - Because Ceruledge died on Turn 2, Turn 3 choices desynchronized from the human replay.
  - The AI RNG seed caused Milotic to select target slot 2 (**Sylveon**, `p2b`):
    ```text
    wanted: |move|p1b: Milotic|Muddy Water|p2b: Sylveon|[spread] p2a,p2b
    ```
- In the reconstructed spectator search:
  - In `infer.mjs` / `reconstruct.mjs`, when reconstructing choices from partial information, the engine generates choices based on legal requests.
  - When guessing or ordering target slots for spread moves, the heuristic picked slot 1 (**Blaziken**, `p2a`).
  - Consequently, the reconstructed simulator emitted:
    ```text
    got:    |move|p1b: Milotic|Muddy Water|p2a: Blaziken|[spread] p2a,p2b
    ```
- **The Fatal Diff**:
  - The diff engine strictly compares protocol lines. Even though both Blaziken and Sylveon were struck by Muddy Water (`[spread] p2a,p2b`) and took identical damage in both lines, the primary target token differed (`p2b: Sylveon` vs `p2a: Blaziken`).
  - In **215 out of 307 rounds** (70.0% of the entire search), the round aborted immediately on Turn 3 with:
    ```text
    round X: reproduces through turn 2 - wanted |move|p1b: Milotic|Muddy Water|p2b: Sylveon|[spread] p2a,p2b, got |move|p1b: Milotic|Muddy Water|p2a: Blaziken|[spread] p2a,p2b
    ```
