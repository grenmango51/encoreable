## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The `Dire Claw` + `Flame Body` Ordinal-Shift & `scoreOf` Trap at Turn 6 (`6.4`)
Even when `6.3` (`|-damage|p1a: Volcarona|53/100`) matched cleanly — across **all 6 starts**, **all 4 search budgets** (`maxProbes` up to `16,000`, `resample: 1`), and **50+ distinct Stat Point combinations** in fallback — `reconstruct` exited Turn 6 with `stop = 'stuck'` at `6.4`:
```
wanted |move|p1a: Volcarona|Quiver Dance|p1a: Volcarona, got |-status|p1a: Volcarona|psn
```

This is caused by an exact interaction between `Dire Claw`'s conditional `sample()` draw, `Volcarona`'s `Flame Body`, `scoreOf()`'s field-sharing tie-break, and `reconstruct.mjs`'s `pairs` / `brought` heuristics:

1. **90% Conditional `Flame Body` Trigger When Skipping `Dire Claw`'s `sample()`**:
   In `sim/battle-actions.ts` (`spreadMoveHit`), `Dire Claw` executes `this.secondaries()` (line 937) **before** `DamagingHit` / `Flame Body` (line 954):
   - Draw `i`: `secondaryRoll = this.battle.random(100)` (`< 30` triggers `Dire Claw`).
   - Draw `i + 1`: `this.sample(["psn", "par", "slp"])` (only called if `secondaryRoll < 30`).
   - Draw `i + 2`: `Flame Body`'s `this.battle.randomChance(30, 100)`.
   
   Whenever `Dire Claw` inflicts `"psn"` (`0` from `sample(["psn", "par", "slp"])`), the 32-bit PRNG output $R$ at **ordinal `i + 1`** is already constrained to $R / 2^{32} \in [0, 1/3)$. When `resolveTurn` (`reconstruct.mjs:1928`) tests `subs[i] = 99` (`>= 30`), `sample()` is skipped and **`Flame Body` shifts down onto ordinal `i + 1`**. Because $R / 2^{32}$ is uniform on $[0, 1/3)$, the probability that `Flame Body` (`randomChance(30, 100)`, i.e. $R / 2^{32} < 0.30$) immediately fires on that shifted ordinal is:
   $$P(R / 2^{32} < 0.30 \mid R / 2^{32} < 1/3) = \frac{0.30}{1/3} = \mathbf{90\%}$$

2. **`scoreOf` Rejects `secondaryRoll = 99` Because `Flame Body` Loses the `shared` Tie-Break**:
   Against `wantHard[4]` (`|move|p1a: Volcarona|Quiver Dance|p1a: Volcarona`):
   - Baseline `|-status|p1a: Volcarona|psn` shares index `0` (`""`, `fields = 1`) **and** index `2` (`"p1a: Volcarona"`), scoring **`[4, 1, 2]`**.

   - Because `[4, 1, 1] < [4, 1, 2]`, `resolveTurn` refuses to commit `subs[i] = 99` even as a `fallback` (`outranks(reach, best)` is `false`).

3. **`pairs` Targets the Unshifted Ordinal (`i + 2`) and `brought` Explicitly Excludes Chances**:

After probing `flipped = ask.get({ ...subs, [i]: 99 })`, line 2009 finds the shifted `b` (`Flame Body`) at `i + 1`, **but line 2010 rejects it**:

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p1@p2` Passed While `p2@p1` and `both@spectator` Gave Up
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: P2's true spreads (`Kingambit 2/3/17/11/27/6`, `Sneasler 2/32/0/0/0/32`, `Delphox 1/0/1/32/0/32`, `Incineroar 32/0/20/0/13/1`) and exact HP integers are fixed from Round 1. Different HP/damage outcomes on Turns 1–5 shift the PRNG draw sequence reaching Turn 6's `Dire Claw`, avoiding the `psn` (`R / 2^32 < 1/3`) + shifted `Flame Body` (`R / 2^32 < 0.30`) ordinal collision, so Turn 6 resolves cleanly.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known) and `both@spectator` (Channel 0)**: Both start with `p2` inferred (`Kingambit` and `Delphox` guessed wrong) and hit Turn 6's `Dire Claw` on an ordinal where `secondaryRoll < 30`, `sample = 0` (`psn`), and the shifted ordinal `i + 1` triggers `Volcarona`'s `Flame Body`, stalling `reconstruct` at `6.4` across all searches.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Allow `brought` (`reconstruct.mjs:2000–2024`) to Handle Shifted `chance` / Wide Draws When Turning a Secondary Off**:
   Line 2010 currently filters out `b` whenever `b.chance || b.hi - b.lo + 1 > 16`:
   ```js
   if (!b || b.chance || b.hi - b.lo + 1 > 16 || before.get(b.i) === `${b.lo}-${b.hi}`) continue;
   ```
   That guard was written for turning a chance *on* (e.g., `Dire Claw` bringing in `sample(["psn", "par", "slp"])`), so it skips `Flame Body` (`b.chance === true`, `100` faces) when turning `Dire Claw` *off* (`va = 99`) shifts `Flame Body` onto ordinal `i + 1`. Scanning `drawsOf(flipped.trace, t)` for any newly-shifted `b` whose `lo-hi` differs from `before.get(b.i)` (or whose `at` is at/before the failing line) and trying `candidates(b)` (`[0, 99]` for chances) would immediately solve `(secondaryRoll = 99, Flame Body = 99)` in 2 probes.
2. **Do Not Reject `va = 99` via `shared` Tie-Break When the Underlying Action/Status Source Changed (`scoreOf`)**:
   In `scoreOf` (`reconstruct.mjs:1667–1679`), `|-status|p1a: Volcarona|psn` outranked `|-status|p2b: Sneasler|brn|[from] ability: Flame Body` (`[4, 1, 2]` vs. `[4, 1, 1]`) solely because `p1a: Volcarona` happened to appear in field 2 of the expected `|move|p1a: Volcarona|Quiver Dance` line. Because `90%` of `"psn"` draws (`R / 2^32 < 1/3`) also satisfy `Flame Body` (`< 0.30`) upon shifting down one ordinal, any `|-status|` / `|-damage|` line emitted by a target's contact ability blocks single-draw removal of the attacker's secondary effect unless `brought` can also settle the shifted `chance` die.
3. **Avoid Re-Evaluating `fallback` on Non-`hpFigure` Protocol Stalls Already Proven Stat-Independent**:
   Once `Start 6` tested `Volcarona`, `Incineroar`, `Kingambit`, and `Sneasler` and established that every spread passing `6.3` failed identically at `6.4` with `stop = 'stuck'`, `fallback` still burned `8 + 8 + 32 + 32 + 32 + 118 = 230` Turn 6 rebuilds across the 6 starts trying Stat Point changes for an RNG proc (`Dire Claw` / `Flame Body`) that no Stat Point can alter.
