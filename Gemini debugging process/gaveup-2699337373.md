## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Three Views Gave Up & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Primary Bug: `planSegment`'s `Revival Blessing` `instaswitch` Assumption (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
In Pokémon Showdown (`sim/battle.ts`), `Revival Blessing` checks `if (action.target.position < action.pokemon.side.active.length)`:
- Only a Pokémon at party `position` `0` or `1` (an empty active slot) receives an automatic `instaswitch` during the turn.
- Here, `p2a: Talonflame` fainted on Turn 2 and was replaced by `p2a: Pawmot`, swapping `Talonflame` to the **bench** (`position >= 2`). When `Pawmot` used `Revival Blessing` at `3.4`–`3.5`, Showdown revived `Talonflame` **on the bench** (`50/100y`) with **no** `instaswitch`.
- Later in Turn 3 (`3.8`), `p1a: Gholdengo` KOed `p2b: Basculegion`, leaving `p2b` empty. At Turn 3 upkeep, Showdown raised a mandatory `forceSwitch: [false, true]` for `p2` to switch `Talonflame` into `p2b`, emitting `|switch|p2b: Talonflame|Talonflame, L50, M|50/100y`.

However, `planSegment()` in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` (lines 134–144) assumes that **any** `switch` line for the revived Pokémon in the same turn is an automatic `instaswitch` that answers no request:
```javascript
if (kind === '-heal' && tagsOf(line).from === 'move: Revival Blessing' && reviver) {
  const side = identSide(parts[2]);
  plan.forced[side].push({ slot: reviver.slot, name: identName(parts[2]) });
  revived = { side, name: identName(parts[2]) };
  reviver = null;
  continue;
}
if (kind === 'switch' && revived && identSide(parts[2]) === revived.side && identName(parts[2]) === revived.name) {
  revived = null;
  continue; // <-- BUG: discards the mandatory upkeep switch line!
}
```
Consequently, `planSegment` discards `|switch|p2b: Talonflame|...|50/100y` from `plan.forced.p2`. When `reconstructOnce` plays Turn 3 (`variant = 0`), `choiceFor` consumes `{ slot: 0, name: 'Talonflame' }` for `Revival Blessing`, leaving `state.forced.p2` empty. When Showdown asks `p2` for the Turn 3 upkeep replacement, `choiceFor` outputs `>p2 pass, pass` (`reconstruct.mjs` line 500), which Showdown **rejects** (`before === after`, setting `state.unsatisfied = 'turn 3: a choice was rejected - nothing advanced'`).

### B. Why the Trace Reported `3.6: got |move|p1a: Gholdengo|Shadow Ball|p2a: Pawmot` (Variant Masking)
Whenever `Gholdengo` KOed `Basculegion` at `3.8`, `variant = 0` matched the entire Turn 3 log through `|upkeep|` before failing on `state.unsatisfied`. Because `found.index === gotHard.length` (the missing upkeep `|switch|p2b: Talonflame|` line), `early` at `reconstruct.mjs:1408` evaluated to `null`, setting `diff.index = -1`. In `scoreOf(run, 3)` (`reconstruct.mjs:1671`):
```javascript
if (!diff || diff.index < 0) return [-1, 0, 0];
```
Because `variant = 0` scored **`[-1, 0, 0]`**, `reconstructOnce` walked `variants` (`v = 1..width-1`) where `Gholdengo` switched its `Shadow Ball` target from `+2` (`p2b: Basculegion`) to `+1` (`p2a: Pawmot`), failing immediately at `3.6` with `scoreOf = [6, 3, 4]`. Since **`[6, 3, 4] > [-1, 0, 0]`**, `best` overwrote the `refused` `variant = 0` run with the wrong-target `variant = 1` run! That turned an upkeep `forceSwitch` refusal into a misleading `3.6: wanted Shadow Ball|p2b: Basculegion, got Shadow Ball|p2a: Pawmot` diagnostic across:
- **Starts 2 & 5 (`usage` starts, Rounds 7–10)**, where `Gholdengo 2/0/0/32/0/32` (`SpA 203`) immediately KOed `Basculegion 2/32/0/0/0/32` (`MaxHP 197`) on `variant = 0`.
- **Starts 1, 3, 4, 6 fallbacks**, every single time fallback perturbed `Basculegion` bulk down (`hp <= 25`, `def >= 7`, `spa >= 7`, or `spe = 32` pulling `hp` down via the 66-SP cap) or raised `Gholdengo spa` (`spa = 32`).

### C. Why Starts 1, 3, 4, 6 Initially Stalled at `3.8` (`got 3/100` / `20/213`) & Exhausted Fallback

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why All Three Views (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator`) Gave Up
Unlike spread-coupling stalls (where knowing one side's exact HP and Stat Points lets `p1@p2` and `p2@p1` pass cleanly), **`mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2699337373` fails identically in all three views** because the blocker is a structural replay-planning bug in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` (`planSegment` lines 134–144):
- In `p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator`, `Talonflame` is at `position >= 2` (on the bench) when `Pawmot` uses `Revival Blessing`, and `Basculegion` faints at `3.8`.
- In all three views, Showdown emits `|switch|p2b: Talonflame|...|50/100y` at Turn 3 upkeep in response to a real `forceSwitch: [false, true]` request, and `planSegment` strips it from `plan.forced.p2`.
- Knowing the exact ground-truth Stat Points on either (or even both) sides cannot fix a missing `forceSwitch` entry in `plan.forced`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` & `scripts/lib/inference/knowledge.mjs`)
1. **Fix `Revival Blessing` Bench-Revival Handling in `planSegment()` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` lines 127–144)**:
   A `|switch|` line for the revived Pokémon in the same turn is **only** an automatic `instaswitch` (requiring no `forceSwitch` entry) if it appears **immediately after** the `|-heal|...|Revival Blessing` line within the same action (before the next `|move|`, `|cant|`, or `|upkeep|`) **and** an active slot on that side was already empty when `Revival Blessing` resolved. When the revived Pokémon stays on the bench and is later sent in during `upkeep` (after another active Pokémon faints, as `p2b: Basculegion` did here), its `|switch|` answers a real `forceSwitch` request and **must** be pushed to `plan.forced[side]`.
2. **Do Not Let a Rejected/Unsatisfied Turn (`index: -1`) Be Overwritten by an Earlier Wrong-Target Variant (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 1671, 2365, 2404)**:
   `scoreOf()` currently maps `diff.index < 0` (`state.unsatisfied` after matching all emitted lines up to `|upkeep|`) to `[-1, 0, 0]`, which is ranked *worse* than a variant that diverges on the very first line of a move (`index = 6`, `3.6: got Pawmot`). When `state.unsatisfied` occurs after matching `gotHard.length` lines (`early === null`), scoring it at `gotHard.length` (or flagging `refused` only when no variant reproduces cleanly without throwing away `best.at` progress) would prevent `reconstructOnce` from masking an upkeep `forceSwitch` failure behind a spurious move-target mismatch.
3. **Avoid `0` Offensive Stat in `defaultSpread()` for Speed-Natured Attackers (`scripts/lib/inference/knowledge.mjs` lines 636–645)**:
   When `nature.plus === 'spe'` (`Timid`, `Jolly`), `defaultSpread` fills `hp = 32`, `spe = 32`, and puts the remaining `2` SP into `def` ahead of `atk`/`spa`, starting `Timid Gholdengo` at `0` SpA SP. Putting `atk`/`spa` (whichever is not `nature.minus`, or the higher base offensive stat) ahead of `def`/`spd` in `defaultSpread` gives special/physical sweepers a realistic offensive starting point before `fallback` spends its budget.
