## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking in Channel 0
In spectator view:
- Neither side reveals exact integer HP. Every damage number, recoil decrement, and status tick is reported as:
  $$\lfloor 100 \times \text{hp} / \text{maxhp} \rfloor \parallel 1$$
- Every single damage exchange has simultaneous unknown variables on both sides:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lfloor \cdot \rfloor\%$$
- Because many different combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` map to the same rounded percentage integer, candidate spread elimination is severely crippled.
- Over 135 million candidate spreads survived across the 12 Pokémon (including 100% of the pool on benched Charizard, Garchomp x2, Kingambit, Floette, and Lycanroc).

### Root Cause 2: The Turn 4 Champions Color Flag Paradox (`20/100y` vs `20/100r`)
The primary failure point of the inference search is the exact color thresholding logic in Showdown's Champions simulator (`pokemon-showdown/sim/pokemon.ts`, lines 2070-2071):
```typescript
const percentage = Math.floor(100 * this.hp / this.maxhp) || 1;
shared = `${percentage}/100`;
if (percentage === 20) {
    shared += this.hp * 5 > this.maxhp ? 'y' : 'r';
}
```
1. In ground truth:
   - Froslass has planted HP SP = 32, giving $\text{maxhp} = 70 + 32 + 75 = 177$.
   - On Turn 4, after taking Aqua Jet from Basculegion, Froslass is reduced to **exactly 36 HP**.
   - $100 \times 36 / 177 = 20.3389\% \rightarrow \lfloor 20.3389 \rfloor = 20\%$.
   - Color parity check: $36 \times 5 = 180 > 177 (\text{maxhp})$.
   - Because $180 > 177$, Showdown outputs:
     `|-damage|p1a: Froslass|20/100y`
2. In candidate search guesses:
   - Many candidate spreads give Froslass a slightly lower or higher maxHP, or slightly different damage from Turn 1 Blizzard / Wave Crash / Aqua Jet.
   - For instance, if Froslass has $\text{maxhp} = 175$ (HP SP 30) and takes damage leaving 35 HP:
     $$100 \times 35 / 175 = 20.000\% \rightarrow 20\%$$
     $$\text{Color Check}: 35 \times 5 = 175 \le 175 (\text{maxhp}) \implies \text{'r' (red bar)}$$
     $$\text{Rebuild Output}: \text{|-damage|p1a: Froslass|20/100r}$$
   - Similarly, if $\text{maxhp} = 180$ and $\text{hp} = 36$: $36 \times 5 = 180 \le 180 \implies \text{'r'}$.
3. Even though the numerical percentage (20%) is completely identical, Showdown's protocol string differs by a single trailing character (`y` vs `r`).
4. Rebuild diff halts immediately at Turn 4 index 4:
   `wanted |-damage|p1a: Froslass|20/100y, got |-damage|p1a: Froslass|20/100r`
5. Across the 216 rounds of search, this exact mismatch stalled the engine **62 times**!
