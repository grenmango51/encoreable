## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up (Root Cause Analysis & Search Pathology)

In `both@spectator`, neither player's team is known (`known = [false, false]`). All 8 active Pokémon possess unknown stat distributions that must be inferred purely from coarse integer percentage brackets. This created a lethal two-tiered oscillation trap:

### 1. Bottleneck 1: Turn 5 Fissure Miss and PRNG Stream Sensitivity
- On Turn 5, `p2a: Snorlax` uses `Fissure` against `p1a: Milotic`.
- In Pokémon Showdown (`runtime/sim/battle-actions.ts`, lines 696–738), OHKO move accuracy is calculated as:
  $$\text{Accuracy} = (\text{User Level} - \text{Target Level}) + 30\% = 30\%$$
- The simulator checks accuracy via:
  ```typescript
  if (accuracy !== true && !this.battle.randomChance(accuracy, 100)) {
  ```
- Because PRNG draws are strictly sequential, any minute variation in earlier turns—such as a single stat perturbation that alters a damage roll's value, changes speed tie resolutions, or shifts secondary effect checks—alters the total number of PRNG draws executed prior to Turn 5.
- When the PRNG counter is displaced by even one draw, the accuracy check for Fissure samples a different pseudo-random roll. In 30% of such draws, the roll is $< 30$, causing **Fissure to HIT Milotic**.
- If Fissure hits Milotic on Turn 5:
  - Milotic faints on Turn 5 (`0 fnt`).
  - In reality, Milotic survives and is active through Turn 11.
  - Reconstruct halts with the diff:
    ```
    wanted |move|p2a: Snorlax|Fissure|p1a: Milotic|[miss], got |move|p2a: Snorlax|Fissure|p1a: Milotic
    ```
- In the trace log, Reconstruct search levels 1, 2, and 3 routinely failed to discover the narrow PRNG dice path that produced the miss. Only **search level 4** (the deepest search budget) explored sufficient dice combinations to reliably reproduce the Fissure miss and proceed into Turn 6+.

### 2. Bottleneck 2: Turn 8 / Turn 9 Multi-Boosted Collision on Slowbro and Staraptor
- When search level 4 successfully passed Turn 5, it encountered a second barrier on Turns 8 and 9:
  - Mega Staraptor's Brave Bird recoil against Rillaboom (`wanted 44/100, got 52/100`, or `wanted 14/100, got 11/100`).
  - Damage dealt to Mega Slowbro (`wanted |-damage|p2b: Slowbro|35/100, got |-damage|p2b: Slowbro|32/100`).

- Because both Slowbro's defensive stats and the attackers' offensive stats are free variables in `both@spectator`, the search sampled candidate spreads that satisfied the percentage gates of Turns 1–4 but diverged by 2–3% on Turn 9.

### 3. The Fallback Oscillation Trap and Catastrophic Collapse
The fundamental reason the inference gave up was an insurmountable coupling between Bottleneck 1 and Bottleneck 2 inside `infer.mjs`:
1. When Reconstruct reached Turn 9 (only possible under search 4), it halted on the Slowbro discrepancy:
   `round 21 (search 4): reproduces through turn 8 - wanted |-damage|p2b: Slowbro|35/100, got |-damage|p2b: Slowbro|32/100`
2. `fallback` was immediately invoked on Turn 9 to perturb stats on Slowbro, Staraptor, Excadrill, or Sneasler.
3. However, when `fallback` tested these single-stat perturbations (e.g., modifying Slowbro's Defense or Special Defense), the altered stats shifted the PRNG draw sequence in Turns 1–4.
4. Consequently, every perturbed candidate guess tested by fallback **crashed back at Turn 5.6** because Fissure suddenly hit Milotic:
   ```
   fallback on turn 9: the guess stops before the turn, at 5.6: wanted |move|p2a: Snorlax|Fissure|p1a: Milotic|[miss], got |move|p2a: Snorlax|Fissure|p1a: Milotic
   ```
5. Because the candidate guesses could not even reach Turn 9, fallback was blind to their effects on Turn 9 and reported:
   `the guess alone does not`.
6. Fallback exhausted its rebuild budget (`spend.rebuilds <= 0`), tripping the `fallbackRebuilds` and `fallbackTotal` limits.
7. To escape, the engine resorted to mixing candidate stats from previously tried guesses:
   `back to a guess already tried; mixing it with the last one: Slowbro's def 0 from the last`

9. **The search suffered a catastrophic collapse from Turn 9 all the way back to Turn 0.**
10. The engine was trapped in a recurring cycle:
    $$\text{Climb to Turn 4} \xrightarrow{\text{Search 4}} \text{Pass Turn 5 Miss} \longrightarrow \text{Fail Turn 9 Slowbro} \xrightarrow{\text{Fallback}} \text{Break Turn 5 Miss} \longrightarrow \text{Collapse to Turn 0}$$
