## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Combatants
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Neither player's exact integer HP is revealed. Every damage event, drain, recoil, Grassy Terrain tick, and Sitrus Berry recovery is rounded to integer percentages: $\lceil \text{currentHP} \times 100 / \text{maxHP} \rceil$.
- 8 Pokémon were brought into the battle (4 per side), and all 8 engaged in active combat exchanges with multiple damage events per turn.
- Every interaction contains simultaneous coupled unknowns:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \longrightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \longrightarrow \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` yield identical spectator percentage logs on early turns, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- 5 Pokémon retained massive spread pools (Staraptor: 34.5M, Garchomp: 36.3M, Indeedee-F: 26.8M, Raichu: 8.8M, Arcanine: 6.7M).

### Root Cause 2: The Multi-Heal Grassy Terrain and Sitrus Berry Deadlock on Turn 6 & 7
The dominant stalling bottleneck across the entire spectator search occurred on Turns 6 and 7:
- On Turn 6, Rillaboom hits Arcanine-Hisui with Grassy Glide, Staraptor hits Rillaboom with Close Combat, and both Arcanine and Rillaboom heal from Grassy Terrain:
  - Ground truth Arcanine Terrain heal: `floor(171 / 16) = 10 HP` (61 -> 71/171 = `42/100`).
  - Ground truth Rillaboom Terrain heal: `floor(195 / 16) = 12 HP` (124 -> 136/195 = `70/100`).
- On Turn 7, Rillaboom hits Arcanine again, triggering Arcanine's Sitrus Berry recovery followed by Grassy Terrain:
  - Arcanine drops to 28/171 (`17/100`).
  - Sitrus Berry restores $\lfloor 171 / 4 \rfloor = 42 \text{ HP}$ (28 -> 70/171 = `41/100` or `42/100`).
  - Grassy Terrain restores 10 HP (70 -> 80/171 = `47/100` or `74/100` cumulative).
- **The Stalling Dilemma**:
  - In candidate spreads where Arcanine's HP or Defense was even slightly different (e.g. 172 HP or 170 HP, or taking 1 HP more damage from Rillaboom), the post-heal percentage shifted across the 1% boundary:
    ```text
    wanted |-heal|p1b: Arcanine|74/100|[from] Grassy Terrain
    got    |-heal|p1b: Arcanine|75/100|[from] Grassy Terrain
    ```
  - In **all 128 fallback rebuild attempts** on Turn 7, modifying Arcanine or Rillaboom to fix Turn 7 broke Turn 6's damage percentages (`wanted |-damage|p2b: Rillaboom|64/100, got |-damage|p2b: Rillaboom|62/100`) or Turn 1's recoil percentage (`wanted |-damage|p1b: Arcanine|73/100, got |-damage|p1b: Arcanine|69/100`).

### Root Cause 3: Long-Range Recoil and Drain Coupling (Turns 1 -> 6 -> 7 -> 8)
- On Turn 1, Arcanine KOes Raichu with Flare Blitz, taking recoil equal to $\lfloor 165 / 3 \rfloor = 55 \text{ HP}$ (or 46 HP depending on overkill rounding).
- If Arcanine's maxHP is guessed at 170 instead of 171, Turn 1 recoil yields `69/100` instead of `73/100`.
- If the solver adjusts Arcanine's HP to satisfy Turn 1, that exact HP is then propagated through Turn 6 Grassy Glide, Turn 6 Terrain heal, Turn 7 Grassy Glide, Turn 7 Sitrus Berry, Turn 7 Terrain heal, and Turn 8 Flare Blitz recoil into Gholdengo.
- Because every one of these 7 distinct HP calculations must simultaneously hit the exact integer rounded spectator percentage, the joint probability of a random candidate guess satisfying all of them is virtually zero.

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Turn Coupling Defeating Local Fallback Probes
The engine's fallback mechanism (`fallback(seen, start, ...)` in `infer.mjs`) probes single-stat variations on active combatants on the failing turn:
1. When Turn 7 fails, fallback probes Arcanine, Staraptor, and Rillaboom stats on Turn 7.
2. In all Turn 7 fallback attempts in the cluster trace log:
