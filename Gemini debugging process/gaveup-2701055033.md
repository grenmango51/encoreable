## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

In Channel 0 (spectator view), both sides are completely unknown and all HP values are masked by integer percentage truncation:
$$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$

### Root Cause 1: Flare Blitz Recoil and Rough Skin Quantization Bottleneck (Turns 1 & 5/6)
On Turn 1, Incineroar hits Garchomp with Flare Blitz. This generates two separate, highly sensitive recoil percentages:
1. **Rough Skin Damage**:
   $$\text{RoughSkinDamage} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{IncineroarMaxHP}}{8} \right\rfloor$$

2. **Flare Blitz Recoil**:
   $$\text{RecoilDamage} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{DamageDealt}}{3} \right\rfloor$$
   The recoil percentage is:
   $$\text{RecoilPct} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times (\text{currentHP} - \text{RecoilDamage})}{\text{IncineroarMaxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
   This non-linear Diophantine relation couples:
   - Incineroar's Attack SP
