## 7. The Turn 7 Locked Move vs Trick Room Expiration Speed Couplings & Fallback Pruning

This mismatch arises from the precise interaction between **Electro Shot's charging mechanics**, **Trick Room expiration**, and **species base speed floors**.

#### 1. Electro Shot Charging Mechanics
In Generation 9, `Electro Shot` is a two-turn move:
- Under Rain, it executes immediately on Turn 1 without a charging turn.
- Outside Rain, the first turn raises the user's Special Attack by 1 and charges. The user becomes locked into the move (`[from] lockedmove`), and must execute the damaging attack on its subsequent action.
- At Turn 5 upkeep, Rain ended (`|-weather|none`). Archaludon queued or began an action that left it locked into executing `Electro Shot` on Turn 7.

#### 2. The Speed Inversion Between Turn 5 and Turn 7

1. **Turn 5 Constraint (Under Trick Room)**:

   - Under Trick Room, slower Pokémon move first.

     $$\text{Spe}(\text{Sylveon}) < \text{Spe}(\text{Archaludon})$$

   - Archaludon base Speed is 85 (level 50 stat: $105 + \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}$).

$$\text{Spe}(\text{Charizard}) = 100 + \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}(\text{Charizard}) + 20 = 120 + \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}(\text{Charizard})$$

$$\text{Spe}(\text{Archaludon}) = 85 + \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}(\text{Archaludon}) + 20 = 105 + \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}(\text{Archaludon})$$

$$105 + \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}(\text{Archaludon}) > 120 + \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}(\text{Charizard}) \ge 120 \implies \text{SP}_{\text{Spe}}(\text{Archaludon}) \ge 16$$
