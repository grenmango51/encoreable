### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Percentage Truncation Across All 8 Combatants
In Channel 0:
- All 8 Pokémon brought into battle participated in combat exchanges.
- Showdown Champions mode calculates spectator percentages via integer truncation:
  $$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- No exact damage, exact HP, or recoil values are visible to the spectator.
- Every turn presents simultaneous unknown variables on both sides:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown maxHP} \rightarrow \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{HP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk/SpA, Def/SpD, maxHP, roll)` yield identical percentages (e.g. `61/100`, `50/100`, `28/100`, `69/100`, `26/100`), `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- At the end of the search, the median candidate pool remained at $10^{7.90} \approx 7.94 \times 10^7$ spreads per Pokémon.

### Root Cause 2: Turn 6 Focus Sash Activation Threshold (The Critical Bottleneck)
The fatal stall of this match occurs on Turn 6:
1. On Turn 6, Rillaboom uses Grassy Glide on Basculegion in Grassy Terrain with Life Orb.
2. In Showdown's mechanics, Focus Sash is an **all-or-nothing threshold trigger**:
   - If $\text{Damage} \ge \text{Basculegion's Current HP}$ (which is 100% full HP): Basculegion endures the hit at 1 HP, consuming Focus Sash and emitting:
     ```
     |-enditem|p2a: Basculegion|Focus Sash
     |-damage|p2a: Basculegion|1/100
     ```
   - If $\text{Damage} < \text{Basculegion's Current HP}$: Focus Sash does NOT trigger. Basculegion simply takes damage and emits:
     ```
     |-damage|p2a: Basculegion|xx/100
     ```
3. In the ground-truth battle:
   - Rillaboom had 163 Atk (+Atk nature), Basculegion had 199 HP and 100 Def.
   - Grassy Glide damage rolls for this spread range from $173$ to $204$ HP.
   - **Only 3 out of 16 damage rolls (rolls 98%, 99%, 100%) deal $\ge 199$ damage to activate Focus Sash!** The other 13 rolls ($173$–$197$ HP) leave Basculegion with 2 to 26 HP without activating Focus Sash.
4. In the spectator inference search:
   - The solver generates joint guesses for Basculegion and Rillaboom.
   - When the solver guesses typical bulk for Basculegion (e.g. 32 HP SP = 227 HP), Grassy Glide's maximum possible roll ($204$ HP) cannot reach $227$ HP.
   - Consequently, the simulator in `reconstruct.mjs` executes Grassy Glide without triggering Focus Sash, emitting `|-damage|p2a: Basculegion|15/100` instead of `|-enditem|p2a: Basculegion|Focus Sash` followed by `|-damage|p2a: Basculegion|1/100`.
   - The divergence at Turn 6 immediately stops the rebuild:
     `round 8, 0.5s: reproduces through turn 5 - wanted |-damage|p2a: Basculegion|1/100, got |-enditem|p2a: Basculegion|Focus Sash`

### Root Cause 3: Fallback Single-Coordinate Mutation Trap
When the search hit this divergence on Turn 6, `infer.mjs` invoked the fallback mechanism to mutate candidate Stat Points:
1. **Mutating Basculegion or Rillaboom alone ("plays out the same")**:
   - `fallback on turn 6: Basculegion hp 0 - plays out the same`
   - `fallback on turn 6: Basculegion def 32 - plays out the same`
   - `fallback on turn 6: Rillaboom atk 0 - plays out the same`
   - `fallback on turn 6: Rillaboom def 32 - plays out the same`
   - Because single-coordinate mutations keep the other Pokémon's guessed stats fixed (e.g., keeping Basculegion at 227 HP while tweaking Rillaboom, or keeping Rillaboom at 145 Atk while tweaking Basculegion), Grassy Glide continues to miss the 1HKO threshold.
2. **Mutating Other Teammates Breaks Earlier Turns**:
   - When fallback attempted to modify earlier combatants to adjust the battle state:
     - `Farigiraf atk 32 / def 32 / spa 32`: breaks Turn 1 Dragon Pulse damage (`stops at 1.7: wanted |-damage|p1b: Farigiraf|61/100, got |-damage|p1b: Farigiraf|54/100`).
     - `Farigiraf spd 32`: breaks Turn 1 (`wanted 61/100, got 65/100`).
     - `Sylveon spa 0`: breaks Turn 3 Hyper Voice into Dragonite (`stops at 3.7: wanted 65/100, got 68/100`).
     - `Sylveon spa 32`: breaks Turn 3 (`wanted 65/100, got 59/100`).
     - `Delphox hp 16`: breaks Turn 2 Flare Blitz recoil (`stops at 2.5: wanted 87/100, got 85/100`).
     - `Delphox atk 32 / def 0 / spe 32`: breaks Turn 2 Flare Blitz damage (`wanted 51/100, got 44/100`).
     - `Delphox def 28 / spd 0`: breaks Turn 4 Hyper Voice damage (`wanted 26/100, got 20/100`).
     - `Delphox spa 12`: breaks Turn 4 Heat Wave damage (`wanted 58/100, got 68/100`).
3. **The Multi-Variable Deadlock**:
   - To satisfy Turn 6, the solver must simultaneously:
     - Co-adjust Rillaboom's Atk to a high value ($\ge 163$).
     - Co-adjust Basculegion's HP to $\le 199$ and Def to $\le 100$.
     - Co-adjust the RNG seed to select one of the top 3 high rolls (98%–100%).
   - But fallback tests only one stat of one Pokémon at a time. It cannot execute 3-variable coordinate updates, leaving the solver trapped at Turn 6 across 114 fallback rebuilds in Phase 6 alone.

### Root Cause 4: Search Restart Escalation Across All 6 Phases (64 Rounds Total)
Because fallback was unable to break past Turn 6, the inference engine exhausted budgets and restarted across all 6 phases:
1. **Phase 1 (`start: 'default'`, budget scale 1)**: Rounds 1–11 (11 rounds). Reached Turn 5, stalled on Turn 6 (`wanted 1/100, got Focus Sash`). Fallback failed in 8 rebuilds. Search level reached limit.
2. **Phase 2 (`start: 'usage'`, budget scale 1)**: Rounds 1–10 (10 rounds). Seeded from Smogon usage statistics. Reached Turn 5, stalled on Turn 6. Fallback failed in 8 rebuilds.
3. **Phase 3 (`start: 'wide'`, budget scale 1)**: Rounds 1–11 (11 rounds). Fallback rebuild limit multiplied by 4. Reached Turn 5, stalled on Turn 6. Fallback failed in 32 rebuilds.
4. **Phase 4 (`start: 'default'`, budget scale 4 / `widest`)**: Rounds 1–11 (11 rounds). All search limits multiplied by 4. Reached Turn 5, stalled on Turn 6. Fallback failed in 32 rebuilds.
5. **Phase 5 (`start: 'usage'`, budget scale 4 / `widest`)**: Rounds 1–10 (10 rounds). Reached Turn 5, stalled on Turn 6. Fallback failed in 32 rebuilds.
6. **Phase 6 (`start: 'wide'`, budget scale 4 / `widest`)**: Rounds 1–11 (11 rounds). 16x fallback rebuilds. Fallback on Turn 6 executed 114 rebuilds over 196.5s, testing every stat of Farigiraf, Sylveon, Delphox, Basculegion, and Rillaboom. All failed. Terminated with `searches`.

Total rounds executed: $11 + 10 + 11 + 11 + 10 + 11 = \mathbf{64\text{ rounds}}$. Total probes: **157,467**. Result: **GAVE UP** (`stop = 'searches'`).
