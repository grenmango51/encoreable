## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

The stall at Turn 3 (`|-damage|p2a: Milotic|42/100`) is caused by two interacting mechanisms between `evidence.mjs` and `fallback` in `infer.mjs`:

### A. `evidence.mjs` Ignores `Beat Up` (`hit.back = true`) and Accepts Unreachable `Milotic` Spreads (`182` / `191` HP, Low `Def`)
1. **Why `Beat Up` Becomes an Unchecked `band('down')`**:
   In `evidence.mjs` (lines 894–900, 972), `Beat Up`'s `onModifyMove` / `basePowerCallback` reads `pokemon.side.pokemon[j].set` for inactive party members (`back.add(p)`). Because the back-party order is not revealed by the replay, `evidence.mjs` marks **`hit.back = true`**, sets **`hit.supported = false`**, and logs:
   > *"its power was read off a Pokemon in the back of the party, whose place there the replay does not show, so it was not used"*
   Consequently, every hit of `Beat Up` on `p2a: Milotic` on Turn 3 is downgraded to **`band('down', ...)`**, which permits **any** downward HP transition from `0` to `h` without consulting `Milotic`'s `Def` or `Whimsicott`'s `Atk`.
2. **How Both Default and Usage Starts Picked Bad `Milotic` Guesses**:
   - **Start 1 (`default`, `Milotic MaxHP = 182`)**: `defaultSpread` gave `Milotic` (`Calm`) `32/0/0/0/32/2` (`Def SP = 0`, `Def = 99`). After Round 5 failed at `46/100` vs `45/100`, `aimFor()` moved `Milotic` to `hp = 12` (**`MaxHP = 182`**) with low `Def`. Because `Beat Up` was only a `band('down')`, `evidencePass(..., exact: true)` proved an exact path (`77/182`, `4 turns exact`) without verifying whether `Beat Up` could deal $\le 7$ damage (`84/182` $\to$ `77/182`).
   - **Start 2 (`usage`, `Milotic MaxHP = 191`)**: Round 1 (`Milotic 32/0/29/0/5/0`, `MaxHP = 202`, `Def = 128`) **reproduced Turns 0–4 cleanly**, failing only on Turn 5 (`Annihilape Bulk Up` vs `Froslass Aurora Veil`). However, Round 1's `evidence` (ignoring `Beat Up` via `band('down')`) moved `Milotic` via `aimFor()` to `hp = 21` (**`MaxHP = 191`**) and a lower `Def`, claiming an exact path (`81/191`, `6 turns exact`) that failed immediately in Round 2!
3. **Why `reconstruct` Overshot `77/182` and `81/191` by `1–2` HP**:
   When `reconstruct` executed `Beat Up` in the simulator under those lower `MaxHP` (`182` / `191`) and lower `Def` guesses:
   - On `MaxHP = 182`, `46/100` is `[84, 85]` HP and `42/100` is `[77, 78]` HP; `Beat Up` Hit 2 dealt a minimum of **`9` damage**, landing on `84 - 9 =` **`75/182`** (`41/100`, trace lines 26, 30, 34, 36, 38) or `85 - 9 = 76/182` (`41/100`).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Milotic` is fixed at its true spread `32/0/2/0/32/0` (`HP 202, Def 101`) and `Sneasler` at `2/32/0/0/0/32` (`Spe 172, Atk 200`), and Channel 2 prints `Milotic`'s exact integer HP (`hp/202`). `Milotic` can never drift to `MaxHP = 182` or `191`, nor can `Def` drift away from `101`, so Turn 3's `Beat Up` reproduces immediately.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1`'s entire brought team — `Floette`, `Kleavor` (`147 HP, 115 Def, 187 Atk, 150 Spe`), `Whimsicott` (`78 Atk, 184 Spe`), and **`Annihilape` in the 4th slot** — is known and fixed. Knowing `Kleavor`'s exact stats locks `Sneasler`'s `Close Combat` / `Rock Slide` brackets and prevents `Milotic` from drifting into the `182`/`191` HP dead end, while `Annihilape`'s presence in `p1`'s party is known from Turn 0.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` & `evidence.mjs`)
1. **Preserve Full-Log `revealOrder` (and `teamPreview` Party) When `fallback` Slices `observed` with `cutAt(t)` (`infer.mjs` line 487–491, `reconstruct.mjs` line 2301)**:
   `cutAt(t)` hides any Pokémon that first switches in on Turn `t+1` or later (`Annihilape` on Turn 4+). For party-dependent moves like `Beat Up`, `reconstruct(cutAt(t))` then builds a *different* `teamPreview` party (`Indeedee-F` BP `10` instead of `Annihilape` BP `16`), allowing `fallback`'s `base` check (`line 609`) to falsely pass Turn 3 and silently suppress stat search. Passing `reveal` from the full `observed` log (or pinning `teamPreview` from `built.choices`) into `fallback`'s `reconstruct` calls would prevent `cutAt(t)` from playing a different party composition.
2. **Do Not Silently Reset `level = 0` and `exact = null` via `stays(handed, 0)` When `fallback` Never Changed `exact` Conditions (`infer.mjs` line 609, 1095)**:
   When `fallback`'s `base` (`exact: null`) returned `stays(handed, 0)` on `77/182` (`Start 1` R9, R11) and `81/191` (`Start 2` R2, R6), it reset `level` from `1` back to `0` and cleared `exact = null` without changing `picks`. Tracking `searched` by the underlying Turn+index (or canonical public line) rather than the `exact`-formatted string — and not resetting `level` below the current level unless `picks` actually changed — would avoid redundant oscillation rounds between percentage and exact-HP `diff` marks.
3. **Bound `Beat Up` Over Eligible Sheet Members Instead of Falling Back to Unconstrained `band('down')` (`evidence.mjs` line 891–900, 972)**:
   When `Beat Up` reads inactive party members (`hit.back = true`), discarding the hit completely into `band('down')` allows `evidencePass(..., exact: true)` to accept `Def`/`HP` guesses (`Milotic` `182`/`191` HP) under which even the minimum possible `Beat Up` damage overshoots the observed percentage. Computing the union of `Beat Up` damage rows across the possible remaining sheet members (or at least bounding the minimum/maximum damage) would preserve soundness while preventing `choose()` from picking impossible `Def`/`HP` combinations.
