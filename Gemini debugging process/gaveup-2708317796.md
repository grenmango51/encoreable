### Architectural Takeaways
1. **Do Not Classify Secondary `|-status|` Lines as Speed-Order Divergences (`infer.mjs:624`)**:
   - Currently, `order` is true whenever `who(diff.actual) !== who(diff.expected)`, even when `diff.actual` is `|-status|p1b: Salamence|brn` (a secondary effect of the *previous* move) and `diff.expected` is `|move|p2a: Archaludon|...` (the *next* action). Restricting the `who(actual) !== who(expected)` shortcut so neither side is a secondary sub-event (`-status`, `-boost`, `-unboost`) unless both lines belong to parallel residual/action-start classes prevents `fallback` from wasting 100% of its 8-rebuild budget binary-searching `Salamence spe` against a 10% `Heat Wave` burn proc.
2. **Direct Secondary-Status Steering in `steerDice` (`reconstruct.mjs:1020`)**:
   - `steerDice` already steers accuracy (`hitStepAccuracy`), critical hits (`getDamage`), damage rolls (`bracket`), and multi-hit counts (`hitStepMoveHitLoop`) directly from `wantHard` before the generic scan runs, but leaves `secondaries()` (`battle.random(100) < chance`, recorded with `chance: false`) unsteered. Steering single-status secondary checks (`status: 'brn' | 'frz' | 'par' | 'psn'`) against the presence/absence of `|-status|<target>|<status>` before `ACTION_START` would settle `Heat Wave` / `Blizzard` non-proc draws in $O(1)$ without stranding all three views (`p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, `both@spectator`) at `3.16`.
