## 7. Multi-View Failure & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p1@p2` and `p2@p1` Also Gave Up (`slowest_gave_up.json`)
- **`p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: Knowing P1's Stat Points and exact HP provides zero help on P2's Turn 1 `Illusion` switch-in (`1.0`). Channel 1 still prints `p2a: Charizard|100/100`, `unmaskBySheet` still cannot unmask line 35, `forced = [35]` still puts `Zoroark-Hisui` in Pos 3 instead of Pos 4, and `reconstruct` still refuses to walk `variants[0] = 1`.
- **`p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: Even though P2's Stat Points are known and Channel 2 prints P2's exact HP, `local-score.mjs` passes only the unpacked 6-Pokémon teams (`sets`) and `observed = replayed.channels[2]` — **not** P2's private `>p2 team 543126` team-preview order, which Showdown does not echo into the battle log. Thus `reconstruct` still synthesizes `>p2 team ...` via `choiceFor(teamPreview)`, still places `Zoroark-Hisui` in Pos 3 (`options[0]`), and still fails at `1.0` (`wanted |switch|p2a: Charizard, got |switch|p2a: Grimmsnarl`) on `forced = [35]` (or if `Zoroark-Hisui`'s exact HP unmasked line 35 automatically, immediately on the plain reading).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `scripts/lib/mimic.mjs`, `scripts/lib/protocol.mjs`)
1. **Place the `Illusion` User in `last` (Pos 4) When a Disguise Names a Lead (`reconstruct.mjs:454–457`)**:
   Excluding lead species from `last` leaves `Zoroark-Hisui` in Pos 3 (`options[0]`), making it structurally impossible for a Turn 1 lead switch-in to disguise `Zoroark-Hisui` as the departing lead. When `disguises` contains a species in `reveal.slice(0, leads)`, `choiceFor(teamPreview)` should prefer placing the `Illusion` user itself at the end of the party (or treat the `Illusion` user's bench slot as a `lastOrders`-style hidden choice prioritized first).
2. **Detect `Illusion` Party-Slot Reads in `watchParty` (`reconstruct.mjs:240–255, 2528–2530`)**:
   `watchParty` currently sets `run.party` only when a bench Pokémon's `.set` getter is accessed during `battle.activeMove`. Because `Illusion.onBeforeSwitchIn` runs during the switch phase (`battle.activeMove === null`) and inspects `side.pokemon[i]`, any turn where an `Illusion` user switches in (or where `disguises[side]` is non-empty) must be included in `carriers` so `reconstruct` walks `variants[0]` when Turn `t` mismatches a switch-in disguise.
3. **Pass Team Sheets to `unmaskIllusion` in `readIntents()` (`mimic.mjs:54`)**:
   In the original replay, `Zoroark-Hisui` used `Icy Wind` and `Protect` while disguised as `Charizard`, which `unmaskBySheet` proves immediately when given `sheets`. Calling `unmaskIllusion(lines, sheets)` inside `readIntents()` would prevent mimic battles from misattributing `Zoroark-Hisui`'s moves to `Charizard` and causing artificial Turn 1 random-switch divergences.
4. **Skip `maxProbes` Exhaustion on Unconditional Immunity Mismatches (`reconstruct.mjs`)**:
   Under `forced = [29]`, `Fake Out` hitting `Charizard` instead of `|-immune|` is a deterministic type-chart failure, yet `resolveTurn` burned the full `maxProbes` budget (`150`–`16,000`) per round across 8 restarts/readings, inflating probe count to `378,384`.
