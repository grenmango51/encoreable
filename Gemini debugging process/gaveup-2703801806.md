## 7. Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)

1. **Do Not Let `disputed` Pair Probes & `Tier 0` `Gardevoir` Exhaust `FALLBACK_REBUILDS` Before Checking `Tier 4` Speeds**:
   In Starts 1 & 2 (`FALLBACK_REBUILDS = 8`), `disputed` pair checks (`Sinistcha` vs `Gardevoir` and `Sinistcha` vs `Incineroar`) consumed all `8` rebuilds (via `2`-rebuild `again` penalties on `4.4`, `6.5`, `0.22`) before `fallback` ever tested single-stat `dims` (`Tyranitar spe 0`). Even in `wide`/`widest` (`32` rebuilds), `Gardevoir spe` (`Tier 0`) bisected `[0..32]` in `6` steps despite `Gardevoir-Mega` (`120..152` Spe) being strictly faster than `Sinistcha` (`81..109` Spe) at every legal SP. When `built.report.hidden` includes Turn `t` (an unseen choice like `Sinistcha`'s flinch) and `reconstruct` had to fall back to a different priority bracket (`Trick Room` `-7`), the real speed conflict is between `Sinistcha` and the flinching attacker (`Tyranitar` in `7.4..7.7`), not `Gardevoir` (`7.8`).
2. **Prioritize Attackers of a Flinched Pokémon in `fallback` Tiering (`cast`)**:
   `Tyranitar` (`p1:4`) caused `Sinistcha`'s flinch at `7.4..7.7`, yet `7.8: |cant|p2b: Sinistcha|flinch` only names `Sinistcha` and `Gardevoir` in `diff.expected`/`diff.actual`. Consequently, `Tyranitar` landed in `Tier 4` (`segment` / `stood`) behind `Gardevoir` (`Tier 0`) and `Sinistcha` (`Tier 0/3`). Recognizing `|cant|...|flinch` and placing the preceding flinch-move user (`Rock Slide` in `segment`) into `Tier 1` (`place.action` / cause) would test `Tyranitar spe 0` immediately on the first single-stat probe.
3. **Avoid Re-Pinning `handed` Roll Dependencies That Break `4.14` When `Tyranitar spe` Changes**:
   In Starts 3, 4, Round 9 of Start 5, and `p1@p2`, testing `Tyranitar spe 0` via `closestSpread` repeatedly regressed to Turn 4 `4.14` (`Sinistcha` `56/100` vs `57/100`, or exact HP mismatch in `p1@p2` Channel 2). Checking why `Tyranitar spe 0` is blocked by an earlier turn's pinned `4.4`/`4.14` damage roll (which `blameTurns(4)` cannot backtrack because `4.4` is in the same turn `t = 4`) — or allowing `same`-turn earlier-hit redraws in `reconstruct.mjs` — would resolve the `p1@p2` give-up directly.
