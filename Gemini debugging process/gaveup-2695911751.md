### E. The Guess Oscillation and Stall Loop
1. When fallback fails, `infer.mjs` increments search level or attempts to mix previous guesses (`mixOf(next, picks, order)`).
2. Occasionally, a combination of guesses fortuitously reproduces through Turn 3 and reaches Turn 4 or Turn 5:
   - Round 8 reproduces through Turn 4 (stopping at Basculegion Wave Crash).
   - Round 9 reproduces through Turn 4 (stopping at Basculegion recoil `wanted 95/100, got 96/100`).

4. Because no exact proof path was found, no turns are handed down as exact, and no dice are locked in.
5. In the very next round (Round 11), the new unanchored guess immediately collapses back to Turn 2:
   $$\verb+round 11: reproduces through turn 2 - wanted Incineroar 90/100 recoil, got 89/100+$$
6. Because the deepest turn reached was Turn 5 (`deepest = [5, ...]`), any round that fails before Turn 5 increments `stalled++`:
   ```js
   if (!deepest || past(at, deepest)) deepest = at;
   else stalled++;
   ```
7. The search enters an oscillation cycle between Turns 2, 3, 4, and 5. With `maxRounds = 32` (scaled by $S = 2$ to 32), `stalled` accumulates until reaching the limit `stalled >= maxRounds` after 139 rounds, terminating the search with `stop: "stalled"`.
