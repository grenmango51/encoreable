5. **Direct Substitution**:
   - Every equation in Channel 1 features known parameters on one side and exact integers on the other. No circular dependencies exist. Inference converged in 10 rounds with 12 rebuilds.

### Why `p1@p2` Resolved in 170.9s (11 rounds, 19 rebuilds, 20,804 probes)
In Channel 2, Player 2's exact stats are fully known:
1. **Exact Defenses Directly Pin P1 Attack Stats**:
   - Raichu's known Def (84 under Reflect) takes exact 140 damage from Basculegion's Wave Crash $\rightarrow$ pins Basculegion's Attack (164).
   - Volcarona's known Def (101 under Reflect) takes exact 63 damage from Sneasler's Dire Claw $\rightarrow$ pins Sneasler's Attack (200).
   - Volcarona takes exact 64 damage from Talonflame's Flare Blitz $\rightarrow$ pins Talonflame's Attack (133).
   - Grimmsnarl's known Def (100 under Reflect) takes exact 49 damage from Salamence's Double-Edge $\rightarrow$ pins Salamence's Attack (155).
2. **Exact Recoil and Spread Damage Directly Pin P1 Bulk and Special Attack**:
   - Rillaboom takes exact 14 recoil from Wood Hammer on Turn 6 ($203 \rightarrow 189$), bounding damage to Salamence to $[41, 43]$. With Rillaboom's known Atk (192 at -1 = 128), Salamence's Defense is isolated.
   - Salamence's Hyper Voice deals exact 17 damage to Rillaboom (known SpD 94 under Light Screen) and exact 15 damage to Grimmsnarl (known SpD 97 under Light Screen) $\rightarrow$ pins Salamence's SpA (162).
3. **Direct Substitution**:
   - All equations resolve linearly without search looping. Inference converged in 11 rounds with 19 rebuilds.

## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

In `both@spectator`, inference ran for **10,635.2s (2.95 hours)** across **218 rounds, 633 rebuilds, and 1,570,528 probes**, hitting all 7 search limits (`backtracks`, `fallbackRebuilds`, `fallbackTotal`, `guessTries`, `probes`, `searches`, `stalled`) and giving up with stop reason `"stalled"`.

The failure was driven by six mutually reinforcing pathologies:

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Rounding Across All 8 Combatants
In Channel 0:
- Every HP value is masked by `Math.floor(100 * hp / maxhp) || 1`.
- All 8 Pokémon brought (4 on P1: Talonflame, Basculegion, Sneasler, Salamence; 4 on P2: Raichu, Grimmsnarl, Volcarona, Rillaboom) engaged in active combat, dealt damage, and took damage.
- There are no known anchor Pokémon on either side. Every single damage exchange involves four simultaneous unknown variables:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Attacker Offense}, \text{unknown Defender Defense}, \text{RNG roll } [0.85, 1.00]) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lfloor \cdot \rfloor\%$$
- Because many combinations of `(Atk/SpA, Def/SpD, maxHP, roll)` map to the same rounded percentage integer, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- Sneasler retained 83.4M spreads (61.0% of initial pool), Basculegion retained 23.7M spreads (17.3%), Volcarona retained 18.5M spreads (13.5%), Salamence retained 11.4M spreads (8.3%), Raichu retained 10.5M spreads (7.7%), Grimmsnarl retained 3.8M spreads (2.8%), and Talonflame retained 3.4M spreads (2.5%).

### Root Cause 2: Turn 1 Speed Inversion Between Choice Scarf Basculegion and Gale Wings Talonflame
On Turn 1:
- P1 leads Basculegion and Talonflame. P2 leads Raichu and Grimmsnarl.
- In ground truth:
  - Basculegion (Jolly, 32 Spe SP, base 78) has raw Spe 143. With Choice Scarf, Spe = $143 \times 1.5 = 214$.
  - Raichu Mega Evolves to Raichu-Mega-Y (Timid, 32 Spe SP, base 130) with Spe 200.
  - Talonflame (Jolly, 27 Spe SP, base 126) has Spe 190.
  - Grimmsnarl uses Prankster Reflect (+1 priority).
  - Priority 0 speed order: Basculegion (214) > Raichu (200) > Talonflame (190).
- Talonflame possesses the ability `Gale Wings` (grants +1 priority to Flying moves at full HP). However, on Turn 1 Talonflame used `Flare Blitz` (a Fire-type move), which executes at normal priority (0).
- When inference samples candidate spreads for P1:
  - If Talonflame is guessed with high Spe SP (e.g. 32 SP $\rightarrow$ Spe 195) and Basculegion is guessed with lower Spe SP (e.g. 17 SP $\rightarrow$ raw Spe 126, Choice Scarf Spe 189):
    Talonflame (195) moves before Basculegion (189)!
  - Talonflame uses Flare Blitz on Raichu first. Raichu survives at full health; Raichu then uses Zap Cannon and KOs Basculegion before Basculegion can use Wave Crash.
  - In Round 4 of the spectator trace:
    `wanted |move|p1b: Basculegion|Wave Crash|p2a: Raichu, got |move|p1a: Talonflame|Flare Blitz|p2a: Raichu`
  - Fallback had to intervene on Turn 1, testing perturbations on Talonflame's speed to force Basculegion to act first, consuming 143.7s in Round 4 alone.

### Root Cause 3: Cumulative Multi-Event Recoil Chaining on Talonflame
Talonflame takes non-stop recoil and damage across almost every turn:
- Turn 1: Flare Blitz recoil on Raichu $\rightarrow$ spectator: `95/100`.
- Turn 3: Takes 3 distinct damage events in a single turn:
  1. Rocky Helmet from Volcarona ($\lfloor \text{maxHP}/6 \rfloor$) $\rightarrow$ spectator: `79/100`.
  2. Flare Blitz recoil ($\lfloor \text{damage} \times 0.33 \rfloor$) $\rightarrow$ spectator: `66/100`.
  3. Spirit Break from Grimmsnarl $\rightarrow$ spectator: `41/100`.
- Turn 4: Flare Blitz recoil on Grimmsnarl $\rightarrow$ spectator: `26/100`.
- Turn 7: Flare Blitz recoil on Rillaboom $\rightarrow$ spectator: `1/100`.
A 1-point difference in Talonflame's max HP (e.g. 159 vs 160 vs 161) shifts the rounded percentage of every intermediate event. In Round 3, guesses produced `96/100` instead of `95/100`. In Round 8, guesses produced `67/100` instead of `66/100`.

### Root Cause 4: Turn 4 Dire Claw / Volcarona Faint Razor
On Turn 4:
- Sneasler uses Dire Claw on sleeping Volcarona.
- Volcarona has Rocky Helmet and 65 HP remaining.
- If Dire Claw rolls low damage and leaves Volcarona with even 1-4 HP:
  - As observed in Round 10 of spectator trace:
    `wanted |-damage|p2a: Volcarona|0 fnt, got |-damage|p2a: Volcarona|4/185 slp`
  - Volcarona does not faint.
  - Volcarona remains on the field at the end of Turn 4.
  - Rillaboom cannot switch in on Turn 4 upkeep.
  - Grassy Surge never activates, Grassy Terrain is never set, and the entire battle diverges irreparably at Turn 5.

### Root Cause 5: The Turn 7 Grassy Terrain Heal Cliff on Rillaboom (33/100 vs 34/100 Razor)
This is the central bottleneck that blocked forward reconstruction.
In Turn 7:
1. Rillaboom enters Turn 7 at 201/203 HP (99/100).
2. Talonflame uses Flare Blitz: deals 125 damage ($201 \rightarrow 76/203$).
   - Spectator: `|-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|37/100` ($76 / 203 = 37.44\%$).
   - However, any remaining HP in $[76, 77]$ produces `37/100` ($\lfloor 100 \times 77 / 203 \rfloor = 37$).
3. Salamence uses Hyper Voice: deals 17 spread damage ($76 \rightarrow 59/203$).
   - Spectator: `|-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|29/100` ($59 / 203 = 29.06\%$).
   - Any remaining HP in $[59, 60]$ produces `29/100` ($\lfloor 100 \times 60 / 203 \rfloor = 29$).
4. Rillaboom uses Wood Hammer on Salamence: Salamence has 5 HP left and faints.
   - Recoil is $\lfloor 5 \times 0.33 \rfloor = 2$ HP ($59 \rightarrow 57/203$).
   - Spectator: `|-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|28/100` ($57 / 203 = 28.08\%$).
   - If Rillaboom had 60 HP after Hyper Voice, $60 - 2 = 58$ HP. $58 / 203 = 28.57\% \rightarrow \mathbf{28/100}$!
   - Both 57 HP and 58 HP display as `28/100`!
5. End of Turn 7 Grassy Terrain Healing:
   - Rillaboom heals $\lfloor 203 / 16 \rfloor = 12$ HP.
   - If Rillaboom had 57 HP: $57 + 12 = 69$ HP.
     $$69 / 203 = 0.33990147... \rightarrow \mathbf{33/100}$$
   - If Rillaboom had 58 HP: $58 + 12 = 70$ HP.
     $$70 / 203 = 0.344827... \rightarrow \mathbf{34/100}$$
   - Notice that $69/203$ is $33.9901\%$, just **$0.0098\%$ below $34\%$**!
   - A single hit point of damage difference shifts the spectator display from `33/100` to `34/100`.
6. **Reconstruct Probe Failure**:
   - Because all preceding lines on Turn 7 (`37/100`, `29/100`, `28/100`) accept both the 57 HP and 58 HP branches, `reconstruct`'s forward probe search routinely accepts rolls that leave Rillaboom at 58 HP.
   - At the end of Turn 7, the Grassy Terrain heal line triggers a diff:
     `expected: |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|33/100, actual: |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|34/100`
   - To correct this, the rebuild must backtrack across 6 consecutive dice (Grassy Terrain heal $\rightarrow$ Wood Hammer recoil $\rightarrow$ Hyper Voice on Grimmsnarl $\rightarrow$ Hyper Voice on Rillaboom $\rightarrow$ Flare Blitz recoil $\rightarrow$ Flare Blitz damage).
   - In `infer.mjs`, budget level 0 has `maxBacktracks: 0` and budget level 1 has `maxBacktracks: 2`. Neither can backtrack 6 dice.
   - Even when tested with the **true packed teams**, a reconstruction with `maxBacktracks: 4` failed on Turn 7 with `actual: 34/100`. Only `maxBacktracks: 10` reached Turn 8.

### Root Cause 6: Cascading Fallback Exhaustion and Stalling Loop
1. When rebuilds stop on Turn 7 with `33/100` vs `34/100`, `fallback(seen, start, ...)` is invoked.
2. Fallback attempts single-stat perturbations on the active Pokémon (`cast`: Rillaboom, Grimmsnarl, Talonflame, Salamence).
3. However, the 1-HP difference on Rillaboom at Turn 7 is coupled across multiple turns:
   - Perturbing Rillaboom's Def or HP changes Rillaboom's Turn 6 Wood Hammer recoil (`93/100`) and Grassy Terrain healing (`99/100`).
   - Perturbing Talonflame's Attack changes Talonflame's Flare Blitz recoil on Turn 1 (`95/100`), Turn 3 (`66/100`), and Turn 4 (`26/100`).
   - Perturbing Salamence's SpA changes Salamence's Hyper Voice damage on Grimmsnarl (`35/100`).
4. Fallback cannot find any 1-stat change that satisfies all 7 turns simultaneously.
5. In each round:
   - Fallback exhausts its per-turn budget $\rightarrow$ hits `fallbackRebuilds`.
   - Across rounds, fallback exhausts its total rebuild budget $\rightarrow$ hits `fallbackTotal`.
   - Candidate guessing across 8 unpinned Pokémon exhausts its permutation budget in `choose(n, cond)` $\rightarrow$ hits `guessTries`.
   - No exact proof is produced; `exact` and `pins` remain `null`.
   - The search escalates through budget levels 0, 1, 2, 3 without progress $\rightarrow$ hits `searches`.
   - Deepest verified turn oscillates between Turn 3, Turn 4, and Turn 6. `stalled` increments on nearly every round until reaching `stalled >= maxRounds` (218 rounds).
   - Search gives up with stop reason `"stalled"`.

## 7. Summary of Facts

1. **Single-View Inferences Passed**:
   - `p2@p1` succeeded in 95.1s (10 rounds, 12 rebuilds, 12,250 probes) because P1's known exact stats and recoil values provided non-circular constraints on P2's stats.
   - `p1@p2` succeeded in 170.9s (11 rounds, 19 rebuilds, 20,804 probes) because P2's known exact stats provided non-circular constraints on P1's stats.
2. **Spectator Inference Stalled**:
   - In `both@spectator`, all 8 Pokémon brought saw active combat and dealt or received damage under percentage masking.
   - The search gave up after 10,635.2s (218 rounds, 633 rebuilds, 1,570,528 probes) with stop reason `"stalled"`, hitting all 7 search limits.
3. **Core Mechanical Bottlenecks**:
   - **Turn 1**: Speed race between Choice Scarf Basculegion and Gale Wings Talonflame using Flare Blitz.
   - **Turn 3**: Triple damage event on Talonflame (Rocky Helmet + Recoil + Spirit Break).
   - **Turn 4**: Sneasler Dire Claw faint razor on Volcarona (sleeping at 4 HP blocks Rillaboom entry and Grassy Terrain).
   - **Turn 7**: Rillaboom Grassy Terrain heal razor where $69/203 = 33.9901\%$ (`33/100`), while $70/203 = 34.4827\%$ (`34/100`), requiring a 6-die backtrack across multiple spread moves and recoils that exceeds rebuild backtracks budgets in earlier levels and fails fallback 1-stat perturbations.
