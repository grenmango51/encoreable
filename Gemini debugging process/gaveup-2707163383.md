## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Turn 4 Residual Tie-Breaker Trap (`4.15`: `Leftovers` `subOrder: 4` vs `Grassy Terrain` `subOrder: 2`)
In Showdown's end-of-turn `fieldEvent('Residual')` (`sim/battle.ts` lines 404–410, 507), both `grassyterrain` (`onResidualOrder: 5, onResidualSubOrder: 2`) and `leftovers` (`onResidualOrder: 5, onResidualSubOrder: 4`) share **`order = 5`** and **`priority = 0`**. Consequently, `comparePriority(a, b)` evaluates:
1. `order` (tied at `5`) $\to$ `priority` (tied at `0`) $\to$ **`speed`** $\to$ **`subOrder`** (`2` before `4`)!

This creates a strict inequality requirement:
- When `Primarina` is **strictly faster** than `Rillaboom` (`112 > 110` in ground truth), `speed` breaks the comparison *before* `subOrder` is checked, so both `Primarina`'s Grassy Terrain (`4.14`) and `Primarina`'s Leftovers (`4.15`) run before `Rillaboom`'s Grassy Terrain (`4.16`).
- Whenever `Primarina` and `Rillaboom` are **Speed-tied** (`105 === 105`), `speed` returns `0` and `comparePriority` falls through to `subOrder`. Because **`Grassy Terrain` (`subOrder 2`) always precedes `Leftovers` (`subOrder 4`)** at equal Speed, **no speed-tie RNG roll can ever print `Primarina Leftovers` before `Rillaboom Grassy Terrain`**: even if `Primarina` wins the tie for Grassy Terrain (`4.14`), `Rillaboom Grassy Terrain` (`subOrder 2`) is forced at `4.15`, pushing `Primarina Leftovers` (`subOrder 4`) to `4.16`!

### B. How Both Default and Usage Starts Converged to the `Spe = 105 == 105` Deadlock

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Primarina` (`166 HP`, `165 SpA`, **`112 Spe`**) and `Rillaboom` (`182 HP`, **`110 Spe`**) are ground-truth constants. There is no `105 == 105` Speed tie at Turn 4 (`4.15`), and `Archaludon` (`Spe >= 112`) is immediately forced to `Spe SP >= 7` (`4.12–4.13`), letting `p2@p1` reconstruct cleanly through Turn 8.
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Archaludon` (`197 HP, 115 SpD, 116 Spe`) and `Basculegion-F` (`167 SpA, 130 Spe`) are fixed constants, and Channel 1/2 exact HP prints `145/166` (`Primarina` `MaxHP = 166`, `hp = 11` exact from Turn 0!). With `Primarina`'s `hp = 11` pinned, `Primarina` has `55` SP of budget left (`11 + 32 + 25 = 68` is impossible, forcing `spa` down immediately!), so `Primarina` smoothly reaches `spe = 32` (`112 > 110`) without ever starving `hp`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Unverified Offensive Priors (`spa = 32`) Starve `hp` and `spe` Under the 66-SP Cap**:
   Because `defaultSpread` (`infer.mjs` / `knowledge.mjs`) and `Modest #1` placed `32` SP into `Primarina.spa` before `Primarina` made any damage-constrained `spa` roll on Turns 1–3, `closestSpread` sacrificed `hp` (`32 -> 9` or `7`) rather than `spa` whenever `spe` moved up. When `fallback` subsequently probed `Primarina spe > 25`, `closestSpread` again preserved `spa = 32` and robbed `hp`, breaking Turns 1–3 before Turn 4. In `fallback`, probing `spe` on an `order` diff should allow slack from unpinned/non-participating offensive stats (or `closestSpread` should not hold `spa = 32` above `hp` when `hp` is already survival-constrained).
2. **Probes That Fail Early Due to 66-SP Budget Trade-Offs Are Excluded from `moved` (`infer.mjs` line 704)**:
   Because `Primarina spe 32, 28, 26` stopped at `1.10` and `3.5` (`r[0] < 4`) solely due to `hp` starvation, `Primarina.spe` had `d.moved = false`. Meanwhile `Primarina spa 0` stopped at `4.15` with `sameOutcome`, so `Primarina.spa` also had `d.moved = false`. Thus the pair search (`lines 833–845`) never explored `(Primarina spe +, Primarina spa -)`. When `order = true` and a Speed end stops early only because the 66-SP cap pulled `hp`/`def`/`spd` down, pairing that Speed bump with reducing a non-tied/free stat (`spa = 0` or `atk = 0`) would break the budget deadlock immediately.
3. **`fallback` Should Not Abort at `reach(base)[0] < t` When `budgets[1]` Reached `t` (`infer.mjs` line 614)**:
   In both usage starts (`Starts 2 & 5`), `dice` (`budgets[1]`, `maxBacktracks: 2`) successfully reached Turn 4 (`4.15`), while `base` (`maxBacktracks: 0`) stopped at `2.5` (`82/100` vs `84/100`) only because `handOn(built, null)` stripped `exact` and `proof.rolls`. Exiting at line 614 (`return none()`) threw away all `32` / `128` fallback rebuilds in Starts 2 & 5 without testing a single stat.
