## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The `teamPreview` Disguise-Placement Bug in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` (`choiceFor`, lines 447–483)
When `reconstructOnce` (`reconstruct.mjs` line 2288) prepares the observed log:
1. `unmaskIllusion(lines, sheets, forced)` detects Turn 4's `|replace|p1a: Zoroark|Zoroark-Hisui, L50, F`, unmasks **line 80** back to `Zoroark`, and records **`disguises.p1 = ['Mamoswine']`**.
2. `revealOrder(unmasked.lines)` records **`reveal.p1 = ['Whimsicott', 'Glimmora', 'Mamoswine', 'Zoroark']`**.
3. When the simulator asks for P1's `teamPreview` choice (`choiceFor()`, `reconstruct.mjs` lines 447–483):
   ```js
   const last = (state.disguises?.[sideId] || [])
     .filter(name => !state.reveal[sideId].slice(0, leads).includes(name))
     .map(name => bench.indexOf(name))
     .filter(at => at >= 0); // [Mamoswine]
   const seen = [];
   for (const name of state.reveal[sideId]) {
     const at = bench.indexOf(name);
     if (at >= 0 && !seen.includes(at) && !last.includes(at)) seen.push(at);
   } // [Whimsicott, Glimmora, Zoroark]
   ```
   Because `!last.includes(at)` **strips `Mamoswine` out of `seen`** and places `Mamoswine` in `ends`, `teamPreview` generates **only one single option (`options.length === 1`)**:
   **`[Whimsicott, Glimmora, Zoroark, Mamoswine]`**.

Now trace what happens when the reconstruction plays `[Whimsicott, Glimmora, Zoroark, Mamoswine]` forward:
- **Turn 0**: `[0: Whimsicott, 1: Glimmora, 2: Zoroark, 3: Mamoswine]`
- **Turn 2** (`Whimsicott` $\to$ `Mamoswine`, swaps `0 <-> 3`): `[0: Mamoswine, 1: Glimmora-Mega, 2: Zoroark, 3: Whimsicott]`
- **Turn 3** (`Glimmora-Mega` $\to$ `Whimsicott`, swaps `1 <-> 3`): `[0: Mamoswine, 1: Whimsicott, 2: Zoroark, 3: Glimmora-Mega]`
- **Turn 4** (`Mamoswine` $\to$ `Zoroark`, swaps `0 <-> 2`): **`[0: Zoroark, 1: Whimsicott, 2: Mamoswine, 3: Glimmora-Mega]`**

Because `Mamoswine` started in slot 4 instead of slot 3, the Turn 2 and Turn 3 switches pushed `Glimmora-Mega` into `index 3`. Consequently, on Turn 4, `Illusion` **always** disguises `Zoroark` as `Glimmora-Mega`, printing **`got |switch|p1a: Glimmora|Glimmora-Mega, L50, M|100/100`** instead of **`wanted |switch|p1a: Mamoswine|Mamoswine, L50, F|100/100`**!

The true team preview order (`[Whimsicott, Glimmora, Mamoswine, Zoroark]`) is **never generated as a `teamPreview` variant**. Because `reconstruct.mjs` withholds `>p1 team ...` in every view (`p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, and `both@spectator`), **every view hits this exact same wall on Turn 4**.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, and `both@spectator` All Failed
Unlike stat-coupled stalls (where knowing one side's exact stats and exact HP unlocks the puzzle), `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` both gave up alongside `both@spectator` (`slowest_gave_up.json`, lines 4797–4800) because **`reconstructOnce` reconstructs `>p1 team ...` from `observed` in every view**. Even when `p1`'s Stat Points are 100% known (`p2@p1`), `choiceFor()` still forces `p1` into `[Whimsicott, Glimmora, Zoroark, Mamoswine]` (`options.length === 1`), making Turn 4 emit `|switch|p1a: Glimmora|` instead of `|switch|p1a: Mamoswine|`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Force Non-Lead Illusion Disguises into the Last Turn-0 Slot (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `choiceFor` lines 449–477)**:
   `choiceFor()` assumes that any non-lead species `Zoroark-Hisui` disguised itself as (`last`) must have started in the last slot at Team Preview (`!last.includes(at)` removes it from `seen`, and `ends` puts it after `back`). In Pokémon Showdown, however, **chosen switches (`switch`) swap `side.pokemon` slots**: a Pokémon brought in slot 3 (`Mamoswine`) can swap to an active slot (`p1a`) on Turn 2 and swap back into slot 4 on Turn 4 when `Zoroark-Hisui` enters—making slot 3 the Illusion template!
   - **Fix**: Keep the `ends`-last ordering as the first/preferred `teamPreview` option, but when `last.length > 0` and any switch occurred on `sideId`, include the remaining permutations of `[...known, ...chosen, ...last]` as subsequent `teamPreview` variants so `[Whimsicott, Glimmora, Mamoswine, Zoroark]` is reachable.
2. **Avoid Testing Already-Unmasked or Post-Faint Switch-Ins in `disguisableStays` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` line 2719, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` line 1145)**:
   `disguisableStays()` inspects raw lines rather than `unmaskIllusion(lines)`, causing `inferSpreads()` to re-run full searches for:
   - **`line 80`**: Already unmasked by Turn 4's `|replace|p1a: Zoroark|` (a 100% duplicate of the `plain` search).
   - **`lines 98 & 99`**: Replacement switch-ins occurring **after** `|faint|p1a: Zoroark` in Turn 4's upkeep, which `unsettledStays()` already marks settled because `Zoroark-Hisui` is fainted.
   Filtering out already-unmasked stays and post-faint stays from `disguisableStays` (or checking `unsettledStays` / `unmasked` before launching a forced reading) would eliminate 3 out of 7 redundant Illusion-reading sub-trees (`~43%` of wasted probe work) in cases like this.
