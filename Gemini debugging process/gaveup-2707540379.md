## 6. Spectator Failure Analysis (`both@spectator`)

### Why `both@spectator` Ran 324 Rounds, 1,223 Rebuilds, and Stalled in 16,634.9s
In Channel 0 (spectator view), neither player's exact HP is revealed; every HP value is an integer percentage out of 100.

#### 1. Under-Constrained Early Exits: Basculegion-F and Sneasler
- **Basculegion-F (78.3M and 88.1M spreads remaining)**:
  - Engaged in only a single combat exchange on Turn 2: P2 Basculegion Shadow Ball vs P1 Basculegion (`30/100`), P1 Basculegion Shadow Ball vs P2 Basculegion (`23/100`), followed by P1 Basculegion fainted (`0 fnt`).
  - P2 Basculegion was switched out on Turn 3 and never returned to combat.
  - Spectator percentages of 30% and 23% over base 120 HP leave HP, SpA, and SpD completely coupled on both sides ($\sim 70\%$ damage can be produced by dozens of combinations of unknown SpA, unknown SpD, and unknown max HP). Over 78 million spreads survive on P1 and 88 million on P2.
- **Sneasler (118.3M and 136.3M spreads remaining)**:
  - P2 Sneasler: Switched in T3, took Snarl (`92/100`), protected T4, used Close Combat T5, and was KOed T5 (`0 fnt`). It has 136,328,207 candidate spreads remaining (99.75% of initial space).
  - P1 Sneasler: Protected T1, switched out T2, switched in T5, took resisted Aura Sphere (`84/100`), protected T6, and switched out T7. It has 118,303,836 spreads remaining (86.57% of initial space).

#### 2. The Mutual Leech Life & Leftovers Circular Dependency (Turns 6-12)
From Turn 6 through Turn 12, the battle consists of a 4-way mirror between `p1a: Golisopod-Mega`, `p1b: Archaludon`, `p2a: Archaludon`, and `p2b: Golisopod-Mega`.

On every turn between Turn 7 and Turn 12:
1. `p1a` Golisopod attacks `p2b` Golisopod with Leech Life:
   - Damage dealt to P2 Golisopod is:
     $$\text{Dmg}_1 = \left\lfloor \left( \frac{22 \times 80 \times \text{Atk}(p1a)}{50 \times \text{Def}(p2b)} + 2 \right) \times 1.5 \times 1.3 \times 0.5 \right\rfloor \times \text{roll}$$
   - Spectator damage percentage shown on P2 Golisopod is:
     $$\Delta\%(p2b) = \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{Dmg}_1}{\text{maxHP}(p2b)} \times 100 \right)$$
   - `p1a` Golisopod heals via drain:
     $$\text{Heal}_1 = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Dmg}_1}{2} \right\rceil$$
   - Spectator heal percentage shown on P1 Golisopod is:
     $$\Delta\%_{\text{heal}}(p1a) = \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{Heal}_1}{\text{maxHP}(p1a)} \times 100 \right)$$
2. `p2b` Golisopod attacks `p1a` Golisopod back with Leech Life (Turns 8, 10, 12):
   - Damage dealt to P1 Golisopod is:
     $$\text{Dmg}_2 = \left\lfloor \left( \frac{22 \times 80 \times \text{Atk}(p2b)}{50 \times \text{Def}(p1a)} + 2 \right) \times 1.5 \times 1.3 \times 0.5 \right\rfloor \times \text{roll}$$
   - Spectator damage percentage shown on P1 Golisopod is:
     $$\Delta\%(p1a) = \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{Dmg}_2}{\text{maxHP}(p1a)} \times 100 \right)$$
   - `p2b` Golisopod heals via drain:
     $$\text{Heal}_2 = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Dmg}_2}{2} \right\rceil$$
   - Spectator heal percentage shown on P2 Golisopod is:
     $$\Delta\%_{\text{heal}}(p2b) = \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{Heal}_2}{\text{maxHP}(p2b)} \times 100 \right)$$
3. Concurrently, both Archaludons heal Leftovers every single turn:
   $$\text{Heal}_{\text{Leftovers}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{maxHP}(\text{Archaludon})}{16} \right\rfloor$$
   which is displayed as rounded integer percentages:
   $$\Delta\%_{\text{Leftovers}} = \text{round}\left( \frac{\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor}{\text{maxHP}} \times 100 \right)$$

This creates an 11-variable mutually dependent non-linear system:
$$\{ \text{Atk}(p1a), \text{Def}(p1a), \text{HP}(p1a), \text{Atk}(p2b), \text{Def}(p2b), \text{HP}(p2b), \text{SpA}(p2a), \text{Def}(p2a), \text{HP}(p2a), \text{SpA}(p1b), \text{Def}(p1b), \text{HP}(p1b) \}$$
All 11 variables are coupled through floor divisions, ceiling drain calculations, and percentage rounding across 7 consecutive turns.

#### 3. Cumulative HP Drift and 1% Discrepancy Wall
Because HP is tracked continuously across turns:
$$\text{HP}_T = \text{HP}_{T-1} - \text{Dmg}_{T-1} + \text{Heal}_{T-1}$$
Any 1 HP discrepancy in damage or drain heal on Turn 6 or Turn 7 carries over into the starting HP of Turn 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12.
When the guessed spreads deviate by even 1 Stat Point:
- In Round 1: stops at Turn 2 (`wanted |-damage|p2a: Golisopod|82/100, got 81/100`).
- In Round 2: stops at Turn 4 (`wanted |-damage|p1b: Sneasler|84/100, got 86/100`).
- In Round 3: stops at Turn 5 (`wanted |-damage|p1a: Golisopod|36/100, got 33/100`).
- In Round 4: stops at Turn 5 (`wanted |-heal|p1a: Golisopod|50/100y, got 47/100`).
- In Round 5: stops at Turn 6 (`wanted |-damage|p2b: Golisopod|65/100, got 62/100`).
- In Round 6: stops at Turn 6 (`wanted |-heal|p1b: Archaludon|68/100, got |-heal|p2a: Archaludon|90/100`).
- In Round 7: stops at Turn 6 (`wanted |-heal|p1b: Archaludon|68/100, got 69/100`).

#### 4. Sequential Guessing Failure and Budget Exhaustion
- In `infer.mjs`, the guess generator `choose` pins candidate spreads sequentially. When all 8 battling Pokémon are unknown and their candidate pools contain tens of millions of combinations, `choose` explores the search space without finding a set of simultaneous pins that yields an exact proof (`proof.exact`).
- When `proof` is null, `infer.mjs` runs `reconstruct` with `exact: null`.
- `reconstruct` without exact pins hits a diff on Turn 6 or Turn 7 where cumulative Leech Life / Leftovers damage drifts by 1% (e.g. `68/100` vs `69/100`).
- Because rebuilds hit the same diff mark repeatedly, `failed.has(markOf(diff))` triggers `fallback`.
- `fallback` executes rebuilds locally, rapidly hitting:
  - `fallbackRebuilds` (`spent >= FALLBACK_REBUILDS * S * W`)
  - `fallbackTotal` (`spend.rebuilds <= 0`)
  - `guessTries` (`tries <= 0`)
- Because no rebuild advances past Turn 7, `deepest` never progresses.
- `stalled` increments on every round until reaching `maxRounds` (324 rounds).
- The search terminates with `stop = "stalled"`.
