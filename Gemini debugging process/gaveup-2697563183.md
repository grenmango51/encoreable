### Root Cause 5: Flame Body Burn & Grassy Terrain End-of-Turn Fractional Drift
- From Turn 3 onward, Golisopod was burned (`brn`), taking 1/16 max HP each turn, and starting on Turn 4 upkeep, Grassy Terrain healed 1/16 max HP each turn.

### Root Cause 6: Two-State Limit Cycle Oscillation Across All Rounds

### Root Cause 7: Single-Stat Fallback Decoupling Failure Across 4 Restarts
