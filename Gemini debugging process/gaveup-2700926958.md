- In spectator view (Channel 0), **not a single exact HP number exists for either side**.

     - Turn 4 Rock Slide damage (`87/100` wanted; shifts to `86/100` if HP/Def change).

     - Turn 5 Rock Slide damage (`82/100` wanted; shifts to `81/100` if HP/Def change).

  4. Because no single-stat perturbation can satisfy the Speed order on Turn 5 without breaking the exact damage percentages on Turn 4 or Turn 5, fallback exhausted its 8 allowed rebuilds on every invocation, adding `fallbackRebuilds` to limits.

### 5. Mutual Infestation Residual Trapping on Both Sides
- From Turn 5 through Turn 10, both Toxapex were mutually trapped by `Infestation`.
- At the end of every turn, both Toxapex took Leftovers recovery ($\lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 16 \rfloor$) and Infestation residual damage ($\lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 8 \rfloor$).

- These continuous double integer truncations over 6 consecutive turns created a non-convex, disconnected slice of surviving HP values that standard sampling failed to penetrate.

### 6. The 4-Hour Stalling Cascade & Restart Loop
- Because candidate guesses could not consistently pass Turn 5, `deepest` verified turn never advanced past Turn 5 / Turn 6.
- The `stalled` counter reached `maxRounds` (16), causing the initial `default` search to give up with `stop = "stalled"`.
- As dictated by `infer.mjs`, when a search gives up:
  1. `start: 'usage'` was launched (up to 16 stalled rounds).
  2. `wide: true` was launched with $4\times$ fallback rebuilds and joint guesses.
  3. `widest: true` was launched, multiplying every search limit by $4\times$ ($64$ rounds per start, $4\times$ probes, $4\times$ backtracks).
- Over 280 cumulative rounds, 675 rebuilds, and 2,318,585 probes across all restarts, the search circled endlessly between:
  - Turn 5 Rock Slide damage on Toxapex/Venusaur
  - Turn 5 Toxapex speed tie order
  - Turn 5 Infestation residual percentage
  - Turn 6 Earth Power damage on Toxapex
- The search finally terminated at 14,222.1 seconds (3.95 hours) having hit 5 search limits: `backtracks`, `fallbackRebuilds`, `probes`, `searches`, and `stalled`.
