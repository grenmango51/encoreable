## 6. Spectator Failure Analysis (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Coarse Percentage Rounding & Cascading Recoil on Incineroar
In spectator view (`Channel 0`), neither side is known (`known = [false, false]`). Both players' HP values are obscured behind integer percentage brackets:
$$\text{display\_pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{current\_hp}}{\text{max\_hp}} \right\rfloor$$

Unlike the original replay where Incineroar fainted on Turn 3, the mimic battle kept Incineroar alive across multiple turns:
- **Turn 1**: Took Fake Out (`91/100` vs `89/100`).
- **Turn 3**: Used Flare Blitz, took Rough Skin damage ($\lfloor \text{MaxHP}/8 \rfloor$), and took 1/3 recoil ($\lfloor \text{Dmg}_{\text{FB}}/3 \rfloor$), resulting in `75/100` recoil wanted vs `74/100` or `76/100` got.

  ```text
  wanted |-damage|p2b: Incineroar|46/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2b: Incineroar|45/100|[from] Recoil
  ```

Because Incineroar's HP pool entering Turn 5 is the cumulative result of **five separate integer truncations** (Fake Out + Rough Skin + Flare Blitz recoil 1 + Parabolic Charge + Sitrus Berry heal), the HP numerator entering Turn 5 was on a knife-edge threshold. Every candidate spread generated by the search evaluated to **45% recoil** instead of the required **46% recoil**.

### Root Cause 2: Failure of Coordinate Fallback at Turn 6

### Root Cause 3: The Multi-Target Parabolic Charge Drain Deadlock (Turns 8–10)
In the wider search restarts (Phases 5 and 6), the engine managed to find candidate sets that pushed past Turn 6, reaching Turns 8, 9, and 10. However, it ran into an even more severe mathematical deadlock.

1. **Mega Garchomp Z Loses Ground Immunity**:

   - On Turns 8 and 9, Bellibolt used Parabolic Charge. Because Mega Garchomp Z is no longer Ground-type, Parabolic Charge hits **both opponents and its own ally**.
2. **Sequential Multi-Target Drain Calculations**:
   - In doubles, Parabolic Charge deals spread damage (0.75×) and drains 50% from each target in turn:
     $$\text{Drain}_{\text{Garchomp}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Damage}(\text{Bellibolt SpA} \to \text{Garchomp SpD})}{2} \right\rfloor$$
     $$\text{Drain}_{\text{Sinistcha}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Damage}(\text{Bellibolt SpA} \to \text{Sinistcha SpD})}{2} \right\rfloor$$
   - Bellibolt's displayed percentage after draining Garchomp is:
     $$\text{pct}_1 = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times (\text{HP}_{\text{initial}} + \text{Drain}_{\text{Garchomp}})}{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Bellibolt}}} \right\rfloor$$
   - Bellibolt's displayed percentage after draining Sinistcha is:
     $$\text{pct}_2 = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times (\text{HP}_{\text{initial}} + \text{Drain}_{\text{Garchomp}} + \text{Drain}_{\text{Sinistcha}})}{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Bellibolt}}} \right\rfloor$$
3. **The Integer Rounding Deadlock**:

     - `wanted |-heal|p1b: Bellibolt|34/100|[from] drain|[of] p1a: Garchomp`
