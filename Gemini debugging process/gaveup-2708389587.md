## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- No Pokémon displays exact HP numbers. Every damage, heal, drain, and recoil event is rounded to integer percentages via `Math.ceil(currentHP * 100 / maxHP)`.
- 8 Pokémon were brought into the battle (4 per side), and 7 engaged in prolonged combat exchanges.
- Archaludon was on the field for 10 consecutive turns (Turns 3–12), during which it experienced:
  - 8 distinct physical attacks (Garchomp Rock Slide crit, Incineroar Flare Blitz x3, Metagross Iron Head x2, Metagross Psychic Fangs).
  - 6 cumulative Stamina Defense boosts (+1 through +6).
  - 10 consecutive Leftovers heal ticks (`floor(maxHP / 16)`).
  - 7 consecutive burn damage ticks (`floor(maxHP / 16)`).
- Every percentage damage equation on Archaludon simultaneously depended on:
  1. Attacker's Attack/SpA Stat Points (Garchomp Atk, Incineroar Atk, Metagross Atk).
  2. Archaludon's Defense/SpD Stat Points.
  3. Archaludon's Max HP Stat Points (determining both percentage denominator and Leftovers/burn step size).
  4. Attacker's Max HP Stat Points (determining Flare Blitz recoil percentage: `floor(damage / 3) / maxHP`).
- When Incineroar's Flare Blitz deals damage, Incineroar takes recoil. In spectator view, `89/100` recoil couples Incineroar's max HP directly to the damage dealt to Archaludon, which couples to Archaludon's Defense and Archaludon's max HP.
- On Turn 6, Golisopod's Leech Life drain (`floor(damage / 2)`) couples Golisopod's max HP, Golisopod's Attack, Metagross's Defense, and Metagross's max HP in the same turn that Archaludon's Electro Shot couples Archaludon's SpA to Incineroar's SpD, Incineroar's max HP, and Incineroar's Sitrus Berry activation threshold (<= 50% max HP).

### Root Cause 2: Speed Tie Oscillation and Turn Order Inversion (Turn 4 vs Turn 8)
- P1 Incineroar (base 60, Careful, SP 26) and P2 Archaludon (base 85, Modest, SP 1) possess **identical raw Speed: 106**.
- Neither Pokémon received any Speed stat stage modifier (Tailwind was benched; Icy Wind missed into Protect; no paralysis occurred).
- In the real battle:
  - **Turn 4**: Archaludon acted before Incineroar. Archaludon used Dragon Pulse and knocked out Garchomp. Incineroar then used Parting Shot, lowering Archaludon's SpA and switching out.
  - **Turn 8**: Incineroar acted before Archaludon. Incineroar used Parting Shot, lowering Archaludon's SpA. Archaludon then used Snarl.
- In `both@spectator`, neither Speed is known:
  - If a candidate guess selects `Archaludon Spe > Incineroar Spe`:
    - Turn 4 succeeds because Archaludon moves first and KOs Garchomp.
    - When the rebuild advances to Turn 8, Archaludon moves before Incineroar. The replay expects `|move|p1b: Incineroar|Parting Shot|p2b: Archaludon`, but gets `|move|p2b: Archaludon|Snarl|...`. Turn 8 fails.
  - If a candidate guess selects `Incineroar Spe > Archaludon Spe`:
    - Turn 4 immediately fails: Incineroar uses Parting Shot before Archaludon acts, lowering Archaludon's SpA to -1.
    - Archaludon's -1 SpA Dragon Pulse fails to KO Garchomp. The replay expects `|faint|p1b: Garchomp`, but Garchomp survives. Turn 4 fails.
  - If a candidate guess selects `Archaludon Spe == Incineroar Spe` (Speed Tie):
    - Showdown's queue invokes `prng.shuffle` to resolve the speed tie.
    - The simulation is only valid if the PRNG ordinal at Turn 4 selects Archaludon first AND the PRNG ordinal at Turn 8 selects Incineroar first.

### Root Cause 3: PRNG Stream Desynchronization from Speed Tie Shuffles
- When a candidate guess assigns strictly unequal speeds (e.g. `Archaludon Spe = 107, Incineroar Spe = 106`), Showdown's `speedSort` does not execute a shuffle for those two actions.
- As a consequence, the PRNG stream does not consume the shuffle die on Turn 4.
- This creates an off-by-one die shift for all subsequent random events in Turns 4, 5, and 6:
  - Turn 4 Rock Slide critical hit roll on Incineroar and damage rolls.
  - Turn 5 damage rolls.
  - Turn 6 Flare Blitz 10% burn roll on Archaludon (fails to burn Archaludon under shifted dice).
  - Turn 6 Sitrus Berry threshold checks on Incineroar.
- A guess that avoids the speed tie on Turn 4 desynchronizes the dice and crashes on Turn 6 (missing burn or wrong recoil). A guess that implements the speed tie requires the exact sequence of dice preceding Turn 4 to match the replay.

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Turn Invalidation Loop Between Turn 3, Turn 4, and Turn 8
1. When a round reaches Turn 7/8 and fails on the Turn 8 speed order, `speed.mjs` extracts the order rule: `Incineroar Spe >= Archaludon Spe`.
2. The joint guess generator (`choose` / `aimFor`) reallocates Stat Points to increase Incineroar's Speed or decrease Archaludon's Speed.
3. Because Stat Points are constrained by the 66 SP cap, increasing Speed forces points away from HP, Defense, or Attack.
4. On the subsequent rebuild (e.g. Round 12), the reallocated stats fail Turn 3 damage checks on Archaludon:
   `wanted |-damage|p2b: Archaludon|84/100, got |-damage|p2b: Archaludon|86/100`
   `wanted |-damage|p2b: Archaludon|52/100, got |-damage|p2b: Archaludon|47/100`
5. Fallback attempts single-stat perturbations on Archaludon and Incineroar on Turn 3. Fixing Turn 3 damage shifts Flare Blitz recoil on Turn 3:
   `wanted |-damage|p1a: Incineroar|89/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1a: Incineroar|88/100`
6. Fixing recoil perturbs stats such that Turn 4 speed order inverts (Incineroar moves before Archaludon), crashing Turn 4.
7. Fixing Turn 4 speed order inverts Turn 8 speed order, returning the search to the state in Step 1.
8. Because no single round could advance deeper than Turn 7, the `stalled` counter incremented on every round.
9. Upon reaching `stalled >= maxRounds`, the search triggered fallback restarts:
   - Start 1 (`default`): stalled after cycling between Turn 3, Turn 4, and Turn 7/8.
   - Start 2 (`usage`): stalled after cycling on the same mutually contradictory speed and damage lines.
   - Start 3 (`wide`): widened fallback rebuilds by 4x, exhausted `guessTries`, and stalled.
   - Start 4 (`widest`): widened all search limits by 4x, exhausted `backtracks`, `probes`, and `fallbackRebuilds`, and stalled.
10. The 4 starts ran 148 rounds, 612 rebuilds, and 1,326,712 probes across 9,029 seconds before halting with `stop = "stalled"`.
