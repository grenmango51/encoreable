### Root Cause 1: The Dire Claw Secondary Effect PRNG Desynchronization (Turn 1)
On Turn 1, Sneasler uses `Dire Claw` on Charizard-Mega-Y. In Showdown's mechanics:
- Dire Claw has a 50% probability of inflicting a status condition.
- When that 50% roll succeeds, the simulator rolls a 3-way uniform random choice: $1/3$ Poison, $1/3$ Paralysis, $1/3$ Sleep.
- In the ground-truth mimic battle (seed `1522,37307,42143,8075`), the PRNG rolls **Paralysis**:
  `wanted |-status|p2b: Charizard|par`

However, the PRNG sequence is stateful and advances on every die roll:
1. Sneasler Dire Claw damage roll (1 die: integer in $[85, 100]$).
2. Dire Claw secondary effect chance roll (1 die: check if $< 50$).
