## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled on Starts 1–4, Passed on Start 5, & Why `p1@p2` Gave Up

### A. Trick Room Speed Inversion (`Sylveon` vs. `Kingambit`) + Invisible `Volcarona` Choice at `4.0`
1. **Why `4.0` Printed `|move|p1a: Volcarona|Protect|p1a: Volcarona`**:
   Because `Volcarona` fainted on Turn 4 before acting, `reconstruct.mjs` (`planSegment` / `choiceFor`) marks `Volcarona` as an invisible choice (`hidden.includes(4)`), and `Variant 0` assigns its first move in `showteam`: **`Protect`** (priority `+4`). Because priority `+4` always beats priority `0` (`recharge`), Variant 0 fails at `4.0` (`got |move|p1a: Volcarona|Protect`).
   `reconstruct` then walks priority-`0` variants (`Heat Wave`, `Overheat`, `Tailwind`), **but** in Starts 1–4's guesses:
   - `Volcarona` was guessed with `spe = 2` (`Spe 122`) or `spe = 24` (`Spe 144`),
   - `Sylveon` (`Quiet`, `-Spe`) was guessed with `spe = 13` (**`Spe 83`**), and
   - `Kingambit` (`Adamant`, neutral Spe) was guessed with `spe = 0` or `spe = 2` (**`Spe 70` or `72`**).
   Under Trick Room, **`Kingambit` (`70..72`) < `Sylveon` (`83`)**, so `Kingambit` **moved before `Sylveon`** (`got |move|p1b: Kingambit|Kowtow Cleave`), still failing at `4.0` with score `[0, ...]` — leaving `Variant 0` (`Protect`) as `best` in `walked[0]`! Only when `Sylveon Spe < Kingambit Spe` (and `Volcarona` chooses a priority-`0` move) does `4.0` emit `|cant|p2b: Sylveon|recharge`!

### B. Why Starts 1, 3, and 4 (`default`, `wide`, `widest` default) Aborted Fallback at `3.3` via `forget(1)`

