## 6. The Anatomy of Search Failure in `both@spectator`

In `both@spectator`, neither player's sets or stats are known (`known = [false, false]`). The search is exposed to three simultaneous mathematical deadlocks:

### Trap 1: The Four-Turn `Leech Life` Drain Feedback Loop
Mega Golisopod executes `Leech Life` on **four consecutive turns** (Turns 2, 3, 4, 5):
$$\text{drainHealing} = \text{Math.ceil}(\text{actualDamage} \times 0.50)$$
$$\text{drainPercentShown} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{drainHealing}}{\text{GolisopodMaxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
The percentage healed on Golisopod in spectator view depends simultaneously on:
1. Golisopod's Attack stat
2. The current defender's Defense stat (Rillaboom Turn 2, Sylveon Turn 3, Raichu Turn 4, Arcanine Turn 5)
3. The defender's Max HP and current HP pool
4. Golisopod's Max HP pool

A difference of just 1 HP in defender damage shifts `Math.ceil(damage / 2)`. When divided by Golisopod's Max HP, this alters the displayed percentage:
- In Turn 2: `wanted 76/100 par, got 75/100 par`
- In Turn 3: `wanted 82/100 par, got 86/100 par` or `84/100 par`
- In Turn 4: `wanted 78/100 par, got 76/100 par`
- In Turn 5: `wanted 43/100 par, got ...`

### Trap 2: Grassy Terrain Multi-Unit End-of-Turn Healing Thresholds

- For P1 Raichu (HP 167): $\lfloor 167 / 16 \rfloor = 10$ HP.
- For P1 Sylveon (HP 179): $\lfloor 179 / 16 \rfloor = 11$ HP.

$$\text{pctAfterHeal} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{current}} + \lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 16 \rfloor}{\text{MaxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
