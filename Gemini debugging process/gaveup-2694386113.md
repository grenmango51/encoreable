## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why Both `both@spectator` and `p2@p1` Stalled at Turn 0 with 6 Broken Proofs

### A. Primary Engine Bug: `speed.mjs` Blind to Turn-0 `SwitchIn` Speed Order (`cachedSpeed === undefined`)
In Showdown's `runSwitch()` (`node_modules/pokemon-showdown/dist/sim/battle-actions.js` lines 177–180):
1. `this.battle.speedSort(allActive)` sorts all 4 active leads by `pokemon.speed` and stores their order in `this.battle.speedOrder`.
2. `this.battle.fieldEvent("SwitchIn", switchersIn)` resolves `SwitchIn` abilities (`Trace`, `Grassy Surge`, `Psychic Surge`) sorted by `handler.speed = pokemon.speed - speedOrderIndex / (activePerHalf * 2)` (`battle.js` line 772).

Inside `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` (line 245), `attachInference()` is only installed right before the first `>p1` / `>p2` input line—**after** `>player p1` and `>player p2` have already constructed all Pokémon and run `setSpecies()` (`pokemon.js` line 1008). During Team Preview, `commitChoices()` (`battle.js` line 2475) calls `battle.updateSpeed()`, which iterates `this.getAllActive()`—still empty before leads switch in—and `switchIn()` / `runSwitch()` never calls `updateSpeed()` or `setSpecies()`.

Consequently, when Turn 0's `fieldEvent("SwitchIn")` invokes `readHandlerSort(list, comparator, true)` (`scripts/lib/inference/speed.mjs` lines 307, 415–417):
```js
const fx = X?.cachedSpeed;
const fy = Y?.cachedSpeed;
if (!fx || !fy || X === Y || !(varies(fx) || varies(fy)) || alike(fx, fy)) continue;
```
**`X.cachedSpeed` and `Y.cachedSpeed` are `undefined` for all four Turn-0 leads (`Gengar`, `Rillaboom`, `Indeedee-F`, `Gardevoir`)!** `readHandlerSort` silently skips the entire Turn-0 `SwitchIn` sort and **never emits the bound `Rillaboom.spe >= Indeedee-F.spe`**.

### B. How `choose()` Broke the Turn-0 Proof (`Default` R3 $\to$ R4 & `Usage` R9 $\to$ R10)
1. In `Default` R3 (and `Usage` R9), `picks` had `Rillaboom.spe > Indeedee-F.spe`. Turn 0 reproduced cleanly with **1 RNG draw** (`Trace`'s `random(2) = 1` for `Grassy Surge`), and `evidencePass` recorded Turn 0 in `exact` (`partial: false`, `exactUpTo: Infinity`, `provedUpTo: Infinity`).
2. Because `speed.mjs` emitted no `Rillaboom >= Indeedee-F` constraint, `knowledge` still permitted `Indeedee-F.spe > Rillaboom.spe`. After Turn 8's `Expanding Force` hit on `Rillaboom`, `choose()` re-picked `Rillaboom` and `Indeedee-F` into **`Indeedee-F.spe > Rillaboom.spe`**.
3. On the next round (`Default` R4 and `Usage` R10), Turn 0 still threw the exact same 1 RNG draw (`Trace` `random(2) = 1`), matching `provedDraws` in `brokenProof()` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 1747–1760) in count, range, and value—yet `Indeedee-F` (`Psychic Surge`) fired before `Rillaboom` (`Grassy Surge`), emitting the extra `|-fieldstart|move: Grassy Terrain|[from] ability: Grassy Surge|[of] p1b: Rillaboom` where `exact.get(0)` required `|turn|1`.
4. `brokenProof()` fired **6 times** (once in each of the 6 starts: Starts 1, 3, 4, 6 at R4; Starts 2, 5 at R10).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p1@p2` Passed While Both `p2@p1` and `both@spectator` Gave Up
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known — `PASS`)**: `p2a: Indeedee-F`'s true Speed SP is fixed and known, and Channel 2 prints exact integer HP for P2 (locking exact damage on `Gardevoir` from `U-turn`). With P2 fixed, `p1b: Rillaboom` never entered a conflicting `Indeedee-F > Rillaboom` guess across rounds, allowing `p1@p2` to reconstruct the full game cleanly.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known — `GAVE UP`) and `both@spectator` (`GAVE UP`)**: `p2a: Indeedee-F` is inferred. Because `speed.mjs` failed to read `Rillaboom >= Indeedee-F` on Turn 0, `choose()` in both `p2@p1` and `both@spectator` was free to move `Indeedee-F.spe` above `Rillaboom.spe`, triggering the exact same Turn-0 `SwitchIn` deadlock and `!(t >= 1)` `fallback` skip.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/speed.mjs`, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Populate `rec.cachedSpeed` for Initial Leads and Fresh Switch-Ins (`speed.mjs` lines 255–276)**:
   `attachSpeed()` relies solely on `setSpecies` and `updateSpeed` overrides to set `rec.cachedSpeed`, but `setSpecies` runs during `>player` (before `attachInference` attaches) and `updateSpeed` is not called on leads before Turn 0's `runSwitch` (nor on mid-battle switch-ins before their first `SwitchIn` sort). Initializing `rec.cachedSpeed` in `attachSpeed` (or on `beforeSwitchIn` / `switchIn` when `rec.cachedSpeed` is unset) via `speedsOf(rec, () => rec.pokemon.getActionSpeed())` ensures `readHandlerSort(..., true)` reads Turn 0's `SwitchIn` order (`Rillaboom >= Indeedee-F`) and prevents `choose()` from breaking Turn-0 proofs.
2. **Allow `fallback()` on Turn 0 or Read Swapped `SwitchIn` End-of-Turn Elimination at `cutoffAt` (`infer.mjs` line 482, `speed.mjs` lines 678–681)**:
   - In `infer.mjs` line 482, `!(t >= 1)` unconditionally disables `fallback` on Turn 0, so any Turn-0 Speed or ability-order divergence leaves the search with no mechanism to move a witness spread out of a Turn-0 deadlock.
   - In `speed.mjs` line 678, when `mine[j].at === prefix.cutoffAt` and `theirs[j]` is `undefined` (because the swapped `SwitchIn` order suppressed a `|-fieldstart|` line right before `|turn|1`), `speedRules` fails to credit the swapped order as reaching `prefix.observedLine` (`|turn|1`). Handling end-of-handler-frame suppression at `cutoffAt` would recover the Turn-0 speed bound even on a failing Turn-0 rebuild.
