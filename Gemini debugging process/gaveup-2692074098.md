## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` (and `p1@p2`) Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

The search did not fail because of a Stat Point impossibility on Turn 2; rather, **two reconstruction bottlenecks (`Dire Claw`'s Turn 1 status at `1.13` and `Heat Wave`'s Turn 2 spread target) collided with a budget-reset and `fallback` mismatch in `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`**:

### A. Turn 1 `1.13` (`|-status|p2b: Volcarona|par`) Oscillation Across `budgets[0..2]` vs. `search 4` (`resample: 1`)
1. Under the primary seed (`seed = sampler(sampleSeed ^ 0x5eed).seed()` with `sampleSeed = 1`), every single `budgets[0..2]` (`resample: 0`) rebuild across all 6 starts — regardless of `maxProbes` (`150`, `600`, `2400`, `4000`, or `16000`!) — exited Turn 1 `resolveTurn` in `~1.5s` (`~450` probes across all `widths[1] = 9` variants) stuck at **`1.13: wanted |-status|p2b: Volcarona|par, got |-status|p2b: Volcarona|slp`** (or **`got psn`** when `Volcarona spe 26` tied `Sneasler` at `Spe 146` on line 185, shifting the PRNG stream by 1 `shuffle` draw).
2. Only **`search 4` (`budgets[3]`, `resample: 1`)**, which switches to `seed = null` (`sampler(2 ^ 0x5eed).seed()`) and clears `pins = null`, reproduced Turn 1 (`par`) — succeeding in **14 out of 14** `search 4` rounds across all 6 starts!
3. **Why `level` reset wiped Turn 1 progress every time `search 4` advanced**:
   In `infer.mjs` line 973:
   ```js
   if (level > floor && !budgets[level].resample && reached && past(at, reached)) floor = level;
   ```

   Because `floor` is explicitly prevented from rising to `3` when `budgets[3].resample` is truthy, whenever a `search 4` round (`R8` at `Froslass 54/100`, `R10` at `Sneasler 77/100`) produced new evidence and moved `picks` (`unchanged = false`), line 1105 reset **`level = floor` (`0`)**! ## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` Passed While Both `p1@p2` and `both@spectator` Gave Up
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:5 Sneasler`'s unusual random spread (`7/17/16/0/23/3`, `Atk 183`, `Spe 143`, `HP 162`, `+1 SpD 184`) is **known ground truth**, and Channel 1 prints `p1b: Sneasler`'s exact `hp/maxhp` (`125/162` or `126/162`). Thus `Volcarona` (`Spe >= 143`) and `Froslass` (`54/100` from known `Atk 183`) are immediately pinned into a tight, consistent region without `Sneasler`'s random spread misaligning Turn 1 speed/damage or Turn 2 `Thunderbolt` (`77/100` $\to$ exact `36–37` damage).
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)** and **`both@spectator` (Channel 0)**: `p1:5 Sneasler` is unknown and starts far from its random spread (`7/17/16/0/23/3`) in both `default` (`28/32/0/0/0/6`) and `usage` (`2/32/0/0/0/32`, `Spe 172 > Volcarona 152`), while `p1b: Sneasler`'s Turn 2 `Thunderbolt` HP is only visible as `77/100` (`Channel 2` shows P2 exact HP, not P1). As a result, `p1@p2` hit the same `search 1..4` cycle on Turn 1 `Dire Claw` (`1.13`) and Turn 2 `Heat Wave`, terminating with `stop = "searches"` alongside `both@spectator`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` & `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **`fallback()` Must Honor the Active `search 4` (`resample: 1`) Context (`infer.mjs` lines 498–501, 603–618)**:
   When `infer.mjs` reaches turn `t = 2` only under `level = 3` (`budgets[3]`, which sets `seed: null, pins: null, sampleSeed: sampleSeed + 1`), calling `fallback()` still rebuilds with `seed` (`resample: 0`) and `budgets[0]`/`budgets[1]`. Because `resample: 0` cannot even pass Turn 1 `1.13`, `fallback()` immediately aborts at line 617 (`stops before the turn, at 1.13`) and `++level >= budgets.length` terminates the search without testing a single Turn 2 perturbation. `fallback()` should preserve the `resample` / `budgets[level]` settings that actually reached turn `t`.
2. **Preserve `floor` Across `search 4` Progress (`infer.mjs` lines 973, 1105)**:
   Because `floor` only updates when `!budgets[level].resample`, every spread update found after a `search 4` round (`R8`, `R10`) resets `level = 0` (`resample: 0`), forcing the search to repeat 3 guaranteed-to-fail `1.13` rounds (`search 1..3`) before returning to `search 4` on Turn 2.
3. **Investigate Why `resolveTurn` Fails to Flip Non-Steered `battle.sample(...)` Draws (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 1786–2045)**:
   Both hard stalls in this trace (`Turn 1 1.13`: `Dire Claw`'s `this.sample(['psn', 'par', 'slp'])` stuck on `slp`/`psn` instead of `par`; and `Turn 2`: `Heat Wave`'s `getRandomTarget()` $\to$ `randomFoe()` $\to$ `this.sample(actives)` stuck on `p1a: Indeedee` instead of `p1b: Sneasler`) occurred on `battle.sample(...)` draws that exited `resolveTurn` as `stuck` in `~1.5s` even with `maxProbes = 16,000`. Fixing `resolveTurn` (or extending `steerDice`) to directly solve these small-radix `sample()` choices (`span = 2` or `3`) eliminates the `resample` dependency on Turn 1 and unblocks Turn 2 `Heat Wave` outright.
