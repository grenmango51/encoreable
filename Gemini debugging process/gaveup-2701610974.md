### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Truncation Across All 8 Combatants
Under spectator view (Channel 0), all HP values are masked to integer percentages:
$$\text{pct} = \lfloor 100 \times \text{hp} / \text{maxhp} \rfloor$$
Every damage event involves 4 coupled unknowns simultaneously:
1. Attacker's offensive stat (Atk or SpA)
2. Defender's defensive stat (Def or SpD)
3. Defender's max HP
4. RNG damage roll multiplier ($[0.85, 1.00]$, index 0–15)

In this battle, all 8 Pokémon brought to battle actively participated in combat and exchanged damage. Because multiple combinations of Atk, Def, and max HP produce identical spectator percentage sequences, `evidencePass` could not establish exact integer pins on any Pokémon except `p1:2` Incineroar's HP.

### Root Cause 2: Turn 6 Flare Blitz Recoil, Focus Sash & Grassy Terrain Healing Razor

  `wanted |-heal|p1b: Incineroar|65/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p1b: Incineroar|66/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`
- The difference between $65\%$ and $66\%$ on Incineroar is razor-thin:
  $$\frac{H + \lfloor M / 16 \rfloor}{M}$$
