   - When the defender's base stats and damage are exact integers, the attacker's Stat Points can be solved directly by inverting the damage formula:
     $$\text{Damage} = \left\lfloor \left( \frac{2 \times 50 / 5 + 2}{50} \times \text{Power} \times \frac{\text{Atk}}{\text{Def}} + 2 \right) \times \text{Mod} \times \text{Roll} \right\rfloor$$

## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Rounding Across Cascading Wave Crash Recoils
In Channel 0 (spectator view), both sides' HP values are concealed behind percentage strings:
$$\text{Display} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{currentHP} \times 100}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rceil\%$$
Basculegion uses Wave Crash on Turns 4, 5, and 6, knocking out three consecutive Pokémon.
Crucially, recoil damage in Showdown is defined as:
$$R_t = \left\lfloor \frac{\min(\text{Damage}_t, \text{Defender Current HP}_t)}{3} \right\rfloor$$
Basculegion's spectator percentage at Turn 6 is the cumulative result of three non-linear operations:
$$\text{HP}_6 = M_{\text{Basc}} - \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{Farig}, 4}}{3} \right\rfloor - \left\lfloor \frac{M_{\text{Char}}}{3} \right\rfloor - \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{Incin}, 6}}{3} \right\rfloor$$
Where:
- $\text{HP}_{\text{Farig}, 4} = M_{\text{Farig}} - \text{Damage}_{\text{Salamence Hyper Voice}}$
- $\text{HP}_{\text{Incin}, 6} = M_{\text{Incin}} - \text{Damage}_{\text{Hyper Voice}} + \text{Leftovers} - \text{Damage}_{\text{Hyper Voice}} + \text{Leftovers} - \text{Damage}_{\text{Flare Blitz crit}} + \text{Leftovers}$

Every single term in this equation depends on the interaction of unknown base/SP stats across **five different Pokémon** (`Basculegion`, `Farigiraf`, `Charizard`, `Incineroar P1`, `Incineroar P2`, `Salamence`). A drift of just **1 or 2 HP** anywhere in this chain causes Basculegion's Turn 6 recoil display to land on `58/100` instead of `57/100`.

### Root Cause 2: Inefficacy of 1-Dimensional Fallback Probes on Wave Crash Recoil
When the search reaches Turn 6 (line index 5), the guess produces:
```

```
`fallback` intercepts the failure and tests single-stat variations:
1. **Varying Basculegion's Attack**:
   - Because Farigiraf, Charizard, and Incineroar all faint (their current HP is strictly less than Wave Crash's full damage roll), Wave Crash recoil is capped at the defender's remaining HP.

### Root Cause 3: The Turn 0 Intimidate Speed-Tie Oscillation
