## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Every Restart Failed

### A. `speed.mjs` Skips Speed Order When the Faster Pokémon Flinches (`|cant|...|flinch`)

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2b: Charizard`'s exact `spe` SP (and `+1` Speed after Baton Pass) and `p2a: Raichu`'s exact `hp/maxhp` integers on Turns 1–3 are known ground-truth constants. Known `p2a: Raichu` HP removes the Turn 1 $\leftrightarrow$ Turn 2/3 backtracking ambiguity completely, and known `+1 Charizard` Speed prevents `p1a: Raichu-Mega-Y` from outspeeding it on Turn 4.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1a: Raichu`'s exact `spe` SP (`Raichu-Mega-Y`'s Speed) and `p1b: Incineroar`'s exact `hp/maxhp` are fixed constants. Furthermore, in `usage` / `default`, `fallback` on Turn 4 only needs `p2b: Charizard`'s `spe` against fixed `p1a: Raichu-Mega-Y` without any `p1a` vs `p2a` HP coupling stalling `base` on Turns 2–3.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/speed.mjs`, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Allow `fallback` to Proceed Using `dice` (`budgets[1]`) When `base` (`maxBacktracks: 0`) Stops Early (`infer.mjs:605–618`)**:
   In `infer.mjs:614`, checking `if (reach(base)[0] < t) return none();` aborts `fallback` whenever a prior turn needs even 1 backtrack (`maxBacktracks >= 1`) without `exact` — **even after `dice = rebuild(start, budgets[1])` was already run at line 610**! When `reach(dice)[0] >= t` (or if `base` is allowed `budgets[1]`'s backtracks when `maxBacktracks: 0` stops early), `fallback` can use `dice` as its baseline (`budget = budgets[1]`) and immediately probe `Charizard spe` / `Raichu spe` on Turn 4 instead of exiting with `0` probes and blacklisting Turn 4 in `spend.fruitless`.
2. **Extract Sound Priority-Bounded Speed Orders from Flinched Actions (`speed.mjs:552–614`)**:
   When `p2b: Charizard` printed `|cant|p2b: Charizard|flinch` before `p1a: Raichu-Mega-Y` used `Focus Blast` (`priority = 0`) in `usage` Round 6, `speed.mjs` emitted no speed constraint because `Charizard` never printed a `|move|` line (`shown.on === false`). However, `Charizard` had no moves in priority brackets `+1..+3` (only `Protect +4`, `Weather Ball 0`, `Heat Wave 0`, `Roar -6`), and `+4 Protect` would have executed before `Rillaboom`'s `+3 Fake Out`. Whenever a flinched/paralysed Pokémon `X` acts after a `+3` move (or has no legal move strictly between `0` and the preceding action's priority) and appears before a `0`-priority move `Y` in the same action queue, `X`'s effective Speed at `priority 0` is provably $\ge$ `Y`'s Speed — which would have preserved `Charizard spe >= 7` in `usage` Round 6 and prevented the Turn 6 $\to$ Turn 4 regression.
