## 6. Spectator Search Failure Analysis (`both@spectator`)

### The Fundamental Spectator Pathology
In `both@spectator` (Channel 0), neither side's true stats are known, and all protocol lines display two-digit integer percentages calculated by integer truncation:
$$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$

Under this transformation:

- Any current HP value in the interval $\left[ \lceil 0.62 \times \text{maxHP} \rceil, \lceil 0.63 \times \text{maxHP} \rceil - 1 \right]$ maps to `62/100`.
- For Sinistcha with max HP = 153:
  - $93 / 153 = 60.78\% \rightarrow 60/100$
  - $94 / 153 = 61.44\% \rightarrow \mathbf{61/100}$
  - $95 / 153 = 62.09\% \rightarrow \mathbf{62/100}$
  - $96 / 153 = 62.75\% \rightarrow 62/100$
