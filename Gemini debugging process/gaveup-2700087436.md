## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why Spectator Inference Gave Up

### 5.1 The Phantom Priority Trap: Protect vs Fainted Slot Move Insertion
In Pokémon Showdown, when a Pokémon is alive at the start of a turn but faints before executing its action, its chosen move is **completely invisible** in the battle log.
On Turn 7:
1. P1 actually selected `Hyper Voice` (priority 0).
2. Because Mega Froslass was faster (Speed 183 vs Speed 81), Froslass moved first with `Blizzard` and knocked Primarina out. Primarina never got to use `Hyper Voice`.
3. When `reconstruct.mjs` reconstructs P1's choice for Turn 7, it must assign a legal move to Primarina.
4. If the solver assigns `move protect` to Primarina, `Protect` has priority **+4**.
5. Consequently, the simulator executes `Protect` before Froslass's 0-priority `Blizzard`, producing `|move|p1a: Primarina|Protect...` and immediately failing against the expected Blizzard line.

### 5.2 The Fallback Speed Misattribution Pathology
When `infer.mjs` receives the mismatch:
```
wanted |move|p2a: Froslass|Blizzard|p1a: Primarina|[spread] p1a,p1b, got |move|p1a: Primarina|Protect|p1a: Primarina
```
It examines the two lines in `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`:
```javascript
const order = (who(diff.actual) && who(diff.expected) && who(diff.actual) !== who(diff.expected))
  || (kind(diff.actual) !== kind(diff.expected) && ACTION_START.has(kind(diff.actual)) && ACTION_START.has(kind(diff.expected)));
```
Because `who(diff.expected)` is `p2a: Froslass` and `who(diff.actual)` is `p1a: Primarina`:
1. The engine classifies the mismatch as an **execution order** error!
2. The engine immediately sorts search dimensions by Speed:
   ```javascript
   if (order) dims.sort((a, b) => (a.stat !== 'spe') - (b.stat !== 'spe'));
   ```
3. Fallback assumes that Primarina moved before Froslass because of a **Speed ordering failure** (i.e., that Primarina is faster than Froslass).
4. **However, this was a priority divergence, not a speed divergence!** `Protect` (+4 priority) will ALWAYS execute before `Blizzard` (0 priority), regardless of whether Primarina has 0 Speed or 1,000 Speed!

### 5.3 The Insoluble Multi-Turn Speed & Damage Coupling
Because fallback believes the Turn 7 failure is a Speed problem, it systematically alters Speed and related stats:
1. **Testing `Froslass spe 32, Primarina spe 0`**:
   - Lowering Primarina's Speed to 0 SP gives Primarina Speed **80**.
   - But on Turn 3, P2 Incineroar has Speed **72** (with 0 SP and Relaxed nature: base 60 + 20 = 80 × 0.9 = 72).
   - If Primarina's Speed is modified or Incineroar runs higher Speed, Incineroar moves before Primarina on Turn 3:
     `fallback on turn 7: stops at 3.3: wanted |move|p1a: Primarina|Calm Mind, got |move|p2a: Incineroar|Parting Shot`
2. **Testing `Froslass spe 32, Primarina spe 0, Incineroar spe 32`**:
   - Breaks Turn 1 Fake Out damage on Incineroar:
     `stops at 1.6: wanted |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|92/100, got |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|90/100`
3. **Testing `Froslass spe 32, Primarina spe 0, Incineroar spe 0`**:
   - Breaks Turn 4 Wood Hammer damage on Froslass:
     `stops at 4.4: wanted |-damage|p2a: Froslass|37/100, got |-damage|p2a: Froslass|26/100`
4. **Testing Primarina Bulk (`hp` and `def`)**:
   - Primarina surviving Turn 6 Wood Hammer at exactly 5/100 HP is a tight survival gate:
     - `Primarina hp 24` -> Primarina faints on Turn 6 (`wanted 5/100, got 0 fnt`).
     - `Primarina def 32` -> Rillaboom recoil drops to 75/100 (`wanted 71/100, got 75/100`).
     - `Primarina def 11` -> Rillaboom recoil becomes 72/100 (`wanted 71/100, got 72/100`).
5. **The Dead-End Loop**:
   Every fallback candidate that changes Speed or bulk either breaks Turn 1 damage, Turn 3 move order, Turn 4 recoil, Turn 5 Blizzard damage, or Turn 6 survival. And any candidate that successfully survives through Turn 6 still encounters the exact same Turn 7 Protect insertion failure!

### 5.4 The Evidence Truncation & Stalling Loop
1. Under `AGENTS.md` Rule 2: "Never remove a possibility the replay allows. A line a shortcut cannot read is set aside, never trusted, and a search that ran out of budget proves nothing."
2. Because rebuilds never got past Turn 7, `cutoffAt` truncated the verified log at Turn 6.
3. Turns 7 and 8 were never evaluated by `evidencePass`.
4. As a result, candidate bounds for all 12 Pokémon remained largely unpruned, leaving median spreads at **10^7.57** (~37 million spreads) and **0.0% exact stats**.
5. The search iterated through 90 rounds across all 4 search phases:
