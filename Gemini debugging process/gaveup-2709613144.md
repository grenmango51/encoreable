## 6. The Anatomy of Search Failure in `both@spectator`

In `both@spectator`, neither player's team is known (`known = [false, false]`). The search is forced to infer both sides simultaneously through Channel 0 (spectator view), where every HP value is rounded to an integer percentage. This exposes the inference engine to three tightly coupled mathematical traps:

### Trap 1: The Incineroar Speed-Order Inversion Dilemma (Turn 0 vs Turn 6)
Both players brought Incineroar (base Speed 60).
- **At Turn 0**: In the real replay, P1 Incineroar activates Intimidate **first**:
  ```
  wanted |-ability|p1b: Incineroar|Intimidate|boost, got |-ability|p2a: Incineroar|Intimidate|boost
  ```
  This requires $\text{Spe}_{\text{P1 Incineroar}} \ge \text{Spe}_{\text{P2 Incineroar}}$.
- **At Turn 6**: Both Incineroars execute `Parting Shot` (priority 0). In the wanted replay, P2 Incineroar executes Parting Shot **first**:
  ```
  wanted |move|p2a: Incineroar|Parting Shot|p1b: Charizard, got |move|p1a: Incineroar|Parting Shot|p1b: Charizard
  ```
  This requires $\text{Spe}_{\text{P2 Incineroar}} \ge \text{Spe}_{\text{P1 Incineroar}}$.
- In the mimic battle, both Incineroars were planted with **identical Speed stats** ($\text{Spe}_{\text{P1}} = \text{Spe}_{\text{P2}}$). The simulator resolved Turn 0 by rolling a 50/50 speed tie won by P1, and resolved Turn 6 by rolling a 50/50 speed tie won by P2.
- When the search engine generates candidate guesses:
  - If it guesses $\text{Spe}_{\text{P1}} > \text{Spe}_{\text{P2}}$, Turn 0 passes, but Turn 6 fails deterministically (`got |move|p1a: Incineroar|Parting Shot`).
  - If fallback attempts to fix Turn 6 by increasing P2 Incineroar's Speed ($\text{Spe}_{\text{P2}} > \text{Spe}_{\text{P1}}$), Turn 0 immediately fails:
    ```
    fallback on turn 6: Incineroar spe 32 - stops at 0.22: wanted |-ability|p1b: Incineroar|Intimidate|boost, got |-ability|p2a: Incineroar|Intimidate|boost
    ```
  - If it sets equal speed, any minor difference in earlier PRNG calls alters the seed ordinal at Turn 0 or Turn 6, flipping the tie.

### Trap 3: The Multi-Turn Coupled Fallback Oscillation (Turn 2 $\longleftrightarrow$ Turn 5/6)

   ```

   ```

$$\begin{aligned}

In `error_cases.json`, both `p1:2` Charizard and `p2:2` Volcarona are recorded with `"seen": false`, despite actively battling in the original replay:
1. In `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`, line 1183 defines:
   ```javascript
   seen: result.seen.has(id)
   ```
   where `result` is computed by calling `settle(built.inputLog, ...)` on the `built` output of the **very last round** before termination.
2. In the final restart phase (Phase 6, Round 19), the search failed on Turn 6 Grassy Terrain healing (`round 19 (search 4): reproduces through turn 5 - wanted |-heal|p1a: Rillaboom|97/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p1a: Rillaboom|98/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`).
3. In that final round's candidate guess, fallback failed to reconcile the move order and switch timing. As a result, `built.inputLog` terminated prematurely before Charizard and Volcarona entered the active battle slots in that simulation run.
4. Because neither Pokémon was registered as having switched in or moved in the final round's simulated `inputLog`, they were omitted from `result.seen`, resulting in the `seen: false` record artifact.
