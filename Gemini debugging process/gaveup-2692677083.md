## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Charizard`'s real low Speed (`spe <= 12`, `Speed <= 145`) is fixed as a known constant. When `order && disputed.size` (or `fallback`) tests `Froslass spe = 0` (`Pre-Mega 143` or `spe = 2` `Pre-Mega 145`), `Froslass` no longer loses the `1.0` Mega Evolution race to `Charizard`, immediately clearing both `1.0` and `1.9` and propagating through the rest of the battle.
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Froslass`'s real `spe <= 2` (`Pre-Mega 143..145`, `Mega 154..156`) and `Arcanine`'s `spe >= 30` (`154..156`) are known constants. Round 1 immediately stops at **`1.0`** (`wanted Froslass-Mega, got Charizard-Mega-Y`), which `fallback` solves in a single Speed step by dropping `Charizard spe` to `0` (`132 <= 143..145`), with `1.9` already satisfied by P2's known spreads.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Allow `escort()` to Handle Earlier Same-Turn Divergences (`infer.mjs` line 728)**:
   `escort()` currently exits immediately whenever `!(stop.turn < t)`, refusing to pair a second Pokémon's Speed when an unescorted Speed perturbation regresses to an **earlier line within the same turn** (such as `1.0` Mega Evolution order before `1.9` move order). Relaxing the guard to allow `stop.turn < t || (stop.turn === t && stop.index < diff.index)` (using `further(baseReach, reach(got.b))`) would let `escort()` move `Charizard spe` alongside `Froslass spe 0` on line 10 and solve Turn 1 in a single extra rebuild.
2. **Track Blocker Speeds When `order && disputed.size` Consumes Single-Stat `done` Signatures**:
   In `infer.mjs` lines 775–778 and 809–816, the `disputed` pair `[{Arcanine: 32}, {Charizard: 0}]` was added to `done` without updating `d.moved` on `Charizard spe`, causing the single-stat loop (`Charizard spe 0`) to skip probing via `done.has(signature)` and leaving `moved.length === 1` (`[Froslass spe]`) so the two-stat loop (`lines 833–845`) never ran. Recording `d.moved` (or testing `spe` pairs between `actor(diff.expected)/actor(diff.actual)` and the blocker named in any `1.0` early stop) would prevent two-Pokémon Speed chains across Mega Evolution from starving the pairwise fallback search.
