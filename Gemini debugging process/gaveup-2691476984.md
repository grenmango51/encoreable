## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Flooring Percentages Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Under the Champions ruleset, Showdown converts every HP display via `Math.floor(100 * hp / maxhp)` (with color tags at 20% and 50%).
- Neither side's maximum HP nor current HP is known.
- All 8 brought Pokémon (`p1:Gholdengo`, `p1:Charizard`, `p1:Incineroar`, `p1:Sinistcha`, `p2:Incineroar`, `p2:Rillaboom`, `p2:Dragapult`, `p2:Gengar`) enter the field by Turn 3.
- Every percentage display in Channel 0 is the floor of a fraction of three unknowns:
  $$\left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \left(M_{\text{def}} - \text{Damage}(Atk_{\text{atk}}, Def_{\text{def}}, \text{Roll})\right)}{M_{\text{def}}} \right\rfloor$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP)` map to the identical integer percentages (`95/100`, `87/100`, `86/100`, `68/100`, `55/100`, `28/100`, `52/100`, `45/100`), the initial evidence pass cannot eliminate spread candidates.

### Root Cause 2: Turn 0/1/2 Speed Tie Pathology Between Opposing Incineroars
- P1 Incineroar and P2 Incineroar share:
  - Base Speed: 60
  - Nature: Careful (+SpD, -SpA; neutral 1.0x on Speed)
  - Planted Stat Points: `spe: 0`
  - Actual Level 50 Speed: $60 + 0 + 20 = \mathbf{80}$.
- They are in an **exact Speed tie**:
  - **Turn 0**: P2 Incineroar wins the tie roll: P2 Intimidate triggers before P1 Intimidate (`0.22` in rebuild).
  - **Turn 1**: P1 Incineroar wins the tie roll: P1 Snarl executes before P2 Flare Blitz (`1.2` in rebuild).
  - **Turn 2**: P2 Incineroar wins the tie roll: P2 Flare Blitz executes before P1 Snarl (`2.2` in rebuild).
- In Round 2, the search's default guess had P2 Incineroar faster than P1 Incineroar, which failed Turn 1 move order.
- `fallback` on Turn 1 attempted single-stat Speed changes:
  - `Incineroar spe 32` (P1) stopped Turn 0 Intimidate order (`0.22`).
  - `Incineroar spe 32, Incineroar spe 32` (both at Spe = 112) reproduced Turn 1 via a speed-tie roll.
- But at Spe 32 (112), Incineroar's Speed tied with Rillaboom (Spe 112), which subsequently broke the end-of-turn Grassy Terrain healing order on Turn 2 (Round 5: `wanted |-heal|p2b: Rillaboom|94/100, got |-heal|p1b: Incineroar|92/100`).

### Root Cause 3: Highly Coupled Multi-Variable System on Turns 4–5
Between Turn 4 and Turn 5, P2 Incineroar and P1 Charizard interact across a non-linear chain of 7 floored percentages:
1. **Turn 4 Heat Wave on Incineroar**: $\lfloor 100(M_{\text{Inc}} - D_1) / M_{\text{Inc}} \rfloor = 68$
2. **Turn 4 Matcha Gotcha on Incineroar**: $\lfloor 100(M_{\text{Inc}} - D_1 - D_2) / M_{\text{Inc}} \rfloor = 55$
3. **Turn 4 Grassy Terrain on Incineroar**: $\lfloor 100(M_{\text{Inc}} - D_1 - D_2 + \lfloor M_{\text{Inc}}/16 \rfloor) / M_{\text{Inc}} \rfloor = 61$
4. **Turn 5 Weather Ball on Incineroar**: Must drop Incineroar to $\le 50\%$ HP: $\lfloor 100(H_3 - D_3) / M_{\text{Inc}} \rfloor = 28$
5. **Turn 5 Sitrus Berry Recovery on Incineroar**: $\lfloor 100(H_4 + \lfloor M_{\text{Inc}}/4 \rfloor) / M_{\text{Inc}} \rfloor = 52$
6. **Turn 5 Flare Blitz on Charizard**: $\lfloor 100(M_{\text{Char}} - D_{\text{FB}}) / M_{\text{Char}} \rfloor = 69$
7. **Turn 5 Flare Blitz Recoil on Incineroar**: $\lfloor 100(H_5 - \lfloor D_{\text{FB}}/3 \rfloor) / M_{\text{Inc}} \rfloor = 45$
8. **Turn 5 Grassy Terrain on Incineroar**: $\lfloor 100(H_6 + \lfloor M_{\text{Inc}}/16 \rfloor) / M_{\text{Inc}} \rfloor = 50\text{g}$

This system simultaneously couples:
$$\{ M_{\text{Inc}}, \text{SpD}_{\text{Inc}}, \text{Def}_{\text{Inc}}, \text{Atk}_{\text{Inc}}, M_{\text{Char}}, \text{SpA}_{\text{Char}}, \text{Def}_{\text{Char}}, \text{SpA}_{\text{Sini}} \}$$
- If a guess perturbs Incineroar's HP to fix the Sitrus Berry percentage (`52/100`), Turn 4 Heat Wave (`68/100`) or Matcha Gotcha (`55/100`) breaks.
- If a guess perturbs Charizard's Def or Incineroar's Atk to fix Flare Blitz damage on Charizard (`69/100`), Flare Blitz recoil on Incineroar (`45/100`) breaks.
- If a guess perturbs Weather Ball damage to fix Incineroar's HP before Sitrus Berry, Sitrus Berry triggers at the wrong threshold or overshoots `52/100`.

### Root Cause 4: Inefficacy of 1D/2D Fallback Probes in an 8-Variable Coupled Minimum
- When rebuilds stop on Turn 5 (at lines 8, 10, or 11):
  - Round 7: `wanted |-heal|p2b: Incineroar|52/100|[from] item: Sitrus Berry, got |-heal|p2b: Incineroar|53/100`
  - Round 8: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Charizard|69/100, got |-damage|p1a: Charizard|75/100`
  - Round 9: `wanted |-damage|p2b: Incineroar|45/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2b: Incineroar|42/100`
- `fallback` tests single-stat perturbations of the Pokémon named in the failing action:
  - Testing Charizard Def alone fails because Incineroar's Atk is wrong for recoil.
  - Testing Incineroar HP alone fails because Turn 4 percentages break.
  - Testing Incineroar Atk alone fails because Charizard HP/Def is wrong.
- `fallback` cannot evaluate simultaneous 5-dimensional coordinate shifts across opposing teams.
- Every perturbation fails, consuming `FALLBACK_REBUILDS = 8` rebuilds per failing line and quickly depleting `spend.rebuilds` (`FALLBACK_TOTAL = 32`).

### Root Cause 5: Combinatorial Explosion in Joint Guess Picker (`choose()`)
- With all 8 Pokémon active by Turn 3, `choose()` must recursively pin candidates for a sequence of 8 Pokémon (`seq.length = 8`).
- At each candidate pin, `choose()` calls `settle()`, which runs up to 8 passes of `evidencePass` simulating the battle.
- In Round 8, `evidence and new guesses` consumed **47.4 seconds and 29 evidence passes** on a single round.
- `tries` (budgeted at $32 \times S \times W = 32$) ran out before finding an exact proof for all 8 Pokémon, logging the `guessTries` limit and returning `proof = null`.

### Root Cause 6: Search Escalation, Cycling, and Stalling Across 339 Rounds
1. Because `choose()` could not find an exact proof and `fallback` returned `null`, `next` was picked via `closestSpread()`.
2. The guesses began cycling between previously visited signatures, invoking `mixOf()` to interpolate previously attempted spreads.
3. Because rebuilds never advanced past the Turn 4–5 barrier, `at` never surpassed `deepest`.
4. `stalled` incremented on every round until `stalled >= maxRounds` (16 rounds), causing the initial `default` start to give up with `stop = 'stalled'`.
5. `infer.mjs` initiated sequential restarts:
   - **`usage` start**: Began from Smogon usage spreads; stalled on the same Turn 4–5 barrier.
   - **`wide` start**: Multiplied fallback rebuilds and guess tries by $WIDE = 4$; stalled on the same barrier.
   - **`widest` start**: Multiplied every search limit and budget by 4 ($budgetScale = 4$, $maxRounds = 64$); re-ran `default`, `usage`, and `wide` with 64 rounds each.
6. The accumulated work across all restarts reached:
   - **339 rounds**
   - **1,307 rebuilds**
   - **3,270,255 probes**
   - **17,477.5 seconds (4.85 hours)**
   - Final stop reason: **`stalled`**
   - Limits hit: `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "fallbackTotal", "guessTries", "probes", "stalled"]`.

Because the search never advanced past Turn 5, Turns 6 through 10 (encompassing Dragapult's Dragon Darts, Gengar's Sludge Bombs, Sinistcha's Matcha Gotcha drains, Rillaboom's U-turns, Rocky Helmet damage, and late-game KOs) were never reached by a reproducing witness, leaving all active Pokémon with massive candidate spread pools.
