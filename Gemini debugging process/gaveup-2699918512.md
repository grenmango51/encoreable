## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Turn 1 `MaxHP` vs. `Wave Crash` Recoil Joint Constraint (`1.5`–`1.7`)
With `p2:5 Basculegion` at **`HP SP = 2` (`MaxHP = 197`)**:
1. **Life Orb recoil** is $\lfloor 197 / 10 \rfloor = 19$ HP.
2. **Post-Recoil `65/100` (`1.6`)** requires $129 \le h_{\text{recoil}} \le 130$ ($\lfloor 12900/197 \rfloor = 65$, $\lfloor 13000/197 \rfloor = 65$, $\lfloor 13100/197 \rfloor = 66$).
3. **Post-Life-Orb `55/100` (`1.7`)** requires $h_{\text{LO}} = h_{\text{recoil}} - 19 \le 110$ ($\lfloor 11000/197 \rfloor = 55$, whereas $\lfloor 11100/197 \rfloor = 56$).
4. Therefore **$h_{\text{recoil}} = 129$** and **`recoil = 197 - 129 = 68`**, which via `Math.round(damageDealt * 33 / 100) === 68` forces:
   $$\text{damageDealt}(\text{Wave Crash} \to \text{Kingambit}) \ge \mathbf{205}$$
   (since $204 \times 0.33 = 67.32 \to 67$, whereas $205 \times 0.33 = 67.65 \to 68$).
5. Because `p1:1 Kingambit` survived at **`1/100` (`1.5`)**, its `MaxHP` MUST satisfy $\text{MaxHP} \ge 205 + 1 = \mathbf{206}$, i.e., **`p1:1 Kingambit HP SP >= 31` (`31` or `32`)**!

Now examine what happens whenever `p1:1 Kingambit` is guessed at **`HP SP = 29` (`MaxHP = 204`)**:
- The maximum non-lethal damage `204 HP` `Kingambit` can take is **`203`**.
- On `203` damage, `Wave Crash` recoil is $\text{round}(203 \times 0.33) = \mathbf{67}$.
- On `197 HP` `Basculegion`, $197 - 67 - 19 = \mathbf{111 / 197} \to \lfloor 11100 / 197 \rfloor = \mathbf{56/100}$!
- **No `Basculegion` Attack stat and no `Kingambit` Defense stat can simultaneously satisfy `1.5` (`Kingambit 1/100`) and `1.7` (`Basculegion 55/100`) when `Kingambit` has `HP SP = 29` (`MaxHP = 204`) and `Basculegion` has `HP SP = 2` (`MaxHP = 197`)!**

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2:5 Basculegion`'s exact `MaxHP = 197` (`hp = 2`) and `Atk` are known ground-truth constants, and Channel 2 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers (`129/197` after recoil, `110/197` after Life Orb). - **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:1 Kingambit`'s exact `MaxHP` (`>= 206`), `Def`, and exact remaining HP (`1/206` or `2/207`) are directly visible in Channel 1, giving `damageDealt = MaxHP - hp_after >= 205` on Turn 1 immediately. ### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`, `knowledge.mjs`)
1. **Guard `replayWith` `same` Check Against Unpinned Damage Rolls (Turn 1 `1.7`)**:
   In `fallback` (`infer.mjs` line 677), `probe()` skips `rebuild()` whenever `replayWith(built.inputLog, ...)` matches `plain`. On Turn 1 here, `built.inputLog` carried a single unpinned roll (`203` damage), under which `1/204` (`hp 29`), `2/205` (`hp 30`), and `4/207` (`hp 32`) all printed `1/100`. Thus `Kingambit hp 30` and `hp 32` were falsely marked `same = true`, skipping `rebuild()` (which would have shifted the roll from `203` to `205` damage) and blocking `Kingambit hp` from entering `moved` and the 2-stat pair loop. Checking the failing hit's full 16-roll `getHealth` set—or bypassing `same` on `MaxHP` probes of any Pokémon participating in the failing action's recoil/drain chain—would let `fallback` resolve `1.7` immediately.
2. **Blame-Directed Backtracking / Cross-Pokémon `MaxHP`–`Recoil` Pruning in `choose()`**:
   In Round 9 (`Starts 1, 3, 4, 6`) and Round 5 (`Starts 2, 5`), `p1:1 Kingambit` (`hp = 29`) at `n = 0` was jointly incompatible with `p2:5 Basculegion` (`hp = 2`) at `n = 6`, yet `settle()` could not detect the cross-Pokémon `victim.MaxHP > dealt` vs. `attacker.recoil(dealt)` contradiction, so `choose()` spent all `32` / `128` / `512` `guessTries` below `n = 0` without ever flipping `p1:1 Kingambit`. Recording which Pokémon/turns caused `evidencePass(..., exact: true)` to return `null` (or propagating minimum `dealt` from recoil/drain onto the victim's `MaxHP` in `evidence.mjs`) would prevent `choose()` from starving under a bad root pin.
3. **Avoid `held` Anchoring Invalidated Early Fallback Spreads & Low-HP Tie-Breaking**:
   Round 2's Turn 1 fallback wrote `0/32/0/0/0/2` into `held` for `p1:1 Kingambit`; even after `hp = 0` was eliminated, `aimed()` kept aiming `closestSpread` at `hp = 0`, biasing `p1:1 Kingambit` to its minimum surviving `hp = 29`. Dropping or re-centering `held.get(id)` once `!containsSpread(kn, held.get(id))`, and breaking `closestSpread` ties toward `aim` / `sum = 66` rather than lowest `k` (`hp`), would avoid trapping `p1:1 Kingambit` at `hp = 29`.
