- No Pokémon displays exact HP numbers. Every damage, heal, drain, and recoil event is rounded by Showdown's `Math.ceil(currentHP * 100 / maxHP)`.
- 8 Pokémon were brought into the battle (4 per side), and 7 engaged in direct combat exchanges.
- Every damage equation contains 3 to 5 simultaneous unknown variables:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk}, \text{unknown Def}, \text{unknown Dice Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \text{rounded to } \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` produce identical rounded percentages in Channel 0, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads:
  - `p1:Blaziken`: 136,663,185 spreads (100.0% remaining)
  - `p1:Raichu`: 102,158,669 spreads (74.75% remaining)
  - `p2:Indeedee-F`: 57,803,987 spreads (42.30% remaining)
  - `p1:Rillaboom`: 34,002,905 spreads (24.88% remaining)
  - `p2:Archaludon`: 16,951,908 spreads (12.40% remaining)
  - `p1:Primarina`: 8,930,072 spreads (6.53% remaining)
  - `p2:Golisopod`: 286,540 spreads (0.21% remaining)

### Root Cause 2: Multi-Turn Cumulative HP Coupling on Golisopod-Mega (Turns 1-6)
Golisopod-Mega participates in a continuous, coupled sequence of 7 HP events across 6 turns:
1. Turn 1: Takes Zap Cannon (`48/100`) and Hyper Voice (`15/100 par`).
2. Turn 3: Deals +2 Leech Life on Primarina. Drains `ceil(damage / 2) = 78 HP` up to `58/100 par`.
   - In ground truth: Primarina has 157 HP and 94 Def. Golisopod deals 156 damage, leaving Primarina on exactly 1 HP (`1/100`).
   - If Golisopod deals 157 damage, Primarina faints (`0 fnt`), preventing Primarina's Hyper Voice and causing immediate branch divergence.
   - If Golisopod deals $\le 154$ damage, Primarina is left at $\ge 3$ HP (`2/100` or higher), failing the replay diff.
   - If Golisopod drains 77 or 79 HP, or enters Turn 3 with 27 or 29 HP (due to Turn 1 Zap Cannon / Hyper Voice rolls or Golisopod SpD), Golisopod's heal percentage is `57/100` or `59/100` instead of `58/100`.
3. Turn 3: Takes Hyper Voice (`26/100 par`).
4. Turn 4: Heals Grassy Terrain `floor(maxHP / 16) = 11 HP` (`32/100 par`).
5. Turn 5: Takes Wood Hammer from Rillaboom (`18/100 par`).
6. Turn 5: Heals Grassy Terrain 11 HP (`24/100 par`).
7. Turn 6: Takes Grassy Glide from Rillaboom (`18/100 par`).
   - In ground truth: $45 - 11 = 34 \text{ HP}$. Percentage: $34 / 182 = 18.68\% \rightarrow 18\%$.
   - If Golisopod enters Turn 6 with 44 HP, Grassy Glide leaves it at 33 HP ($33 / 182 = 18.13\% \rightarrow 19\%$ or $18\%$).
   - If Rillaboom deals 12 damage, Golisopod is left at $32 / 182 = 17.58\% \rightarrow 18\%$.
   - If Golisopod has an odd max HP (e.g. 181 or 183), Grassy Terrain heals 11 or 12, causing Turn 6 to yield `16/100` or `17/100`.
- In spectator rebuilds, candidate guesses systematically hit:
  - Round 6 diff: `wanted |-damage|p2b: Golisopod|18/100 par, got |-damage|p2b: Golisopod|16/100 par`
  - Round 9 diff: `wanted |-damage|p2b: Golisopod|18/100 par, got |-damage|p2b: Golisopod|17/100 par`

### Root Cause 3: Turn 7 Triple-Damage Cascade on Rillaboom
On Turn 7, Rillaboom experiences three consecutive HP reductions in a single turn:
1. Takes Dragon Pulse from Archaludon:
   - Entering HP: 127 HP (accumulated through Turn 4 recoil -5, Turn 4 terrain +5, Turn 5 recoil -8, Turn 5 Flash Cannon -66, Turn 5 terrain +11, Turn 6 terrain +11).
   - Dragon Pulse deals 69 HP: $127 - 69 = 58 \text{ HP}$.
   - Percentage: $58 / 179 = 32.40\% \rightarrow 32\%$.
   - Rebuilds without exact rolls hit Round 10 diff: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|32/100, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|26/100`.
2. Takes Rocky Helmet from Indeedee-F:
   - Damage: $\lfloor 177 / 6 \rfloor = 29 \text{ HP}$.
   - HP: $58 - 29 = 29 \text{ HP}$.
   - Percentage: $29 / 179 = 16.20\% \rightarrow 16\%$.
   - Rebuilds hit Round 11 diff: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|16/100, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|15/100`.
3. Takes Wood Hammer recoil from Indeedee-F:
   - Indeedee-F entered Turn 7 with 117 HP (from Turn 1 Hyper Voice).
   - Raichu's Focus Blast on Turn 7 dealt 74 damage, leaving Indeedee-F at 43 HP ($117 - 74 = 43 \text{ HP}$).
   - Wood Hammer KOes Indeedee-F. Recoil is $\lfloor 43 / 3 \rfloor = 14 \text{ HP}$.
   - Rillaboom HP: $29 - 14 = 15 \text{ HP}$.
   - Percentage: $15 / 179 = 8.379\% \rightarrow 8\%$.
   - Rebuilds hit Round 12 diff: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|8/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|9/100|[from] Recoil`.
- Any deviation in Raichu SpA, Primarina SpA, Indeedee maxHP/SpD, Archaludon SpA, or Rillaboom maxHP/SpD propagates directly into Turn 7 percentages.

### Root Cause 4: Turn 8 Weather Ball Survival and Turn 9 1-HP Recoil
1. Turn 8: Raichu survives Pelipper's Rain Weather Ball on 11 HP ($161 - 150 = 11 \text{ HP}$).
   - Percentage: $11 / 161 = 6.83\% \rightarrow 6\%$.

2. Turn 9: Rillaboom Wood Hammers Pelipper (at 1 HP):

   - Rebuilds hit Round 14 diff: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|7/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|8/100|[from] Recoil`.

### Root Cause 5: Failure of `choose()` Joint Guessing via `guessTries` Exhaustion
In `infer.mjs`:
1. When candidate spreads are selected between rounds, `choose(n, cond)` attempts to recursively assemble a joint set of spreads for the 7 active Pokémon.
2. The search budget for joint combinations is limited to:
   $$\text{tries} = 32 \times S \times W = 32$$
3. Because the active Pokémon have tens of millions of candidate spreads each ($34\text{M} \times 136\text{M} \times 8.9\text{M} \times 102\text{M} \times 57\text{M} \times 17\text{M} \times 286\text{K}$ combinations), and every partial pin is checked against `settle()`, `tries` decrements to 0 after exploring a tiny fraction of candidate permutations.
4. `choose()` exhausts `guessTries` and returns `null`.
5. When `choose()` returns `null`:
   - `exact` is cleared to `null`.
   - `pins` (handed-on dice) is cleared to `null`.
   - Each Pokémon's guess falls back to independent nearest-neighbor selection:
     ```javascript
     next[s][i] = closestSpread(kn, aimed(id, kn, picks[s][i])) || next[s][i];
     ```
6. Because uncoupled nearest-neighbor guesses fail the knife-edge thresholds on Turn 1, the rebuild collapses all the way back to Turn 0 / Turn 1 (e.g. Round 15: `reproduces through turn 0 - wanted |-damage|p2b: Golisopod|48/100, got |-damage|p2b: Golisopod|46/100`).

### Root Cause 6: Inefficacy of Single-Stat Fallback Rebuilds
When the search hits a recurring diff:
1. `fallback(seen, start, ...)` attempts single-stat variations (`probe(changes)`) on the Pokémon named in the failing turn.
2. For Turn 6 Golisopod diff (`18/100 par`):
   - Probing `Golisopod hp 25`: causes Turn 3 Leech Life drain to diverge (`stops at 3.4: wanted |-heal|p2b: Golisopod|58/100, got 60/100`).
   - Probing `Golisopod hp 32`: causes Turn 5 Flash Cannon to diverge (`stops at 5.11: wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|58/100, got 55/100`).
   - Probing `Golisopod atk 19`: causes Turn 3 Leech Life to fail (`stops at 3.3: wanted |-damage|p1a: Primarina|1/100, got 15/100`).
   - Fallback reports `nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`.
3. For Turn 3 Primarina diff (`1/100`):
   - Probing Primarina `hp 0`, `def 0`, `def 7`, `spa 13`, `spa 32`: each variation either fails Turn 3 Leech Life (`got 10/100`, `12/100`, `15/100`) or breaks Turn 1/2 Hyper Voice (`stops at 1.9`, `stops at 2.8`).
   - Fallback reports `nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`.
4. Single-stat adjustments cannot resolve multi-turn cumulative discrepancies. Fallback repeatedly returns `null` and depletes `fallbackTotal`.

### Root Cause 7: Search Stalling and Restart Escalation
1. Consecutive rounds fail to advance `deepest` beyond Turn 6 / Turn 7 / Turn 8 / Turn 9.
2. `stalled` reaches `maxRounds` (16), triggering `stop = 'stalled'`.
3. `inferSpreads` escalates through sequential restarts:
   - Restart 1: `start: 'usage'` -> stalls again at Turn 6/7/8/9.
   - Restart 2: `wide: true` (4x fallback rebuild budget, `FALLBACK_REBUILDS = 32`) -> spends hundreds of additional fallback rebuilds, depletes `fallbackTotal`, stalls again.
   - Restart 3: `widest: true` (4x search budget scale, `maxProbes: 2400`, `maxBacktracks: 24`) -> runs massive backtracking tree searches across millions of dice probes.
4. Total execution accumulated **320 rounds, 1,026 rebuilds, 4,226,865 probes**, and **19,011.6 seconds (~5.28 hours)** before terminating with `GAVE UP` (`stop = 'stalled'`).

   - Triggered when a single fallback invocation spent all allotted rebuilds (`spent >= FALLBACK_REBUILDS * S * W`) without finding a spread that reproduced the failing line.

   - Hit in `infer.mjs` line 485 / 506.
