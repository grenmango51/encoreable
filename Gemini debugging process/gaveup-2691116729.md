## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Rounding Across 7 Active Combatants
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Every HP value is truncated: $\lfloor 100 \times \text{currentHP} / \text{maxHP} \rfloor$.
- Across the 6 turns, 7 Pokémon engage in combat:
  - P1: Staraptor, Sinistcha, Grapploct, Garganacl
  - P2: Raichu, Rillaboom, Incineroar
- For every attack, recoil, drain heal, and status damage event, the attacker's stat, the defender's stat, the damage roll, and the defender's max HP are all simultaneously unknown.
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP)` map to the same rounded percentage integer, candidate pools cannot be collapsed by single-pass elimination.

6. `p1b` Sinistcha drain 1 heal ($\to 69/100$).

**The Drain 2 Arithmetic Constraint**:
- Sinistcha's HP after drain 1 is 124 HP.
- To display `78/100`, Sinistcha's HP must reach:
  $$\lfloor 100 \times \text{HP} / 178 \rfloor \ge 78 \iff \text{HP} \ge 139$$
- Therefore, drain 2 ($\lfloor D_{\text{incin}} / 2 \rfloor$) must be at least $139 - 124 = 15$ HP, requiring $D_{\text{incin}} \ge 30$ damage.
- Under Doubles spread reduction (base power $60 \times 0.75 = 45$), Matcha Gotcha deals between 26 and 31 damage to Incineroar. Only the top 2 out of 16 damage rolls ($\ge 30$) yield 15 HP drain.
- Combined with the 20% burn chance on Rillaboom, the joint probability of hitting both the burn and the high drain roll is:
  $$0.20 \times \frac{2}{16} = 0.025 \quad (1 \text{ in } 40)$$

**Verification with True Spreads**:

- At `maxProbes: 150`, under `sampleSeed: 1` (the seed passed by the scorer), `reconstruct` fails 100% of the time at Turn 4 index 16:

### Root Cause 3: The Search Level Reset Trap (`level = floor`)
1. In `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`, search level budgets progress through:
   - Level 0: `maxProbes: 150`
   - Level 1: `maxProbes: 600`
   - Level 2: `maxProbes: 4000`
2. `level` advances **only** when `unchanged` is true (line 1102):
   ```javascript
   if (unchanged) {
     if (++level >= budgets.length) { stop = 'searches'; break; }
   } else {
     level = floor;
   }
   ```
3. In `both@spectator`, because 7 active Pokémon retain millions of candidate spreads, evidence passes from earlier turns shift the candidate picks on almost every round (`unchanged = false`).
4. Every time `unchanged` is false, `infer.mjs` resets `level` to `floor = 0` (`maxProbes: 150`).
5. As a result, every new guess is rebuilt with `maxProbes: 150`. Under `sampleSeed: 1`, `maxProbes: 150` is mathematically incapable of finding the Turn 4 dice sequence, causing the rebuild to halt at Turn 4 index 16.
6. The inference interprets this rebuild failure as an incorrect guess, triggering further evidence shifts and fallback probes.

### Root Cause 4: The Turn 3 Hospitality Fallback Exhaustion Trap

### Root Cause 5: Recoil and Knockout Coupling on Turn 3
1. On Turn 3, Rillaboom KOes Grapploct with Wood Hammer. In Showdown (`sim/battle.ts`), recoil on a knockout is:
   $$\text{recoil} = \lfloor \min(\text{target.hp}, \text{damage}) / 3 \rfloor$$
2. Because Grapploct faints, Rillaboom's recoil is strictly $\lfloor \text{Grapploct's remaining HP} / 3 \rfloor$.
3. When Grapploct's post-Hospitality HP deviates by as little as 2 HP, Rillaboom's recoil changes from 26 to 27 HP, altering Rillaboom's percentage from `79/100` to `80/100`.

## 7. Diagnostic Facts Summary
1. Single-view inferences (`p1@p2` in 9.2s, `p2@p1` in 37.5s) resolved rapidly because the observer's exact integer HP is revealed on every line, exact recoil values on Turns 1, 3, and 6 directly decouple attacker Attack and defender Defense/HP, and observer speeds are known constants.
