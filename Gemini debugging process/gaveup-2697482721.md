### The 56% vs. 55% Rough Skin Rounding Chasm

At Turn 4 line 4.5 (and Turn 3 line 3.4), the simulation encountered a persistent 1% discrepancy:
$$\text{Wanted: } \texttt{|-damage|p2a: Garchomp|56/100|[from] ability: Rough Skin|[of] p1b: Garchomp}$$
$$\text{Got: } \texttt{|-damage|p2a: Garchomp|55/100|[from] ability: Rough Skin|[of] p1b: Garchomp}$$

#### Mathematical Origin in Simulator Mechanics
In Pokémon Showdown:
1. **Rough Skin Damage Formula**:
   $$\text{damage} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{target.maxhp}}{8} \right\rfloor$$
   Rough Skin deals exactly $\frac{1}{8}$ of the attacker's maximum HP.
2. **Spectator Percentage Quantization**:
   In spectator view (channel 0), the protocol emits:
   $$\text{pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{hp}}{\text{maxhp}} \right\rfloor$$
   For Garchomp at Level 50 in Champions Reg M-C:
   $$\text{Base HP} = 108 \implies \text{maxhp} = 108 + \text{SP} + 75 = 183 + \text{SP} \quad (\text{SP} \in [0, 32])$$
   - If $\text{SP} = 0 \implies \text{maxhp} = 183$: $\lfloor 183 / 8 \rfloor = 22$ damage ($12.02\%$).
   - If $\text{SP} = 2 \implies \text{maxhp} = 185$: $\lfloor 185 / 8 \rfloor = 23$ damage ($12.43\%$).
3. **The 1% Flip**:
   Depending on whether Garchomp's HP before the contact hit was at an odd or even integer value, subtracting 22 vs 23 HP shifts the integer percentage across the $0.56$ threshold:
   $$\left\lfloor \frac{100 \times 103}{185} \right\rfloor = 55\% \quad \text{vs} \quad \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times 104}{185} \right\rfloor = 56\%$$
   A difference of just 1 HP in Garchomp's initial bulk or previous damage rolls caused candidate guesses to land at 55/100 instead of 56/100.

### The Turn 5 Metal Burst Multi-Stat Coupling Deadlock

At Turn 5 line 5.16, the simulation stalled attempting to reproduce the mutual damage exchange between Dragonite and Bastiodon:
$$\text{Wanted: } \texttt{|-damage|p1a: Dragonite|35/100}$$
$$\text{Got: } \texttt{|-damage|p1a: Dragonite|34/100} \quad (\text{or } 32/100, 33/100, 36/100)$$

#### The Metal Burst Reflection Dependency
On Turn 5, Dragonite attacked Bastiodon (line 5.7 dealing damage down to `18/100`, and line 5.14 down to `2/100`). Bastiodon then executed **Metal Burst**:
$$\text{Metal Burst Damage} = \lfloor 1.5 \times \text{Damage Taken That Turn} \rfloor$$
This creates a direct, rigid algebraic coupling:
$$\Delta \text{HP}_{\text{Dragonite}} = \lfloor 1.5 \times (\text{HP}_{\text{Bastiodon, start}} - \text{HP}_{\text{Bastiodon, end}}) \rfloor$$
In spectator view, this interaction couples four separate stat parameters simultaneously:
1. Bastiodon's Special Defense ($\text{SpD}$) and HP ($\text{maxhp}_{\text{Bastiodon}}$).
2. Dragonite's Special Attack ($\text{SpA}$) and damage dice roll (1 of 16 values).
3. Bastiodon's Chople Berry activation and damage reduction.
4. Dragonite's HP ($\text{maxhp}_{\text{Dragonite}}$) and Special Defense ($\text{SpD}$).

In Phase 5, the inference engine ran 32 consecutive single-stat perturbation rebuilds on Turn 5 (consuming 440.5s in round 9 and 381.6s in round 10). Every single perturbation was systematically rejected:

### Why the Inference Engine Gave Up
Because Metal Burst multiplies incoming damage by $1.5\times$, matching the exact spectator percentage on Dragonite (`35/100`) while preserving Bastiodon's exact survival percentages (`18/100` and `2/100`) requires **simultaneously adjusting Dragonite's SpA and Bastiodon's HP/SpD in coordinated lockstep**.

Every single-stat perturbation either caused Bastiodon to faint prematurely (`0 fnt`), deal the wrong reflected damage to Dragonite (`32/100`, `33/100`, `34/100`), or propagate backwards to invalidate Turn 2 or Turn 3 damage thresholds.
