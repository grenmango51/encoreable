By Round 8, the search found spreads where Archaludon survived Turn 1 and both Pelipper and Archaludon fainted on Turn 2, allowing the simulation to reproduce through Turn 2 and reach Turn 3 (`reproduces through turn 2`).

However, the search immediately collided with an impassable numerical discrepancy on Turn 3:
```text
round 9: reproduces through turn 2 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Indeedee|77/100, got |-damage|p1b: Indeedee|75/100
round 10: reproduces through turn 2 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Indeedee|126/163, got |-damage|p1b: Indeedee|122/163
```
1. **The Hand-off Pinning Effect**:
   - In Round 9, the verified state through Turn 2 was compared in percentage format (`wanted 77/100, got 75/100`).
   - In Round 10, because the evidence engine identified an exact HP path through Turn 2 for Indeedee with an initial guess of 18 HP SP ($70 + 18 + 75 = 163$ max HP), Indeedee's remaining HP was pinned at $126/163$ ($126/163 = 77.3\% \rightarrow 77/100$).
   - The rebuild simulator evaluated Scizor-Mega's Brutal Swing damage against this candidate and yielded $122/163$ ($122/163 = 74.8\% \rightarrow 75/100$).
2. **The Numerical Arithmetic Gap**:
   - In ground truth, Indeedee has max HP 177 (32 HP SP) and Def 117 (32 Def SP, Relaxed nature).

   - Brutal Swing (BP 60, spread 0.75x, Dark vs Psychic 2x, Colbur Berry 0.5x) inflicts 39 damage:
     $$177 - 39 = 138 \text{ HP remaining} \implies \left\lfloor \frac{138}{177} \times 100 \right\rfloor = 77\%$$
   - In the rebuild's candidate guesses, Indeedee was assigned only 18 HP SP (max HP 163) or lower Defense (e.g. Def 21). At lower Defense and lower max HP, Brutal Swing deals 41–42 damage, leaving $121/163 \dots 122/163$, which rounds down to **74% or 75%**!

### Root Cause 4: The Turn 2 / Turn 3 Brutal Swing Target Disruption Knife-Edge
When `fallback on turn 3` attempted to resolve this 77/100 mismatch by testing single-stat perturbations on Indeedee's Defense (trace lines 449–458):

2. **The Target Re-Aiming Mismatch**:

   - When Indeedee's Defense was increased to Def $\ge 29$, the altered damage allocations caused Gardevoir to take less damage or survive Brutal Swing on Turn 2 with 1–2 HP remaining!
   - Because Gardevoir survived Turn 2, Gardevoir remained active on the field in slot `p1a` entering Turn 3, and Torkoal was never brought in.
   - On Turn 3, when Scizor-Mega executed Brutal Swing, `MimicPlayer.aim()` looked for Torkoal (the intended target in the replay). Because Torkoal was not on the field, `aim()` fell back to random sampling among standing foes:
