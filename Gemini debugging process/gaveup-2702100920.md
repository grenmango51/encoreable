## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- No Pokémon displays exact HP numbers. Every damage event, Leftovers recovery tick, Grassy Terrain tick, and recoil is reported as `Math.floor(100 * hp / maxhp) || 1`.
- 8 Pokémon saw active combat engagement (Basculegion, Pelipper, Raichu, Archaludon on P1; Milotic, Raichu, Rillaboom, Gholdengo on P2).
- Every single exchange couples unknown attacker stats with unknown defender stats and unknown defender max HP:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown SpA/Atk}, \text{unknown SpD/Def}, \text{Dice Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lfloor \cdot \rfloor\%$$
- Because many combinations of `(SpA, SpD, maxHP, roll)` produce the same rounded percentage in Channel 0, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- As seen in Section 2:
  - Benched Pokémon retained all **136,663,185** spreads (100.0%).
  - Gholdengo retained **51,930,149** spreads (38.00%) because its active presence was limited to two turns.
  - Pelipper retained **37,849,808** spreads (27.70%) due to mostly using Wide Guard, Helping Hand, or sleeping.
  - Rillaboom, P2 Raichu, Basculegion, Raichu, and Milotic retained between 326,486 and 4.57 million candidate spreads.

### Root Cause 2: Deep Multi-Turn Compounding of Leftovers, Terrain, and Stat Boosts
The match involves an exceptionally dense web of persistent multi-turn status modifiers across 12 turns:
1. **Continuous Leftovers Recovery**:
   - `p2a` Milotic holds Leftovers: recovers $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$ every turn from Turn 1 to Turn 12 (12 consecutive Leftovers ticks).
   - `p1b` Archaludon holds Leftovers: recovers $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$ every turn from Turn 7 to Turn 12 (6 consecutive Leftovers ticks).
   - In spectator percentages, $\lfloor 12 / 202 \times 100 \rfloor = 5\%$ or $6\%$. A 1-HP difference in maxHP changes recovery ticks across all 12 turns.
2. **Grassy Terrain Cycles**:
   - Grassy Terrain was set twice: Turn 1 (lasted Turns 1-5) and Turn 8 (lasted Turns 8-12).
   - Every grounded Pokémon (Milotic, Rillaboom, Gholdengo, Archaludon, Raichu, Basculegion) healed $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$ at the end of every active turn.
3. **Compound Stat Stage Multipliers**:
   - `p2a` Milotic accumulated **Competitive boosts (+6 SpA)** from Snarl unboosts and Muddy Water accuracy drops, plus **Coil boosts (+2 Atk, +2 Def, +2 Acc)**.
   - `p1b` Archaludon accumulated **Stamina boosts (+5 Def)** from Muddy Water, Zap Cannon, and Grassy Glide hits.
   - `p2b` Gholdengo used Nasty Plot (+2 SpA).
4. Because damage calculations multiply by fractional stat stage modifiers ($(2 + \text{stage}) / 2$), any minute error in estimated base stats is amplified up to $\times 4.0$ by stage multipliers in late turns.

### Root Cause 3: The Turn 12 Double-Critical Muddy Water Razor
On Turn 12, the battle reaches its climax:
1. Ground Truth Sequence:
   - `p2b` Gholdengo uses Nasty Plot (+2 SpA).
   - `p2a` Milotic (+5 SpA) uses Muddy Water on Basculegion (deals 96 HP down to `82/227`, `36/100`) and Archaludon (deals 61 HP down to `32/197`, `16/100 par`).
   - `p1a` Basculegion uses Muddy Water:
     - Lands a critical hit on `p2a` Milotic, dealing 34 HP down to `128/202` (`63/100`).
     - Lands a critical hit on `p2b` Gholdengo, dealing 94 HP down to `6/177` (`3/100`).
   - `p1b` Archaludon uses Snarl:
     - Hits Gholdengo for 6+ HP, fainting Gholdengo (`0 fnt`).
     - Hits Milotic for 14 HP down to `114/202` (`56/100`).
2. Why Spectator Guesses Fail at Turn 12:
   - For Gholdengo to display `3/100` before fainting to Snarl:
     $$\lfloor 100 \times \text{HP}_{\text{after Muddy Water}} / \text{maxHP}_{\text{Gholdengo}} \rfloor = 3$$
   - In ground truth, Gholdengo had entered Turn 11 with 89 HP, healed 11 HP from Grassy Terrain to 100 HP, and took exactly 94 critical damage from Basculegion's Muddy Water, leaving exactly 6 HP ($6 / 177 = 3.39\% \rightarrow 3/100$).
   - In candidate guesses across Rounds 21-24:
     - Guesses frequently produced:
       `wanted |-damage|p2b: Gholdengo|3/100, got |-damage|p2b: Gholdengo|5/100`
     - If Gholdengo's estimated SpD or HP is slightly higher, Muddy Water leaves 8 or 9 HP ($8 / 177 = 4.52\% \rightarrow 4/100$ or $9 / 177 = 5.08\% \rightarrow 5/100$).

### Root Cause 4: Inefficacy and Destructive RNG Feedback of Fallback Rebuilds
When Round 21 stopped on Turn 12 with `wanted 3/100, got 5/100`:
1. `fallback(seen, start, ...)` attempted single-stat perturbations on the active cast (Gholdengo, Basculegion, Milotic, Archaludon).
2. Look at lines 49-51 of `trace_2702100920.log`:
   - `fallback on turn 12: |-damage|p2b: Gholdengo|3/100 - the guess alone (Gholdengo 7/0/0/32/9/18, Basculegion 0/16/8/30/11/1, Milotic 32/15/8/0/5/6, Archaludon 29/3/0/3/24/7, Raichu 5/7/2/24/0/15) does not, 7.0s`
   - `fallback on turn 12: Gholdengo hp 0 - stops at 12.15: wanted |move|p1b: Archaludon|Snarl|p2a: Milotic|[spread] p2a,p2b, got |-unboost|p2a: Milotic|accuracy|1, 13.4s`
3. Why did perturbing Gholdengo's HP cause `got |-unboost|p2a: Milotic|accuracy|1`?
   - Muddy Water has an 85% accuracy check and a 30% chance to drop accuracy on each target.
   - Showdown's PRNG consumes dice for:
     1. Move hit / miss roll for Target A (Milotic).
     2. Move hit / miss roll for Target B (Gholdengo).
     3. Critical hit check for Target A.
     4. Damage roll for Target A.
     5. Secondary effect chance (accuracy drop) for Target A.
     6. Critical hit check for Target B.
     7. Damage roll for Target B.
     8. Secondary effect chance (accuracy drop) for Target B.
   - When Gholdengo's HP reached 0 early or when stat perturbations altered whether Gholdengo fainted before Snarl, the ordinal PRNG dice sequence desynchronized!
   - In ground truth, Muddy Water did NOT drop Milotic's accuracy on Turn 12.
   - Under the perturbed candidate spread, the desynchronized PRNG triggered Muddy Water's secondary accuracy drop on Milotic!
   - When Milotic's accuracy dropped:
     `|-unboost|p2a: Milotic|accuracy|1`
     `|-ability|p2a: Milotic|Competitive|boost`
     `|-boost|p2a: Milotic|spa|2`
   - This protocol line was completely unexpected by the replay verifier:
     `wanted |move|p1b: Archaludon|Snarl|p2a: Milotic|[spread] p2a,p2b, got |-unboost|p2a: Milotic|accuracy|1`
4. In Round 24 (search 4):
   - A different perturbation caused Muddy Water to roll an accuracy drop on Gholdengo:
     `wanted |move|p1b: Archaludon|Snarl|p2a: Milotic|[spread] p2a,p2b, got |-unboost|p2b: Gholdengo|accuracy|1`
   - Gholdengo has Good as Gold, but the dice roll was executed before the ability check blocked it, producing protocol divergence.
5. In Stage 2 (`usage` start), fallback on Turn 3 encountered another bottleneck on Milotic's Turn 1 Leftovers and Turn 1 Muddy Water damage (`stops at 1.19: wanted 86/100, got 85/100` and `stops at 1.7: wanted 74/100, got 79/100`), where "nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds".

### Root Cause 5: Search Restart Escalation and Exhaustion of Limits
1. In `infer.mjs`:
   - Every round that failed to advance the deepest turn incremented `stalled`.
   - When `stalled >= maxRounds` (or when fallback searches on Turn 12 failed to find any candidate satisfying the double-crit razor), the search gave up and escalated:
     - Stage 1: `default` start gave up after 24 rounds, hitting search 4 on Turn 12.
     - Stage 2: `usage` start gave up after 22 rounds, hitting search 4 on Turn 12.
     - Stage 3: `wide` restart (4x fallback rebuilds and joint guess tries) ran 24 rounds, stalled on Turn 12, and gave up.
     - Stage 4: `widest` restart (all limits scaled 4x) ran 48 rounds across default and usage starts, again stalling on Turn 12.
2. In the full sweep run, the process accumulated **142 rounds, 208 rebuilds, 135,747 probes**, and ran for **2,643.8 seconds** before terminating with `stop="searches"`.
3. It collected 5 search limits: `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches", "variants"]`.

## 7. Summary of Diagnosis

1. **Why Single-View Resolved Rapidly**:
   - In `p2@p1` (47.9s) and `p1@p2` (66.9s), the observer's exact integer HP values and known stats on one side decoupled all damage equations.
   - P1's exact bulk directly pinned P2 Milotic's, Raichu's, and Rillaboom's offensive stats, while P2's exact defenses directly isolated P1 Basculegion's and Raichu's Special Attack.
   - Speed order unambiguously pinned exact Speed on both P1 Raichu (178) and P1 Basculegion-F (100). Both single views succeeded with 0 fallback rebuilds.

2. **Why Spectator View Stalled and Gave Up**:
   - Universal percentage masking (`Math.floor(100 * hp / maxhp)`) across 8 active combatants left millions of candidate spreads surviving per Pokémon (including 51.9M on Gholdengo and 37.8M on Pelipper).
   - Combat state was deeply coupled across 12 turns via Rain, 2 Grassy Terrain cycles, 18 Leftovers ticks, 6 Stamina defense boosts, 6 Competitive special attack boosts, and 2 Coil boosts.
   - On Turn 12, a high-precision damage razor occurred: Basculegion landed double critical hits on both Milotic and Gholdengo, dropping Gholdengo to exactly 3/100 HP before Archaludon's Snarl KO.
   - Candidate guesses consistently mispredicted Gholdengo's remaining HP as 5/100.
   - Perturbations attempted by `fallback` on Turn 12 shifted the PRNG dice stream, triggering Muddy Water secondary accuracy drops on Milotic (`got |-unboost|p2a: Milotic|accuracy|1`) or Gholdengo (`got |-unboost|p2b: Gholdengo|accuracy|1`), causing immediate protocol desynchronization.
   - The search exhausted all restart stages (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, `widest`), hitting the maximum search restart limit (`stop="searches"`) after 2,643.8 seconds and 135,747 probes.
