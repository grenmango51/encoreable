## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All 3 Views Stalled at Turn 6 (`6.2`) & Why Fallback Cycled

### A. Primary Bug: `planSegment()` Discards `[from] lockedmove` When `Encore` Lands Earlier in the Same Turn (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
In `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `planSegment()` parses each turn's protocol lines to build `plan.actions[side][slot]`, and `recoverChargeTargets(plans)` links two-turn charging moves (like `Electro Shot||[still]` on Turn 5) to their execution turn (Turn 6) so `reconstruct()` knows both the preferred target (`+2`, `p1b: Annihilape`) and which earlier turn carried a target into Turn 6 (`carriedInto(plans, 6)`):

```js
// scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs lines 170-183
if (kind === '-start' && /Encore$/.test(parts[3] || '')) {
  encored.add(parts[2]);
  continue;
}

if (kind === 'move') {
  if (toID(parts[3]) === 'revivalblessing') reviver = { slot: identSlot(parts[2]) };
  if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;
  const from = tagsOf(line).from;
  if (from && from !== 'lockedmove') continue;
  ...
```

Because `p1a: Whimsicott` used Prankster `Encore` on `p2b: Archaludon` on Turn 6 (`6.0`–`6.1`) **before** `Archaludon` released its Turn 5 charged `Electro Shot` (`6.2`):
1. Line `6.1` (`|-start|p2b: Archaludon|Encore`) added `'p2b: Archaludon'` to `encored`.
2. Line `6.2` (`|move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot|p1b: Annihilape|[from] lockedmove`) hit **`if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;` at line 177** before checking `from === 'lockedmove'`, so `plans[6].actions.p2[1]` remained **`undefined`**.
3. Consequently, `recoverChargeTargets(plans)` (lines 208–225) scanned `plans.slice(6)` for a matching `electroshot` on `p2` slot `1`, found **nothing**, and left `plans[5].actions.p2[1]` with **`target: null`** and **`landsOn: undefined`**.
4. **First Consequence (Wrong Turn 5 Variant)**: On Turn 5, `choiceFor()` had `preferred = null`, so `targetOptions(side, 1, 'normal', null)` returned `[' +1', ' +2', ' -1']`. Variant `0` (`variants[5] = 0`) targeted **`+1` (`p1a: Whimsicott`)** instead of **`+2` (`p1b: Annihilape`)**. Because `Electro Shot||[still]` prints no target on the charge turn, variant `0` matched Turn 5 completely and never tried `variants[5] = 1` (`+2`).
5. **Second Consequence (Dead `carriedInto` Recovery on Turn 6)**: When Turn 6 failed at `6.2` (`wanted p1b: Annihilape, got p1a: Whimsicott`), `reconstruct()` called `carriedInto(plans, 6)` (lines 228–234, 2528) to re-walk the variants of earlier turns that carried a choice into Turn 6. Because `landsOn` was `undefined`, `carriedInto(plans, 6)` returned **`[]`**. And on Turn 6 itself, `Archaludon`'s `lockedmove` request offered only `move 1` with no target (`width = 1`), so Turn 6 had no choice variants either.
6. **Third Consequence (Useless Die Backtracking)**: Unable to flip `variants[5]`, `reconstruct()` entered `redrawTurn` (`hpFigure = false`, `strict = true`), probed Turns 5 down to 0 for any die that changed `6.2`'s target, found none, and terminated with **`stop = 'stuck'`**.

Crucially, this bug depends **only on the protocol log (`unmasked.lines`)**, not on Stat Points or HP visibility—which is why **`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator` all failed identically** in the cluster sweep.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why All Three Views (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator`) Failed Identically
Unlike damage-parity or HP-rounding stalls (where knowing one side's exact `MaxHP` and seeing exact integer HP breaks the deadlock), this failure is **100% independent of Stat Points and HP precision**:
- In `p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator` alike, `planSegment()` runs on the public move/effect sequence of Turn 6, sees `|-start|p2b: Archaludon|Encore` before `|move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot|p1b: Annihilape|[from] lockedmove`, and discards `Electro Shot` from `plans[6].actions.p2[1]`.
- Even with every Stat Point on one side known (`p1@p2` or `p2@p1`), Turn 5 defaults to targeting `Whimsicott` (`+1`) and Turn 6 has no `carriedInto` link back to Turn 5, guaranteeing a `stuck` reconstruction at `6.2` in every view.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` & `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Discard `[from] lockedmove` Lines Under `encored` in `planSegment()` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 177–183)**:
   When a Pokémon is Encored earlier in the same turn that it releases a two-turn locked move (`|move|...|Electro Shot|p1b: Annihilape|[from] lockedmove`), `Encore` does **not** replace the executing move or target—the move was already locked on Turn `t - 1`. Checking `const from = tagsOf(line).from;` *before* `encored.has(parts[2])` (i.e. `if (encored.has(parts[2]) && from !== 'lockedmove') continue;`, or recording `lockedmove` target metadata for `recoverChargeTargets`) allows `recoverChargeTargets()` to recover `target = 'p1b: Annihilape'` and `landsOn = 6`, immediately resolving Turn 5's `Electro Shot` target on the very first attempt (`+2`) and enabling `carriedInto(plans, 6)` if redirected.
2. **Avoid Wasting Fallback Rebuilds on Target-Only `[from] lockedmove` Mismatches**:
   Once `recoverChargeTargets` preserves `[from] lockedmove` lines under `Encore`, Turn 6 will resolve directly inside `reconstruct()`. More broadly, whenever `diff.expected` and `diff.actual` differ only in target slot (`parts[4]`) on a `[from] lockedmove` line, no single-turn Stat Point perturbation in `fallback` can ever change the target chosen on Turn `t - 1`.
