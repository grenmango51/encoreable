## 8. Root Cause: Multi-Turn Drains, Continuous Status/Terrain, Cascading Percentage Sensitivity, and Tree Failure

### 1. Nine Consecutive Turns of Draining Attacks (`Matcha Gotcha`)
From Turn 4 through Turn 12, Sinistcha uses `Matcha Gotcha` on every single active turn:
- **Turn 4**: Drains from Rillaboom (Malamar protects).
- **Turn 5**: Drains from both Rillaboom and Malamar.
- **Turn 6**: Drains from Rillaboom (Malamar protects).
- **Turn 7**: Drains from both Glimmora and Malamar (Glimmora faints).
- **Turn 8**: Drains from Rillaboom.
- **Turn 9**: Drains from Incineroar.
- **Turn 10**: Drains from Incineroar.
- **Turn 11**: Drains from Incineroar.
- **Turn 12**: Drains from Incineroar.

In Pokémon Showdown, draining moves heal $\lfloor \text{damage dealt} / 2 \rfloor$, where damage dealt is capped at the target's actual remaining HP.
Every turn, Sinistcha's HP is updated by:
$$HP_{\text{new}} = \min\left(HP_{\text{current}} + \sum \left\lfloor \frac{\text{damage}_i}{2} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{HP_{\text{max}}}{16} \right\rfloor_{\text{terrain}} - \left\lfloor \frac{HP_{\text{max}}}{8} \right\rfloor_{\text{poison}}, HP_{\text{max}}\right)$$

In Channel 0, this value is observable only as an integer percentage:
$$\left\lfloor 100 \times \frac{HP_{\text{new}}}{HP_{\text{max}}} \right\rfloor$$

### 2. Multi-Turn Cascading Sensitivity
Because Sinistcha's HP at Turn $T$ depends on its HP at Turn $T-1$, any 1-HP difference in damage roll or stat in Turn 1 cascades across all subsequent turns:
- On Turn 1, Kingambit's Iron Head deals 158 damage to 160 HP Glimmora, leaving Glimmora with exactly 2 HP.
- On Turn 7, Glimmora enters via U-turn and faints to Matcha Gotcha. Sinistcha drains $\lfloor 2 / 2 \rfloor = 1$ HP.
- If a candidate guess had Kingambit deal 159 damage instead of 158 damage on Turn 1, Glimmora would have 1 HP remaining on Turn 7.
- Draining 1 HP yields $\lfloor 1 / 2 \rfloor = 0$ HP healed!
- A difference of 0 HP vs 1 HP healed on Turn 7 alters Sinistcha's displayed percentage on Turn 7 (`30/100` vs `29/100`).
- This in turn shifts Sinistcha's HP going into Turn 8, altering whether Turn 8 Grassy Terrain and poison round up or down, and whether Turn 9 drain crosses a percentage boundary.

### 3. Exhaustion of Joint Candidate Sampling (`choose` & `guessTries`)
1. In `infer.mjs`, when a rebuild reproduces up to Turn 8 or Turn 9, all 7 battling Pokémon are active:
   `order = [Glimmora, Incineroar, Rillaboom, Malamar, Raichu, Kingambit, Sinistcha]` ($N = 7$).
2. To find a candidate guess that satisfies all turns simultaneously, `choose(n, cond)` searches recursively through all 7 Pokémon.
3. Each candidate evaluation calls `settle` twice on an 8- to 10-turn battle input log, consuming tries from `tries = 32 * S * W = 64`.
4. With 64 tries allocated, `choose` can evaluate fewer than 32 branches of a 7-variable combinatorial tree ($5^7 = 78,125$ states).
5. `tries` reached 0 without finding an exact path across all 9 turns, triggering `limits.add('guessTries')` and returning `proof = null`.

### 4. Loss of Pinned Dice and Search Regression Mechanics
1. When `choose` returns `null`:
   - `exact = null`
   - `pins = null`
2. In Round 18 of the test trace:
   The rebuild had reached Turn 9 (`reproduces through turn 9`). However, `choose` failed to find an exact proof across all 9 turns, setting `exact = null` and `pins = null`.
3. In Round 19:
   The next rebuild ran without pinned dice. The unpinned dice search diverged on Turn 2:
   `round 19: reproduces through turn 2 - wanted |-heal|p1b: Malamar|69/100 par|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p1b: Malamar|68/100 par|[from] Grassy Terrain`
   The verified turn regressed from Turn 9 back to Turn 2.
4. Because the Turn 2 Grassy Terrain line had failed previously (in Round 5), `again = true` was triggered, invoking `fallback`.
5. Fallback spent rebuilds finding dice to pass Turn 2 under search level 2 (`round 20 (search 2)`), but then diverged again on Turn 5/6.
6. This cycle repeated: crawling forward to Turn 8/9 $\to$ `choose` failing $\to$ losing pins $\to$ regressing to Turn 2 $\to$ invoking fallback $\to$ consuming fallback rebuild budgets $\to$ stalling.
7. Across all 4 start tiers (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, `widest`), the search performed 320 rounds, 907 rebuilds, and 5,314,979 probes before terminating with `stop = "stalled"`.
