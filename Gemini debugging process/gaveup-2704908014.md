## 6. Spectator Failure Analysis (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Coarse Percentage Rounding Across Dual Recovery Channels
In spectator view (`Channel 0`), neither side is known (`known = [false, false]`). All HP updates are reported strictly as rounded percentages:
$$\text{pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{current\_hp}}{\text{max\_hp}} \right\rfloor$$

Across Turns 3 through 10, the field features **three overlapping continuous health modifiers**:
1. **Leftovers on Milotic**: heals $+1/16$ max HP per turn ($\lfloor \text{max\_hp} / 16 \rfloor$).
2. **Grassy Terrain on Grounded Combatants** (Milotic, Rillaboom, Sinistcha, Kingambit): heals $+1/16$ max HP per turn.
3. **Wood Hammer Recoil on Rillaboom**: deals $1/3$ of actual damage dealt ($\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor$).
4. **Matcha Gotcha Drain on Sinistcha**: heals $50\%$ of actual damage dealt ($\lfloor \text{damage} / 2 \rfloor$).

Because all four mechanisms involve integer truncation operations compounded turn-after-turn, an ambiguity of a single Stat Point on HP or Defense shifts the cumulative integer HP by 1–2 points, flipping display percentages back and forth across consecutive turns.

### Root Cause 2: Turn 10 Infinite 2-State Oscillation Cycle
The core failure in spectator search is a rigid 2-state oscillation cycle at Turn 10 between two mutually incompatible lines:
- **State A (Odd Rounds, e.g. 23, 25, ... 75)**:
  ```text
  reproduces through turn 9 - wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|43/100, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|46/100
  ```
- **State B (Even Rounds, e.g. 24, 26, ... 76)**:
  ```text
  reproduces through turn 9 - wanted |-heal|p2a: Sinistcha|51/100|[from] drain|[of] p1a: Rillaboom, got |-heal|p2a: Sinistcha|52/100|[from] drain|[of] p1a: Rillaboom
  ```

Every time the solver adjusted Rillaboom's defensive stats or Sinistcha's Special Attack to satisfy Rillaboom's damage percentage (`43/100`), the resulting drain heal on Sinistcha evaluated to `52/100` instead of `51/100`. Conversely, adjusting Sinistcha's HP or drain calculation to produce `51/100` caused Rillaboom's damage to revert to `46/100`.
