## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- No Pokémon displays exact HP numbers. Every damage, heal, and recoil event is reported as an integer percentage rounded by Showdown's `Math.ceil(currentHP * 100 / maxHP)`.
- 8 Pokémon were brought into the battle (4 per side), and 7 engaged in direct combat exchanges.
- Every damage equation contains 3 to 5 simultaneous unknown variables:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk}, \text{unknown Def}, \text{unknown Item}, \text{unknown Dice Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \text{rounded to } \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` produce the same rounded percentage in Channel 0, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads. All 7 battling Pokémon retained between 24.7 million and 117.3 million spreads (with P2 Rillaboom at 383,000).

  - Final HP = $137 - 14 = 123$
  - $123 / 205 = 60.000\% \rightarrow 60\%$
- Because $123 / 205$ is exactly on the $60.0\%$ boundary:
  - If Rillaboom enters Turn 9 with even 1 less HP (e.g. 136 instead of 137, caused by Raichu's Focus Blast dealing 117 instead of 116, or Leftovers healing 1 less HP under an odd maxHP value):
    $$(136 - 14) / 205 = 122 / 205 = 59.51\% \rightarrow 60\%$$
    $$(135 - 14) / 205 = 121 / 205 = 59.02\% \rightarrow 60\%$$
    $$(134 - 14) / 205 = 120 / 205 = 58.53\% \rightarrow 59\%$$
  - If Wood Hammer deals 45 damage instead of 43:
    $$\text{recoil} = \lfloor 45 / 3 \rfloor = 15 \rightarrow (137 - 15) / 205 = 122 / 205 = 59.51\%$$
  - If Wood Hammer deals 48 damage (roll 94):
    $$\text{recoil} = \lfloor 48 / 3 \rfloor = 16 \rightarrow (137 - 16) / 205 = 121 / 205 = 59.02\%$$
- In spectator rebuilds, candidate guesses systematically hit:
  - Round 6 diff: `wanted |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|78/100, got |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|80/100`
  - Round 7 diff: `wanted |-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|60/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|58/100|[from] Recoil`

### Root Cause 3: Inefficacy of Fallback Rebuilds on Cumulative Multi-Turn Lines
When the rebuild stops on Turn 8 or Turn 9 with a percentage mismatch:
1. `infer.mjs` triggers `fallback(seen, start, ...)` on Turn 8/9.
2. `fallback` identifies the Pokémon named in the failing turn (P2 Rillaboom, P1 Incineroar).
3. `fallback` attempts single-stat variations (`probe(changes)`) on Rillaboom and Incineroar for the failing turn.
4. However, the Turn 9 recoil mismatch cannot be resolved by changing Rillaboom's or Incineroar's stats on Turn 9 alone:
   - Changing Rillaboom's max HP changes all Grassy Terrain and Leftovers step sizes starting from Turn 1, breaking Turn 1, Turn 2, and Turn 7 verified lines.
   - Changing Rillaboom's Attack or Incineroar's Defense alters the Wood Hammer damage on Turn 9, but fixing the damage percentage `78/100` causes the recoil percentage `60/100` to diverge, and vice-versa.
   - The underlying offset was introduced 2 turns earlier by Raichu-Mega-Y's Focus Blast roll on Turn 7.
5. As a result:
   - Fallback spends all allotted rebuilds without getting past the line.
   - `fallbackRebuilds` and `fallbackTotal` limits are added.
   - Fallback returns `null`.

### Root Cause 4: Search Stalling and Restart Escalation
1. In `infer.mjs`:
   ```javascript
   const at = [diff?.turn ?? -1, diff?.index ?? -1];
   if (!deepest || past(at, deepest)) deepest = at;
   else stalled++;
   ```
2. Because rebuilds repeatedly stall at Turn 8 / Turn 9, `stalled` increments every round.
3. Once `stalled >= maxRounds` (16), the search aborts with `stop = 'stalled'`.
4. `inferSpreads` escalates to sequential restarts:
   - Restart 1: `start: 'usage'` -> stalls again at Turn 8/9.
   - Restart 2: `wide: true` (4x fallback rebuild budget) -> spends hundreds of additional fallback rebuilds probing Turn 8/9, exhausts `fallbackTotal`, stalls again.
   - Restart 3: `widest: true` (4x search budget scale, running default, usage, and wide) -> spends millions of probes running deeper tree searches on Turn 8/9.
5. In spectator view, each round's `evidencePass` takes 50-70 seconds on 1 thread because 8 Pokémon have tens of millions of candidate spreads, requiring extensive recursive key-checking across 9 turns.
6. The combined escalation resulted in **138 rounds, 774 rebuilds, 4,483,135 probes**, and **25,659.4 seconds (~7.12 hours)** before terminating with `GAVE UP` (`stop = 'stalled'`).

## 7. Search Limits Analysis

The sweep recorded `limits: ["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "fallbackTotal", "guessTries", "probes", "searches", "stalled"]`.

1. **`stalled`**:
   - Hit in `infer.mjs` line 976 / 1130.

2. **`searches`**:

3. **`guessTries`**:
   - Hit in `infer.mjs` line 1027.
   - In `choose(n, cond)`, `tries` starts at `32 * S * W`. When recursive combinations of joint guesses across the 8 active Pokémon failed to find an exact proof, `tries` decremented to 0.
4. **`fallbackRebuilds`**:
   - Hit in `infer.mjs` line 506.
   - Triggered when a single fallback invocation spent all allotted rebuilds (`spent >= FALLBACK_REBUILDS * S * W`) without finding a spread that reproduced the failing line.

   - Hit in `infer.mjs` line 485 / 506.

6. **`probes`**:
   - Hit in `reconstruct.mjs`.

7. **`backtracks`**:
   - Hit in `reconstruct.mjs`.
