## 8. Why `both@spectator` Failed (The Diagnosis)

### Factor 1: Percentage Quantization in Spectator View (Channel 0)
In Channel 0, every damage and heal event is printed as an integer percentage:
$$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{HP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$
Neither player's true max HP nor current HP is revealed. Every percentage value maps to an interval of multiple discrete integer HP values. For example:
- Golisopod displayed at `83/100` with max HP $182$ could correspond to any HP in $[151, 152]$.
- Floette displayed at `43/100` with max HP $169$ could correspond to any HP in $[72, 74]$.
- Sneasler taking Rocky Helmet recoil ($100\% \to 83\%$) only indicates recoil was $\approx 17\%$, admitting any max HP between $165$ and $190$.

### Factor 2: The Multi-Turn Draining & Recoil Dependency Web
The battle features 5 draining moves and 2 Rocky Helmet recoil events across 4 different Pokémon:
1. **Turn 4**: Golisopod uses `Leech Life` on Floette.
   - Floette takes damage $D_1$ (depends on Golisopod Atk and Floette Def).
   - Golisopod heals $\lceil D_1 / 2 \rceil$.
   - Golisopod's post-heal HP depends on:
     $$HP_{\text{goli}} = \text{maxHP}_{\text{goli}} - D_{\text{fakeout}} - D_{\text{moonblast}} + \left\lceil \frac{D_1}{2} \right\rceil$$
     where $D_{\text{moonblast}}$ depends on Floette SpA and Golisopod SpD, and $D_{\text{fakeout}}$ depends on Incineroar Atk and Golisopod Def.
   - Floette's starting HP on Turn 4 depends on Rock Slide damage $D_{\text{rockslide}}$ from Sneasler Atk vs Floette Def on Turn 3.
2. **Turn 5**: Sinistcha uses `Matcha Gotcha` on Golisopod.
   - Sinistcha takes $D_{\text{claw}}$ from Sneasler Atk vs Sinistcha Def, dropping to $28\%$.
   - Sneasler takes $\lfloor \text{maxHP}_{\text{sneasler}} / 6 \rfloor$ Rocky Helmet contact damage.
   - Sinistcha deals $D_{\text{matcha}}$ to Golisopod (Sinistcha SpA vs Golisopod SpD, crit, resisted).
   - Sinistcha heals $\lceil D_{\text{matcha}} / 2 \rceil$, rising from $28\%$ to $32\%$.
3. **Turn 6**: Golisopod uses `Leech Life` on Floette again ($43\% \to 17\%$, Golisopod heals $75\% \to 88\%$).
4. **Turn 8**: Golisopod uses `Leech Life` on Sinistcha (KO, heals $59\% \to 75\%$) and simultaneously takes $\lfloor \text{maxHP}_{\text{goli}} / 6 \rfloor$ Rocky Helmet contact damage ($75\% \to 59\%$).
5. **Turn 9**: Golisopod uses `Leech Life` on Kingambit ($100\% \to 70\%$, Golisopod heals $59\% \to 73\%$).

Every draining move couples the attacker's HP and defensive stat with the defender's HP and defensive stat. In `both@spectator`, Golisopod's HP changes **10 separate times** across the battle (Turn 1 Fake Out, Turn 4 Moonblast, Turn 4 Leech Life heal, Turn 5 Matcha Gotcha crit, Turn 6 Leech Life heal, Turn 7 Fake Out, Turn 8 Kowtow Cleave, Turn 8 Leech Life heal, Turn 8 Rocky Helmet recoil, Turn 9 Leech Life heal). A slight discrepancy in any single stat earlier in the battle compounds into every subsequent percentage calculation.

### Factor 3: Turn 4 Speed Gating Under Tailwind
On Turn 4, Tailwind (set Turn 1) is active for Player 2:
- Golisopod-Mega base Spe = 40. At Level 50: $\text{Spe} = 40 + \text{SP}_{\text{goli}} + 20 = 60 + \text{SP}_{\text{goli}}$.
  Under Tailwind ($2\times$): $\text{Spe}_{\text{goli}} \in [120, 184]$.
- Floette-Mega base Spe = 102. At Level 50: $\text{Spe} = 102 + \text{SP}_{\text{floette}} + 20 = 122 + \text{SP}_{\text{floette}}$.
  Without Tailwind: $\text{Spe}_{\text{floette}} \in [122, 154]$.

The Speed ranges overlap:
- If Floette moves first: Floette deals Moonblast damage to Golisopod *before* Golisopod uses Leech Life. Golisopod drops to $121$ HP and can heal the full $31$ HP from Leech Life up to $152$ ($83\%$).
- If Golisopod moves first: Golisopod uses Leech Life while still at $175/182$ HP. Leech Life healing is capped at max HP ($+7$ HP to $182$), after which Moonblast damage reduces it to $128$ ($70\%$).
- Therefore, the protocol sequence on Turn 4 strictly requires:
  $$\text{Spe}_{\text{floette}} > \text{Spe}_{\text{goli}} \iff 122 + \text{SP}_{\text{floette}} > 120 + 2 \times \text{SP}_{\text{goli}} \iff \text{SP}_{\text{floette}} \ge 2 \times \text{SP}_{\text{goli}} - 1$$
In random joint sampling, whenever $\text{SP}_{\text{goli}} \ge 2$ while $\text{SP}_{\text{floette}}$ is low, Golisopod acts before Floette, causing an immediate order mismatch:
`wanted |move|p1b: Floette|Moonblast|p2a: Golisopod, got |move|p2a: Golisopod|Leech Life|p1b: Floette` (as observed in round 5).

### Factor 4: Breakdown of Sequential Pinning in `choose()`
In `infer.mjs`, candidate spreads for unknown Pokémon are selected by pinning them in sequence:
`seq = [p1:Sinistcha, p1:Floette, p1:Incineroar, p1:Kingambit, p2:Pelipper, p2:Golisopod, p2:Sneasler]`.
When Pokémon $A$ (e.g. Floette) is pinned to an arbitrary candidate spread, `settle()` runs to verify if remaining Pokémon have any compatible spreads. Because Golisopod's HP and Def must simultaneously satisfy Turn 1 Fake Out, Turn 4 Moonblast, Turn 4 Leech Life, Turn 5 crit Matcha Gotcha, and Turn 6 Leech Life against Floette and Sinistcha's pinned stats, pinning Floette or Sinistcha frequently leaves Golisopod with zero surviving spreads.
This triggers `blocks(keyed, n)`, causing `choose()` to backtrack, reorder `seq`, and burn through its attempt budget ($32 \times S \times W = 128$). The engine repeatedly hits the `guessTries` limit.

### Factor 5: Ineffectiveness of Single-Stat Coordinate Fallback
When a diff repeats (`again`), `fallback()` probes single-stat variations:
```javascript
const probe = async (changes, from = knowledge) => { ... }
```
It tests altering one stat at a time (e.g. `Floette hp 0`, `Floette atk 32`, `Golisopod hp 18`, etc.) holding all other Pokémon at their guessed spreads.
However, matching Turn 4's spectator line:
`|-heal|p2a: Golisopod|83/100|[from] drain|[of] p1b: Floette`
requires simultaneously satisfying:
1. Golisopod max HP (HP SP)
2. Floette SpA and Golisopod SpD (Moonblast damage)
3. Golisopod Atk and Floette Def (Leech Life damage)
4. Floette Spe and Golisopod Spe ($\text{Spe}_{\text{floette}} > 2 \times \text{Spe}_{\text{goli}}$)

Varying any single stat in isolation fails to match the required percentage or breaks earlier turns (Turn 1, Turn 2, or Turn 3). ### Factor 6: Oscillation Between Turn 4 and Turn 5 Leading to Stagnation
- When the search finds a joint guess that slips past Turn 4 (e.g. in round 9), it immediately encounters the drain calculation on Turn 5:
  `wanted |-heal|p1a: Sinistcha|32/100|[from] drain|[of] p2a: Golisopod, got |-heal|p1a: Sinistcha|33/100|[from] drain|[of] p2a: Golisopod`
- Adjusting Sinistcha or Golisopod's stats to correct the Turn 5 drain modifies the damage and heal values on Turn 4 (or Turn 2/3), causing the subsequent rebuild to regress back to Turn 4.
- Because `deepest` never advances beyond Turn 5, `stalled` increments on every round that stops at Turn 4 or Turn 5.
- Once `stalled >= maxRounds` (32), the engine cycles to the next restart (`usage`, then `wide`, then `widest`). Across all restarts, the search repeats this cycle for 287 rounds, 800 rebuilds, and 2,099,459 probes over 13,925.4 seconds before finally aborting with `stop="stalled"`.
