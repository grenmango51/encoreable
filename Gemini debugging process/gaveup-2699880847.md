## 5. Single-View Resolution Mechanics (`p2@p1` and `p1@p2`)

### Why `p2@p1` Resolved Rapidly (Channel 1, P1 known, infer P2, < 45s)
In Channel 1, Player 1 is the observer. Player 1's exact HP values and team parameters are constants:
1. **Observer Exact Damage Taken Isolates Enemy Offensive Statistics**:
   - Turn 3: Sylveon has known bulk ($202$ HP, $152$ SpD). Delphox's spread Heat Wave deals exact integer damage $202 - 152 = 50$ HP under Rain, directly pinning Delphox-Mega's Special Attack to an exact integer bracket.
   - Turn 3: Politoed has known bulk ($197$ HP, $120$ SpD). Delphox's spread Heat Wave deals exact damage $197 - 164 = 33$ HP, corroborating Delphox's SpA.
   - Turn 4: Sylveon's exact physical Defense ($85$) and HP ($152$) are known. Psyshock's lethal damage ($> 152$ HP) confirms +2 SpA Delphox-Mega.
   - Turn 6: Politoed takes exact integer damage $197 - 110 = 87$ HP from Sinistcha's Matcha Gotcha. With Politoed's exact SpD ($120$) known, Sinistcha's Special Attack is resolved to a single value.
2. **Exact Speeds on P1 Eliminate Turn Order Ambiguities**:
   - P1 knows Politoed's exact Speed ($92$). Because Sinistcha moved before Politoed under Trick Room on Turn 6, Sinistcha's Speed is immediately constrained to $\le 90$.
3. **Decoupled One-Sided Search**:
   - Because only P2's team is unknown, the candidate search space has no cross-team combinatorial explosion. The solver converged in ~8 rounds without hitting backtracking limits.

### Why `p1@p2` Resolved Rapidly (Channel 2, P2 known, infer P1, < 60s)
In Channel 2, Player 2 is the observer. Player 2's exact HP values and team parameters are constants:
1. **Exact Damage Received Pins P1 Attacking Statistics**:
   - Turn 3: Delphox has known HP ($159$) and SpD ($120$). Sylveon's spread Hyper Voice deals exact damage $159 - 126 = 33$ HP, isolating Sylveon's SpA.
   - Turn 3: Incineroar has known HP ($202$) and SpD ($112$). Sylveon's Hyper Voice deals exact damage $202 - 117 = 85$ HP, locking Sylveon's Special Attack.
   - Turn 4: Weather Ball Ice deals exact damage $94 - 73 = 21$ HP to Delphox, isolating Politoed's Special Attack.
   - Turn 6: Sinistcha has known HP ($178$) and SpD ($145$). Farigiraf's Psychic deals exact damage $139 - 84 = 55$ HP, isolating Farigiraf's SpA.
   - Turn 9: Sinistcha takes exact damage $148 - 18 = 130$ HP from Hydreigon's Dark Pulse, immediately pinning Hydreigon's Special Attack.
2. The single-view solver converged in ~10 rounds.

## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

In Channel 0 (spectator view), both sides are completely unknown and all HP values are masked by integer percentage truncation:
$$\text{percentage} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$

The spectator inference stalled permanently on **Turn 5 upkeep into Turn 6**, cycling fruitlessly through 290 rounds across 6 search phases before giving up. The failure is driven by four deeply coupled pathologies:

### Root Cause 1: The Turn 5.11 Leftovers Barrier (Trapping Fallback Mutations)
The most striking pathology revealed by the cluster trace log is that **every single fallback rebuild on Turn 6 stopped dead before Turn 6 even started**:
```
fallback on turn 6: the guess stops before the turn, at 5.11: wanted |-heal|p1a: Politoed|99/100|[from] item: Leftovers, got |-heal|p1a: Politoed|100/100|[from] item: Leftovers
```
1. In Turn 5 upkeep, Politoed heals with Leftovers ($\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$).
2. Politoed's HP entering Turn 5 upkeep is the cumulative result of:
   - Starting full HP ($197$ in ground truth).
   - Heat Wave damage on Turn 3 (took 33 HP $\rightarrow 164$ HP).
   - Turn 3 Leftovers heal (+12 HP $\rightarrow 176$ HP).
   - Turn 4 Leftovers heal (+12 HP $\rightarrow 188$ HP).
   - Turn 5 Leftovers heal (+12 HP $\rightarrow 200 > 197 \rightarrow 197/197 = 100/100$).
3. In candidate guesses where Politoed had slightly different HP SP (e.g. 28 SP vs 32 SP) or where Delphox's SpA roll on Turn 3 was 1 HP higher:
   - Politoed's HP before Turn 5 Leftovers was 1 HP lower, causing Leftovers to heal to $99/100$ instead of $100/100$ (or vice versa).
4. Whenever `fallback` on Turn 6 attempted to mutate any candidate Stat Point (to fix the Turn 6 speed tie or damage numbers), the candidate mutation altered the battle simulation on Turn 3 or Turn 4.
5. Consequently, `reconstruct.mjs` aborted at protocol line 5.11 (`|-heal|p1a: Politoed|...`). Fallback was **completely blind to Turn 6**: it could never evaluate whether its mutation resolved the Turn 6 bottleneck because it crashed at line 5.11!

### Root Cause 2: Trick Room Speed Ordering Inversion Between Politoed and Sinistcha
On Turn 6, Trick Room is active (Turn 4 of 5).
1. In the ground-truth battle:
   - Politoed has planted Modest nature, `{ spe: 2 }` $\rightarrow$ Speed $92$.
   - Sinistcha has planted Calm nature, `{ spe: 0 }` $\rightarrow$ Speed $90$.
   - Under Trick Room, the slower Pokémon acts first: Sinistcha ($90$) moves BEFORE Politoed ($92$).
   - Ground truth protocol:
     ```
     |move|p2b: Sinistcha|Matcha Gotcha|p1a: Politoed|[spread] p1a,p1b
     ...
     |move|p1a: Politoed|Muddy Water|p2a: Incineroar|[spread] p2a,p2b
     ```
2. In the solver's candidate guesses:
   - Usage stats for Sinistcha frequently assign Speed SP $\ge 2$ (e.g. 12 SP $\rightarrow$ Spe 102; 4 SP $\rightarrow$ Spe 94).
   - Whenever Sinistcha's guessed Speed is $\ge 92$, Sinistcha is faster than Politoed in standard speed.
   - Under Trick Room, this inverts the order: Politoed moves BEFORE Sinistcha!
   - This caused hundreds of rebuilds to diverge on action order:
     ```
     round 7: wanted |move|p2b: Sinistcha|Matcha Gotcha|p1a: Politoed, got |move|p1a: Politoed|Muddy Water|p2b: Sinistcha
     ```
3. Because Politoed and Sinistcha share identical base Speed (70), their relative Trick Room ordering hinges on a tiny $\pm 2$ SP difference, creating a knife-edge bifurcation in the action tree.

### Root Cause 3: Matcha Gotcha 20% Burn RNG Divergence
On Turn 6, Sinistcha uses Matcha Gotcha, a spread move that has an independent 20% chance to burn each target hit (Politoed and Farigiraf):
1. In the ground-truth mimic replay:
   - The battle RNG rolled a burn on Farigiraf: `|-status|p1b: Farigiraf|brn`.
   - The battle RNG did NOT roll a burn on Politoed.
2. In Reconstruct's simulation:
   - When the dice roll for secondary status effects differed, the simulator frequently applied a burn to Politoed, emitting `|-status|p1a: Politoed|brn`.
   - Because the ground-truth log contains no burn on Politoed, `reconstruct` immediately failed:
     ```
     round 10: wanted |move|p1a: Politoed|Muddy Water..., got |-status|p1a: Politoed|brn
     round 11: wanted |move|p1a: Politoed|Muddy Water..., got |-status|p1a: Politoed|brn
     round 12: wanted |move|p1a: Politoed|Muddy Water..., got |-status|p1a: Politoed|brn
     ```
   - This false status divergence occurred in more than 40 separate rounds across the trace log!

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Target Drain Quantization Deadlock
Matcha Gotcha drains 50% of the damage dealt to both targets to heal Sinistcha:
$$\text{Drain Heal} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Damage}(\text{Politoed})}{2} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Damage}(\text{Farigiraf})}{2} \right\rfloor$$
1. Each drain emits a separate spectator percentage:
   ```
   |-damage|p1a: Politoed|56/100
   |-heal|p2b: Sinistcha|57/100|[from] drain|[of] p1a: Politoed
   |-damage|p1b: Farigiraf|80/100
   |-heal|p2b: Sinistcha|70/100|[from] drain|[of] p1b: Farigiraf
   ```
2. The percentage emitted depends simultaneously on:
   - Sinistcha's SpA and damage roll into Politoed.
   - Politoed's SpD and max HP.
   - Sinistcha's SpA and damage roll into Farigiraf.
   - Farigiraf's SpD and max HP.
   - Sinistcha's own max HP ($178$).
3. If any single variable shifts by 1 integer point, the drain heal percentages diverge:
   ```
   wanted |-heal|p2b: Sinistcha|79/100, got |-heal|p2b: Sinistcha|80/100
   wanted |-heal|p2b: Sinistcha|78/100, got |-heal|p2b: Sinistcha|77/100
   ```
4. The solver was forced to solve a 6-variable non-linear Diophantine system across masked percentages while trapped behind the Turn 5.11 Leftovers barrier.
