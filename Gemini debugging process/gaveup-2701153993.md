## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across All 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- No Pokémon displays exact HP numbers. Every damage, heal, and recoil event is reported as an integer percentage rounded by Showdown's `Math.ceil(currentHP * 100 / maxHP)`.
- All 8 Pokémon brought into the battle (4 per side) engaged in combat exchanges.
- Every damage equation contains 3 to 5 simultaneous unknown variables:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk}, \text{unknown Def}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because hundreds of combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` produce the same rounded percentage in Channel 0, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- All 8 battling Pokémon retained massive candidate spaces: Charizard (135.5M), Meowscarada (116.2M), Tyrantrum (82.5M), Gallade (32.0M), Kingambit (26.6M), Sylveon (17.9M), Venusaur (12.3M), and Incineroar (2.8M).

### Root Cause 2: The Turn 5 Multi-Party Interdependent Constraint Knot
Turn 5 contains a tightly coupled, 4-way dependency chain involving Venusaur, Sylveon, Meowscarada, and Incineroar:

1. **Venusaur Giga Drain on Sylveon**:
   - Sylveon's HP entering Turn 5 is:
     $$H_{\text{Sylveon, T5}} = \text{maxHP}_{\text{Sylveon}} - \text{FakeOut}_{\text{T3}} - \text{ThroatChop}_{\text{T4}}$$
   - Giga Drain deals damage $D_1$ to Sylveon. Sylveon drops to $H_{\text{Sylveon, T5}} - D_1$, which must display as `52/100`.
   - Venusaur heals $\lceil D_1 / 2 \rceil$ HP.
   - Venusaur entered Turn 5 with HP resulting from 3 Leftovers heals, 1 previous Giga Drain heal (T4), and 3 previous damage events (T2 Sacred Sword, T2 Rock Slide, T3 Outrage).
   - Venusaur's post-drain HP must display as `50/100g`.
   - The `g` suffix in Showdown indicates that Venusaur's post-drain HP is strictly greater than half of maxHP: $\text{HP} > \text{maxHP} / 2$.
   - For ground-truth Venusaur (max HP 187), half is 93.5; healing 17 HP brings it to 94 HP ($94 > 93.5 \rightarrow 50.26\% \rightarrow \text{50/100g}$).
   - Any candidate guess where Venusaur heals 1 less HP (93 HP) or has an even max HP produces `51/100` or `50/100y`, failing line 124 of Turn 5 (seen in Round 9: `wanted |-heal|p2a: Venusaur|50/100g, got |-heal|p2a: Venusaur|51/100`).

2. **Meowscarada Triple Axel on Venusaur**:
   - Triple Axel hits 3 times with progressive base powers: 20 BP, 40 BP, 60 BP.
   - The replay requires 3 exact percentage lines:
     - Hit 1: `|-damage|p2a: Venusaur|36/100`
     - Hit 2: `|-damage|p2a: Venusaur|9/100`
     - Hit 3: `|-damage|p2a: Venusaur|0 fnt`
   - Meeting all 3 thresholds simultaneously requires Meowscarada's Atk, Venusaur's Def, Venusaur's entering HP (94 HP), and 3 distinct random damage rolls to line up exactly. In Round 10, the guess produced `40/100` on Hit 1 instead of `36/100`.

3. **Incineroar Flare Blitz Recoil and Sitrus Berry Trigger**:
   - Incineroar uses Flare Blitz to KO Sylveon.
   - Recoil is $\lfloor (H_{\text{Sylveon, T5}} - D_1) / 3 \rfloor$.
   - Incineroar's HP entering Turn 5 was $202 - 80 = 122$ HP (from Turn 1 Rock Slide).
   - Incineroar's HP after recoil must be:
     $$122 - \lfloor 94 / 3 \rfloor = 122 - 31 = 91 \text{ HP}$$
   - This must produce `45/100` ($91 / 202 = 45.05\% \rightarrow 45/100$).
   - It must satisfy $91 \le \lfloor 202 / 2 \rfloor = 101$ to trigger Sitrus Berry.
   - Sitrus Berry heals $\lfloor 202 / 4 \rfloor = 50$ HP, reaching $91 + 50 = 141$ HP, which must produce `69/100` ($141 / 202 = 69.80\% \rightarrow 69/100$).

If any single stat in this 4-party chain (Venusaur SpA/Def/HP, Sylveon maxHP/SpD/Def, Meowscarada Atk, Incineroar Atk/maxHP/Def) is off by even 1 Stat Point, the entire chain of 6 consecutive Turn 5 percentage assertions collapses.

### Root Cause 3: Failure of Joint Guessing & Lack of Exact Proof
1. In `infer.mjs`, when `evidencePass` processes Turn 5, `choose(n, cond)` attempts to pin candidates across all 8 active Pokémon to produce an exact proof (`proof.exact === true`).
2. Because the Turn 5 system is severely over-constrained by percentage thresholds (`50/100g`, `36/100`, `9/100`, `0 fnt`, `45/100`, Sitrus trigger, `69/100`), recursive search combinations exhaust `tries` (`tries <= 0 -> limits.add('guessTries')`).
3. When `choose` fails to find an exact proof, `infer.mjs` falls back to unpinned aim guesses (`next[s][i] = closestSpread(...)`).
4. These unpinned guesses do not guarantee that earlier verified turns remain reproducible.

### Root Cause 4: Catastrophic Backtracking to Turn 1 and Fallback Loops
1. When unpinned guesses are evaluated in the next round, they fail on Turn 1 (e.g. Round 11: `wanted |-damage|p2b: Kingambit|1/100, got |-damage|p2b: Kingambit|0 fnt`).
2. Because this line had already failed in an earlier round (`failed.has(markOf(diff)) === true`), `infer.mjs` invokes `fallback(seen, start, ...)` on Turn 1.
3. Fallback spends rebuilds probing Kingambit and Gallade stats until it finds a spread that reproduces Turn 1 (e.g. Kingambit Def 32).
4. However, the verified turn depth is reset back to Turn 1 (`at = [1, 6]`), while `deepest` was already at Turn 4 / Turn 5.
5. In `infer.mjs`:
   ```javascript
   if (!deepest || past(at, deepest)) deepest = at;
   else stalled++;
   ```
6. Because `at` is at Turn 1 while `deepest` is at Turn 4 / Turn 5, `past(at, deepest)` is `false`.
7. `stalled` increments on every round of this loop.
8. Once `stalled >= maxRounds` (16), the search aborts with `stop = 'stalled'`.

---

### Root Cause 5: Search Stalling and Restart Escalation
1. Sequential restart attempts in `inferSpreads`:
   - **Start 1 (`default`)**: Runs 16 rounds, stalls at Turn 4 / Turn 5, triggers Turn 1 fallback, aborts with `stop = 'stalled'`.
   - **Start 2 (`usage`)**: Restarts from Smogon usage priors, encounters the same Turn 5 coupling, stalls again.
   - **Start 3 (`wide`)**: Multiplies fallback rebuilds and joint guess tries by 4 (`WIDE = 4`). Spends hundreds of fallback rebuilds, hits `fallbackTotal` and `fallbackRebuilds` limits, stalls again.
   - **Start 4 (`widest`)**: Multiplies all limits by 4 (`budgetScale = 4`: probes, backtracks, rounds, fallback rebuilds, guess tries).
2. Under `widest`:
   - Each rebuild allows up to 16,000 probes and 16,000 backtracks.
   - Every round's `settle` and `evidencePass` across 8 active Pokémon with tens of millions of candidates takes 30–60+ seconds.
   - Exhaustive backtracking through dice trees on Turn 4 and Turn 5 hits `probes` and `backtracks` limits.
3. The cumulative escalation ran **219 rounds, 959 rebuilds, 2,778,839 probes**, and **21,204 seconds (~5.89 hours)** before exhausting all restarts and terminating with `GAVE UP` (`stop = 'stalled'`).
