## 5. Single-View Resolution Mechanics (`p2@p1` and `p1@p2`)

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Succeeded in ~120s–130s
In single views (`p2@p1` in Channel 1 and `p1@p2` in Channel 2), one player's exact stats are fully known from the start:

1. **Exact Anchor HP Values**:
   - In Channel 1, P1's exact HP values are known: Sneasler (`157`), Delphox (`150`), Garchomp (`185`), Sinistcha (`178`).
   - Every damage event dealt to P1 provides exact integer damage. For example:
     - On Turn 1, P2 Delphox deals exact 87 damage to Sneasler and exact 32 damage to Delphox. With Delphox's known SpD (145), P2 Delphox's SpA is locked directly.
     - On Turn 5, Kommo-o deals exact 64 damage to Sinistcha. With Sinistcha's known SpD (120), Kommo-o's SpA is locked directly.
     - On Turn 8, Kingambit deals exact 70 damage to Garchomp. With Garchomp's known Def (115), Kingambit's Atk is locked directly.
2. **In Channel 2 (`p1@p2`)**:
   - P2's exact stats are known: Incineroar (`202`), Delphox (`154`), Kommo-o (`160`), Kingambit (`207`).
   - On Turn 1, Sneasler deals exact 15 damage to Incineroar $\rightarrow$ locks Sneasler's Atk.
   - On Turn 3, Garchomp deals exact 98 damage to Delphox and exact 70 damage to Incineroar $\rightarrow$ locks Garchomp's Atk.
   - On Turn 3, Delphox deals exact 78 damage to Incineroar $\rightarrow$ locks Delphox's SpA.
3. **Absence of Circular Search Loops**:
   - Because one side has fixed integers, `evidencePass` establishes direct algebraic bounds on the unknown side without circular dependencies.
   - The search converges in standard rounds without triggering cascading restart escalations.

## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

In `both@spectator`, inference ran for **8,384.4s (2.33 hours)** and gave up with stop reason `"stalled"`, hitting all 7 search limits:
`["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "fallbackTotal", "guessTries", "probes", "searches", "stalled"]`.

The failure was driven by eight mutually reinforcing pathologies:

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across All 8 Combatants
- In Channel 0, all HP figures are masked by Showdown's integer truncation: `Math.floor(100 * hp / maxhp) || 1`.
- All 8 Pokémon brought (4 on P1: Sneasler, Delphox, Garchomp, Sinistcha; 4 on P2: Delphox, Incineroar, Kommo-o, Kingambit) actively fought, dealt damage, and took damage.
- There are NO known anchor Pokémon on either side. Every single damage exchange involves four simultaneous unknown variables:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Attacker Offense}, \text{unknown Defender Defense}, \text{RNG roll } [0.85, 1.00]) \rightarrow \text{Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lfloor \cdot \rfloor\%$$
- Because dozens of combinations of `(Atk/SpA, Def/SpD, maxHP, roll)` map to the exact same percentage integer, `evidencePass` cannot pin stats.
- After all 9 turns, massive spread spaces remain: Sneasler retains 67.6M spreads (49.5%), Sinistcha retains 47.8M spreads (35.0%), Delphox-P1 retains 29.9M spreads (21.9%), Kingambit retains 25.8M spreads (18.9%), Delphox-P2 retains 22.4M spreads (16.4%), Incineroar retains 9.2M spreads (6.7%), and Garchomp retains 7.4M spreads (5.4%).

### Root Cause 2: Turn 1 Fake Out Percentage Razor & Delphox Speed Tie Inversion
- On Turn 1:
  - Sneasler uses Fake Out on Incineroar. Incineroar has 202 max HP.
  - In ground truth: deals 15 damage ($187/202 = 92.57\% \rightarrow \mathbf{92/100}$).
  - If Incineroar takes 14 damage: $188/202 = 93.069\% \rightarrow \mathbf{93/100}$.
  - The default initial guess produced `93/100` instead of `92/100`, triggering fallback on Turn 1 in Round 1.
- Furthermore, both P1 Delphox-Mega and P2 Delphox-Mega have base Spe 134, Timid nature, reaching raw Spe 204:
  - In ground truth, a 50/50 speed tie occurred, and P2 Delphox won, using Heat Wave before P1 Delphox used Nasty Plot.
  - When candidate guesses assign P1 Delphox higher Spe SP than P2 Delphox (or if speed tie rolls P1), P1 Delphox acts first:
    `wanted |move|p2a: Delphox|Heat Wave..., got |move|p1b: Delphox|Nasty Plot...`
  - This immediately breaks Turn 1 protocol ordering.

### Root Cause 3: Turn 3 Delphox Speed Tie and KO Pre-emption Razor
- On Turn 3:
  - Garchomp uses Rock Slide, dealing 98 damage to P2 Delphox (`154 -> 56/154`, $36/100$).
  - Next at priority 0: Both Delphox are at Spe 204.
  - In ground truth, P1 Delphox won the speed tie and used Heat Wave, KOing P2 Delphox (`56 -> 0 fnt`).
  - In P2's turn choice: `>p2 move heatwave, move partingshot +1`. P2 Delphox queued Heat Wave!
  - If any candidate guess gives P2 Delphox even 1 more Speed than P1 Delphox, or if P2 Delphox wins the speed tie:
    P2 Delphox uses Heat Wave BEFORE fainting. Heat Wave damages Garchomp and P1 Delphox, drastically altering field state and causing total divergence on Turn 3.

### Root Cause 4: Turn 6 Heat Wave Accuracy Miss & Clanging Scales Critical Hit Trap
- On Turn 6:
  - P1 Delphox (+1 SpA) uses Heat Wave targeting both Kommo-o and Kingambit.
  - Heat Wave has 90% accuracy. In ground truth, the 10% miss roll occurred against Kingambit (`|-miss|p1b: Delphox|p2a: Kingambit`).
  - Next, Kommo-o uses Clanging Scales targeting Sinistcha and Delphox.
  - Showdown rolls a 1/24 critical hit check for each target.
  - In the unpinned reconstruct search, die 144 rolls 0 (critical hit on Sinistcha), printing `|-crit|p1a: Sinistcha`.
  - The replay has no critical hit (`|-damage|p1a: Sinistcha|26/100`).
  - Because `|-crit|` is printed before `|-damage|`, this difference halts forward reconstruction.
  - Budget levels 0 and 1 have only 0 and 2 backtracks, which are insufficient to resolve the multi-target spread move sequence.

### Root Cause 5: Turn 7 Sucker Punch Invisible Target Failure
- On Turn 7:
  - Kingambit uses Sucker Punch.
  - In ground truth, Kingambit targeted `p1b: Delphox` (who used Nasty Plot, a status move), so Sucker Punch failed:
    `|move|p2a: Kingambit|Sucker Punch||[still]`
    `|-fail|p2a: Kingambit`
  - Showdown prints `||[still]` when Sucker Punch fails, completely hiding which opponent was targeted.
  - But `p1a: Garchomp` was also on the field and used Earthquake (an attack move).
  - When `reconstruct` attempts to guess the invisible target choice on Turn 7:
    - If Kingambit targets Garchomp, Sucker Punch succeeds and deals damage, producing `|move|p2a: Kingambit|Sucker Punch|p1a: Garchomp`.
    - This conflicts with the expected `||[still]` / `|-fail` lines, causing reconstruction to fail on Turn 7.

### Root Cause 6: Turn 8 Sucker Punch Rough Skin + Earthquake KO Razor
- On Turn 8:
  - Kingambit has 85/207 HP ($41/100$).
  - Kingambit hits Garchomp with Sucker Punch ($185 \rightarrow 115/185$, $62/100$) and takes 25 Rough Skin damage ($85 \rightarrow 60/207$, $28/100$).
  - Garchomp uses Earthquake: deals 60+ damage, KOing Kingambit (`60 -> 0 fnt`).
  - If Garchomp's Atk SPs, Kingambit's Def SPs, or Kingambit's HP SPs vary even slightly:
    - If Kingambit survives Earthquake with 1–4 HP, Kingambit stays on the field.
    - P2 cannot bring in Incineroar on Turn 8 upkeep.
    - Incineroar's Intimidate never triggers on Garchomp/Delphox, causing Turn 9 to diverge completely.

### Root Cause 7: Turn 9 Helping Hand + Clanging Scales Double KO Razor
- On Turn 9:
  - Incineroar uses Helping Hand on Kommo-o.
  - Garchomp uses Earthquake: crits Incineroar (KOed) and deals 90 damage to Kommo-o ($137 \rightarrow 47/160$, $29/100$).
  - Kommo-o uses Helping Hand-boosted Clanging Scales:
    - KOs Garchomp.
    - Leaves Delphox at EXACTLY 11/150 HP ($7/100$).
  - A 1-HP difference in Delphox's bulk or Kommo-o's SpA either KOs Delphox prematurely or shifts the percentage display from `7/100` to `8/100`.
  - In addition, in the original human replay, Player 1 won on Turn 9. But in mimic self-play, the game reached Turn 10 opening before stopping at the 9-turn cap.

### Root Cause 8: Cascading Fallback Exhaustion Across All 4 Search Restarts
- In `inferSpreads`:
  1. `start: 'default'`: Rebuild stops on Turn 1 / Turn 3 / Turn 6 / Turn 7. Fallback attempts 1-stat perturbations, but stats are tightly coupled across multiple turns. Fallback exhausts its rebuild budgets (`fallbackRebuilds`, `fallbackTotal`) and round stalled counter reaches `maxRounds` (16 rounds). Stop: `"stalled"`.
  2. `start: 'usage'`: Searches again from Smogon usage spreads. Hits the same speed tie, Sucker Punch, and percentage razors across another 16 rounds. Stop: `"stalled"`.
  3. `wide: true`: Restarts with $4\times$ fallback rebuilds (32 rebuilds per line) and $4\times$ joint guess tries. Performs dozens of heavy reconstruct probes across another 16 rounds. Stop: `"stalled"`.
  4. `widest: true`: Escalates with `budgetScale = 4`! Max probes = 16,000, max backtracks = 24, max rounds = 64 rounds per start, fallback rebuilds = 128. Runs all 3 starts again under these massive limits!
- The cumulative search across 8 unpinned Pokémon, multi-turn recoil/heal percentages, invisible move targets, and 4 restarts ran for **8,384.4 seconds (2.33 hours)** before exhausting all budgets, ultimately terminating with `stop: 'stalled'` and limits `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "fallbackTotal", "guessTries", "probes", "searches", "stalled"]`.
