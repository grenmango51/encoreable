### Root Cause 1: The Razor-Thin 0.04% Percentage Rounding Boundary on Sneasler's Turn 5 Grassy Terrain Heal
The primary numerical roadblock that halted all 4 search phases occurs on Turn 5 at line 13:

For Sneasler with planted SP `hp: 2`, $H_{\text{max}} = 80 + 2 + 75 = 157$.
$\lfloor 157 / 16 \rfloor = 9$ HP.

$$157 - 75 = 82\text{ HP}$$
