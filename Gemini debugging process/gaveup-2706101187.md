## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Adaptability Even-Damage Parity Trap at Turn 7 (`7.4: |-damage|p2b: Floette|57/100`)
In Showdown's damage pipeline (`sim/battle-actions.ts`), `Mystic Water` modifies Base Power *before* the random roll (`onBasePower`: `40 → 48` BP), whereas `Adaptability` applies `STAB = 2.0` (`8192 / 4096`) *after* the 16-step `[0.85..1.00]` integer truncation, with neutral `1.0×` Fairy type effectiveness and no weather or screen modifiers. Therefore:
$$\text{Damage}(\text{Aqua Jet} \to \text{Floette}) = 2 \times \left\lfloor \text{BaseDamage} \times \frac{r}{100} \right\rfloor \equiv 0 \pmod 2$$
**Every possible damage roll of `Basculegion`'s `Aqua Jet` against `Floette` is strictly an EVEN integer (`2k`).**

Now examine what happens whenever the inference engine guesses **`Floette HP SP = 1` (`MaxHP = 150`)**:
1. **Turn 2 (`2.4: 45/100`) Locks Even HP**: For `MaxHP = 150`, $\lfloor 100 \times h_2 / 150 \rfloor = 45$ holds **only for $h_2 = 68$ (EVEN)** ($67 \to 44\%$, $69 \to 46\%$).
2. **Turn 3 (`3.10: 22/100` & `3.11: 98/100`) Locks Odd Pre-Turn-7 HP**: Whenever `Whimsicott` has `SpD SP >= 1` (`SpD >= 96`) or `Floette` has `SpA SP <= 31` (`SpA <= 226`), `Draining Kiss` deals **105 damage** (`31/136 = 22/100`) and drains $\lceil 105 \times 3/4 \rceil = 79$ HP (**ODD**), locking `Floette` at **$h_3 = 68 + 79 = 147 / 150$ (ODD, `98/100`)**.
3. **Turn 7 (`7.4: 57/100`) Requires Even HP — Parity Contradiction!**: For `MaxHP = 150`, $\lfloor 100 \times h_7 / 150 \rfloor = 57$ holds **only for $h_7 = 86$ (EVEN)** ($85/150 = 56.67\% \to 56/100$; $87/150 = 58.00\% \to 58/100$).
4. Because $\text{ODD} - \text{EVEN} = \text{ODD}$, $147 - 2k$ is **always odd** (`87` $\to$ `58/100`, `85` $\to$ `56/100`, `83` $\to$ `55/100`, `81` $\to$ `54/100`, `79` $\to$ `52/100`). **Zero out of 16 PRNG rolls, for any `Basculegion` Attack stat, can ever land on `86 / 150 = 57/100`!**

This directly explains the straddling behavior in `trace_2706101187.log`:
- Starts 1, 3, 4, 6 (`Floette 1/32/0/31/0/2`, `Def 107`, `Basculegion Atk 24`): rolls `62` dmg $\to$ $147 - 62 = 85/150 \to$ **`got |-damage|p2b: Floette|56/100`** (lines 51, 149, 219, 424).
- Starts 2, 5 (`Floette 1/0/3/30/0/32`, `Def 110`, `Basculegion Atk 24`): rolls `60` dmg $\to$ $147 - 60 = 87/150 \to$ **`got |-damage|p2b: Floette|58/100`** (lines 79, 270).

By contrast, in the **ground-truth battle**, `Floette` had **`HP SP = 2` (`MaxHP = 151`)**, where `57/100` corresponds to **`hp = 87` (ODD)**: Turn 2 left `Floette` at `151 - 82 = 69` (**ODD**), Turn 3 (`Floette SpA = 32` vs `Whimsicott SpD = 0`) dealt `106` damage for **`+80` drain (EVEN)** to reach `69 + 80 = 149` (**ODD**), and Turn 7 subtracted **`62` (EVEN)** to reach `149 - 62 = 87 / 151 = 57/100`!

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Floette`'s exact `MaxHP = 151` (`2` SP), `Rillaboom`'s unusual `Spe = 133` (`28` SP), and `p2b: Kingambit`'s `MaxHP = 176` (`1` SP) are known ground-truth constants, and Channel 2 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers (`69/151`, `149/151`, `87/151`). Knowing `Floette` drained `+80` HP (`69 → 149`) immediately forces `Draining Kiss` damage to `106` (locking `Whimsicott` bulk) and eliminates the `MaxHP = 150` parity trap completely.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Whimsicott` (`137 HP, 95 SpD`), `Basculegion` (`180 Atk, 130 Spe`), and `Charizard` (`167 HP, 126 Def`) are fixed constants, and Channel 1 prints `31/137` (`106` exact damage dealt by `Floette`!) and `149/167` (`18` exact damage dealt by `Rillaboom`). On Turn 7 `7.0`, `Rillaboom` moving before known `Basculegion` (`Spe 130`) immediately restricts `Rillaboom` to `Spe >= 130` (`Spe SP >= 25`), and exact `106` Draining Kiss damage gives `+80` drain so `Floette` cleanly satisfies `57/100` at `MaxHP = 151`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Prevent Irrelevant Off-Stat Perturbations from Polluting `held` via the 66-SP Cap**:
   In Round 11's Turn 2 fallback (line 35), testing `Floette atk 32` on a special attacker only reproduced `2.4` because saturating the 66-SP cap stripped `Floette`'s `def` from `5` to `0` and `hp` from `4` to `1` (`1/32/0/31/0/2`). Recording that entire spread into `held` locked 32 useless Attack SP onto `Floette` for the rest of the run, starving `hp`, `def`, and `spa` of budget slack on Turn 7. `fallback` should either skip probing `atk` on Pokémon that never used a physical attack (unless Foul Play / Confusion / Strength Sap applies) or avoid holding non-participating stats at 32.
2. **Guard `replayWith` `same` Check Against Unpinned Failing-Turn Dice (Line 339)**:
   In `fallback` (`infer.mjs` line 677), `probe()` skips `rebuild()` when `replayWith(built.inputLog, ...)` matches `plain`. However, on the failing turn `t`, `built.inputLog` carries a single arbitrary unpinned PRNG roll (`68` damage $\to$ `52/100`), under which `Floette hp 1` (`79/150`) and `Floette hp 2` (`80/151`) rounded to the same `52/100` even though their minimum-roll parity at `57/100` (`85/150 = 56%` vs `87/151 = 57%`) differed! Comparing `replayWith` only through `t - 1` (or checking the failing hit's full 16-roll `getHealth` set before declaring `same = true`) would prevent `fallback` from skipping valid `MaxHP` boundary shifts.
3. **Include Prior Drain-Target Defenders in `lender` / `cast` When a Returning Pokémon Stalls**:
   `Floette`'s pre-Turn-7 HP parity (`147` vs `149`) was governed by Turn 3 `Draining Kiss` against `Whimsicott`, who fainted on Turn 5 and was therefore absent from Turn 7's `cast`. Linking a drain move's target to the drainer in `lent` would allow `fallback` (and `choose` fail-first ordering) to coordinate `Whimsicott`'s `SpD` alongside `Floette`'s `HP` and `SpA`.
