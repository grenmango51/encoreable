## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

In Channel 0 (spectator view), the search ran 138 rounds, performed 674 rebuilds and 1,068,276 probes, and gave up after 13,049.1 seconds.

### Root Cause 1: Floored Percentage Ambiguity Across 8 Combatants
In Channel 0:
- Every HP value is reported as $\lfloor 100 \times \text{currentHP} / \text{maxHP} \rfloor$.
- All 8 Pokémon brought into the match engaged in direct combat exchanges.
- No player stat, HP base, or recoil value is anchored to an exact integer.
- A single reported spectator percentage (e.g. Politoed taking High Horsepower to `64/100`) is consistent with:
  - Politoed $H \in [165, 197]$
  - Damage $D \in [58, 71]$
- However, as demonstrated in Section 4, Politoed's subsequent Grassy Terrain (`71/100`) and Leftovers (`77/100`) require:
  - $(H = 197, D = 69)$: $128/197 = 64.97\% \rightarrow 64\%$, $140/197 = 71.07\% \rightarrow 71\%$, $152/197 = 77.16\% \rightarrow 77\%$.
  - If $D = 70$: $127/197 = 64.47\% \rightarrow 64\%$, but $139/197 = 70.56\% \rightarrow 70\%$, causing Round 7 diff `wanted 71/100, got 70/100`.
- In spectator mode, dozens of candidate spreads for Rillaboom and Politoed produce $D = 70$, satisfying the damage line but failing the healing line 2 lines later.

### Root Cause 2: Multi-Turn Cumulative Recoil and Healing Chains
The battle features a 5-turn cascade of physical recoil moves and passive terrain heals:
- Incineroar uses Flare Blitz on Turns 4, 5, 6, 7, and 9 (5 separate recoil events).
- Blaziken uses Flare Blitz on Turns 7, 8, and 9 (3 separate recoil events).
- Grassy Terrain heals all grounded combatants across Turns 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 (5 separate healing phases).

Because recoil is $\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor$ and terrain heal is $\lfloor \text{maxHP} / 16 \rfloor$:
- Any perturbation in a Pokémon's max HP changes its terrain healing step size for all subsequent turns.
- Any perturbation in an attack roll or defensive stat changes the recoil taken, shifting the baseline HP entering every subsequent turn.

### Root Cause 3: Long-Range Cross-Turn Recoil Coupling
When an attack deals lethal damage, Showdown calculates recoil based on the defender's *remaining HP*, not the move's raw damage output:
1. **Turn 8 Recoil is Coupled to Turn 1 Damage**:
   - On Turn 8, Blaziken KOs Floette-Mega with Flare Blitz.
   - Blaziken takes recoil = $\lfloor \text{Floette remaining HP} / 3 \rfloor = \lfloor 27 / 3 \rfloor = 9$ HP.
   - Floette's remaining HP (27) was determined on Turn 1 when Golisopod used Iron Head on Floette.
   - If a candidate guess had Golisopod dealing 127 damage instead of 126 on Turn 1:
     - Floette enters Turn 8 with 26 HP.
     - Blaziken recoil becomes $\lfloor 26 / 3 \rfloor = 8$ HP.
     - Blaziken HP becomes $29 - 8 = 21/157 = 13.37\% \rightarrow 13/100$.
     - The rebuild fails on Turn 8 with diff: `wanted |-damage|p2a: Blaziken|12/100|[from] Recoil, got 13/100`.
2. **Turn 7 Recoil is Coupled to Turns 4 and 6 Damage**:
   - On Turn 7, Blaziken KOs Rillaboom with Flare Blitz.
   - Blaziken takes recoil = $\lfloor \text{Rillaboom remaining HP} / 3 \rfloor = \lfloor 70 / 3 \rfloor = 23$ HP.
   - Rillaboom's 70 HP entering Turn 7 depends on Farigiraf's Psychic damage on Turn 4 (81 dmg) and Turn 6 (85 dmg), plus 3 turns of Grassy Terrain healing (+36 HP).
   - If Rillaboom enters Turn 7 with 72 HP:
     - Recoil becomes $\lfloor 72 / 3 \rfloor = 24$ HP.
     - Blaziken HP becomes $130 - 24 = 106/157 = 67.51\% \rightarrow 67/100$ (failing spectator `68/100`).

### Root Cause 4: Turn 9 Sitrus Berry Activation Threshold Cliff
On Turn 9:
- Incineroar takes Flare Blitz from Blaziken, then KOs Blaziken with Flare Blitz.
- Incineroar's HP after recoil is:
  $$\text{HP}_{\text{final}} = 201 - 24 + 12 - 15 + 12 - 49 + 12 - 29 + 12 + 12 - 39 - 5 = 100 \text{ HP}$$
- Incineroar's maximum HP is 201.
- Sitrus Berry activates if and only if $\text{HP} \le \lfloor 201 / 2 \rfloor = 100 \text{ HP}$.
- Incineroar's HP lands on the exact boundary ($100 \le 100$).
- If Incineroar had taken even 1 less point of damage across any of the preceding 5 turns, its HP would be 101 HP:
  - $101 / 201 = 50.25\% > 50.0\%$.
  - Sitrus Berry does not trigger.
  - The rebuild immediately diffs on Turn 9: `wanted |-enditem|p1a: Incineroar|Sitrus Berry|[eat], got nothing`.

### Root Cause 5: Inefficacy of Fallback Rebuilds on Cumulative Multi-Turn Drift
When a rebuild stops on Turn 7, 8, or 9 with a recoil or percentage mismatch:
1. `infer.mjs` identifies the Pokémon named in the failing line (e.g. `p2a: Blaziken` on Turn 8 recoil, or `p1a: Incineroar` on Turn 9 recoil).
2. `fallback` probes single-stat variations on that Pokémon for the failing turn.
3. Probing Blaziken on Turn 8 cannot fix the recoil mismatch because the recoil is governed by Floette's remaining HP from Turn 1.
4. Probing Incineroar on Turn 9 cannot fix the Sitrus Berry activation without breaking the verified percentage lines on Turns 4, 5, 6, and 7.
5. `fallback` exhausts its rebuild budget (`spent >= FALLBACK_REBUILDS * S * W`), logs `limits.add('fallbackRebuilds')`, and returns `null`.

### Root Cause 6: Search Stalling and Exhaustion
1. Because `fallback` returns `null`, the search cannot advance past the failing turn.
2. `stalled` increments on every round where `!past(at, deepest)`.
3. The joint candidate selector `choose` repeatedly burns its budget across all 8 active Pokémon (`limits.add('guessTries')`).
4. Once `stalled >= maxRounds`, the plain search gives up and triggers restarts:
   - `usage` start: repeats the circularity from common ladder spreads.
   - `wide` start: widens fallback rebuilds and guess tries by $4\times$.
   - `widest` start: widens all search limits by $4\times$.
5. After 138 rounds and 674 rebuilds, all search levels are exhausted without finding a spread combination that simultaneously satisfies all 9 turns of floored percentages, recoils, and Sitrus Berry activation.
6. The search terminates with `stop = 'searches'`, having hit `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "guessTries", "probes", "searches", "stalled"]`.

## 7. Summary of Facts
1. **Match Scope**: 9-turn doubles match in `[Gen 9 Champions] VGC 2026 Reg M-C`. All 8 brought Pokémon were active in direct combat exchanges.
2. **Single-View Asymmetry**: In `p2@p1` (21.6s) and `p1@p2` (24.8s), exact integer observer HP and recoil values isolated attacker and defender stats turn-by-turn in 6–7 rounds.
3. **Spectator Circularity**: In `both@spectator`, universal percentage truncation (`Math.floor`) masked exact HP values, while 8 Flare Blitz recoils, 5 Grassy Terrain healing ticks, and a Turn 9 Sitrus Berry 50% activation threshold bound all 8 Pokémon across 9 turns.
4. **Local Search Failure**: Turn 7 and Turn 8 recoils depended on defender remaining HP set on earlier turns (Floette on Turn 1, Rillaboom on Turns 4 & 6). Fallback probing of the attacking Pokémon on the failing turn could not resolve drift rooted in earlier turns, exhausting fallback budgets and causing the search to give up.
