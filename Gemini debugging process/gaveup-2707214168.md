- **Leftovers Recovery Rounding**: Leftovers heals `floor(maxHP / 16)`. For Archaludon (base HP 90, level 50 HP `[165..197]`), the heal is either 10 HP, 11 HP, or 12 HP per turn depending on whether max HP is `< 176`, `[176..191]`, or `>= 192`.
- **Stamina Defense Ramping**: Every physical or special hit triggers `Stamina`, incrementing Defense by +1 Stage (+50% per stage). Over 10 turns, P1 Archaludon accumulates up to +5 Def, while P2 Archaludon accumulates up to +6 Def.
- **Integer Percentage Granularity**: Because percentage damage reports truncate fractional HP, multiple candidate HP and Def values produce identical percentage outcomes on Turns 3 and 4, but diverge on Leftovers heal rounding on Turns 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.
- In `trace_2707214168.log`, rounds repeatedly tripped on:
  - Turn 3 Leftovers: wanted `|-heal|p1a: Archaludon|90/100`, got `89/100` (Round 7).
  - Turn 6 Leftovers: wanted `|-heal|p1a: Archaludon|100/100`, got `99/100` (Round 10, 12).

### 2. The Antagonistic Oscillation: Turn 4 Sneasler vs. Turn 8 Incineroar
The trace log reveals an intractable two-state oscillation that persisted for over 150 consecutive rounds across all restarts:
- **State A (Failure at Turn 4)**:
  `reproduces through turn 4 - wanted |-damage|p2a: Sneasler|2/100, got |-damage|p2a: Sneasler|11/100` (or `13/100`).
  - Here, P1 Archaludon's Turn 5 Dragon Pulse on Sneasler leaves Sneasler with `11/100` instead of `2/100`.
  - To push Sneasler's remaining HP down to `2/100`, candidate generation must increase P1 Archaludon's SpA or decrease Sneasler's SpD/HP.
- **State B (Failure at Turn 8)**:
  `reproduces through turn 8 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|62/100, got |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|65/100` (or Turn 7 `77/100` vs `78/100`).
  - To fix Turn 4, the engine adjusts the candidate spreads for P1 Archaludon, Sneasler, and Basculegion-F.
  - However, under the strict 66 Stat Point budget, reallocating Stat Points across P1 Archaludon, Basculegion-F, and Incineroar alters the multi-target `Muddy Water` damage rolls on Turns 7, 8, and 9.
  - On Turn 9, Basculegion-F's Muddy Water on Incineroar must deal enough damage to bring Incineroar from `78/100` to exactly `62/100`.
  - With the candidates that satisfied Turn 4, Incineroar takes less damage, arriving at `65/100` instead of `62/100`.
- When the engine adjusts Incineroar's SpD or Basculegion-F's SpA to fix Turn 8, the new spread immediately breaks Turn 4 Sneasler (`2/100` vs `11/100`).
- The engine alternates incessantly:
  - Round N: Fails at Turn 4 Sneasler (`2/100` vs `11/100`).
  - Round N+1: Fails at Turn 8 Incineroar (`62/100` vs `65/100`).

### 3. Fallback Rebuild Failure & Fruitless Blacklisting
When candidate guesses diverge, `infer.mjs:fallback` attempts single-stat and pairwise perturbations to break past the failing turn:
```
fallback on turn 5: |-damage|p2a: Sneasler|2/100 - the guess alone does not
fallback on turn 5: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds
fallback on turn 8: |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|78/100 - the guess alone does not
fallback on turn 8: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds
fallback on turn 9: |-damage|p1b: Incineroar|62/100 - the guess alone does not
fallback on turn 9: nothing gets past the line in 3 rebuilds
```
Because no single or pairwise stat modification can simultaneously satisfy:
1. P1 Archaludon's Leftovers heals on Turns 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9,
2. P1 Archaludon's Dragon Pulse on Sneasler on Turn 5,
3. Passho Berry damage reduction on Incineroar on Turn 8, and
4. Basculegion-F's Muddy Water damage rolls on Incineroar on Turn 9,
fallback exhausted its rebuild budget on both Turn 4 and Turn 8/9.

Under line 484 of `infer.mjs`:
```javascript
searched.set(mark, 2); spend.fruitless.add(mark);
```
Both the Turn 4 Sneasler diff mark and the Turn 8/9 Incineroar diff marks were permanently placed into `spend.fruitless`.
From that moment on, fallback was permanently disabled whenever any future round hit Turn 4 or Turn 8. The engine was reduced to blind mixing of earlier failed candidates (`mixOf`).

### 4. Severe Evidence Time Penalty: 146 Seconds per Stalled Round
A crucial operational bottleneck discovered in the trace log is the massive computational cost of `evidence and new guesses` during the late turns:
- Round N (stopping at Turn 4): takes 4.0s simulation + 3.7s evidence (~7.7s total).
- Round N+1 (stopping at Turn 8): takes 5.1s simulation + **145.8s** evidence (~151s total).
- Why does evidence take 145 seconds on Turn 8?
  By Turn 8, the simulator has tracked:
  - 10 full turns of actions across 8 different Pokémon,
  - 2 Mega Evolutions, 2 active terrain/weather conditions,
  - 12 Leftovers residual heal events,
  - Multiple multi-target spread moves (`Snarl`, `Muddy Water`, `Heat Wave`),
  - 15+ stat stage changes (`Stamina`, `Intimidate`, `Electric Seed`, `Snarl`, `Muddy Water`, `Close Combat`).
  Computing the interval evidence and joint PRNG consistency across all active spreads took nearly 2.5 minutes per round!
- Spending 146 seconds on every alternating round caused the engine to consume **5,931 seconds (1.65 hours)** in the trace run across just 180 rounds.
