### Root Cause 1: The Turn 4 Staraptor Brave Bird Recoil & Sandstorm Boundary Trap
The primary failure point of the entire search occurred at Turn 4 line 4.10:
`wanted |-damage|p1a: Staraptor|71/100|[from] Sandstorm, got |-damage|p1a: Staraptor|72/100|[from] Sandstorm`

Under Showdown Champions rules:
$$\text{Spectator\%} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{HP}}{\text{maxHP}} \right\rfloor$$

For Staraptor with planted HP SP 18 ($\text{maxHP} = 85 + 18 + 75 = 178$):
- Turn 4 Sandstorm damage is fixed at $\lfloor 178 / 16 \rfloor = 11$ HP.
- The protocol requires Sandstorm damage to leave Staraptor at `71/100`:
  $$\left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{HP}_{\text{end}}}{178} \right\rfloor = 71 \implies \text{HP}_{\text{end}} \in [127, 128]$$
- Because $\text{HP}_{\text{end}} = \text{HP}_{\text{after recoil}} - 11$, this strictly requires:
  $$\text{HP}_{\text{after recoil}} \in [138, 139]$$
- Staraptor started Turn 4 at full health (178/178). Therefore, Brave Bird recoil **must be exactly 39 or 40 HP**:
  $$\text{Recoil} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Damage to Golisopod}}{3} \right\rfloor \in [39, 40] \implies \text{Damage to Golisopod} \in [117, 122]$$
- If a candidate spread guess deals 114–116 HP damage to Golisopod:
  - Recoil is $\lfloor 116 / 3 \rfloor = 38$ HP.
  - $\text{HP}_{\text{after recoil}} = 178 - 38 = 140$.
  - Recoil spectator percentage: $\lfloor 14,000 / 178 \rfloor = 78/100$ (matches protocol).
  - But after Sandstorm: $140 - 11 = 129$ HP.
  - End-of-turn spectator percentage: $\lfloor 12,900 / 178 \rfloor = \mathbf{72/100}$!
  - The simulator rejects the rebuild: `wanted 71/100, got 72/100`!

**A single point of HP difference in recoil (38 vs 39 HP) is completely invisible in the recoil line (`78/100` matches for both 139 and 140 HP), but flips the subsequent Sandstorm damage line between 71% and 72%**.
