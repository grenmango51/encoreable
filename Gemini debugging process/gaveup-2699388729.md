## 5. Why Single-View Inference Succeeded while `both@spectator` Failed

In single-observer views (`p1@p2` and `p2@p1`), the inference engine easily completed within **<30 seconds** because having one side's complete team sheet (including exact HP numerators, items, and known moves) completely uncouples the system of equations. In stark contrast, **`both@spectator` gave up after 3,308.8s in the cluster sweep (and 1,951.3s on 15 dedicated threads on `malbolge`)**, hitting `stop = "searches"`. 

The core mathematical barrier is the **spectator integer feedback loop under highly coupled non-linear mechanics**:

### 1. Integer Quantization Noise Across Both Teams
In `both@spectator`, every single HP value is observable only as a truncated integer percentage:
$$HP_{\text{visible}}\% = \left\lfloor 100 \times \frac{HP_{\text{exact}}}{MaxHP} \right\rfloor$$
where $MaxHP = \text{base} + SP_{\text{HP}} + 75$. In this match, 6 different non-linear damage/healing modifiers operated simultaneously across 9 consecutive turns:
1. **Matcha Gotcha Drain**: Restores exactly $\lfloor \text{damage dealt} / 2 \rfloor$. The damage dealt is itself a function of the attacker's SpA, the defender's SpD, spread move penalty ($0.75\times$), type effectiveness, and the RNG damage roll ($\lfloor \dots \rfloor$).
2. **Life Dew Team Healing**: Restores exactly $\lfloor MaxHP_{\text{target}} / 4 \rfloor$ to all allies.
3. **Hospitality Switching**: Restores exactly $\lfloor MaxHP_{\text{ally}} / 4 \rfloor$ upon entering the field.
4. **Rocky Helmet Recoil**: Inflicts exactly $\lfloor MaxHP_{\text{attacker}} / 6 \rfloor$ upon contact.
5. **Recoil Penalties**: Flare Blitz and Brave Bird inflict $\lfloor \text{actual HP damage dealt} / 3 \rfloor$.
6. **Sandstorm and Burn Residuals**: Sandstorm deals $\lfloor MaxHP / 16 \rfloor$ to non-Rock/Ground/Steel types, while burn deals $\lfloor MaxHP / 16 \rfloor$ per turn.

Because both the numerator ($HP_{\text{exact}}$) and the denominator ($MaxHP$) are unknown on both sides, a change of just $1$ Stat Point in $SP_{\text{HP}}$, $SP_{\text{Def}}$, or $SP_{\text{SpD}}$ on an attacking or defending Pokémon shifts the resulting integer percentage by $1\%$. 

### 2. Multi-Turn Error Compounding
A minute discrepancy on Turn 4 (e.g. Incineroar's Flare Blitz damage into P2 Sinistcha) alters:
- P2 Sinistcha's remaining HP prior to Life Dew and Sandstorm.
- Incineroar's recoil damage integer ($\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor$).
- Whether Incineroar drops to $\le 50\%$ HP to trigger Sitrus Berry on Turn 4.
- P1 Sinistcha's drain heal integer on Turn 5 when hitting P2 Sinistcha with Matcha Gotcha.
- Staraptor's remaining HP on Turn 5 after taking burn, Sandstorm, and Life Dew.

By Turn 7 and Turn 9, the accumulated rounding error between simulated candidate spreads and the true log caused the simulator to continually miss integer target lines by exactly $1\%$ (e.g., `wanted 75/100, got 76/100` or `wanted 7/100, got 6/100`).

## 7. Conclusion & Architectural Insights

1. **Why `both@spectator` Gave Up**:
   The search gave up not due to a mechanics crash, a broken proof, or an invalid rule, but because of **combinatorial quantization entrapment**. The simultaneous presence of:
   - Double Sinistcha Hospitality and Matcha Gotcha drain healing,
   - Life Dew team recovery,
   - Dual Rocky Helmet recoil,
   - Flare Blitz and Brave Bird recoil, and
   - Sandstorm and burn residual chip,
   created a 6-way non-linear coupling. Without exact HP numerators from a player perspective, candidate spreads oscillating between Turn 7 drain ($75\%$ vs $76\%$) and Turn 9 recoil ($7\%$ vs $6\%$) burned the entire fallback budget across 6 search phases.

2. **Why Usage Priors Failed to Resolve the Match**:
   In matches where usage priors succeed (such as `mimic:2699344151`), the majority of brought Pokémon run standard Smogon sets ($0$ or $32$ SPs in extreme stats). In `mimic:2699388729`, the deterministic seed assigned highly non-standard random spreads to several key active combatants:
   - P1 Sinistcha: `{ hp: 14, atk: 2, def: 2, spa: 3, spd: 25, spe: 20 }`
   - P1 Incineroar: `{ hp: 31, atk: 0, def: 20, spa: 0, spd: 13, spe: 2 }`

   - P2 Staraptor: `{ hp: 24, atk: 10, def: 0, spa: 0, spd: 0, spe: 32 }`
   Because the real spreads were far from the modes in `usage-spreads.json`, seeding the search from usage priors did not land in the correct basin of attraction, causing the usage restarts to fail as well.

3. **Soundness Preserved**:
   Despite giving up after 160 rounds, 689 rebuilds, and 319,907 probes:
   - **0 real spreads were removed**.
   - **0 rebuilt spreads were removed**.
   - **0 broken proofs occurred**.
   The search correctly gave up rather than unsoundly eliminating valid candidate spreads, fully adhering to Hard Rule #2.
