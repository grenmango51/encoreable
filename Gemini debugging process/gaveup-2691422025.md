In the cluster sweep, the same battle consumed **2,133.9 seconds** across multiple nested restarts before giving up.

### 1. Loss of Integer HP Anchors in Spectator Channel
In Channel 0, all HP entries are quantized to integer percentages:
`85/100`, `87/100`, `91/100`, `93/100`, `97/100`, `100/100`, `81/100`, `51/100`, `54/100`, `85/100`, `20/100y`, `68/100`, `14/100`, `60/100`, `69/100`, `3/100`, `9/100`, `75/100`, `78/100`, `49/100`.
Neither side's Max HP, current HP, nor true damage dealt is known. All 6 active Pokémon had 136.6 million unconstrained spread possibilities.

### 2. Failure of Joint Guess Proofs (`guessTries` Exhaustion)
In `infer.mjs`, when multiple Pokémon are simultaneously unconstrained, `choose()` attempts a branch-and-bound search across the active combatants to identify a joint set of Stat Points that satisfies `evidencePass(..., { exact: true })`.
- Active combatants spanned 6 Pokémon: Pawmot, Rillaboom, Gholdengo, Whimsicott, Gengar, Incineroar.
- A single traversal across 6 Pokémon consumes multiple tries for keying and spread-narrowing.
- The per-round try budget (`tries = 32 * S * W`) is rapidly exhausted before evaluating combinations that simultaneously satisfy all percentage lines across Turns 1 through 6.
- Consequently, `choose()` returned `proof.exact = null` and `pins = null`.

### 3. Rebuild Divergence and Fallback on Turn 6
Because `proof.exact` was null, `reconstruct` re-simulated from Turn 1 using independent guesses from `closestSpread(kn)`:
- In Round 6, simulation reached Turn 6 line 6.8:
  `reproduces through turn 5 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Pawmot|78/100, got |-damage|p1b: Pawmot|79/100`
- In Round 7, evidence had pinned Pawmot's Max HP tentatively to 152 (HP SP 7):
  `reproduces through turn 5 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Pawmot|120/152, got |-damage|p1b: Pawmot|121/152`
- Line 6.8 had failed in consecutive rounds (`again = true`), triggering `fallback()`.

## 7. Turn 4, Turn 5, & Turn 6 Mutual Dependency Mathematical Breakdown

The failure of spectator inference centers on non-linear mathematical coupling spanning Turns 3, 4, 5, and 6:

### 1. Turn 4 Yellow Health Bar Threshold (`20/100y`)
On Turn 4, Mega Garchomp-Z uses `Draco Meteor` on P2 Rillaboom:
$$\text{Damage} = \left( \left\lfloor \frac{\lfloor 22 \times 130 \times \text{SpA}_{\text{Garchomp}} / \text{SpD}_{\text{Rillaboom}} \rfloor}{50} \right\rfloor + 2 \right) \times \text{roll} \times 1.5$$

In the ground truth:
- $\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}} = 207$, $\text{SpD}_{\text{Rillaboom}} = 92$.
- Mega Garchomp SpA = 112.
- Draco Meteor deals $174$ dmg:
  $$\text{Remaining HP} = 193 - 174 = 19\text{ HP}$$
  $$\text{Spectator percentage} = \lfloor 19 \times 100 / 207 \rfloor = \mathbf{9/100} \text{ (or } \mathbf{20/100y} \text{ in mimic)}$$
Showdown formats Champions health tokens via:
```js
this.hp * 2 > this.maxhp ? 'g' : 'y'
```
Since $19 \times 2 = 38 \le 207$, the token is `'y'`.
Any 1-stat perturbation in Rillaboom's HP, Def, or SpD shifts the post-damage HP above 50% or drastically alters the percentage:
- If Rillaboom's SpD is guessed at 0 ($90$): damage increases, dropping Rillaboom to $6/100$ or $14/100$.
- If Rillaboom's SpD is guessed higher ($94$): damage decreases, emitting $16/100$ or `'g'`.
Fallback testing `Rillaboom spd 0` or `Rillaboom def 23` immediately stopped at line 4.4 (`wanted 20/100y, got 6/100`).

### 2. Turn 6 U-turn Damage Quantization on Pawmot
On Turn 6, Rillaboom uses `U-turn` (Power 70, physical, Bug type) on Pawmot:
- Pawmot is Electric/Fighting (Bug is resisted: $\times 0.5$).
- Rillaboom has no STAB on Bug.
- Damage formula:
  $$\text{Base} = \left\lfloor \frac{\lfloor 22 \times 70 \times 194 / 90 \rfloor}{50} \right\rfloor + 2 = \left\lfloor \frac{3319}{50} \right\rfloor + 2 = 66 + 2 = 68$$
- Across the 16 discrete damage rolls ($\lfloor 68 \times \text{roll} / 100 \rfloor \times 0.5$):
  - Rolls 85–88: $57–59 \times 0.5 \implies 28–29\text{ dmg} \implies \text{post-HP } 118–119/147 \implies \mathbf{80/100}$
  - Rolls 89–91: $60–61 \times 0.5 \implies 30\text{ dmg} \implies \text{post-HP } 117/147 \implies \mathbf{79/100}$
  - Rolls 92–97: $62–65 \times 0.5 \implies 31–32\text{ dmg} \implies \text{post-HP } 115–116/147 \implies \mathbf{78/100} \text{ (mimic target)}$
  - Rolls 98–99: $66–67 \times 0.5 \implies 33\text{ dmg} \implies \text{post-HP } 114/147 \implies \mathbf{77/100}$
  - Roll 100: $68 \times 0.5 \implies 34\text{ dmg} \implies \text{post-HP } 113/147 \implies \mathbf{76/100}$

Notice that only 6 of the 16 rolls ($37.5\%$) land in the target window $\mathbf{78/100}$:
- If Pawmot's Max HP is perturbed by even $\pm 5$ HP (e.g. Max HP 152 when SP is guessed as 7):
  $$\frac{120}{152} = 78.94\% \implies \mathbf{78/100}, \quad \text{but } \frac{121}{152} = 79.6\% \implies \mathbf{79/100}$$
  A difference of just 1 HP in damage dealt flips the spectator token between $78/100$ and $79/100$!

### 3. Turn 6 Strict Speed Ordering Constraint
Mega Gengar and Pawmot have a strict speed requirement:
$$\text{Spe}_{\text{Mega Gengar}} (190) > \text{Spe}_{\text{Pawmot}} (172)$$
- If Mega Gengar's speed is guessed lower than 172 (such as when fallback perturbs `Gengar spe 0`, where Spe drops to 168):
  - Pawmot moves first and executes `Revival Blessing` before Gengar acts.
  - Gengar's subsequent `Shadow Ball` hits Gholdengo or Garchomp.
  - This inverts the protocol sequence, immediately failing line 6.2 (`wanted |move|p2a: Gengar|Shadow Ball, got |move|p1b: Pawmot|Revival Blessing`).

### 4. PRNG Stream Coupling Across Turns 1–5
Because PRNG rolls are settled deterministically:
- Every attack roll in Turn 1 (Fake Out, Rough Skin), Turn 3 (Moonblast, Icy Wind), Turn 4 (Draco Meteor, Moonblast, Parting Shot), and Turn 5 (Pawmot Fake Out) advances the PRNG state.
- When fallback modifies Pawmot's Def or HP (e.g. `Pawmot def 0` or `Pawmot hp 0`), the altered damage calculation triggers a branch difference in the simulator (such as an altered damage threshold or flinch draw), consuming a different number of PRNG frames.
- When Turn 6 is simulated, the shifted PRNG stream causes Rillaboom's U-turn to hit a critical hit (`got |-crit|p1b: Pawmot`), causing immediate divergence.

### 5. Circular Impossibility for 1-Stat Fallback
- Fixing Turn 6 U-turn damage via `Pawmot def` requires Def 90, which is incompatible with unpinned Max HP guesses.
- Fixing Pawmot's HP to 147 requires Pawmot HP SP 2, which cannot be discovered while Pawmot Def is wrong.
- Fixing Rillaboom's Atk to 194 alters Turn 4 Draco Meteor survival and Turn 1 Fake Out damage.
- Fixing Gengar's Speed alters Turn 3 Icy Wind damage and Turn 4 Moonblast damage.

Because the system of constraints requires simultaneously solving:
$$\begin{cases}
\text{Spe}_{\text{Gengar}} > \text{Spe}_{\text{Pawmot}} \\
\text{Dmg}_{\text{U-turn}}(\text{Atk}_{\text{Rilla}}, \text{Def}_{\text{Pawmot}}, \text{Roll}_{\text{T6}}) \in [31, 32] \\
\text{HP}_{\text{Pawmot}} - \text{Dmg}_{\text{U-turn}} \equiv 78\% \pmod{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Pawmot}}} \\
\text{Dmg}_{\text{Draco}}(\text{SpA}_{\text{Garchomp}}, \text{SpD}_{\text{Rilla}}, \text{Roll}_{\text{T4}}) \implies \text{Rillaboom HP} \le 50.0\% \\
\text{Dmg}_{\text{Moonblast}}(\text{SpA}_{\text{Whims}}, \text{SpD}_{\text{Gengar}}, \text{Roll}_{\text{T3}}) \implies 81/100
\end{cases}$$
and fallback can only vary one variable at a time, the search cycled continuously across line 6.8, line 4.4, line 3.6, and line 5.8 across 64 rounds and 240 fallback rebuilds, exhausting all probe and restart budgets and terminating with `stop="searches"`.
