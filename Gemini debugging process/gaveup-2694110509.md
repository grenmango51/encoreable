## 5. Diagnostic Investigation: Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up

### A. The Symmetrical Periodic Healing and Weather Engine
This match represents one of the most mechanically coupled board states in the entire benchmark corpus:
1. **Double Leftovers**: Both `p1a: Milotic` and `p2a/p2b: Milotic` carry Leftovers, generating a 1/16 (6.25%) Max HP heal every active turn.
2. **Double Grassy Terrain Windows**: Grassy Terrain was active on Turns 1–5 and again on Turns 10–14, healing all grounded Pokémon for 1/16 Max HP.
3. **Double Sandstorm Windows**: Sandstorm was active on Turns 5–9 and again on Turns 10–14, dealing 1/16 Max HP chip damage to non-Rock/Ground/Steel Pokémon.
4. **Discretization and Non-Linear Integer Rounding**:
   In Champions level 50, Milotic's HP formula is:
   $$\text{HP} = 95 + \text{SP} + 75 = 170 + \text{SP} \quad (\text{domain } [170, 202])$$
   - When $\text{HP} = 170$, $\lfloor 170 / 16 \rfloor = 10 \text{ HP} \implies \text{round}(100 \times 10 / 170) = 5.88\% \rightarrow 6\%$.
   - When $\text{HP} = 176$, $\lfloor 176 / 16 \rfloor = 11 \text{ HP} \implies \text{round}(100 \times 11 / 176) = 6.25\% \rightarrow 6\%$.
   - When $\text{HP} = 192$, $\lfloor 192 / 16 \rfloor = 12 \text{ HP} \implies \text{round}(100 \times 12 / 192) = 6.25\% \rightarrow 6\%$.
   Depending on whether a guess has an HP stat dividing cleanly by 16 or leaving a remainder, the spectator percentage string fluctuates between adjacent integers (e.g. `75/100` vs `76/100` on Turn 9/10, or `72/100` vs `73/100` on Turn 8).
   Throughout Rounds 1–15, guesses consistently reproduced the battle mechanics through Turn 8 or 9, only to stumble by a single rounded percentage point on Leftovers or Grassy Terrain.

### B. Mirror Speed Ties and Action Order Sensitivity
Both players utilized identical species in lead slots: `p1a: Milotic` and `p2b: Milotic`, both sharing base Speed 81.
In doubles, Pokémon with identical base speeds have their order determined strictly by their Stat Points (and nature):
1. **Turn 1 Move Order**: `p1a: Milotic` uses `Coil` while `p2b: Milotic` uses `Ice Beam`. If a guess allocates even 1 SP more to `p2b: Milotic` than `p1a: Milotic`, the order reverses (`stops at 1.3: wanted |move|p1a: Milotic|Coil, got |move|p2b: Milotic|Ice Beam`).
2. **Turn 2 Simultaneous Switch Order**: On Turn 2, P1 switches to Incineroar and P2 switches to Metagross. Showdown executes simultaneous switches in the order of the active Pokémon's speed. If `p2b` is faster, Metagross switches before Incineroar; if `p1a` is faster, Incineroar switches before Metagross. This reversal triggers:
   `stops at 2.0: wanted |switch|p1b: Incineroar, got |switch|p2b: Metagross`.
3. **Turn 8–10 Weather Damage Resolution Order**: Showdown processes end-of-turn residual effects (Sandstorm damage) in descending order of current Speed.
   In Round 12 of the `usage` start:
   `reproduces through turn 9 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|51/100|[from] Sandstorm, got |-damage|p1a: Milotic|64/100|[from] Sandstorm`.
   Because Rillaboom (base 85) and Milotic (base 81) were active, a guess where Milotic's speed Stat Points exceeded Rillaboom's forced Milotic to take Sandstorm damage before Rillaboom, violating the public protocol.
