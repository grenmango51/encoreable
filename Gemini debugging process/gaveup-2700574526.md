## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Gave Up (Root Cause Analysis)

### 1. The Channel 0 Spectator Quantization Veil
In spectator view (Channel 0), **zero exact integer HP values exist for any Pokémon on either side**.
Under Champions mod rules:
$$\text{percentage} = \lfloor 100 \times \text{hp} / \text{maxhp} \rfloor$$
Every damage, recoil, and healing event is quantized into coarse integer percentages (`90/100`, `76/100`, `14/100`, `44/100`, `50/100g`, `53/100`, `74/100`, `66/100`, `69/100`, `75/100`). ### 3. The Turn 2 `50/100g` Green Border Quantization Trap

1. Max HP check: True max HP is $87 + 2 + 75 = 164$.
2. Life Orb recoil: $\lfloor 164 / 10 \rfloor = 16$ HP recoil $\implies 164 - 16 = 148$ HP.
3. Mirror Coat counter damage: Milotic took 41 HP special damage from Make It Rain $\implies$ Mirror Coat dealt $2 \times 41 = 82$ HP $\implies 148 - 82 = 73$ HP.
4. Spectator damage percentage: $\lfloor 100 \times 73 / 164 \rfloor = 44\%$ (`|-damage|p2b: Gholdengo|44/100`).
5. Grassy Terrain end-of-turn recovery: Grounded Gholdengo recovers $\lfloor 164 / 16 \rfloor = +10$ HP $\implies 73 + 10 = \mathbf{83}$ **HP**!

**The Discontinuous Protocol Border**:
- Ratio: $83 / 164 = 0.50609...$
