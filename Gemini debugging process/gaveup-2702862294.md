## 5. Root Cause Analysis: `both@spectator` (`PASS`) vs. `p1@p2` (`GAVE UP`)

### A. Why `both@spectator` Passed Cleanly in 12 Rounds (`62.6s`, 0 Fallback Rebuilds)
In `both@spectator` (Channel 0), both sides are inferred simultaneously (`known = [false, false]`) and both sides print percentage HP (`xx/100`):
1. **Unconstrained Turn 1 Start**: `both@spectator` initialized `p2:4 Gliscor` at `defaultSpread` (`32/0/0/0/2/32`, `Atk = 115`) and `p1:5 Whimsicott` at `32/0/0/2/0/32` (`HP = 167, Def = 105`). `Gliscor`'s `Acrobatics` dealt `138..164` damage (`got 8/100` in Round 1), avoiding `Focus Sash` and immediately giving `evidencePass` a valid Turn 1 scaffold.
2. **Continuous Single-Line Progress Without `fallback`**: In every round (R1–R11), `evidencePass` narrowed the percentage-HP constraints on the failing hit, `choose()` produced an exact HP path (`2..7 turns exact`) and handed forward `1..16` dice pins, and `next` moved cleanly (`unchanged = false`). Neither `backtracks` nor `fallback()` ever fired, and Round 12 reproduced Turn 6 in `0.1s`.

### B. Why `p1@p2` Alone Gave Up in the Cluster Sweep (`130.4s`, `stop: "searches"`)
In `slowest_gave_up.json` (lines 6113–6127), **`p1@p2`** (`channel = 2`, `known = [false, true]`) terminated with:
- `seconds: 130.4`
- `stop: "searches"`
- `limits: ["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches"]`

Notice both what triggered and what did **not** trigger:
- **`guessTries`, `stalled`, `variants`, `dieBacktracks`, and `fallbackTotal` did NOT trigger.** With only 3 seen Pokémon on P1 (`Whimsicott`, `Charizard`, `Garchomp`), `choose()` never ran out of `guessTries`; instead, `reconstruct` hit `maxProbes` (`probes`) and `maxBacktracks` (`backtracks`: `0` in `budgets[0]`, `2` in `budgets[1]`, `6` in `budgets[2..3]`), `fallback()` exhausted `fallbackRebuilds` (`8` / `32` / `128`) without clearing the stall, and `++level >= budgets.length` exhausted all 6 search starts (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, `widest default`, `widest usage`, `widest + wide`) in `130.4s` (`stop = "searches"`).

The structural difference between `p1@p2` and `both@spectator` creates three compounding bottlenecks on P1:
1. **Locked P2 Ground-Truth Spreads + Exact Channel 2 HP (`hp/maxhp`) Eliminate Two-Sided Slack**:
   - In `both@spectator`, P2's `Gliscor`, `Rillaboom`, `Raichu`, and `Gholdengo` only had to hit `%` HP brackets (`17/100`, `90/100`, `17/100`, `29/100`, `46/100`, `36/100`) and their own spreads could adjust alongside P1's.
   - In `p1@p2`, all 4 P2 Pokémon are fixed to their planted usage spreads and Channel 2 exposes **exact `hp/maxhp` integers** on every hit P2 takes (`Gliscor` Turn 2 `Heat Wave`, `Raichu` Turn 3 `Heat Wave`, `Gholdengo` Turn 5 `Earthquake`). Because `p2` HP is exact, `redrawTurn` can **only** vary P1's hidden `%` HP (`Whimsicott` Turn 1, `Charizard` Turns 1–2, `Garchomp` Turns 3–5) when blaming an earlier turn.
2. **Multi-Turn / Multi-Target Coupling Across Only 3 P1 Pokémon (`Charizard` and `Garchomp`)**:
   - `p1:0 Charizard` must simultaneously satisfy Turn 1 `Wood Hammer` (`72/100` `%` HP) and Turn 2 `Acrobatics` (`21/100` `%` HP) from locked P2 attackers while dealing exact integer `Heat Wave` damage to locked `p2b: Gliscor` (Turn 2) and locked `p2a: Raichu-Mega-Y` (Turn 3) under the 66-SP cap (`HP + Def + SpA <= 66`).
