### Root Cause 3: The 3-Way Speed Order Ambiguity on Turn 5
On Turn 5, under active Rain and active Tailwind:

Because both Pokémon have their speeds doubled by their respective field conditions ($2\times$ Tailwind vs $2\times$ Swift Swim), their relative turn order depends entirely on their unboosted raw speeds:
$$\text{Spe}_{\text{Rilla}} = 85 + \text{SP}_{\text{spe}} + 20 \quad \text{vs} \quad \text{Spe}_{\text{Bascu}} = 78 + \text{SP}_{\text{spe}} + 20$$
Unless Basculegion-F has at least 8 more Speed SP than Rillaboom, **Rillaboom moves before Basculegion-F**!

### Root Cause 4: Stamina Defense Boost Coupling on Turn 5
In Turn 5, Archaludon is attacked twice in the same turn:
1. Mega Dragonite hits Archaludon with Dragon Pulse:
