2. **Floette's Rare Non-Usage Random Spread (19 SpA SP)**:

3. **Giga Drain Multi-Turn Fractional Healing Feedback (Turns 4 & 5)**:

## 7. Search Limits Analysis & Architectural Recommendations

### Limits Triggered in the Cluster Sweep (`both@spectator` View)
The sweep recorded `limits: ["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches"]` at 2,023.5s:
- `backtracks`: Reached during PRNG dice alignment and damage branch evaluations across Turn 3 Overheat accuracy rolls and Turn 5 Flash Cannon critical hits.
- `fallbackRebuilds`: Hit repeatedly during Turn 5 fallback sweeps across 8, 32, and 128 rebuild budgets.
- `probes`: Exhausted hundreds of thousands of simulation probes exploring dead-end usage candidates for Floette and Scovillain (426,629 probes in trace).
- `searches`: Terminated when the orchestrator exhausted standard starts, wide starts, and fallback restart schedules across all four search tiers.

---

### Root Causes
1. **Coupled Multi-Variable Resistance to 1D Fallback**:
   - The engine's fallback perturbation mechanism assumes that if a candidate guess reaches Turn $T$, perturbing a single stat of one active Pokémon will bridge the divergence.
   - On Turn 4 and Turn 5 of this match, the divergence was governed by an intractable system of simultaneous equations:
     $$\text{Mirror Coat Damage} = 2 \times \text{Dazzling Gleam Damage}(\text{Floette}_{\text{SpA}}, \text{Milotic}_{\text{SpD}})$$
     $$\text{Giga Drain Recovery} = \lfloor \text{Damage}(Scovillain_{\text{SpA}}, Eelektross_{\text{SpD}}) / 2 \rfloor \pmod{Scovillain_{\text{maxHP}}}$$
   - Changing Floette's SpA without simultaneously adjusting Milotic's SpD and Scovillain's HP broke prior damage lines. A 1D search in an $N$-dimensional coupled grid is mathematically incapable of finding the witness spread.
2. **Usage Prior Penalty on Non-Usage Mimic Seeds**:
   - Both Floette-Eternal (`{ hp: 15, atk: 5, def: 0, spa: 19, spd: 20, spe: 7 }`) and Excadrill (`{ hp: 4, atk: 4, def: 19, spa: 25, spd: 10, spe: 4 }`) were planted with random 66-SP spreads far in the tails of competitive distributions.
   - Candidate generation heavily weights Smogon usage spreads, continually generating 32 SpA Floette spreads that produce $22/100$ Mirror Coat damage instead of $32/100$.
3. **Premature Exact HP Hand-off on Quantized Channels**:
   - In Rounds 11 and 15, the evidence engine deduced and handed on exact current/max HP values for Scovillain (`161` and `157`) based on unverified percentage rounding guesses.
   - When the re-simulation ran with these exact figures, integer truncation caused Turn 3 damage lines to reject otherwise valid candidate branches (`13/161` vs `14/161`).

---

### Architectural Recommendations

1. **Mirror Coat / Counter Coupled Constraint Propagator**:
   - *Problem*: Mirror Coat and Counter enforce a strict $2\times$ deterministic relationship between the damage taken by the user and the damage dealt to the target. Currently, the evidence engine treats both damage events as independent hits.
   - *Fix*: In `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs`, hook `Mirror Coat` and `Counter` to enforce:
     $$\Delta\text{HP}_{\text{target}} = 2 \times \Delta\text{HP}_{\text{user}}$$
     When Floette takes $112\text{ HP}$ from Mirror Coat, immediately constrain Milotic's damage taken from Floette's attack to exactly $56\text{ HP}$, restricting Floette's Special Attack candidates directly to those that produce $56\text{ HP}$ into Milotic.

2. **Drain / Recoil Dependent Bulk Bounds**:
   - *Problem*: Giga Drain heals the user for $50\%$ of the damage dealt. The solver repeatedly guessed bulk spreads for Scovillain that were incompatible with the percentage healed from Eelektross.
   - *Fix*: In `evidence.mjs` (`drainChange`), tie the percentage increase of the drainer directly to the victim's damage dealt:
     $$\Delta\%_{\text{drainer}} \times \text{maxHP}_{\text{drainer}} \approx \frac{1}{2} (\Delta\%_{\text{victim}} \times \text{maxHP}_{\text{victim}})$$
     Reject any candidate pair $(\text{maxHP}_{\text{drainer}}, \text{maxHP}_{\text{victim}})$ whose integer rounding cannot satisfy the observed spectator percentage jump.

3. **Multi-Stat Pairwise Fallback for Coupled Exchanges**:
   - *Problem*: Fallback rebuilds probe single stats in isolation (`Scovillain atk 32`, `Floette spa 32`). When two Pokémon are coupled through an exchange (Floette Dazzling Gleam $\to$ Milotic Mirror Coat), single-stat probes never find a valid state.
   - *Fix*: In `infer.mjs`, when a turn divergence involves a coupled move (`Mirror Coat`, `Counter`, `Giga Drain`, `Leech Life`, `Pain Split`), allow fallback to perform a 2D grid probe over the coupled attacker-defender stat pair $(\text{Atk/SpA}_{\text{source}}, \text{Def/SpD}_{\text{target}})$ before exhausting its budget.

4. **Guard Against Premature Exact HP Denominator Hand-offs**:
   - *Problem*: In `both@spectator`, handing on exact HP paths where the max HP denominator is only an unproven guess (`161` or `157`) causes spurious line rejections on integer division boundaries.
   - *Fix*: In `infer.mjs`, ensure that a Pokémon's max HP is only handed on as exact when its Stat Points in HP are rigorously bounded to a single integer value, rather than a wide range.
