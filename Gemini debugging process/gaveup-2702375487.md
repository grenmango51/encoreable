## 8. The Core Deadlock: Turn 4 Leech Life, Drain, and Rocky Helmet Recoil Mechanics

### 1. The Three Interdependent Equations on Turn 4
In Turn 4, a single attack—Golisopod's `Leech Life` on Incineroar—generates three tightly coupled protocol lines that must be matched simultaneously:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Line 1: } & \text{|-damage|p2b: Incineroar|46/100} \\
\text{Line 2: } & \text{|-heal|p1b: Golisopod|98/100|[from] drain|[of] p2b: Incineroar} \\
\text{Line 3: } & \text{|-damage|p1b: Golisopod|82/100|[from] item: Rocky Helmet|[of] p2b: Incineroar}
\end{aligned}$$

The mathematical relationships connecting these three lines are:
1. **Damage Dealt ($D$)**:
   $$D = \left\lfloor \left( \left\lfloor \frac{22 \times 80 \times \text{Atk}_{\text{Golisopod}}}{\text{Def}_{\text{Incineroar}} \times 50} \right\rfloor + 2 \right) \times 1.5 \times \frac{\text{roll}}{100} \right\rfloor$$
   Incineroar's observed health percentage is:
   $$\text{Pct}_{\text{Incineroar}} = \left\lfloor 100 \times \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{Incineroar, max}} - D}{\text{HP}_{\text{Incineroar, max}}} \right\rfloor = 46\%$$

2. **Drain Healing ($H_{\text{drain}}$)**:
   $$H_{\text{drain}} = \left\lfloor \frac{D}{2} \right\rfloor$$
   Golisopod's post-heal health percentage is:
   $$\text{Pct}_{\text{Golisopod, heal}} = \left\lfloor 100 \times \frac{\min(\text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod, T3}} + H_{\text{drain}}, \text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod, max}})}{\text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod, max}}} \right\rfloor = 98\%$$

3. **Rocky Helmet Recoil ($R$)**:
   $$R = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod, max}}}{6} \right\rfloor$$
   Golisopod's post-recoil health percentage is:
   $$\text{Pct}_{\text{Golisopod, final}} = \left\lfloor 100 \times \frac{\min(\text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod, T3}} + H_{\text{drain}}, \text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod, max}}) - R}{\text{HP}_{\text{Golisopod, max}}} \right\rfloor = 82\%$$

### 2. The Narrow 1% Discrepancy Trap
In `both@spectator`, because neither Golisopod's nor Incineroar's true Stat Points are known, the inference samples candidate stat combinations:
- In Round 6:
  `wanted |-heal|p1b: Golisopod|98/100, got |-heal|p1b: Golisopod|95/100` (or `96/100`)
- In Round 7, Round 18, Round 19, Round 20:
  `wanted |-damage|p1b: Golisopod|82/100|[from] item: Rocky Helmet, got |-damage|p1b: Golisopod|81/100`

Why did almost all candidate guesses land on `81/100` instead of `82/100`?
- For Golisopod with planted HP 13 SP (max HP = 163):
  - Rocky Helmet recoil $R = \lfloor 163 / 6 \rfloor = 27$ HP.
  - If Golisopod healed to 159 HP ($97.5\% \to 98\%$ display), then $159 - 27 = 132$ HP.
  - $132 / 163 = 0.80981... \to \mathbf{80\%}$ or $\mathbf{81\%}$!
  - To reach $82/100$, Golisopod's post-recoil HP must be at least $\lceil 0.815 \times 163 \rceil = 133$ or $134$ HP ($134 / 163 = 0.822 \to 82\%$).
  - For post-recoil HP to reach 134 HP, Golisopod's post-drain HP must be at least $134 + 27 = 161$ HP!
  - But reaching 161 HP requires Leech Life to deal at least $2 \times (161 - 107) = 108$ damage!
  - If Leech Life deals 108 damage to Incineroar (max HP = 194):
    $194 - 108 = 86$ HP $\implies 86 / 194 = 44.3\% \to \mathbf{44/100}$ (which contradicts the expected `46/100`)!

This creates an extremely razor-thin feasibility window where:
- Damage must be low enough so Incineroar stays at $\ge 45.5\%$ (`46/100`).
- Yet damage must be high enough so $50\%$ drain heal lifts Golisopod to $\ge 97.5\%$ (`98/100`) and leaves $\ge 81.5\%$ (`82/100`) after 27 HP Rocky Helmet recoil.

### 3. Exhaustion of the Joint Guess Picker (`choose`)
When the reconstruct diff fails on:
`reproduces through turn 3 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Golisopod|82/100, got |-damage|p1b: Golisopod|81/100`
`infer.mjs` invokes the joint guess picker:
`choose(0, ...)`
The picker attempts to sample a joint assignment of Stat Points across all active Pokémon.
Because:
1. Golisopod's Attack, Defense, and HP,
2. Incineroar's Attack, Defense, and HP,
3. The damage rolls of both Turn 3 Throat Chop and Turn 4 Leech Life
must align perfectly to satisfy all three protocol lines, the combinatorial search space of joint guesses is enormous.
The picker exhausted its allotted candidate tries (`tries = 32 * S * W` hitting 0, adding `limits.add('guessTries')`).
Without a joint pin, `choose` returned `proof = null`, handing forward zero dice pins.

### 4. Failure of the Single-Stat `fallback` Mechanism
When progress regressed, `infer.mjs` invoked `fallback` on Turn 4:
`fallback on turn 4: |-damage|p2b: Incineroar|46/100`
`fallback` systematically alters one stat of one Pokémon at a time:
- Testing `Incineroar def 0` through `def 32`:
  - Higher Def (e.g., `def 14`, `def 16`): Leech Life deals less damage $\to$ stops at `wanted 46/100, got 53/100` or `55/100`.
  - Lower Def (e.g., `def 4` through `def 11`): Leech Life deals more damage $\to$ Incineroar damage matches, but post-recoil HP produces `got 81/100` instead of `82/100`.
- Testing `Golisopod atk 31`: stops at `wanted 46/100, got 39/100`.
- Testing `Incineroar hp 10` through `hp 32`: stops at `wanted 46/100, got 41/100`.
- Summary in trace: `fallback on turn 4: nothing gets past the line in 128 rebuilds, 297.4s`.

Because `fallback` is designed to fix single-stat discrepancies, it is fundamentally incapable of resolving a multi-variable coupled collision where changing any single stat immediately breaks another protocol line.
