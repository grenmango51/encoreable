## 6. Why `both@spectator` Failed (103 Rounds, 430 Rebuilds, 850,536 Probes, Searches Limit Hit)

### A. Equal Base Speed Ambiguity & Mirror Rillaboom Collisions

### B. High-Order Recoil & Grassy Terrain Percentage Discretization
In spectator mode, all HP values are quantized to whole percentage integers ($0$–$100\%$). The battle features four distinct, mathematically coupled recoil and healing chains:

1. **Turn 2 Resisted Wood Hammer & Grassy Terrain**:
   - P2 Rillaboom hits P1 Rillaboom with Wood Hammer.
   - Recoil taken is $\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor$.
   - Both take Grassy Terrain healing ($\lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 16 \rfloor$).
   - A difference of 1 HP in either Rillaboom's Max HP or Defense changes the recoil display between `88/100` and `89/100`, or the terrain heal between `71/100` and `72/100`.
2. **Turn 5 Coaching + Ice Shard + Close Combat + Grassy Terrain**:
   - Coaching-boosted Mega Baxcalibur takes Close Combat from Mega Staraptor, dropping to spectator `45/100`.
   - Baxcalibur then heals $+ \lfloor \text{MaxHP} / 16 \rfloor$ from Grassy Terrain.
   - In candidate rebuilds, Baxcalibur's resulting HP constantly flipped between `64/100` and `65/100`:
     ```
     round 5: reproduces through turn 3 - wanted |-heal|p2a: Baxcalibur|64/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2a: Baxcalibur|66/100
     round 9: reproduces through turn 3 - wanted |-heal|p2a: Baxcalibur|64/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2a: Baxcalibur|65/100
     ```
3. **Turn 8 Massive Wood Hammer onto Focus Sash Arcanine**:
   - P2 Rillaboom attacks P1 Arcanine-Hisui with Wood Hammer.
   - Arcanine-Hisui is brought from 100% down to its Focus Sash threshold ($1$ HP).
   - Showdown calculates Wood Hammer recoil based on the **actual damage dealt to the victim**:
     $$\text{damage dealt} = \text{CurrentHP}_{\text{Arcanine}} - 1 = \text{MaxHP}_{\text{Arcanine}} - 1$$
     $$\text{recoil} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Arcanine}} - 1}{3} \right\rfloor$$
   - This recoil is subtracted from P2 Rillaboom's HP:
     $$\text{HP}_{\text{Rillaboom, final}} = \text{CurrentHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}} - \left\lfloor \frac{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Arcanine}} - 1}{3} \right\rfloor$$
   - In spectator view:
     $$\left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP}_{\text{Rillaboom, final}}}{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}}} \times 100 \right\rfloor = 71\%$$
   - In `both@spectator`, Arcanine's Max HP and Rillaboom's Max HP are simultaneously unknown variables. Candidate spreads repeatedly generated `64/100`, `69/100`, or `72/100` instead of `71/100`:
     ```
     round 13: reproduces through turn 7 - wanted |-damage|p2b: Rillaboom|72/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2b: Rillaboom|64/100|[from] Recoil
     round 16: reproduces through turn 7 - wanted |-damage|p2b: Rillaboom|72/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2b: Rillaboom|69/100|[from] Recoil
     ```

### C. Pin Failure in `paths()` and Reset of Verified Turns
When the simulation reached Turn 7/8:
- The quantized percentage mismatch on Wood Hammer recoil or Grassy Terrain recovery caused `evidencePass({ exact: true })` to execute `paths(raw)`.
- Inside `evidence.mjs` (`paths()`), recoil damage transitions must strictly match the damage dealt:
  ```js
  if (dealt && (amounts[j] !== dealt.amount || tag % SPAN !== dealt.v)) continue;
  ```
- Because neither side's HP was anchored by an exact integer observation, no candidate path could satisfy the simultaneous integer percentage bounds for Arcanine Max HP, Rillaboom Max HP, and Wood Hammer damage.
- As a result, `paths()` returned `null`.
- When `proof.exact` returned `null`:
  - `ctx.exact` became `null`.
  - **Zero exact turns and zero dice were handed on to subsequent rounds**:
    ```
    round 8: evidence and new guesses, 30.1s (0 turns exact, 0 dice handed on)
    round 13: evidence and new guesses, 6.5s (0 turns exact, 0 dice handed on)
    ```
- Without handed-on exact dice and turn pins, the next round selected spreads independently via `closestSpread()`, causing the simulation to desynchronize as early as Turn 3 or Turn 4:
  ```
  round 14: reproduces through turn 3 - wanted |-heal|p2a: Baxcalibur|64/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2a: Baxcalibur|65/100
  ```
