- The damage dealt to each target $k$ is calculated independently using that target's Def/SpD, nature, and type effectiveness:
  $$\text{Damage}_k = \left\lfloor \left( \frac{2 \times 50 / 5 + 2}{50} \times 65 \times \frac{\text{SpA}_{\text{Belli}}}{\text{SpD}_k} + 2 \right) \times \text{Mod}_k \right\rfloor$$

  $$\text{Heal}_k = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Damage}_k}{2} \right\rfloor$$
- Bellibolt's displayed spectator percentage is quantized after summing:
  $$\text{DisplayPct}_{\text{Belli}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{CurrentHP} + \sum \text{Heal}_k}{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Belli}}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
