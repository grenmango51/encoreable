## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Rounding Across 4 Interacting Leads
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- Every damage, heal, and recoil event is rounded by Showdown's `Math.ceil(currentHP * 100 / maxHP)`.
- In Turns 1 and 2, all 4 lead Pokémon (`p1:Garchomp`, `p1:Rillaboom`, `p2:Incineroar`, `p2:Clefable`) are active and exchanging attacks simultaneously.
- Every event has 4 to 6 unknown variables:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk}, \text{unknown Def}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown maxHP} \rightarrow \text{rounded to } \lceil \cdot \rceil\%$$
- Because multiple combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP)` yield the identical percentage strings (`88/100`, `54/100`, `23/100`, `29/100`, `60/100`), the initial evidence pass cannot eliminate candidate spreads.

### Root Cause 2: Non-Linear Coupling of Turn 2 Damage, Recoil, Berry, and Terrain
In Turn 2, Rillaboom's Wood Hammer against Clefable links 4 distinct percentage thresholds in a single chain:
1. **Wood Hammer Damage on Clefable**:
   - Log requires: `|-damage|p2b: Clefable|9/100`
   - Condition: $8 < \frac{(M_{\text{Clef}} - D) \times 100}{M_{\text{Clef}}} \le 9 \implies 0.91 \le \frac{D}{M_{\text{Clef}}} < 0.92$
   - If $D \ge M_{\text{Clef}}$ (Clefable faints at `0 fnt`): Clefable never consumes its Sitrus Berry, never uses Moonblast, Garchomp never faints, and the entire battle diverges.
2. **Sitrus Berry Trigger and Recovery on Clefable**:
   - Clefable must drop to $\le 50\%$ HP to trigger Sitrus Berry.
   - Heals $\lfloor M_{\text{Clef}} / 4 \rfloor$. Log requires: `|-heal|p2b: Clefable|34/100|[from] item: Sitrus Berry`.
3. **Wood Hammer Recoil on Rillaboom**:
   - Recoil is $R = \lfloor D / 3 \rfloor$.
   - Log requires: `|-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|71/100|[from] Recoil`.
   - Condition: $70 < \frac{(M_{\text{Rilla}} - R) \times 100}{M_{\text{Rilla}}} \le 71 \implies 0.29 \le \frac{R}{M_{\text{Rilla}}} < 0.30$.
4. **End-of-Turn Grassy Terrain Healing**:
   - Heals $\lfloor M_{\text{Rilla}} / 16 \rfloor$.
   - Log requires: `|-heal|p1b: Rillaboom|76/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`.
   - Clefable heals $\lfloor M_{\text{Clef}} / 16 \rfloor$. Log requires: `|-heal|p2b: Clefable|40/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`.
5. **Subsequent Action in Turn 2**:
   - Clefable's Moonblast must deal enough damage to KO Garchomp (`0 fnt`).
   - Garchomp's Earth Power must deal enough damage to KO Incineroar (`0 fnt`).

### Root Cause 3: Inefficacy of 1-Dimensional Fallback Probes in a Multi-Dimensional Local Minimum
When candidate guesses in spectator view reach Turn 2:
- The default guess picker sets Clefable's HP to 10 SP (180 HP instead of 202) and Rillaboom's Attack to 14 SP (174 Atk instead of 165).
- Rebuilding with this guess stops at Turn 2, index 8:
  `wanted |-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|71/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1b: Rillaboom|73/100|[from] Recoil`
- `fallback` triggers on Turn 2 and tests single-stat perturbations:
  1. **Probing Rillaboom HP**:
     - At $HP = 0, 5, 10$: Wood Hammer recoil produces `69/100` or `70/100` (fails at 2.8).
     - At $HP = 15$: Wood Hammer recoil lands on `71/100`, but Grassy Terrain heal lands on `77/100` instead of `76/100` (fails at 2.13).
     - At $HP \ge 20$: Wood Hammer recoil produces `72/100` or `73/100` (fails at 2.8).
  2. **Probing Clefable Def**:
     - At $Def = 2, 5, 10$: Because Clefable's HP is guessed at 10 (180 HP) and Rillaboom's Atk is guessed at 14 (174 Atk), Wood Hammer deals $\ge 180$ damage, causing Clefable to faint at `0 fnt` (fails at 2.5).
     - At $Def \ge 19$: Wood Hammer deals too little damage, so recoil on Rillaboom lands on `73/100` or `74/100` (fails at 2.8).
  3. **Probing Rillaboom Atk or Clefable HP**:
     - Adjusting Rillaboom Atk breaks Turn 1 High Horsepower damage on Incineroar (`54/100`).
     - Adjusting Clefable HP breaks Turn 1 Moonblast damage and Grassy Terrain percentages.
- **Why Fallback Fails**:
  To reproduce Turn 2, the search must simultaneously move **four variables across two Pokémon**:
  $$\text{Clefable HP} \uparrow (\sim 32), \quad \text{Clefable Def} \downarrow (\sim 2), \quad \text{Rillaboom Atk} \downarrow (\sim 5), \quad \text{Rillaboom HP} \uparrow (\sim 32)$$
  Because `fallback` evaluates single-stat variations in isolation (and 2-stat variations only for Speed orders via `escort`), every single variation fails to pass Turn 2.
- `fallback` exhausts its per-line limit (`FALLBACK_REBUILDS = 8`) and total budget (`FALLBACK_TOTAL`), logging `fallbackRebuilds` and returning `null`.

### Root Cause 4: Search Escalation, Restarts, and Limit Exhaustion
1. When `fallback` returns `null` without moving `picks`, `infer.mjs` increments `level`.
2. When `level >= budgets.length`, the initial `default` start gives up with `stop = 'searches'`.
