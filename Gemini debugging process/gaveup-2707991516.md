$$\text{Drain} = \left\lceil\frac{\text{DamageDealt}}{2}\right\rceil$$
When the defending target is knocked out, $\text{DamageDealt}$ is bounded by the target's current remaining HP:
$$\text{DamageDealt} = \min(\text{RawDamage}, \text{CurrentHP}_{\text{Indeedee}})$$

$$\text{MaxDrain} = \left\lceil\frac{\text{MaxHP}_{\text{Indeedee}}}{2}\right\rceil$$
