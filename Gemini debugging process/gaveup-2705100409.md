### A. The Turn 1 / Turn 2 Trick Room Speed-Tie Proof

1. **Turn 1 (Normal Speed)**: `Sinistcha` moved before `Sylveon` $\implies$ $\text{Speed}(\text{Sinistcha}) \ge \text{Speed}(\text{Sylveon})$ (`90 + Sinistcha_spe >= 80 + Sylveon_spe`).
2. **Turn 2 (Trick Room)**: `Sinistcha` moved before `Sylveon` $\implies$ $\text{Speed}(\text{Sinistcha}) \le \text{Speed}(\text{Sylveon})$ (`90 + Sinistcha_spe <= 80 + Sylveon_spe`).

Combining both verified order cuts from `speed.mjs` forces an **exact Speed tie** (`Sinistcha.spe === Sylveon.spe`), i.e.:
$$\text{Sylveon}_{\text{spe}} = \text{Sinistcha}_{\text{spe}} + 10, \quad \text{with } \text{Sinistcha}_{\text{spe}} \in [0, 22] \text{ and } \text{Sylveon}_{\text{spe}} \in [10, 32]$$
