## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up (Root Cause Analysis & Search Pathology)

In `both@spectator`, neither player's team is known (`known = [false, false]`). All active Pokémon have unconstrained stat points that must be inferred purely from coarse integer percentage brackets. This resulted in an unresolvable search bottleneck on Turn 5:

### 1. The Recoil Arithmetic Collision at Turn 5
- On Turn 5, `p2b: Incineroar` strikes `p1b: Gholdengo` with `Flare Blitz`.
- Gholdengo has already endured:
  - Turn 1: Delphox Heat Wave (`|-damage|p1a: Gholdengo|23/100` in mimic spectator view).
  - Turn 1: Life Orb recoil from Shadow Ball (10% max HP).
  - Turn 5: A second Life Orb recoil from Shadow Ball (10% max HP).
- Gholdengo's remaining HP prior to the Flare Blitz impact is therefore tiny (between 4% and 8% of max HP).
- In Pokémon Showdown (`runtime/sim/battle-actions.ts`), Flare Blitz recoil is defined as:
  $$\text{recoil} = \left\lfloor \frac{33}{100} \times \text{damageDealt} \right\rfloor$$
  where $\text{damageDealt}$ is strictly bounded by the target's current remaining HP.
- In the ground truth replay, Incineroar's recoil reduces its HP to **`49/100`**:
  `wanted |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|49/100|[from] Recoil`
- In every reconstructed simulation round, the candidate guesses for Delphox SpA, Gholdengo SpD/HP, and Incineroar Atk/Def produced **`48/100`**:
  `got |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|48/100|[from] Recoil`
- The difference between `49/100` and `48/100` represents a single integer HP difference on Incineroar (e.g., 99 HP remaining vs 98 HP remaining out of 202 max HP).

### 2. The Multi-Turn Dependency Network
To produce `49/100` instead of `48/100` on Incineroar's recoil at Turn 5:
1. Gholdengo must have had slightly less remaining HP when Flare Blitz hit, OR Incineroar must have had a slightly different max HP / current HP before recoil.
2. But Incineroar's current HP entering Turn 5 was governed by Turn 5 Kommo-o's `Clanging Scales` spread damage (`wanted 55/100` vs `51/100`).
3. Kommo-o's Clanging Scales damage was boosted by Turn 4 `Clangorous Soul` (+1 SpA), and depended on Kommo-o's SpA and Incineroar's SpD.
4. Gholdengo's remaining HP entering Turn 5 was governed by Turn 1 Delphox Heat Wave damage and Gholdengo's Life Orb integer truncation (`floor(HP / 10)`).
5. Any candidate stat perturbation that shifted Delphox's SpA or Gholdengo's SpD altered Turn 1 damage from `23/100` to `25/100` or `27/100`, violating the Turn 1 percentage bracket.
