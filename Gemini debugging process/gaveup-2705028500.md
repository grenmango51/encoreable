### Budget Exhaustion in `choose()` and Search Circling
1. **Budget Exhaustion in `choose()`**:
   - In spectator view, 8 Pokémon are active (`seq.length = 8`).
   - The recursive function `choose(n, cond)` in `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` has a total try budget of `tries = 32 * S * W = 64`.
   - Each recursive node down the tree invokes `await settle(...)`, consuming 2 tries per depth level (`8 * 2 = 16` tries per branch).
   - Because the 4-way and 8-way mutual dependencies require precise alignment of HP, Def, and Atk values, heuristic spread selections fail `evidencePass({ exact: true })`.
   - `tries` reaches 0, setting `limits.add('guessTries')`, and `choose()` returns `null`.
2. **Loss of Pins and Fallback to Uncoupled Guess**:
   - When `choose()` returns `null`, lines 1072–1079 in `infer.mjs` execute:
     `exact = null; pins = null;`
     `next[s][i] = closestSpread(kn, aimed(...));`
   - All exact turns and RNG dice pins are discarded. Each Pokémon independently takes its nearest spread from unpinned knowledge.
3. **Catastrophic Desynchronization**:
   - Without exact turns and dice pins, the subsequent round's rebuild desynchronizes on earlier turns (Turn 4 Leftovers `38/100 vs 39/100`, Turn 2 Recoil `56/100 vs 57/100`, or Turn 0 EQ `28/100 vs 43/100`).
4. **Triggering Expensive Fallback Rebuilds**:
   - Because those earlier diffs were previously seen, `again = failed.has(markOf(diff))` evaluates to `true`.
   - Fallback executes up to 8 rebuilds across Stat Point variants on Turn 5 (34.7s) and Turn 7 (42.3s), hitting `fallbackRebuilds`.
   - Guess mixing (`mixOf`) combines guesses, causing the rebuild to reset back to Turn 0 / Turn 1.
5. **The 211-Round Loop**:
   - The search cycles: advancing from Turn 0 to Turn 8/9 -> failing exact proof in `choose()` -> losing pins -> collapsing to Turn 4, 2, or 0 -> triggering fallback rebuilds -> repeating.
   - When the default start stalls (`stalled >= maxRounds`), inference restarts from `usage`, then restarts with `wide`, then restarts with `widest`.
   - Total execution across all restarts reaches 211 rounds, 874 rebuilds, 1,418,970 probes, and 9,825.7 seconds (2.73 hours) before terminating with `stop="stalled"`.
