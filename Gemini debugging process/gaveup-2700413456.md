## 6. Spectator Search Pathology & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled (`both@spectator`)

### A. Root Cause 1: Untargeted Spread Move `Heat Wave` & Unsteered `randomFoe()` Draw (182 / 276 Rounds = 65.9%)
When `p2b: Charizard` executes `Heat Wave` (`target: "allAdjacentFoes"`), neither `MimicPlayer` nor `reconstruct.mjs`'s `choiceFor()` specifies a target slot (`CHOOSABLE_TARGETS` excludes `"allAdjacentFoes"`).
