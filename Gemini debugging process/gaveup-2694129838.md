## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Swampert`'s exact planted spread (`15/10/8/7/14/12`, `MaxHP = 190`, `Mega Atk = 198`) and `Politoed`'s exact `Spe = 90` are fixed constants, and Channel 1 prints Swampert's exact HP integers (`135/190` after recoil, `146/190` after Grassy Terrain). Observing `190 - 135 = 55` exact recoil immediately locks `Wave Crash` damage to `{166, 167}` (since `Math.round(165 * 0.33) = 54`), which forces `Indeedee-F` `MaxHP >= 176` (`hp >= 31`, and uniquely `hp = 32` / `MaxHP = 177` for `6/100`), completely bypassing the `Indeedee hp = 29` trap.
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Indeedee-F`'s exact `MaxHP = 177` (`hp = 32`), `Def = 128` (`def = 32`), `Spe = 105`, and `Gardevoir`'s `Spe = 132` are fixed constants, and Channel 2 prints exact HP (`11/177` after `Wave Crash`, `55/177` after `Sitrus Berry`). Observing `177 - 11 = 166` exact damage immediately determines `recoil = 55`, so `Swampert`'s `1.12` (`71/100`) and `1.16` (`76/100`) solve cleanly on the first pass.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Permanently Anchor `held` on `hp` When Fixing a `def`/`spd` Survival Failure (`1.9` $\to$ `1.16`)**:
   In Round 6/5, `fallback` on `1.9` only needed to raise `Indeedee def` from `22` to `32`, yet `held.set('p2:1', 29/0/32/0/0/5)` froze `Indeedee`'s `hp = 29` (`MaxHP = 174`) for every subsequent `choose()` pass. Because `aimFor` only probes quantiles `{0.25, 0.5, 0.75}` and `held` forces `hp = 29` first, `choose()` never escaped `MaxHP <= 175` to test `hp = 32` (`MaxHP = 177`). Clearing or relaxing `held` when a later line on the same turn (`1.16`) exposes a contradiction—or including range endpoints (`0.0`, `1.0`) in `choose()` when `exact` fails—would break the deadlock.
2. **Guard `replayWith` `same` Check Against Percentage Rounding Equivalence Under Frozen Dice**:
   In all six starts, `fallback` skipped rebuilding `Swampert hp 15` (`MaxHP = 190`) because `replayWith` replayed Round 7's frozen `recoil = 54` roll, where both `148/191` and `147/190` truncate to `77/100`. Comparing exact HP tokens (or bypassing `same` when changing a Pokémon's `hp` SP on a turn that fails an HP/heal percentage line for that same Pokémon) would prevent `fallback` from falsely skipping ground-truth `hp` shifts (`15` vs `16`).
3. **Prioritize Cross-Pokémon Recoil/Drain Dependencies in `fallback` Pairwise Search**:
   When a recoil (`1.12`) or recoil-coupled residual (`1.16`) line fails, the damage dealt depends on the attacker's `atk` and the victim's `hp`/`def`, while the recoil percentage depends on the attacker's `hp`. Running pairwise perturbations between the failing Pokémon's `hp` and the target's `hp`/`def` before spending 100+ rebuilds on unrelated active Pokémon (`Politoed`, `Gardevoir`, `Rillaboom`) would resolve two-sided percentage deadlocks within `budgets[0]`.
