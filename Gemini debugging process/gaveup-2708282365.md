### 2. The Evidence Pass Blind Spot
- Hard Rule 2 & the design of `evidencePass`: a line a rebuild stops on proves nothing; evidence is collected only from turns the scaffold reproduces (Turns 0, 1, 2).
- Garchomp was benched on Turn 0 and Turn 1, and only entered via Parting Shot at the end of Turn 2.
- Garchomp took no damage, used no moves, and underwent no speed sorts in Turns 0–2.
- Consequently, the evidence pass learned **zero** information about Garchomp's Speed. Garchomp's Speed domain in knowledge remained `[0, 32]`.
- When `closestSpread` selected the next round's guess, Garchomp's Speed did not move from `spe: 2`.

### 3. The Fallback Roll-Dropping Deadlock (`reach(base)[0] < t`)
Because evidence did not move the guess and the rebuild repeatedly stopped on Turn 3 line 5 (`failed.has(mark)`), `infer.mjs` line 1091 called `fallback(seen, start, ...)` on Turn 3.
However, inside `fallback`:
1. Fallback initializes `handed = handOn(built, null)` (line 492).
   - In round 4's main rebuild, Turn 2's Light of Ruin damage roll had succeeded because `choose` found an exact proof whose `proof.rolls` had pinned the roll.
   - Because `handOn(built, null)` is called with `rolls = null`, `handed` only contained substitutions from `built.pins.subs`. The proof roll for Turn 2 was **not** present in `handed.subs`.
2. Fallback runs `base = await rebuild(start, budget)` (lines 604 and 606) with `maxBacktracks = 0`.
3. Without the pinned roll and with zero backtracks allowed, the simulator rolled the natural damage die for Floette's Light of Ruin against Volcarona (whose guessed SpD was 0 / raw 125).
4. The unbacktracked damage roll resulted in:
   - Target log: `|-damage|p2a: Floette|68/100|[from] Recoil`
   - `base` rebuild: `|-damage|p2a: Floette|69/100|[from] Recoil`
5. `base` failed at **Turn 2, line 7** (`reach(base) = [2, 7]`).
6. Fallback executes line 614:

     const at = base.report.diffs[0];
