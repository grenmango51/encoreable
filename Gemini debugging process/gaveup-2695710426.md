## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled and Gave Up (Root Cause Analysis & Search Pathology)

In `both@spectator`, neither player's team is known (`known = [false, false]`). All 8 active Pokémon possess unknown stat distributions that must be inferred purely from coarse integer percentage brackets. This created a lethal two-tiered oscillation trap:

### 1. Bottleneck 1: Turn 5 Rock Slide Flinch vs Paralysis Check and PRNG Stream Sensitivity
- On Turn 5, `p2b: Garchomp` executes `Rock Slide` targeting `p1b: Sneasler`.
- In Pokémon Showdown (`runtime/sim/pokemon.ts`, lines 1210–1260 and `runtime/sim/battle-actions.ts`):
  1. Showdown checks for **flinch** before checking for full paralysis.
  2. Rock Slide has a 30% chance to flinch:
     ```typescript
     if (this.battle.randomChance(30, 100)) {
       pokemon.addVolatile('flinch');
     }
     ```
  3. When a Pokémon is flinched, its turn is canceled immediately:
     ```typescript
     this.battle.add('cant', pokemon, 'flinch');
     return false;
     ```
  4. Only if the flinch check **fails** (70% probability) does Showdown proceed to check the 25% chance of full paralysis:
     ```typescript
     if (this.battle.randomChance(1, 4)) {
       this.battle.add('cant', pokemon, 'par');
       return false;
     }
     ```
- In the ground-truth replay, Sneasler **did not flinch**, but was **fully paralyzed**:
  `|cant|p1b: Sneasler|par`
- Because PRNG draws are strictly sequential, any minute variation in candidate stats in Turns 1–4—such as an unpinned stat perturbing Dragonite's Dragon Pulse damage roll, Sneasler's Fake Out roll, Zap Cannon damage, or Grassy Terrain recovery thresholds—alters the total number of PRNG draws executed prior to Turn 5.
- When the PRNG counter is displaced by even one draw, the accuracy/secondary effect check on Turn 5 samples a different pseudo-random roll. In 30% of such draws, the roll is $< 30$, causing **Rock Slide to flinch Sneasler**.
- Showdown outputs:
  `|cant|p1b: Sneasler|flinch`
- But Reconstruct requires:
  `wanted |cant|p1b: Sneasler|par, got |cant|p1b: Sneasler|flinch`
- In the trace log, Reconstruct search levels 1, 2, and 3 routinely failed to discover the narrow PRNG dice path that avoided the flinch and satisfied the paralysis check. Only **search level 4** (the deepest search budget) explored sufficient dice combinations to reliably bypass Turn 5 and proceed into Turn 6+.

### 2. Bottleneck 2: Turn 8 Wood Hammer Recoil Percentage Discrepancy on Rillaboom
- When search level 4 successfully passed Turn 5, it encountered a second barrier on Turn 8:
  - On Turn 8, `p2a: Rillaboom` executes `Wood Hammer` on `p1b: Basculegion`.
  - Wood Hammer inflicts 1/3 recoil damage based on the damage dealt to Basculegion.
  - Basculegion is brought down from full HP to its Focus Sash (1 HP).
  - The recoil damage dealt back to Rillaboom is recorded in spectator percentages:
    `wanted |-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|61/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|62/100|[from] Recoil`
    *(or in other search branches: `wanted 62/100, got 63/100` / `65/100`)*.
- Rillaboom's recoil damage depends on:
  - Rillaboom's Attack Stat Points (and Miracle Seed / Grassy Terrain boosts).

### 3. The Fallback Oscillation Trap and Catastrophic Collapse
The fundamental reason the inference gave up was an insurmountable coupling between Bottleneck 1 and Bottleneck 2 inside `infer.mjs`:
1. When Reconstruct reached Turn 8 (only possible under search level 4), it halted on the Rillaboom recoil discrepancy:
   `reproduces through turn 8 - wanted |-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|61/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2a: Rillaboom|62/100|[from] Recoil`

3. However, when `fallback` tested these single-stat perturbations (e.g., modifying Rillaboom's HP or Attack, or Basculegion's Defense):
   The altered stats shifted the PRNG draw sequence in earlier turns.
4. Consequently, every perturbed candidate guess tested by fallback **crashed back at line 5.10** because Rock Slide suddenly flinched Sneasler:
   ```
   fallback on turn 9: the guess stops before the turn, at 5.10: wanted |cant|p1b: Sneasler|par, got |cant|p1b: Sneasler|flinch, 6.8s
   ```
