## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Three-Way Speed Ordering Trap at Turn 1 `1.8` (`|cant|p1b: Garchomp|flinch`)
To emit `|cant|p1b: Garchomp|flinch` at line `1.8` (immediately after `Raichu`'s `+3` `Fake Out` and `Rough Skin`), `Garchomp` must have strictly higher (or tie-winning) Speed than **both** `Whimsicott` and `Espathra`:
$$\text{Spe}(\text{Garchomp}) \ge \max\big(\text{Spe}(\text{Whimsicott}),\, \text{Spe}(\text{Espathra})\big)$$
Specifically, even with `Garchomp` at its maximum possible Speed (`spe = 32`, **Spe `169`**), **both** `Whimsicott` (`149..184`, needing `spe <= 17` for `Spe <= 168`, or `18` for tie `169`) **and** `Espathra` (`137..172`, needing `spe <= 29` for `Spe <= 169`) must be moved below `169` **at the same time**.

However, in **both** search initializations, `Garchomp` already started at `169` while **both** opponents started faster than `169`:
- **`default` (`Starts 1, 3, 4, 6`)**: `Whimsicott` = `32/0/2/0/0/32` (**Spe `184`**), `Espathra` = `32/0/2/0/0/32` (**Spe `172`**), `Garchomp` = `8/0/6/0/0/32` (**Spe `169`**).
- **`usage` (`Starts 2, 5`)**: `Whimsicott` = `2/0/0/32/0/32` (**Spe `184`**), `Espathra` = `2/0/0/32/0/32` (**Spe `172`**), `Garchomp` = `2/32/0/0/0/32` (**Spe `169`**).

In the verified prefix of Turn 1 (`1.0`–`1.7`), only `+3` priority `Fake Out` resolves before line `1.8`, so `speed.mjs` observes zero same-priority speed comparisons (`0` speed rules learned). Consequently, `Whimsicott`, `Garchomp`, and `Espathra` retain `[0, 32]` Speed SP in `knowledge`, and `choose()` keeps both `Whimsicott` (`184`) and `Espathra` (`172`) at `spe = 32` on every round (`unchanged = true`).

### B. Why `fallback` Could Never Drop Both `Whimsicott` and `Espathra` Together
When `fallback` ran on Turn 1 `1.8` (`infer.mjs` lines 478–914), three architectural interactions guaranteed failure regardless of budget (`8`, `32`, or `128` rebuilds):

1. **`disputed` Pair-Pushing Only Moves One Adversary at a Time (`infer.mjs` lines 761–786)**:
   Because `Garchomp` flinched (`cant`), `reconstruct` had to substitute an invisible move for `p1b` on Turn 1, so Turn 1 was in `built.report.hidden` (`disputed.size > 0`). Line `772` iterated `db` over `[Whimsicott, Espathra]` **individually** alongside `da = Garchomp`:
   - `[Garchomp spe 32, Whimsicott spe 0]` (`Spe 169` vs `149`, trace lines 14, 34, 56, 105, 152, 200): dropped `Whimsicott` below `Garchomp`, immediately exposing `Espathra` (`Spe 172`) at `1.8` (`got |move|p2b: Espathra|Calm Mind|p2b: Espathra`).
   - `[Garchomp spe 32, Espathra spe 0]` (`Spe 169` vs `137`, trace lines 16, 36, 58, 107, 154, 202): dropped `Espathra` below `Garchomp`, leaving `Whimsicott` (`Spe 184`) at `1.8` (`got |move|p1a: Whimsicott|Moonblast|p2b: Espathra`).
   - Note: `escort()` (`infer.mjs` lines 726–741) only fires when `sideOf(got) === 'early'` (`stop.turn < t`), so it never triggers when the exposed second opponent blocks on the same turn (`stop.turn === t = 1`).

2. **`Espathra spe` Can Never Enter `moved`, Locking It Out of the Pair Search (`infer.mjs` lines 704, 825–845)**:

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, `p1` Known)**: `p1:1 Whimsicott`'s slow `10/18/16/2/17/3` (**Spe `152`**) and `p1:4 Garchomp`'s `2/32/0/0/0/32` (**Spe `169`**) are fixed ground-truth constants. On Turn 1 `1.8`, only `Espathra` (`Spe 172` vs `Garchomp 169`) blocks `|cant|p1b: Garchomp|flinch`. Pushing `[Garchomp 32, Espathra 0]` immediately drops `Espathra` (`Spe 137`) below both `Garchomp` (`169`) and `Whimsicott` (`152`) in 1 rebuild, letting `speed.mjs` read `Garchomp >= Espathra` and resolving Turns 1–5 cleanly.
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, `p2` Known)**: `p2:1 Espathra`'s `31/0/0/18/3/14` (**Spe `152`**) is a fixed ground-truth constant. On Turn 1 `1.8`, only `Whimsicott` (`Spe 184` vs `Garchomp 169`) blocks `|cant|p1b: Garchomp|flinch`. Pushing `[Garchomp 32, Whimsicott 0]` immediately drops `Whimsicott` (`Spe 149`) below both `Garchomp` (`169`) and `Espathra` (`152`) in 1 rebuild, letting `speed.mjs` read `Garchomp >= Whimsicott` and resolving Turns 1–5 cleanly.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Allow `fallback` to Push Multiple Faster Adversaries Below the Expected Actor on an Order Line (`infer.mjs` lines 761–786)**:
   When an order line (`order === true`) expects actor `X` (`Garchomp`) to move before `actual` (`Whimsicott`), moving `Whimsicott` below `Garchomp` can expose a *third* active Pokémon `Z` (`Espathra`) on the **same turn `t`** (`stop.turn === t`, `stop.index === diff.index`). Currently `escort()` (`line 728`) only activates when `stop.turn < t` (an *earlier* turn), and `disputed` (`lines 771–785`) only tests pairs `[X, actual]` and `[X, next]`, never `[X, actual, next]` together (nor chaining `escort` on `stop.turn === t` when `stop.index === diff.index` exposes a new actor on the same order line). Allowing `escort` (or multi-actor Speed pushing) when a Speed move on `t` keeps stopping at `(t, diff.index)` with a different actor `Z` would immediately solve `[Garchomp spe 32, Whimsicott spe 0, Espathra spe 0]` in 1–2 rebuilds.
2. **Do Not Flag Same-Turn Regressions as `d.moved = true` in `judge()` (`infer.mjs` line 704)**:
   Line 704 checks `r[0] >= t && outcome(got.b) !== baseOutcome`, which treats a probe that stopped *earlier* within turn `t` (`r[0] === t && r[1] < baseReach[1]`, e.g., `Raichu hp/atk/def` breaking `1.6` instead of reaching `1.8`) as having "moved" the failing line `1.8`. Checking `!further(baseReach, r)` (`r[0] > t || (r[0] === t && r[1] >= baseReach[1])`) instead of `r[0] >= t` prevents `fallback` from burning dozens of rebuilds exhaustively enumerating stats that broke earlier hits in the same turn.
3. **Seed `moved` From `disputed` Pair Probes When One Member's Speed Was Moved (`infer.mjs` line 779)**:
   Because `disputed` calls `judge(changes, got)` without a `dim` object, `[Garchomp spe 32, Whimsicott spe 0]` changed the outcome at `1.8` (`Whimsicott Moonblast` $\to$ `Espathra Calm Mind`) without setting `Whimsicott spe`'s `moved = true`, and `done` later skipped `[Whimsicott spe 0]` in the single-stat `ends` loop (`line 675`), delaying `Whimsicott spe` from entering `moved` until `Whimsicott spe 16` ran in the middle loop (`line 820`) — which `Starts 1–5` (`8` and `32` rebuild caps) never even reached.
