### Root Cause 3: Random Spread Obstruction on Archaludon and Garchomp
1. In `mimic.mjs`, each Pokémon has an 80% chance (`USUAL = 0.8`) of drawing a spread from `usage-spreads.json`, and a 20% chance of receiving a completely random spread from `randomSpread(rng, 66)`.
2. In this battle:
   - **Archaludon** received a random spread: `{ hp: 4, atk: 15, def: 3, spa: 6, spd: 11, spe: 27 }`.
   - **Garchomp** received a random spread: `{ hp: 17, atk: 19, def: 0, spa: 2, spd: 3, spe: 25 }`.
3. Because Archaludon was the central combatant from Turn 5 through Turn 8 (taking 4 separate attacks, receiving 2 Leftovers heals, gaining 3 Stamina boosts, and executing 3 moves), its exact HP, Def, SpA, and SpD were critical to reproducing the replay.
4. When Start 2 restarted the search from "the spreads people run" (`usage`), it seeded candidate guesses with standard competitive Archaludon spreads (e.g., Modest `252 HP / 252 SpA` or Calm `252 HP / 252 SpD`). None of these usage spreads matched the planted `4 HP / 3 Def / 6 SpA / 11 SpD` distribution, ensuring that Start 2 failed identically to Start 1.

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Tier Restart Escalation and Search Budget Exhaustion
The search sequentially exhausted all 4 restart configurations:
1. **Start 1 (Default)**:
   - Advanced rounds 1 through 7, stalled on Turn 8 line 8.2 (`wanted Tyranitar, got Indeedee`).
   - Fallback spent 8 rebuilds, found nothing, incremented search level from 1 to 4 (`search 2, search 3, search 4`), and gave up.
2. **Start 2 (Usage Priors)**:
   - Seeded from `usage-spreads.json`. Advanced to Turn 8, hit the identical line 8.2 diff.
   - Fallback spent 8 rebuilds, advanced search level to 4, and gave up.
3. **Start 3 (Wide Fallback)**:
   - Multiplied fallback rebuilds and guess tries by 4.
   - Fallback spent 32 rebuilds on Turn 8, found nothing, advanced search level to 4, and gave up.
4. **Start 4 (Widest - All Limits ×4)**:
   - Multiplied all budgets by 4 (`budgetScale = 4`).
   - Fallback on Turn 8 spent **109 rebuilds** probing 50+ individual stat combinations across Archaludon, Tyranitar, Indeedee, and Basculegion.
   - Every single probe failed, either playing out identically or diffing on Turn 1, Turn 2, Turn 6, or Turn 7.
   - Search level incremented to 4, triggering `stop = 'searches'`.
5. In the cluster sweep, the exhaustive multi-start search consumed **3,837.0 seconds** before terminating. In the diagnostic trace, it terminated in **655.3 seconds** with 349,519 probes.
