## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Three Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Structural `reconstruct.mjs` Bug: `Encore` Before a Two-Turn Charge Move Discards `recoverChargeTargets` & `carriedInto`
This stall is **not** caused by Stat Point arithmetic, damage parity, or spread search heuristics — which is why **`p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, and `both@spectator` all gave up**. It is a deterministic choice-planning bug in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`:

1. **`planSegment()` Skips Encored Move Lines Unconditionally (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 120–122, 170–177)**:
   ```js
   if (kind === '-start' && /Encore$/.test(parts[3] || '')) {
     encored.add(parts[2]);
     continue;
   }
   if (kind === 'move') {
     ...
     if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;
   ```
   On Turn 4, `p2a: Whimsicott` moves before `p1a: Archaludon` and inflicts `|-start|p1a: Archaludon|Encore`. Immediately afterward, `p1a: Archaludon` executes `|move|p1a: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]`. Because `encored.has('p1a: Archaludon')` is `true`, `planSegment()` discards the move line completely, leaving **`plans[4].actions.p1[0] = undefined`**.

2. **`recoverChargeTargets(plans)` Cannot Recover Turn 5's Target (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 208–225)**:
   ```js
   if (!action || action.target || !action.needsTarget) continue;
   ```
   On Turn 5, `planSegment()` reads `|move|p1a: Archaludon|Electro Shot|p2b: Charizard|[from] lockedmove` (allowed by line 183: `if (from && from !== 'lockedmove') continue;`). However, when `recoverChargeTargets(plans)` scans backward to Turn 4, `plans[4].actions.p1[0]` is `undefined`! As a result:
   - Turn 4 never receives `action.target = 'p2b: Charizard'` (`preferred = ' +2'`).
   - Turn 4 never receives `action.landsOn = 5`.

3. **`choiceFor()` Defaults Turn 4 to `+1` (`p2a`) and Turn 4 Reproduces Silently (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 525–544)**:
   Because `plans[4].actions.p1[0]` is `undefined`, `choiceFor()` enters the `if (!action)` unseen-choice branch at the start of Turn 4 (before `Encore` is applied, so all 4 moves of `Archaludon` are enabled). `usable[0]` is `Electro Shot`, and `targetOptions(side, 0, 'normal')` (with `preferred = null`) yields `[' +1', ' +2', ' -2']`. Variant `0` (`variants[4] = 0`) therefore chooses **`move 1 +1`** (`p2a: Whimsicott`).
   - When `Whimsicott` applies `Encore`, Showdown locks `Archaludon` into `Electro Shot` while **retaining the `+1` target**!
   - Because Turn 4 only charges (`Electro Shot||[still]`) and prints no target, Variant `0` (`+1`) reproduces Turn 4 100% identically (`badTurn` advances to `5`).

4. **Turn 5 Has `carriedInto(plans, 5) === []` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 228–234, 2528–2548)**:
   On Turn 5, `Archaludon` is locked in `twoturnmove` (`request.active[0].moves` offers only `Electro Shot` with no target option), so the simulator fires `Electro Shot` at slot `+1` (`p2a: Glimmora`), failing at `5.10`:
   `wanted |move|p1a: Archaludon|Electro Shot|p2b: Charizard|[from] lockedmove`
   `got    |move|p1a: Archaludon|Electro Shot|p2a: Glimmora|[from] lockedmove`
   Normally, `reconstruct.mjs` lines 2528–2548 call `carriedInto(plans, 5)` to walk the earlier turn's hidden target variants (`variants[4] = 1` $\to$ `move 1 +2`). Because `plans[4].actions.p1[0]` was skipped by `planSegment`, `landsOn: 5` was never recorded and **`carriedInto(plans, 5)` returns `[]`**. `reconstruct` never tries `variants[4] = 1`, instead exhausting `probes` and `backtracks` on Turn 5 and failing every rebuild at `5.10`.

### B. Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled Across 8, 32, and 128 Rebuilds Without Advancing Past `5.10`
Across all 6 starts (lines 7–342), Rounds 1–7 quickly learned the damage/speed evidence from Turns 0–4 and reached Turn 5 (`reproduces through turn 4`), where Round 7 (`5.10`) had `exact = 6 turns exact, 11 dice handed on` and `again = true`, triggering `fallback` on Turn 5 (`5.10`):

1. **No Stat Point Can Move `5.10`'s Target**:
   Because `5.10` is a `lockedmove` target mismatch fixed by Turn 4's `variants[4] = 0` (`+1`), **zero Stat Point combinations on any Pokémon can alter `Electro Shot`'s target from `p2a: Glimmora` to `p2b: Charizard`**. Every surviving spread either:
   - **Broke an earlier turn (`< 5`)** (`1.4`, `1.9`, `1.14`, `2.4`, `3.5`, `3.7`, `3.13`), triggering an extra `fresh` `rebuild(chosen, budgets[1], { given: null })` check (`infer.mjs` lines 686–689) that doubled its `spent` cost (`2` rebuilds per probe!);
   - **Played out the same through Turn 5 (`same = true`)** via `replayWith` (`42` skips in Start 6 alone, lines 216, 220, 227, 237–245, 252, 276–298, 303, 307–315, 318–329); or
   - **Stopped at `5.2`** (when `Charizard spe >= 15` or `Glimmora spe <= 22` reversed their Turn 5 speed order, lines 93, 236, 265, 272–275, 320–321), **`5.4`** (`Glimmora spa 16/18` shifting `Earth Power` from `24/100` to `23/100`, lines 234, 270), or **`5.10`** itself (`Glimmora` still targeted instead of `Charizard`).

2. **Rapid Exhaustion of `8`, `32`, and `128` Budgets and `searches` Escalation**:
   - In **Starts 1 & 2** (`cap = 8`), `base` (`1`) + `dice` (`1`) + just **3** probes (`Archaludon hp 27`, `atk 15`, `def 15`, each stopping at Turn 3 and spending `2` rebuilds with the `fresh` check) immediately hit `8/8` (`nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`).
   - In **Starts 3, 4, 5** (`cap = 32`) and **Start 6** (`cap = 128`), `fallback` swept through `cast = [Archaludon, Charizard, Glimmora, Golisopod, Sylveon]` until hitting `32/32` or `128/128`, marking `5|10|...` in `spend.fruitless`.
   - With `picks` unchanged after Round 7, Rounds 8–10 (`search 2`, `search 3`, `search 4`) skipped `fallback` (`spend.fruitless.has(mark)`), hit `5.10` under `budgets[1..3]`, and terminated with `stop = 'searches'`.

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Also Gave Up in the Sweep
Unlike HP-parity stalls (such as `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2706101187`), knowing either side's exact Stat Points and exact HP integers does **not** help `p2@p1` or `p1@p2` resolve Turn 5:
- Both `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` pass the exact same `observed` lines through `reconstruct.mjs`'s `planSegment()` and `recoverChargeTargets()`.
- On Turn 4, `planSegment()` skips `|move|p1a: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]` because `encored.has('p1a: Archaludon')`, leaving `plans[4].actions.p1[0] = undefined` and `carriedInto(plans, 5) = []` in **every view**.
- Consequently, every single rebuild across `p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, and `both@spectator` locks `Archaludon` onto `+1` (`p2a`) on Turn 4 and crashes into the exact same Turn 5 `5.10` wall.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` & `scripts/lib/mimic.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Discard Charge-Move Metadata for Encored Pokémon (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 177, 208–234)**:
   When `encored.has(parts[2])` is true on a `|move|` line, the player's *move* choice is hidden, **but** if the executed Encored move is a two-turn charge move (`||[still]` / `needsTarget`), Turn `k`'s hidden choice (specifically `Encore`'s target slot when the user's chosen move had a target, or the random target drawn when they chose `Protect`/Switch) still **carries into Turn `k + 1`** (`|move|...|[from] lockedmove`)!
   - Keeping a minimal `{ moveid, target: null, needsTarget, encored: true }` entry (or recording `landsOn` from Turn `k + 1`'s `lockedmove` execution) in `planSegment()` / `recoverChargeTargets()` ensures two things:
     1. `carriedInto(plans, 5)` includes Turn `4`, so `reconstruct` will automatically try Turn 4's other variants (`variants[4] = 1` $\to$ `+2`) as soon as `5.10` diverges!
     2. In `choiceFor()`'s `if (!action || action.encored)` branch, `targetOptions` can still prioritize `preferred = ' +2'` (`p2b: Charizard`) first when the player chose a targeted move (which covers the common case where the Pokémon was Encored into the same move or another targeted move), while `carriedInto` cleanly covers the fallback case where they chose an untargeted move like `Protect`.
2. **Read `|-anim|` Targets for Charge Moves in `MimicPlayer` (`scripts/lib/mimic.mjs` lines 99–110)**:
   While unrelated to why inference failed, `readIntents()` ignored `|-anim|p2a: Charizard|Solar Beam|p1b: Pelipper` on Turn 1 because it only checked `kind === 'move'`. Populating `target` from `|-anim|` when a turn's `|move|...||[still]` had `target: null` keeps mimic battles faithful to the original replay's targeting on single-turn Sun `Solar Beam` / Rain `Electro Shot` turns.
