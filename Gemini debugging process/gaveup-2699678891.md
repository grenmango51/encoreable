## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p1@p2` Passed While `p2@p1` and `both@spectator` Gave Up
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2:1` `Indeedee-F` is fixed to its ground-truth Stat Points (`Spe` constant), while `p1:4` `Rillaboom` starts at `Spe 107` (`default`) or `105` (`usage`) and takes **no damage** in Turns 0–2. Thus `Rillaboom` never drops below `Indeedee-F` before Turn 3; and even if `Kowtow Cleave` on Turn 3 narrows `Rillaboom`'s `hp/def` enough to trigger `aimFor` on `Rillaboom`'s `spe`, `aimFor` jumps `Rillaboom`'s `spe` **upward** (~`14–15`), keeping `Rillaboom` *faster* than `Indeedee-F` and preserving Turn 0 cleanly.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:4` `Rillaboom` is fixed to its ground-truth Speed, while `p2:1` `Indeedee-F` is inferred and starts at `spe = 2` (`default`, `Spe 107`) or `spe = 0` (`usage`, `Spe 105`). Turn 2 `Make It Rain` (even with P1's `Gholdengo` known) still forces `Indeedee-F` to require high `hp + spd` bulk, shrinking `values.spe.length < 33` and causing `aimFor` to jump `Indeedee-F`'s `spe` above `Rillaboom`'s planted Speed — triggering the exact same Turn 0 stall and `!(t >= 1)` fallback block.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/speed.mjs`, `infer.mjs`, `knowledge.mjs`)
1. **Allow `handlerSorts` (`speed.mjs:674`) to Extract Speed Rules When Swapping Makes a Handler Silent**:
   When `readHandlerSort` swaps `(X, Y)` on `SwitchIn` and `X`'s handler becomes silent (like `Rillaboom`'s `Grassy Surge` when `Grassy Terrain` is already active), `theirs` ends 1 line earlier than `mine`, causing `j === theirs.length` to hit `continue` at `speed.mjs:674`. If `mine[j].at === prefix.cutoffAt` and `j === theirs.length` (meaning the swapped order matched every line up to `prefix.cutoffAt` and omitted the unexpected extra line right before `|turn|1`), `Y >= X` (`Rillaboom >= Indeedee-F`) is directly proved.
2. **Remove the `t >= 1` Exclusion on `fallback()` (`infer.mjs:482`)**:
   Turn 0 `SwitchIn` ability ordering (Terrains, Weather, Intimidate, Trace, Download) is a genuine Stat Point (`spe`) dependency. Changing `!(t >= 1)` to `!(t >= 0)` (or allowing `fallback` on `t === 0` whenever `diff.index >= 0`) lets `fallback` immediately test `spe` ends on Turn 0's `SwitchIn` actors instead of wasting all 6 starts without a single perturbation.
3. **Preserve `prev[s]` in `aimFor()` When Only the 66-SP Cap Shrunk a Stat's Range (`knowledge.mjs:806`)**:
   `Indeedee-F`'s `spe` was never excluded by any speed evidence — `spe = 0` or `spe = 2` remained valid — yet `BUDGET = 66` removed `spe > 25` and triggered `values.length < SPAN`, causing `aimFor` to jump `spe` to `~14` and break a previously working Turn 0 tie/order. Only shifting `aim[s]` when `prev[s]` itself is no longer alive (or when `s` was directly constrained by evidence rather than budget slack alone) would prevent spurious Turn 0 speed inversions.
