## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Primary Root Cause: `Revival Blessing` Bench-Revive + End-of-Turn Faint Switch Bug (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` lines 134–144)
In Showdown (`sim/battle.js` lines 2291–2308), `Revival Blessing` **only** queues an automatic `instaswitch` when `action.target.position < action.pokemon.side.active.length` (i.e., when the revived Pokémon is still sitting fainted in an empty active slot). When the revived Pokémon was already replaced on the field and is revived **on the bench** (`position >= active.length`), no switch happens mid-turn; if another active Pokémon subsequently faints on the same turn, the player receives a normal `forceSwitch` at `upkeep` and explicitly chooses `switch` to bring the revived Pokémon in.

However, `planSegment()` in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` (lines 134–144) unconditionally assumes the next `|switch|` of the revived Pokémon is an automatic `instaswitch`:
```js
if (kind === '-heal' && tagsOf(line).from === 'move: Revival Blessing' && reviver) {
  const side = identSide(parts[2]);
  plan.forced[side].push({ slot: reviver.slot, name: identName(parts[2]) });
  revived = { side, name: identName(parts[2]) };
  reviver = null;
  continue;
}
if (kind === 'switch' && revived && identSide(parts[2]) === revived.side && identName(parts[2]) === revived.name) {
  revived = null;
  continue;
}
```
In Turn 2 of `2699898128`:
1. `Pawmot` had already replaced `Delphox` in `p2a` at the end of Turn 1, so `Delphox` was revived **on the bench** at `2.3`.
2. `Kowtow Cleave` then fainted `p2b: Indeedee` at the end of Turn 2, and `p2` switched `p2b: Delphox` in at `upkeep` (`|switch|p2b: Delphox|Delphox, L50, M|50/100y`).
3. `planSegment()` consumed `|switch|p2b: Delphox|` as `revived` and discarded it instead of pushing `{ slot: 1, name: 'Delphox' }` onto `plan.forced.p2`.
4. Consequently, when `p2b: Indeedee` fainted at the end of Turn 2, `plan.forced.p2` was empty (`[]`), causing `choiceFor()` (`reconstruct.mjs` line 500) to submit `'pass, pass'` for `forceSwitch: [false, true]`. Showdown rejected `'pass'` because `p2` had a live `Delphox` on the bench, yielding:
   `turn 2: a choice was rejected - nothing advanced` (`diff.index = -1`).

Because `planSegment()` runs identically for **every** channel (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator`), **Turn 2 is structurally unreproducible regardless of Stat Points or viewpoint** — explaining why `slowest_gave_up.json` shows all three views (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, `both@spectator`) giving up!

## 7. Multi-View Failure Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why ALL Three Views (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator`) Gave Up
Unlike cases caused by hidden-HP parity traps on Channel 0, **every single view (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, `both@spectator`) fails on Turn 2** because `planSegment()` in `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` misparses the protocol log whenever a Pokémon revived from the bench by `Revival Blessing` later enters via a normal `upkeep` faint replacement on the same turn. Even with full ground-truth spreads on one side (`p1@p2` and `p2@p1`), `plan.forced.p2` is still missing `Delphox` at the end of Turn 2, so Showdown rejects `'pass, pass'` on `p2`'s `forceSwitch`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Fix `Revival Blessing` Switch Tracking in `planSegment()` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, lines 134–144)**:
   - `Revival Blessing` only triggers an automatic `instaswitch` (without a `forceSwitch` choice) when the revived Pokémon is already in an empty active slot (`target.position < active.length`) — in which case its `|switch|` line appears **immediately** after `|-heal|...|Revival Blessing` (before any `|move|`, `|cant|`, `|faint|`, or `|upkeep|`), **not** at the end of the turn after another Pokémon faints!
   - Clearing `revived = null` as soon as any subsequent action/event occurs (or only treating a `|switch|` before the next `move`/`cant`/`upkeep`/`faint` as the automatic `instaswitch` — better yet, tracking active slots in `planSegment` so `revived` is only skipped if that Pokémon was still sitting fainted in an active slot when `Revival Blessing` fired) will restore `{ slot: 1, name: 'Delphox' }` to `plan.forced.p2` and immediately unblock all three views on `2699898128`!
2. **Do Not Rank End-of-Turn Choice Refusals (`diff.index === -1`) Below Earlier Line Mismatches (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs` line 1671, `scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` line 511)**:
   - Currently, `scoreOf(run, t)` returns `[-1, 0, 0]` for `diff.index < 0`, and `reach(b)` returns `[turn, -1]`. When a turn has `gotHard.length > 5` verified lines printed *before* a rejected `forceSwitch` at `upkeep`, scoring it as `-1` causes `resolveTurn` and `fallback` to treat reaching the end of the turn as *worse* than failing at line `2.5` (`-1 < 5`), hiding the real choice rejection behind a phantom damage stall! Scoring `diff.index < 0` using `gotHard.length` (the number of lines that actually agreed before the refusal, e.g. via a sub-priority or negative tiebreak `(gotHard.length, -1)`) prevents `resolveTurn` and `fallback` from retreating to an earlier line and accurately surfaces `wanted turn 2: a choice was rejected`!
