## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Three Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Primary Root Cause: `planSegment()` + `recoverChargeTargets()` `Encore` / Two-Turn Move Blind Spot (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
This failure is **a pure turn-planning / choice-recovery bug in `reconstruct.mjs`**, completely independent of Stat Points or exact HP visibility:

1. **`planSegment()` Discards the Charge Turn When `Encore` Lands First (`reconstruct.mjs`, lines 170–177)**:
   ```js
   if (kind === '-start' && /Encore$/.test(parts[3] || '')) {
     encored.add(parts[2]);
     continue;
   }
   if (kind === 'move') {
     ...
     if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;
   ```
   Because `p1a: Whimsicott` used `Encore` on `p2b: Archaludon` *before* `p2b: Archaludon` acted on Turn 2 (`|-start|p2b: Archaludon|Encore` precedes `|move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]`), `planSegment()` marks `p2b: Archaludon` as `encored` and **skips its Turn 2 `|move|` line**. Consequently, **`plans[2].actions.p2[1]` is `undefined`**.

2. **`recoverChargeTargets()` Cannot Recover Turn 3's Target (`reconstruct.mjs`, lines 208–225)**:
   ```js
   for (const [slot, action] of plan.actions[side].entries()) {
     if (!action || action.target || !action.needsTarget) continue;
     ...
   ```
   Because `plans[2].actions.p2[1]` is `undefined`, `recoverChargeTargets()` skips `p2b: Archaludon` on Turn 2. It **never** sets `action.target = 'p1b: Charizard'` and **never** sets `action.landsOn = 3`.

3. **`choiceFor()` Defaults Turn 2 `variant = 0` to `Whimsicott` (`+1`) (`reconstruct.mjs`, lines 525–544)**:
   With `plans[2].actions.p2[1]` missing, `choiceFor()` treats `p2b: Archaludon` on Turn 2 as an invisible choice (`!action`). It builds `options` across `usable` moves (`Electro Shot`, `Dragon Pulse`, `Flash Cannon`, `Protect`) × `targetOptions(side, 1, 'normal')` (`[' +1', ' +2', ' -1']`).
   - At **`variant = 0`**, `choiceFor()` picks **`move 1 +1`** (`Electro Shot` aimed at **`p1a: Whimsicott`**).
   - Because `move 1` is `Electro Shot`, `Encore`'s `onOverrideAction` (`node_modules/pokemon-showdown/dist/data/moves.js` line 4811) returns `undefined` (no override, no `getRandomTarget` call).
   - Showdown stores `lastMoveTargetLoc = +1` (`p1a: Whimsicott`) and adds `twoturnmove`. Because Turn 2's prepare line (`|move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]`) omits the target, **Turn 2 matches the observed log 100% identically under the wrong target `+1`**.

4. **Turn 3 Fails at `3.11` and `carriedInto(plans, 3)` Returns Empty (`reconstruct.mjs`, lines 228–234, 2523–2549)**:
   - On Turn 3, `Archaludon` is locked by `twoturnmove` (`side.js` line 444 reuses `pokemon.lastMoveTargetLoc = +1`), firing `Electro Shot` at **`p1a: Whimsicott`** instead of **`p1b: Charizard`**.
   - On Turn 3 (`width = 1`, since `Archaludon`'s locked move has `targetOptions = ['']`), `reconstructOnce` checks `carriedInto(plans, 3)` (line 2528) to walk earlier turns whose stored choice landed on Turn 3.
   - Because `landsOn = 3` was never set on Turn 2, **`carriedInto(plans, 3)` returns `[]`!** Thus `reconstructOnce` **never tries `variants[2] = 1` (`move 1 +2`)**!
   - Since `3.11` is a `|move|` line (`hpFigure = false`), `redrawTurn()` tries Turn 2's dice (`Charizard` Heat Wave damage/crit/burn rolls), finds no die that changes `Electro Shot`'s target, and exits with `stop = 'stuck'`.

## 7. All-View Failure & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator` All Gave Up
In `slowest_gave_up.json`, `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2705233123` is one of the cases where **every view (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator`) failed with `GAVE UP` and `stop = "searches"`**. Because the Turn 3 `3.11` mismatch is caused by `reconstruct.mjs` discarding `p2b: Archaludon`'s Turn 2 `Electro Shot` action after `Encore` and defaulting `variant = 0` to `+1` (`Whimsicott`), knowing P1's exact HP/spread (`p2@p1`) or P2's exact HP/spread (`p1@p2`) cannot fix Turn 2's missing `landsOn = 3` link or trigger `carriedInto(plans, 3)` to test `move 1 +2` (`Charizard`).

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Drop Two-Turn Charge Actions Under `Encore` in `planSegment()` (`reconstruct.mjs`, lines 170–194)**:
   - Currently, `if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;` discards `|move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]` whenever `Encore` landed earlier in the same turn.
   - Even when a Pokémon is Encored before acting, if it executes a targetless charging move (`||[still]`, `CHOOSABLE_TARGETS.has(move.target)`), `recoverChargeTargets()` still needs an entry (or an explicit `landsOn` link) so `carriedInto(plans, t)` includes Turn `k` when the release turn `t` (`[from] lockedmove`) fails to hit the observed target.
2. **Respect Showdown's `Encore` Target Semantics When `move.id === encoredMove` (`battle-actions.js`, lines 213–220)**:
   - In Showdown, `Encore` only calls `getRandomTarget()` when `OverrideAction` changes the chosen move (`move.id !== this.effectState.move`). If the Encored player chose the same move (`electroshot +2`), Showdown preserves their explicit target (`+2`) into `twoturnmove` and `lastMoveTargetLoc`.
   - Recording the Encored move with `action.target` recovered from the subsequent `[from] lockedmove` line (and trying `move +recoveredTarget` as `variant = 0` in `choiceFor()` before falling back to random-target overrides) lets `reconstruct.mjs` hit `p1b: Charizard` on Turn 3 immediately without backtracking or cycling `fallback`.
