## 6. Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Exploded (Root Cause Analysis)

### 1. The Asymmetric Information Loss in Spectator View (Channel 0)
- In channel 0, **zero exact HP numbers exist for either side**. Every single damage and heal event is rendered as an integer percentage (`43/100`, `29/100`, `92/100`, `39/100`, `58/100`, `65/100`, `24/100`, `52/100`, etc.).
- Both attacker and defender stats are unknown for all 12 slots across both teams.
- Any damage percentage $P = \text{round}(D / \text{MaxHP} \times 100)$ admits a 3-dimensional manifold of valid $( \text{AttackerStat}, \text{DefenderStat}, \text{DefenderMaxHP} )$ tuples.

### 2. The Five-Turn `Leech Life` Drain Feedback Loop
- Mega Golisopod uses `Leech Life` on **every single active turn** from Turn 2 through Turn 6:
  - **Turn 2**: Leech Life on Milotic (Milotic takes damage, Golisopod heals $+39\%$ from drain, Milotic drops to $29\%$ triggering Sitrus Berry $+25\%$, Grassy Terrain heals $+6.25\%$).
  - **Turn 3**: Leech Life on Gholdengo (Gholdengo takes damage, Golisopod heals $+7\%$ from drain).
  - **Turn 4**: Leech Life on Staraptor (Staraptor takes damage, Golisopod heals $+21\%$ from drain).
  - **Turn 5**: Leech Life on Mega Staraptor (Critical hit, Staraptor takes damage, Golisopod heals $+8\%$ to $100\%$).
  - **Turn 6**: Leech Life on Mega Staraptor (Lethal damage).
- **The Mechanics of Drain in Showdown**:
  $$\text{drainHealing} = \text{Math.ceil}(\text{actualDamage} \times 0.50)$$
  $$\text{drainPercentShown} = \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{drainHealing}}{\text{AttackerMaxHP}} \times 100 \right)$$
- The percentage healed on Golisopod in spectator view depends simultaneously on:
  1. Golisopod's Attack
  2. The Defender's Defense
  3. The Defender's current HP and Max HP (determining the exact integer damage dealt)
  4. Golisopod's Max HP (determining the drain percent rounding)
- A shift of just 1 HP in the defender's actual damage changes `Math.ceil(damage / 2)`, which when divided by Golisopod's Max HP changes the displayed percentage (e.g. `65/100` vs `66/100`, as seen in Round 5).

### 3. The Turn 3 Base Speed Inversion
- Archaludon base Speed = 85.
- Gholdengo base Speed = 84.
- In unconstrained sampling, Archaludon is naturally faster than Gholdengo (105 vs 104 with 0 SP).
- However, the actual battle requires Gholdengo to move first on Turn 3 (`|move|p1a: Gholdengo|Shadow Ball|p2b: Golisopod` precedes `|move|p2a: Archaludon|Electro Shot`).
- Initial guesses regularly draw Archaludon faster than Gholdengo, failing Round 1 immediately at the start of Turn 3.

### 4. Life Orb Recoil Integer Percentage Rounding
- On Turn 3, Gholdengo uses Shadow Ball with Life Orb.
- Before the move, Gholdengo is at $49\%$ ($82/167$).
- Life Orb recoil is exactly $10\%$ of max HP: $\lfloor 167 / 10 \rfloor = 16$ HP.
  $$82 - 16 = 66 \text{ HP} \implies \text{round}(66 / 167 \times 100) = 39.52\% \rightarrow 39\%$$
- If a candidate guess assigns Gholdengo a slightly different Max HP (e.g. 157 or 172 HP):
  $$\lfloor 160 / 10 \rfloor = 16 \implies \text{round}((80 - 16) / 160 \times 100) = 40.0\% \rightarrow 40\%$$
- In Round 4, the rebuild failed with:
  `wanted |-damage|p1a: Gholdengo|39/100|[from] item: Life Orb, got |-damage|p1a: Gholdengo|40/100|[from] item: Life Orb`

### 5. Why Fallback Failed and Rebuilds Stalled
- When Round 4 failed on Gholdengo's Life Orb line (`39/100` vs `40/100`), the engine triggered `fallback` on Turn 3.
- `fallback` tests single-parameter variations on unknown Pokémon (e.g. `Gholdengo hp 0`, `Gholdengo hp 32`, `Gholdengo atk 32`):
  - Perturbing `Gholdengo hp 0`: breaks Turn 2 Archaludon Electro Shot damage (`43/100` wanted, `39/100` got).
  - Perturbing `Gholdengo hp 32`: breaks Turn 2 Grassy Terrain heal (`49/100` wanted, `50/100y` got).
  - Perturbing `Gholdengo atk 32`: breaks Turn 2 Electro Shot damage (`43/100` wanted, `40/100` got).
- Because Gholdengo's Max HP is simultaneously constrained by Turn 2 Electro Shot damage from Archaludon, Turn 2 Grassy Terrain healing, Turn 3 Life Orb recoil, and Turn 3 Leech Life drain from Golisopod, **no single-stat perturbation can pass Turn 3 without breaking Turn 2**.
- `fallback` exhausted all 8 rebuild attempts without finding a candidate that reproduced Turn 3, adding `fallbackRebuilds` to limits.
- Over 286 rounds, the search repeatedly looped between:
  1. Fixing Turn 2 damage ratios $\rightarrow$ breaks Turn 3 Speed order or Life Orb recoil.
  2. Fixing Turn 3 Life Orb recoil $\rightarrow$ breaks Turn 3 Golisopod drain heal (`65/100` vs `66/100`).
  3. Fixing Turn 3 drain heal $\rightarrow$ breaks Turn 4 Staraptor Leech Life damage (`52/100` vs `62/100`).
  4. Fixing Turn 4 Staraptor Leech Life $\rightarrow$ shifts Golisopod/Staraptor stats and breaks Turn 2 or Turn 3 again.
- Because `deepest` verified turn never reached Turn 5 or Turn 6, the `stalled` counter incremented until reaching `maxRounds` (16), setting `stop = "stalled"`.
