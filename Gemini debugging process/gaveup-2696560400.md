3. In `firstGuesses()`, every Pokémon on both sides falls back to `defaultSpread(dex, set, undefined)`, which assigns 32 HP Stat Points to all Pokémon.

### B. Dimensional Collapse in `choose()` Across 8 Unknown Active Pokémon
1. In `both@spectator`, `seq` inside `choose()` contains **8 active unknown Pokémon**:

3. The quota `tries` is strictly capped at $32 \times S \times W$ (32 in `default`, 128 in `wide`, 512 in `widest`).
4. Because all 8 Pokémon are coupled through cross-side damage, recoil, and Grassy Terrain healing, assigning a spread to an earlier Pokémon in `seq` causes `blocks()` to trigger on a subsequent Pokémon, or causes the final exact check to fail.
5. The search repeatedly exhausts `tries`, records `guessTries` in `limits`, and returns `proof.exact = null`.

### C. Loss of Exact Dice Handoff and Deterministic Rebuilds
1. When `proof.exact` is null, the engine cannot construct `exact` or `pins`:

2. Without `exact`, the next round cannot skip turns or force dice. `reconstruct()` must search dice from scratch on every rebuild using stochastic die sampling.
3. Because damage rolls across 8 unknown spreads must simultaneously hit tight integer percentages across multiple turns, die search frequently runs out of backtracks (`maxBacktracks = 0` in budget 0, `2` in budget 1, `6` in budget 2), failing to reproduce verified turns.

### D. The Multi-Body Recoil & Percentage Coupling Bottleneck (Turns 10–12)
From Turn 10 through Turn 12, damage exchanges across both teams form an intricately coupled system where a 1-HP difference in any Pokémon cascades into a failure:
1. **Turn 10 Lethal Flare Blitz Recoil**:

     $$\text{Recoil} = \lfloor \text{Gardevoir HP}_{\text{rem}} / 3 \rfloor$$
   - Gardevoir's remaining HP at Turn 10 depends on:
     - Gengar's Sludge Bomb damage on Turn 4 (Gengar SpA vs Gardevoir SpD/HP)

2. **Turn 11 Mutual Flare Blitz Exchange**:

### E. Fallback Failure and Fruitless Marking
1. When rebuild fails on a diff such as:
   `|-heal|p2a: Incineroar|84/100|[from] Grassy Terrain` (expected `84/100`, got `83/100`)
   or:
   `|-damage|p1b: Incineroar|45/100|[from] item: Rocky Helmet` (expected `45/100`, got `44/100`)
2. `fallback()` inspects `names(diff.expected)` and identifies only the single Pokémon named on the failing line.
3. Fallback tests single-stat variations of that Pokémon.
4. However, the 1-HP discrepancy on Turn 10 or Turn 12 is the cumulative result of 4 different Pokémon's attacks, defenses, and recoils from prior turns.
5. Single-stat adjustments cannot bridge this multi-body gap. Fallback exhausts its quota (`fallbackRebuilds` added to `limits`), marks the line fruitless, and returns `none()`.

### F. The 168-Round Restart Cascade

2. After 16 stalled rounds, Start 1 (`default`) terminates with `stop: "stalled"`.

## 8. Summary Conclusion
The inference search in `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2696560400` gave up after 168 rounds and 12,369.9 seconds due to the following factual search dynamics:
1. Single-view inference (`p2@p1` in 42.1s, `p1@p2` in 68.6s) succeeded because one side was fully known, exact HP was populated on Channel 1 and Channel 2, and `choose()` only had to search 4 unknown Pokémon, finding `proof.exact` on every round.
2. In `both@spectator`, all exact HP values were withheld by Channel 0, defaulting all 8 active Pokémon to 32 HP Stat Points.
3. `choose()` had to resolve an 8-way joint candidate space across both teams, which exceeded its recursive `tries` allocation and forced `proof.exact = null`.
4. Without exact turns or handed-on dice, rebuilds had to search dice from scratch across Turns 10–12, where lethal Flare Blitz recoil, mutual Flare Blitz exchanges, Rocky Helmet damage, and Grassy Terrain healing created a multi-body integer percentage bottleneck.
5. Fallback probing could not bridge multi-body discrepancies via single-stat adjustments, causing the search to stall for 16 consecutive rounds across all 4 restart tiers (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, `widest`) until terminating with `stop: "stalled"`.
6. Kingambit retained 100% of candidate spreads (136.6M) because it only took lethal overkill damage, used a priority move, had Life Orb recoil that matches all HP values on Channel 0, and dealt damage only to an unknown Sneasler.
