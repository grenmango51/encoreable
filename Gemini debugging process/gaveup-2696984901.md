## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Coarse Integer Percentage Rounding on Multi-Step Recoil
In spectator view (`Channel 0`), neither side is known (`known = [false, false]`). Both sides' HP pools are obscured behind integer percentage brackets:
$$\text{display\_pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \times \text{current\_hp}}{\text{max\_hp}} \right\rfloor$$

On Turn 6, Incineroar participates in a complex four-stage arithmetic chain:
1. Takes damage from Archaludon's +1 SpA Electro Shot under Rain: $\text{HP}_1 = \text{max\_hp} - \text{damage}_{\text{ElectroShot}} \implies \text{observed: } 23/100$.
2. Consumes Sitrus Berry: $\text{HP}_2 = \text{HP}_1 + \lfloor \text{max\_hp} / 4 \rfloor \implies \text{observed: } 48/100$.
3. Deals damage to Archaludon with Flare Blitz in Rain against +1 Def: $\text{damage}_{\text{Archaludon}}$.
4. Takes recoil: $\text{recoil} = \lfloor \text{damage}_{\text{Archaludon}} / 3 \rfloor$.
   $\text{HP}_3 = \text{HP}_2 - \text{recoil} \implies \text{observed: } 34/100$.

Because $\text{HP}_3$ is the compound result of two unknown attack stats (Archaludon SpA, Incineroar Atk), two unknown defense stats (Incineroar SpD, Archaludon Def), two random damage rolls, and two integer floor divisions, candidate guesses generated by `infer.mjs` consistently produced **36% or 35% remaining HP** instead of the required **34% remaining HP**:
```text
wanted |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|34/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p2a: Incineroar|36/100|[from] Recoil
```

### Root Cause 3: Search Budget Escalation & Final Termination
Because the failed Turn 6 recoil line proved nothing about which spreads were legal (rule: a line a rebuild stops on proves nothing), the candidate spread bounds remained completely unchanged (`unchanged == true` in `infer.mjs`).
When bounds fail to move between rounds:
1. `infer.mjs` increments the search budget level:
   - `round 16: search 1`
   - `round 17: search 2`
   - `round 18: search 3`
   - `round 19: search 4`
2. At `level >= budgets.length` (4 levels), the start gave up (`stop = 'searches'`).
3. The engine cycled through all 6 restart tiers:
   - Phase 1: `start 1 (default)` (19 rounds)
   - Phase 2: `searching again from the spreads people run` (17 rounds)
   - Phase 3: `searching once more with 4 times the fallback's rebuilds and the joint guess tries` (19 rounds)
   - Phase 4: `searching from each again with every limit 4 times wider` (19 rounds)
   - Phase 5: `searching again from the spreads people run` (wide) (17 rounds)
   - Phase 6: `searching once more with 4 times the fallback's rebuilds` (widest) (19 rounds)
4. Across all 6 phases, every restart stalled at the identical Turn 6 recoil line (`34/100` vs `36/100`).
5. After 110 rounds, 418 rebuilds, and 1,006,401 probes, all search budget tiers were completely exhausted, resulting in final termination with `stop = "searches"` and result `GAVE UP`.
