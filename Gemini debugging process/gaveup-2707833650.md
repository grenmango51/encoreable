## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Primary Bug: `planSegment()` Swallows Post-`Revival Blessing` Forced Switch-Ins (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`, lines 134–144)
In `planSegment()` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 123–144), `Revival Blessing` is parsed as follows:
```js
if (kind === '-heal' && tagsOf(line).from === 'move: Revival Blessing' && reviver) {
  const side = identSide(parts[2]);
  plan.forced[side].push({ slot: reviver.slot, name: identName(parts[2]) });
  revived = { side, name: identName(parts[2]) };
  reviver = null;
  continue;
}
if (kind === 'switch' && revived && identSide(parts[2]) === revived.side && identName(parts[2]) === revived.name) {
  revived = null;
  continue;
}
```
The comment on lines 125–126 explains the intended case: *"One revived into an empty active slot is switched in by the simulator itself, so its switch line answers no request."* However, **`revived` is never cleared when Turn 2 moves on to subsequent actions or `|upkeep|`**!

In Turn 2 of `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmc-2707833650`:
1. When `p1b: Pawmot` used `Revival Blessing` on `Staraptor`, both `p1a: Politoed` and `p1b: Pawmot` were active, so Showdown did **not** auto-switch `Staraptor` in — it remained on the bench at `50/100y`, leaving `revived = { side: 'p1', name: 'Staraptor' }` armed.
2. Later in Turn 2, `p1b: Pawmot` fainted. At `|upkeep|`, `p1` received a real `forceSwitch` request for `p1b` and sent in `Staraptor` (`|switch|p1b: Staraptor|Staraptor, L50, M|50/100y`).
3. Line 141 matched `revived` and **executed `continue;`**, silently dropping `Staraptor` from `plan.forced.p1`!
4. Thus `plan.forced.p1` held only the single entry consumed mid-turn by `Revival Blessing` itself, leaving `plan.forced.p1` **empty** for the end-of-turn `forceSwitch`. When `forceSwitch` fired at upkeep, `choiceFor()` returned `'pass'` (`reconstruct.mjs` line 500), which Showdown silently rejected (`before === after`, line 1385: `state.unsatisfied = "turn 2: a choice was rejected - nothing advanced"`).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why All Three Views (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, and `both@spectator`) Gave Up Equally
- In `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmc-2707833650`, `slowest_gave_up.json` confirms that **neither `p1@p2` nor `p2@p1` passed** (`"views": ["p1@p2", "p2@p1", "both@spectator"]`).
- Even with one player's spreads and exact HP completely fixed, `reconstruct.mjs` constructs `planSegment()` purely from the protocol log (`lines`). Because `planSegment()` swallowed `|switch|p1b: Staraptor|50/100y` on Turn 2, `plan.forced.p1` was missing `Staraptor` in **every view**, causing the exact-match `Spirit Break +1` variant to fail with `state.unsatisfied` (`scoreOf = [-1, 0, 0]`) regardless of whether P1 or P2 spreads were known.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Expire `revived` in `planSegment()` Once Another Event Occurs (Lines 134–144)**:
   When Showdown auto-switches a Pokémon revived by `Revival Blessing` into an empty active slot, that `|switch|` is emitted **immediately** as part of the `Revival Blessing` resolution, before any subsequent `|move|`, `|cant|`, `|faint|`, or `|upkeep|` line. Clearing `revived = null` upon encountering any `move`, `cant`, `faint`, or `upkeep` line (or tracking whether an empty slot actually existed on `side`) prevents `planSegment()` from swallowing a later `|upkeep|` replacement switch-in of the revived Pokémon.
2. **Distinguish Mid-Turn `forceSwitch` Refusals from Earlier Line Mismatches in `scoreOf()` / `playThrough()`**:
   Even when `state.unsatisfied` triggers at the end of a turn (`index = -1`), a variant whose `gotHard` matched `wantHard` strictly further (`gotHard.length > idx_SB`) was penalized below a variant that emitted an illegal-looking wrong target (`idx_SB`) before reaching `upkeep`. While fixing `planSegment()` resolves the `Revival Blessing` case directly, `scoreOf()` ranking `diff.index === -1` (`[-1, 0, 0]`) strictly below an earlier line mismatch (`[idx_SB, ...]`) whenever `found.index === gotHard.length` can mask late-turn `forceSwitch` bugs behind spurious early target mismatches.
