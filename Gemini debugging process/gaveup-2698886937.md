   - Turn 8: Sylveon Hyper Voice deals exact damage `122 -> 35` (87 damage) to Rillaboom (known SpD 92).
   - Turn 10: Rillaboom's Wood Hammer recoil is exact (`37 -> 26`, 11 recoil, damage in `[33, 35]`), pinning Incineroar's Defense.
3. Every exchange resolved through direct substitution into known P1 parameters. The search converged in 12 rounds without fallback rebuilds.

## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- No Pokémon displays exact HP numbers. Every damage event, healing tick, recoil loss, and status damage is reported as `Math.floor(100 * hp / maxhp) || 1`.
- All 8 Pokémon brought to the match saw active combat engagement.
- Every damage exchange contains simultaneous unknown variables on both sides:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk/SpA}, \text{unknown Def/SpD}, \text{unknown Dice Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lfloor \cdot \rfloor\%$$
- Because many combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` produce the same rounded percentage in Channel 0, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads.
- As seen in Section 2:
  - Incineroar retained **136,663,185** spreads (100.0% of the entire pool) because its only non-switch/non-Protect actions occurred on Turn 10.
  - Basculegion retained **132,145,967** spreads (96.69%) because it never used an attack before fainting.
  - Sylveon, Garchomp, Sneasler, and Salamence retained between 4.5 million and 22.6 million spreads.

### Root Cause 2: Deep Multi-Turn Recoil and HP Coupling Chain on Rillaboom
Rillaboom participates in an unbroken chain of HP events from Turn 4 through Turn 10:
1. **Turn 5 Recoil**: Takes Wood Hammer recoil after KOing Farigiraf.
   - The recoil is:
     $$\text{recoil} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Farigiraf HP}_{\text{entering T5}}}{3} \right\rfloor$$
   - Farigiraf's HP entering Turn 5 was determined by:
     $$\text{HP}_{\text{entering T5}} = \text{maxHP}_{\text{Farigiraf}} - \text{HyperVoice}_{T1} - \text{HyperVoice}_{T2} + \left\lfloor \frac{\text{maxHP}_{\text{Farigiraf}}}{4} \right\rfloor$$
   - Thus, Rillaboom's Turn 5 recoil percentage depends on Salamence's SpA on Turn 1 and Turn 2, Farigiraf's maxHP, Farigiraf's SpD, and both Turn 1/Turn 2 damage rolls.
   - In ground truth: Farigiraf has 72 HP left. $\lfloor 72 / 3 \rfloor = 24$ recoil. $205 - 24 = 181 / 205 = 88.29\% \rightarrow 88/100$.
   - In rebuilds, candidate guesses systematically hit:
     - Round 7: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|88/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|89/100|[from] Recoil`
2. **Turn 6 Double Loss**:
   - Takes Rough Skin from Garchomp: $\lfloor \text{maxHP}_{\text{Rillaboom}} / 8 \rfloor = \lfloor 205 / 8 \rfloor = 25$ HP ($193 \rightarrow 168 / 205 = 81.95\% \rightarrow 81/100$).
   - Takes Wood Hammer recoil from Garchomp: $\lfloor \text{damage} / 3 \rfloor = \lfloor 136 / 3 \rfloor = 45$ HP ($168 \rightarrow 123 / 205 = 60.000\% \rightarrow 60/100$).
   - The final percentage after recoil is exactly $123 / 205 = 60.000\%$.
   - If Rillaboom has 122 HP instead of 123 HP, $122 / 205 = 59.51\% \rightarrow 59/100$.
   - In rebuilds, candidate guesses hit:
     - Round 10: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|81/100|[from] ability: Rough Skin, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|82/100`
     - Round 11: `wanted |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|60/100|[from] Recoil, got |-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|57/100`

### Root Cause 3: The Turn 7 / Turn 8 Garchomp Poison Survival Razor
Garchomp's sequence of damage across Turns 6-8:
1. Turn 6: Takes Fake Out (17 HP down to 168), takes Wood Hammer (136 HP down to 32), heals Grassy Terrain (+11 HP to 43), takes poison damage (-23 HP down to 20).
2. Turn 7: Heals Grassy Terrain (+11 HP to 31), takes poison damage (-23 HP down to 8).
3. Garchomp survives Turn 7 with **exactly 8 HP** (4.32% $\rightarrow$ `4/100 psn`).
4. On Turn 8, the replay requires:
   - `|move|p1a: Rillaboom|Grassy Glide|p2a: Garchomp`
   - `|-damage|p2a: Garchomp|0 fnt`
   - `|faint|p2a: Garchomp`
   - `|-damage|p1a: Rillaboom|59/100|[from] ability: Rough Skin|[of] p2a: Garchomp`
5. If Garchomp had taken even 8 HP more damage across Fake Out or Wood Hammer on Turn 6, Garchomp would reach 0 HP at the end of Turn 7 and faint to poison!
6. If Garchomp faints at the end of Turn 7, Rillaboom cannot target Garchomp with Grassy Glide on Turn 8, and Rillaboom does not take the Rough Skin recoil that reduces it to `59/100`. The entire battle protocol diverges irreparably at Turn 8.

### Root Cause 4: Inefficacy of Fallback Rebuilds on Cascading Multi-Turn State
When rebuilds stop on Turn 5 or Turn 6 with percentage mismatches:
1. `fallback(seen, start, ...)` attempts 1-stat perturbations on the Pokémon active on that turn (`cast`).
2. However, the discrepancy on Turn 5 (Rillaboom recoil `88/100` vs `89/100`) is caused by Farigiraf's remaining HP, which was determined by Salamence's Hyper Voice on Turn 1 and Turn 2.
3. Salamence fainted on Turn 4 and is not active on Turn 5. Fallback does not modify Salamence's Turn 1/2 damage.
4. When fallback modifies Farigiraf's HP or SpD on Turn 5:
   - Modifying Farigiraf's SpD or max HP breaks the Turn 2 Sitrus Berry activation or healing percentage (`wanted 31/100, got 32/100`).
   - Modifying Rillaboom's max HP alters Grassy Terrain and Rough Skin step sizes across all subsequent turns.
5. In Round 12-14:
   - Fallback modified Sylveon's SpD to 0 on Turn 1 to pass `61/100`.
   - In Round 13, that modification caused Turn 2 Sitrus Berry healing to fail (`wanted 31/100, got 32/100`), where "nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds".
   - In Round 14, fallback patched Basculegion Def to 32 on Turn 3.
   - In Round 15, the rebuild immediately failed on Turn 5 Thunderbolt (`wanted 59/100, got 62/100`).
6. Fallback repeatedly exhausts its per-turn rebuild budget (`fallbackRebuilds`) and eventually exhausts the total fallback rebuild budget (`fallbackTotal`).

### Root Cause 5: Search Stalling and Four-Stage Escalation
1. In `infer.mjs`:
   ```javascript
   const at = [diff?.turn ?? -1, diff?.index ?? -1];
   if (!deepest || past(at, deepest)) deepest = at;
   else stalled++;
   ```
2. Because rebuilds repeatedly stall between Turn 4 and Turn 6, `stalled` increments in almost every round.
3. When `stalled >= maxRounds`:
   - Stage 1 (`default` start) terminates with `stop="stalled"`.
   - Stage 2 (`usage` start) starts from Smogon's common spreads. It encounters the same percentage bottlenecks and stalls again.
   - Stage 3 (`wide` restart) widens fallback rebuilds and joint guess tries 4x (`FALLBACK_TOTAL * WIDE`). It spends all fallback rebuilds, hitting `fallbackRebuilds` and `fallbackTotal`, and stalls again.
   - Stage 4 (`widest` restart) scales all budgets 4x (`budgetScale: 4`), running through up to 183 rounds and 725 rebuilds.
4. Across all stages, the search accumulated **6,096,010 probes** and **17,320.6 seconds** before terminating with `stop="stalled"`, collecting all 7 limits:
   `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "fallbackTotal", "guessTries", "probes", "searches", "stalled"]`.

## 7. Summary of Diagnosis

1. **Why Single-View Resolved Rapidly**:
   - In `p1@p2` (20.2s) and `p2@p1` (41.0s), the observer's exact HP values and exact integer recoil amounts (`floor(damage / 3)`) directly pinned opposing attack stats and defender bulk independently, without simultaneous multi-variable coupling.
2. **Why Spectator View Stalled and Gave Up**:
   - Universal percentage masking (`Math.floor(100 * hp / maxhp)`) across 8 active Pokémon left millions of surviving candidate spreads per Pokémon (including 136.6M on Incineroar and 132.1M on Basculegion).
   - Combat state is deeply coupled across 10 turns through multi-turn recoil, Rough Skin, Grassy Terrain, Sitrus Berry, and poison ticks:
     - Turn 5 Rillaboom Wood Hammer recoil depends on Turn 1 & 2 Salamence Hyper Voice damage onto Farigiraf.
     - Turn 6 Rillaboom Wood Hammer recoil and Rough Skin depend on Garchomp's Defense and Rillaboom's entering HP.
     - Turn 7 Garchomp poison survival depends on Turn 6 Fake Out and Wood Hammer damage to leave exactly 8 HP (4%) so Garchomp survives into Turn 8.
   - Local perturbations in `fallback` on failing turns systematically corrupt earlier or later turns, causing the search to oscillate between Turn 1, Turn 2, Turn 5, and Turn 6.
   - Rebuilds could not advance past Turn 6, causing `stalled` to reach limit across all four restart stages (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, `widest`), exhausting 6.1 million probes over 4.81 hours.
