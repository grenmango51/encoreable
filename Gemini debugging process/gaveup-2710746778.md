## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. `planSegment` Drops Encored Charging Moves (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs:170–194`)
This stall is caused by a **structural bug in `reconstruct.mjs` (`planSegment` + `recoverChargeTargets` + `carriedInto`)** rather than Stat Point inference:

1. **Encored Move Skipped in `planSegment` (`reconstruct.mjs:170–185`)**:
   On Turn 4, `Whimsicott` uses `Encore` on `Archaludon`, emitting `|-start|p1b: Archaludon|Encore`, which executes line 171:
   ```js
   if (kind === '-start' && /Encore$/.test(parts[3] || '')) {
     encored.add(parts[2]);
     continue;
   }
   ```
   When `Archaludon` subsequently executes `|move|p1b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]` on Turn 4, line 177 immediately skips it:
   ```js
   if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;
   ```
   Line 177 was intended to avoid treating an Encored Pokémon's forced move as its original choice, because a Pokémon Encored before acting only gets a restricted move choice (or already chose something else that `Encore` overrode). Consequently, **`plans[4].actions.p1[1]` is left `undefined`**.

2. **`recoverChargeTargets` Misses the Charging Move (`reconstruct.mjs:208–225`)**:
   `recoverChargeTargets(plans)` iterates over `plan.actions[side]` looking for `!action.target && action.needsTarget` so it can copy the execution target (`p2b: Charizard`) from Turn 5's `[from] lockedmove` line back onto Turn 4 and set `action.landsOn = 5`. Because `plans[4].actions.p1[1]` is `undefined`, **`recoverChargeTargets` skips Turn 4 completely** — Turn 4 never learns `p2b: Charizard`, and `landsOn = 5` is never recorded.

3. **`choiceFor` Defaults Turn 4 to `+1` (`p2a: Whimsicott`) (`reconstruct.mjs:525–544`)**:
   During Turn 4 reconstruction, `choiceFor` sees `!action` for `p1b: Archaludon` and treats it as an invisible choice (`state.invisible = true`). `Encore` leaves only `Electro Shot` enabled, so `targetOptions(side, 1, 'normal', null)` builds `[' +1', ' +2', ' -1']`. At `variants[4] = 0`, `choiceFor` writes `move 1 +1` (`p2a: Whimsicott`). Because Turn 4's charging line `|move|p1b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]` does not print its target, **`variants[4] = 0` reproduces Turn 4 completely**.

4. **`carriedInto(plans, 5)` Returns Empty on Turn 5 (`reconstruct.mjs:228–234, 2523–2549`)**:
   On Turn 5, `p1b: Archaludon`'s `twoTurnMove` lock causes `side.activeRequest` to offer `Electro Shot` with no target field; the simulator fires at Turn 4's stored target (`+1`, `p2a: Whimsicott`), failing at `5.8`. `reconstructOnce` then checks `carriedInto(plans, 5)` (line 2528) specifically to re-walk earlier turns' hidden target variants (`variants[4] = 1` $\to$ `' +2'`). However, `carriedInto` only checks `a?.landsOn === 5` on `plans[k].actions`. ## 7. View Independence & Architectural Takeaways

### Why All Three Views (`p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, `both@spectator`) Gave Up
Unlike Stat Point parity stalls (where knowing one side's exact HP/stats in `p2@p1` or `p1@p2` breaks the deadlock), the failure at `5.8` in `2710746778` is **100% view-independent**:
- `planSegment` (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs:177`) skips `|move|p1b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]` on Turn 4 solely because `p1b: Archaludon` is in `encored`, regardless of which side's spreads or exact HP are known.
- Without `plans[4].actions.p1[1]`, `recoverChargeTargets` never copies `p2b: Charizard` onto Turn 4 and never sets `landsOn = 5`, so `carriedInto(plans, 5)` never searches Turn 4's hidden target variants (`+1` vs `+2`) in any view.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Discard Charging Moves Under `Encore` in `planSegment` (`reconstruct.mjs:170–194`)**:
   - Line 177 (`if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;`) exists so an Encored Pokémon's move isn't mistaken for the move it *chose* before getting Encored. However, when that Encored move is a **two-turn charging move** (`||[still]` followed by `|-prepare|`) whose target is locked in on the charging turn and resolved on a later turn (`[from] lockedmove`), dropping it from `plan.actions` breaks both `recoverChargeTargets` (preventing target recovery when no redirect occurred) and `carriedInto` (preventing variant backtracking when a redirect or empty target occurred).
   - Even when Encored, recording the move's `needsTarget` / `landsOn` link (or letting `carriedInto` / `recoverChargeTargets` also inspect turns where a slot executed `|-prepare|` / `state.invisible` and later fired `[from] lockedmove`) would immediately recover `p2b: Charizard` (`+2`) onto Turn 4 and allow `carriedInto(plans, 5)` to walk `variants[4]` if needed.
2. **Invariant Check for `carriedInto`**:
   - Whenever `planSegment` records a `[from] lockedmove` move on turn `t` (`action.from === 'lockedmove'`) that did *not* get linked by `recoverChargeTargets` to any `landsOn === t` on an earlier turn, `carriedInto(plans, t)` should automatically fall back to including the preceding charging turn (`t - 1`) in `carriers` so its hidden target choices are never unreachable.
