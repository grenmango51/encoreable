## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Bottleneck 1 (Starts 1, 3, 4, 6 — Turn 1 `1.6` vs `1.7` Double-Dependency Trap)
In `defaultSpread()` (`scripts/lib/inference/knowledge.mjs` lines 636–645), bulk and nature-boosted stats receive 32 SP first, leaving only `2` SP in Speed:
- `p1:3 Rillaboom` (`Adamant`): guessed `32/32/0/0/0/2` (**Atk 194**, Spe 107; true: `32/4/1/22/4/3`, **Atk 163**)
- `p2:3 Kleavor` (`Adamant`): guessed `32/32/0/0/0/2` (**HP 177**, Def 115, **Spe 107**; true: `2/32/0/0/0/32`, **HP 147**, **Spe 137**)
- `p2:5 Charizard` (`Calm`): guessed `32/0/0/0/32/2` (**Spe 122**; true: `3/21/1/6/24/11`, **Spe 131**)

This created an unbreakable single-stat deadlock on Turn 1:
1. **Why Round 1 learned nothing about Speed**: With `Rillaboom Atk 194` vs. `Kleavor HP 177, Def 115`, `Fake Out` deals `13..15` damage, and `177 - 13 = 164 / 177 = 92/100`, matching `1.6`. The rebuild immediately failed at `1.7` (`wanted |cant|p2a: Kleavor|flinch, got |move|p2b: Charizard|Heat Wave|...`) because `Kleavor Spe 107 < Charizard Spe 122`. Because `1.7` was the mismatch line outside the verified prefix, `speed.mjs` never recorded `Kleavor > Charizard`, leaving `next` completely unchanged across Rounds 1–4.
2. **Why `fallback` on Turn 1 failed in 8, 32, and 128 rebuilds**:

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `p2:3 Kleavor` (`147 HP, 115 Def, 137 Spe`), `p2:5 Charizard` (`156 HP, 131 Spe`), `p2:4 Primarina` (`183 HP, 95 Def, 176 SpA`), and `p2:2 Rillaboom` (`207 HP, 110 Def`) are all known constants, and Channel 2 prints P2's exact integer HP (`136/147` on Turn 1 Fake Out, `179/207` on Turn 3 Fake Out, `46/207` on Turn 4 Sandstorm). Knowing `Kleavor` has `Spe 137` and `147 HP` prevents the Turn 1 `1.6`/`1.7` trap immediately, and knowing `Primarina`'s `176 SpA` and `Rillaboom`'s `207 HP` eliminates the Turn 4 `4.8` and `4.12` ambiguity.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `p1:3 Rillaboom` (`4` Atk SP $\to$ `Atk 163`) and `p1:0 Tyranitar` (`177 HP, 186 Atk, 120 SpD, 124 Spe`) are known constants, and Channel 1 prints P1's exact integer HP (`98/177` after Turn 4 Hyper Voice). Fixing `Rillaboom Atk = 163` lets Turn 1 `fallback` raise `Kleavor spe` without ever breaking `1.6: 92/100`, and fixing `Tyranitar`'s exact `177 HP / 120 SpD` + exact `98/177` pins `Primarina`'s Turn 4 damage right away.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Drop `exact` on `resample` (`search 4`) or Invalidate Partial-Turn `exact` After Later Failures (`infer.mjs` lines 960–965)**:
   When `budgets[level].resample` clears `seed` and `pins`, `infer.mjs` still hands `exact` from the previous failed round to `reconstruct`. Here, `search 3` failed at `4.6`, so `exact` locked `p2:2 Rillaboom`'s `4.5` exact HP to a value that broke `4.12` (`23/100` vs. `22/100`) without ever having checked `4.12`. Clearing `exact` when `resample` is set (or clearing it when `reconstruct` fails at a later line on that same turn) would let `search 4` freely sample `4.5` to satisfy `4.12`.
2. **Coordinate Attacker `atk`/`spa` with Defender `spe`/`hp` on `order` Failures (`fallback`, `infer.mjs` lines 624–635, 833–845)**:
   On Turn 1 (`1.7`), `Kleavor` could not take Speed SP from its `32/32/0/0/0/2` (`66`-SP-saturated) default spread without dropping `hp` and breaking the preceding `Fake Out` (`1.6`) against `Rillaboom`'s oversized `32` Atk SP guess. When raising `spe` on an `order` stall breaks an earlier damage line on the same turn, `fallback` should either reduce the attacker's offensive stat alongside the defender's `spe` or allow `spe` probes to borrow SP from non-defensive stats (`atk`) first.
3. **Avoid Resetting `level` to `0` (`search 1`) When Only `search 4` Cleared an Earlier RNG Stall (`infer.mjs` lines 1102–1106)**:
   Whenever `search 4` (`resample: 1`) advanced from `4.6` to `4.12` and `evidence` moved a stat, `level` reset to `floor` (`0`), returning control to `search 1` (`seed 1`), which immediately stalled back at `4.6` and erased `search 4`'s progress. Tracking `floor = Math.max(floor, level)` when `budgets[level].resample` is the only level that passed an earlier line (or keeping `resample` active for `fallback` rebuilds once `level` reached `resample`) would prevent the 69-round `4.6 <-> 4.12` oscillation loop.
