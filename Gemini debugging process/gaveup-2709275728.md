### Root Cause 1: The Coupled Double-Target Bulldoze Zero-Sum Dilemma (Turn 6)
On Turn 6, Steelix-Mega uses `Bulldoze`. Because Bulldoze is an earthquake-style spread move in doubles, it hits all active non-protecting Pokémon:
