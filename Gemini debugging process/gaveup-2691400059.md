## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Indeedee-F` (`HP 177, Def 139, Spe 94`) and `Corviknight` (`HP 205, Def 172, -1 Spe 52`) are known ground-truth constants, and Channel 1 prints exact integers for P1's HP. With `Indeedee-F` fixed, Turn 3 `Gyro Ball` has only **2** unknown variables (`Avalugg Atk`, `Avalugg Spe`), so `evidence.mjs` hooks `Gyro Ball` cleanly (`others.length = 2 <= 2`), immediately pinning `Avalugg`'s exact `Atk`/`Spe` relationship on Turn 3 (`Spe <= 52` from Turn 6 order as well).
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Avalugg` (`Atk`, `Def`, `Spe`) is a fixed constant, and Channel 2 prints exact integers for P2's HP. With `Avalugg` fixed, Turn 3 `Gyro Ball` has only **2** unknown variables (`Indeedee Def`, `Indeedee Spe`), so `evidence.mjs` hooks `Gyro Ball` cleanly (`others.length = 1 <= 2`), narrowing `Indeedee`'s `Def` and `Spe` directly from Turn 3 and avoiding the `15/0/23/13/0/15` detour.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` & `evidence.mjs`)
1. **Do Not Treat Early-Line Regressions Inside Turn `t` as `moved = true` in `fallback` (`infer.mjs` line 704)**:
   In `fallback on turn 6` (`6.4`), `Pangoro spe 32` (`6.2: 4/100 slp`) and `Pangoro hp/def/spa/spd` (`6.2: 0 fnt` / `21/100 slp`) broke an *earlier* line (`6.2`) in the same turn `t = 6` due to 66-SP budget displacement. Because `judge()` checks `r[0] >= t && outcome(got.b) !== baseOutcome` instead of `further(r, baseReach)` (or `r[0] > t || r[1] >= baseReach[1]`), regressions to earlier indices `r[1] < baseReach[1]` marked `d.moved = true` and burned **~90 of 128 rebuilds (~400s)** on irrelevant stats.
2. **Track Cross-Turn Stat Dependencies (`Gyro Ball` Target Speed) in `lent` (`evidence.mjs` / `infer.mjs`)**:
   Turn 6 (`6.4`) failed because `Avalugg`'s Turn 3 `Gyro Ball` depended on off-cast `Indeedee-F`'s `Spe` and `Def`, yet `Indeedee-F` was neither named in Turn 6 nor listed in `Avalugg`'s `lent` (which only records `storedStats` writes). Recording read-side dependencies (or prior-turn hit targets whose `Spe`/`Def` shifted `Avalugg`'s `held` spread) would let `fallback` include `Indeedee-F` when `Avalugg` stalls.
3. **Avoid Locking Unproven Mid-Battle Speeds into `held`, or Revisit `Gyro Ball` (`others.length > 2`)**:
   When `Gyro Ball` fell back to `band('down')` in `both@spectator`, `choose()` certified an impossible `exact` Turn 3 (`77/160`), and Start 6's Turn 3 `fallback` locked `Avalugg spe = 16` into `held`, creating an unsolvable contradiction on Turn 6. Either allowing a fallback-`held` entry to be unpinned when a later turn's `order` fails on that Pokémon's Speed, or supporting `Gyro Ball`'s 2-Pokémon `(Atk, Spe)` vs. `(Def, Spe)` evaluation when both sides are unknown, would resolve both stalls directly.
