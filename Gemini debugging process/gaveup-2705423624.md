## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Focus Sash Threshold Deadlock at Turn 4 (`4.4: |-damage|p1a: Ninetales|1/100`)
In `sim/data/items.ts`, `Focus Sash` fires whenever `target.hp === target.maxhp && damage >= target.hp`. To print `|-damage|p1a: Ninetales|1/100` **without** `|-enditem|p1a: Ninetales|Focus Sash`, the minimum damage roll of `+1` `Volcarona`'s spread `Heat Wave` against full-HP `Ninetales-Alola` must be **strictly less than `Ninetales`'s `MaxHP`** (and fall inside the `[1, 3]` HP window giving `floor(100 * hp / maxhp) || 1 === 1`). Every guess across all 6 starts violated this inequality by a wide margin, for **two opposite reasons** depending on the start family:

1. **Default / Wide Starts (Starts 1, 3, 4, 6 — `Volcarona SpA = 29` Coupled to Bloated `Annihilape` Bulk)**:
   - `defaultSpread` initialized `Volcarona` at `32/0/0/32/0/2` (`SpA = 32`), which Rounds 1–4 settled to **`Ninetales 32/0/2/0/0/32` (`180 HP, 120 SpD`), `Volcarona 32/0/0/29/0/2` (`+1 SpA = 303`), and `Annihilape 9/32/7/0/10/2` (`194 HP, 120 SpD`)** (lines 17, 61, 124, 243).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Volcarona`'s `16/0/15/5/0/30` (`SpA = 5` $\to$ `264` boosted SpA) is a **fixed ground-truth constant** (alongside `Incineroar 32/0/20/0/14/0` and `Basculegion 2/32/0/0/0/32`). Consequently, Turn 3 `Heat Wave` (`39/100`) immediately restricts `Annihilape` to low special bulk without any `Volcarona SpA` ambiguity, and on Turn 4 `4.4` `fallback` only needs to raise `Ninetales`'s own bulk (`HP/SpD`) to survive known `264`-SpA `Heat Wave` at `1/100`.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Annihilape`'s exact `187` HP / `110` SpD (`2/32/0/0/0/32`) and `Ninetales`'s exact `177` HP / `123` SpD (`29/0/7/0/3/27`) are **known constants**, and Channel 1 prints exact integer HP (`73/187` $\to$ `119/187` $\to$ `27/187` on Annihilape; `3/177` or `1/177` on Ninetales). Observing exact `114` damage on `Annihilape` (`187 -> 73`) on Turn 3 immediately pins `Volcarona SpA` to **`5` SP** before Turn 4 even starts, eliminating the Focus Sash mismatch altogether.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs`)
1. **Treat `early` Breakage and `same` Threshold Traps as Pair-Search Triggers in `fallback`**:
   `fallback` currently defines `moved = dims.filter(d => d.moved)` strictly as stats that reach turn `t` and change `diff.actual` (`outcome(got.b) !== baseOutcome`). When a guess sits behind a **two-sided binary threshold** (such as `Focus Sash` requiring `damage < MaxHP` on Turn 4):
   - In Default/Wide starts, lowering `Volcarona spa` broke Turn 3 (`early` at `3.4`/`3.6`) because `Annihilape spd/hp` was compensating for it; in Usage starts, raising `Ninetales hp` or lowering `Volcarona spa` individually still fell on the lethal side of Focus Sash (`same`).
   - Including `(attacker offensive stat, defender HP/SpD)` pairs across recent hits (specifically linking Turn 3's `Heat Wave` target `Annihilape` via `lent` and pairing `Volcarona spa` with `Ninetales hp/spd`)—or allowing `fallback`'s pair search to combine stats that hit `same` (threshold-saturated) with stats that moved earlier hits (`early`)—would directly resolve `4.4` in 1–2 probes.
2. **Avoid Moving Un-falsified Usage Guesses Away From Extreme Ends via `aimFor` on Round 1**:
   In Starts 2 & 5 (`usage`), `Volcarona` started at `#1` usage `21/0/15/5/1/24` (`SpA = 5`, its true `SpA`!). When Round 1 stopped at `4.4` (`Ninetales` had `HP = 2` and had never been narrowed on Turns 0–3), `choose()` immediately ran `aimFor(kn, picks[s][i])`, pushing `Volcarona SpA` from `5` to the midpoint **`18`**. That single midpoint shift destroyed the exact `SpA = 5` witness that Usage Start had been handed, turning a 1-stat `Ninetales hp` fix into an unreachable 2-stat `(Ninetales hp, Volcarona spa)` jump. Keeping `picks[s][i]` before `aimFor` when the guess already survived the settled prefix (or only `aimFor`-centering stats on Pokémon that actually caused an earlier turn's failure) would let Usage Start solve Turn 4 on Round 2.
