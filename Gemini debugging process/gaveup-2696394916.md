- If Sneasler has lower Attack SP, or Volcarona has higher Def/HP, Volcarona survives with $> 8$ HP ($> 4.5\%$), which fails the observed `2/100` percentage check.
- In `both@spectator`, because both Sneasler's Atk and Volcarona's HP/Def are simultaneous unknowns, almost every randomly sampled guess results in Volcarona either fainting or surviving with the wrong percentage.

### 2. Dual-Die Unsteered RNG Requirement on Turn 4 (Target Selection + 95% Accuracy)
On Turn 4, `p2a: Gholdengo` uses `Make It Rain`:
- **Unsteered Target Selection**: P2 sent the un-aimed command `>p2 move makeitrain`. In doubles, Showdown resolves an un-aimed spread move by calling `getRandomTarget()`, which invokes `Side.randomFoe()` $\rightarrow$ `this.battle.sample(actives)`. This consumes an RNG draw from range 2 (`random(2)`):
  - Draw 58 is consumed in `getActionSpeed` during turn queueing.
  - Draw 59 is consumed in `BattleActions.runMove` when the move is executed.
  - In the real battle, Draw 59 rolled `1`, targeting `p1b: Sneasler`, producing the line:
    `|move|p2a: Gholdengo|Make It Rain|p1b: Sneasler|[spread] p1a,p1b`
  - In the reconstruct pseudo-random seed, Draw 59 rolls `0`, targeting `p1a: Incineroar`, producing:
    `|move|p2a: Gholdengo|Make It Rain|p1a: Incineroar|[spread] ...`
- **Champions Format Modified Accuracy**: In the `gen9championsvgc2026regmc` ruleset, `Make It Rain` has **95% accuracy** (overriding standard 100% accuracy). Thus, accuracy checks are rolled for each target via `randomChance(95, 100)`:
  - Draw 60 checks accuracy against `p1a: Incineroar`.
  - Draw 61 checks accuracy against `p1b: Sneasler`.
  - Under the initial rebuild seed, Draw 61 rolls `97` ($\ge 95$), causing Make It Rain to miss on Sneasler:
    `|-miss|p2a: Gholdengo|p1b: Sneasler`
- **The 2-Die Barrier**:
  - `steerDice` only tracks damage, crit, and accuracy; it does not steer `sample()` calls for move targets (Draw 59).
  - If Draw 59 is changed alone (0 $\rightarrow$ 1): Make It Rain targets Sneasler, but misses due to Draw 61 = 97, leaving Sneasler out of `[spread]`.
  - If Draw 61 is changed alone (97 $\rightarrow$ 0): Make It Rain hits both targets, but its primary target is Incineroar, leaving the move header as `Make It Rain|p1a: Incineroar`.
  - Neither single-die probe in `resolveTurn` advances the score past line 11.

### 3. The Evidence Regression Feedback Loop
1. When `reconstruct` hits the Turn 4 line 11 divergence (`Make It Rain` missing or targeting the wrong Pokémon), it fails to settle the turn with 1-die probes.
2. `inferSpreads` triggers `fallback` on Turn 4, spending 8 fallback rebuilds testing extreme stat variations (HP 0, HP 32, Atk 0, Atk 32, Def 0, Def 32, SpA 0, SpA 32, SpD 32, Spe 0). None of these stat variations resolve the line because stat points do not alter move accuracy or random targeting.
3. The search escalates through Search Levels 1, 2, 3, and 4 (increasing probe budgets).
4. Occasionally, at Search Level 4, stochastic multi-die sampling or a wide probe gets past line 11 and advances to Turn 4 line 14 (`wanted |-damage|p1b: Sneasler|40/100, got |-damage|p1b: Sneasler|24/100`).
5. `evidencePass` runs on the partial reconstruction up to Turn 4 line 14, collecting damage constraints on Gholdengo SpA and Sneasler SpD/HP.
6. A new candidate guess is chosen for the next round based on this tightened knowledge.
7. In the next round, the new guess is rebuilt starting from Turn 1 at Search Level 1. Because settled pins are cleared on new guesses, Search Level 1 immediately re-encounters the exact same Turn 4 line 11 barrier.
8. Because the turn cannot be verified deeper than the previous record, `stalled` increments in every round.
9. After `stalled >= maxRounds` (15 rounds), the search restarts from `usage`, then `wide` (4x budget), then `widest` (4x all limits).
10. Across all restarts, the search circled through 146 rounds, 574 rebuilds, and 1,326,712 probes, taking 9,539.7s before exhausting all restart tiers and halting with `stop="stalled"`.
