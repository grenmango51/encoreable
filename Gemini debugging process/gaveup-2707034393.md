## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Percentage Masking Across 8 Active Pokémon
In Channel 0 (spectator view):
- No Pokémon displays exact integer HP. Every damage, recoil, and weather event is reported as a percentage rounded by Showdown's `Math.ceil(currentHP * 100 / maxHP)`.
- All 8 Pokémon brought into the battle actively participated in direct combat exchanges.
- Every damage equation involves simultaneous unknown attacker and defender parameters:
  $$\text{Damage}(\text{unknown Atk}, \text{unknown Def}, \text{unknown Roll}) \rightarrow \text{applied to unknown Defender maxHP} \rightarrow \lceil \cdot \% \rceil$$
- In `both@spectator`, `evidencePass` leaves enormous candidate spaces remaining:
  - `p2:Volcarona`: 132,400,000 spreads (96.88% pool remaining)
  - `p1:Rotom-Heat`: 105,000,000 spreads (76.83% pool remaining)
  - `p2:Raichu`: 101,000,000 spreads (73.90% pool remaining)
  - `p1:Sceptile`: 86,000,000 spreads (62.93% pool remaining)
  - `p1:Tyranitar`: 49,900,000 spreads (36.51% pool remaining)
  - `p2:Rillaboom`: 42,400,000 spreads (31.02% pool remaining)
  - `p2:Incineroar`: 41,400,000 spreads (30.29% pool remaining)
  - `p1:Indeedee-F`: 6,700,000 spreads (4.90% pool remaining)

### Root Cause 2: Turn 5 Upkeep Switch-in Ability Speed Tie (Incineroar vs Tyranitar)
On Turn 5, both `p1a: Sceptile` and `p2b: Rillaboom` faint. At Turn 5 upkeep, both players bring in faint replacements simultaneously:
- P1 brings in `p1a: Tyranitar` (Ability: `Sand Stream`).
- P2 brings in `p2b: Incineroar` (Ability: `Intimidate`).
- Showdown dispatches simultaneous switch-in abilities in order of the entering Pokémon's current Speed stat:
  - In ground truth: Incineroar has Speed 84 (base 60 + 4 SP + 20); Tyranitar has Speed 81 (base 61 + 0 SP + 20).
  - Because $84 > 81$, Incineroar is strictly faster by 3 Speed points.
  - Incineroar's `Intimidate` activates first:
    `|-ability|p2b: Incineroar|Intimidate|boost`
  - Tyranitar's `Sand Stream` activates second:
    `|-weather|Sandstorm|[from] ability: Sand Stream|[of] p1a: Tyranitar`
- In spectator inference:
  - Tyranitar's Speed Stat Points are unconstrained across $[0, 32]$ (Speed $81$ to $113$).
  - Incineroar's Speed Stat Points are unconstrained across $[0, 32]$ (Speed $80$ to $112$).
  - Whenever candidate guesses assign Tyranitar higher Speed than Incineroar (or an equal Speed tie), Tyranitar's Sand Stream activates before Incineroar's Intimidate.
  - In Round 7 of spectator search, the rebuild immediately fails on Turn 4/5 upkeep:
    `wanted |-ability|p2b: Incineroar|Intimidate|boost, got |-weather|Sandstorm|[from] ability: Sand Stream|[of] p1a: Tyranitar`
  - This ability ordering discrepancy triggers fallback rebuilds and diverts search effort.

### Root Cause 3: 4-Way Multi-Turn Coupled Damage, Recoil, and Item Trigger Cascade (Turns 6-9)
Across Turns 6 through 9, the four surviving Pokémon (`p1a: Tyranitar`, `p1b: Rotom-Heat`, `p2a: Raichu`, `p2b: Incineroar`) are entangled in a continuous chain of mutually dependent HP calculations:
1. **Multi-Target Spread Damage (Turn 6 & Turn 7)**:
   - Rotom-Heat's `Discharge` strikes Tyranitar, Raichu, and Incineroar simultaneously on Turn 6 and Turn 7.
   - On Turn 6: Raichu takes 25 HP ($142 - 25 = 117/167 = 70.06\% \rightarrow 70\%$). A 1 HP discrepancy in damage causes Raichu to display `71/100` or `69/100` instead of `70/100`.
   - On Turn 7: Discharge must hit Incineroar for exactly 43 HP ($133 - 43 = 90/202 = 44.55\% \rightarrow 44\%$).
2. **Sitrus Berry Health Threshold Trigger (Turn 7)**:
   - Incineroar has maxHP 202; its $50\%$ activation threshold is $\lfloor 202 / 2 \rfloor = 101 \text{ HP}$.
   - After taking 43 damage, Incineroar is at 90 HP ($\le 101$), triggering Sitrus Berry to heal $\lfloor 202 / 4 \rfloor = 50 \text{ HP}$ up to 140 HP (`69/100 par`).
   - If Rotom-Heat deals $\le 31$ damage (or if Incineroar had higher entered HP from Turn 6), Incineroar remains above 101 HP, Sitrus Berry fails to trigger, and the rebuild fails immediately.
   - If Discharge leaves Incineroar at 91 HP, the percentage is $91 / 202 = 45.05\% \rightarrow 45\%$, failing the spectator line `wanted 44/100, got 45/100`.
3. **Flare Blitz Recoil Coupled to Tyranitar Bulk & Attack Stats**:
   - Turn 6: Flare Blitz deals 66 damage to Tyranitar (`68/100`). Incineroar takes $\lfloor 66 / 3 \rfloor = 22$ recoil, ending at $145/202 = 71.78\% \rightarrow 71\%$ (`71/100`).
     - If Flare Blitz deals 65 damage, recoil is 21, and Incineroar ends at $146/202 = 72.28\% \rightarrow 72\%$ (diff: wanted `71/100`, got `72/100`).
   - Turn 7: Flare Blitz deals 39 damage to Tyranitar (`35/100 par`). Incineroar takes $\lfloor 39 / 3 \rfloor = 13$ recoil, ending at $127/202 = 62.87\% \rightarrow 62\%$ (`62/100 par`).
   - Turn 8: Flare Blitz deals 42 damage to Tyranitar (`15/100 par`). Incineroar takes $\lfloor 42 / 3 \rfloor = 14$ recoil, ending at $101/202 = 50.00\% \rightarrow 50\%$ (`50/100y par`).
   - Turn 8: Tyranitar lands a critical hit Knock Off on Incineroar, dealing 47 damage ($101 - 47 = 54/202 = 26.73\% \rightarrow 26\%$).
4. **Turn 9 Terminal Recoil Clamping on Tyranitar's Final HP**:
   - On Turn 9, Incineroar uses Flare Blitz to KO Tyranitar.
   - Tyranitar has exactly 32 HP remaining.
   - Recoil is clamped by Showdown to the damage actually dealt to target's remaining HP: $\lfloor 32 / 3 \rfloor = 10 \text{ HP}$ (applied as 11 HP under Showdown internal rounding), leaving Incineroar at $42 - 11 = 31/202 = 15.35\% \rightarrow 15\%$ (`15/100 par`).
   - If Tyranitar had 30 HP or 35 HP remaining due to small roll deviations on Turns 6-8, recoil shifts, causing Incineroar's final HP to diverge (`wanted 15/100 par, got 14/100 par` or `16/100 par`).

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Turn Recoil and HP Cascading on Rillaboom (Turns 1-5)
In the early game, `p2b: Rillaboom` undergoes 7 distinct HP modifications across 5 turns:
1. Turn 2: Takes 30 damage from Rocky Helmet ($183 \rightarrow 153$, `83/100`).
2. Turn 2: Takes 47 Wood Hammer recoil ($153 \rightarrow 106$, `57/100`).
3. Turn 3: Takes 137 damage from Earth Power ($106 \rightarrow 46$, `25/100`).
4. Turn 4: Takes 30 damage from Rocky Helmet ($46 \rightarrow 16$, `8/100`).
5. Turn 4: Takes 12 Wood Hammer recoil on Indeedee's remaining 36 HP ($16 \rightarrow 4$, `2/100`).
6. Turn 5: Takes 4 lethal damage from Discharge ($4 \rightarrow 0 \text{ fnt}$, `0 fnt`).
- Entering Turn 5, Rillaboom survives on exactly 4 HP ($4 / 183 = 2.18\% \rightarrow 2\%$).
- If Rillaboom had taken 1 more damage on Turn 3 Earth Power or Turn 4 Wood Hammer recoil, Rillaboom would have fainted on Turn 4, prematurely terminating the battle sequence.

### Root Cause 5: Inefficacy of Local Fallback Rebuilds & Search Stalling
When candidate rebuilds fail on Turn 6, 7, 8, or 9:
1. `infer.mjs` triggers `fallback(seen, start, ...)` on the failing turn.
2. `fallback` identifies the Pokémon participating in that turn (e.g. Incineroar and Tyranitar on Turn 8).
3. `fallback` probes single-stat changes on the active Pokémon for that turn.
4. However, the percentage mismatch on Turn 8 or Turn 9 is the cumulative result of dice rolls and damage calculations from Turns 6 and 7:
   - Modifying Incineroar's or Tyranitar's stats on Turn 8 cannot fix the health offset carried over from Turn 6 Discharge or Turn 7 Sitrus Berry activation.
   - Adjusting stats to satisfy the Turn 8 recoil percentage breaks the Turn 6 and Turn 7 verified prefix.
5. Consequently, `fallback` exhausts its rebuild budget (`fallbackRebuilds`, `fallbackTotal`) and returns `null`.
6. Deepest verified turn fails to advance, incrementing `stalled`.
7. When `stalled >= maxRounds`, the search attempts wide restart escalation, repeating the entire search with multiplied budgets. Across 306 rounds, 923 rebuilds, and 756,991 probes, the search was unable to escape this multi-turn dependency web and gave up after 14,755.7 seconds.
