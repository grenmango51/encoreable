- Because many combinations of `(Atk, Def, maxHP, roll)` produce identical integer percentages, `evidencePass` cannot eliminate candidate spreads. Corviknight retained 100.6M spreads, Sneasler 80.4M, Politoed 59.7M, Farigiraf 29.9M, and Tyranitar 7.4M.

### Root Cause 2: Turn 8 Leech Life Drain Coupling with Tyranitar's Turn 2 Damage
On Turn 8, Golisopod Mega Evolves, takes Flamethrower from Salamence (`71/100`), and uses Leech Life on Tyranitar (`0 fnt`), healing via drain (`82/100`):
1. In Showdown, drain healing when a target faints is capped at 50% of the target's remaining HP:
   $$\text{drain} = \left\lfloor \frac{\min(\text{Damage}, \text{Tyranitar}_{\text{remaining HP}})}{2} \right\rfloor$$
2. In the ground-truth battle:
   - Tyranitar was reduced to 18 HP on Turn 2 by Rillaboom's Wood Hammer (`9/100`).
   - Tyranitar healed 12 HP on Turn 2 (`30/192`, `15/100`) and 12 HP on Turn 3 (`42/192`, `21/100`) from Grassy Terrain.
   - Tyranitar entered Turn 8 with exactly 42 HP.
   - Drain healed $\lfloor 42 / 2 \rfloor = 21$ HP.
   - Golisopod HP after Flamethrower was 130/182 (`71%`).
   - After drain: $130 + 21 = 151/182 \rightarrow \lfloor 151 \times 100 / 182 \rfloor = 82\%$.
   - On Turn 9, Grassy Terrain healed 11 HP: $151 + 11 = 162/182 \rightarrow \lfloor 162 \times 100 / 182 \rfloor = 89\%$.
3. If Tyranitar enters Turn 8 with 40 or 41 HP (caused by Rillaboom's Wood Hammer dealing slightly more damage on Turn 2, or Tyranitar having a different max HP):
   - Drain heals only $\lfloor 41 / 2 \rfloor = 20$ HP.
   - Golisopod HP becomes $130 + 20 = 150/182$ (still 82%).
   - On Turn 9, Grassy Terrain heals 11 HP: $150 + 11 = 161/182 \rightarrow \lfloor 161 \times 100 / 182 \rfloor = 88\%$!
   - This produces the exact mismatch observed in Round 13:
     `wanted |-heal|p2b: Golisopod|89/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2b: Golisopod|88/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`
4. The Turn 8 drain event tightly couples Golisopod's HP on Turn 9 all the way back to Rillaboom's Attack, Tyranitar's Defense, and Tyranitar's max HP on Turn 2.

### Root Cause 3: Politoed's Turn 6 Sandstorm Needle (`20/100y`)
Politoed participates in a multi-turn damage and healing chain across Turns 4, 5, 6, and 7:
1. Turn 5: Draco Meteor 1 (`43/100`), Grassy Terrain heal (`49/100`), Leftovers heal (`55/100`).
2. Turn 6: Draco Meteor 2 (`26/100`), Sandstorm damage (`20/100y`), Leftovers heal (`26/100`).
3. Turn 7: Draco Meteor 3 (`8/100`), Knock Off KO (`0 fnt`).
4. In Champions mode, when `percentage === 20`, Showdown inspects:
   ```javascript
   if (percentage === 20) {
     shared += this.hp * 5 > this.maxhp ? "y" : "r";
   }
   ```
5. For Politoed's max HP of 197:
   - $197 / 5 = 39.4 \rightarrow \text{requires } \text{HP} \ge 40$.
   - Percentage is 20 if $39.4 \le \text{HP} < 41.37 \rightarrow \text{requires } \text{HP} \in \{40, 41\}$.
   - If Politoed has $\le 39$ HP: it emits `19/100` or `20/100r`.
   - If Politoed has $\ge 42$ HP: it emits `21/100`.
6. To satisfy this line, the cumulative damage of two Draco Meteors, one Grassy Terrain heal, one Sandstorm tick, and one Leftovers tick across Turns 5 and 6 must leave Politoed with exactly 40 or 41 HP. Any 1-point deviation in Salamence's SpA, Politoed's SpD, or Politoed's max HP causes Turn 6 Sandstorm to fail (observed in Round 8: `got |-heal|p2b: Politoed|50/100y`).

### Root Cause 4: Rillaboom Cumulative Healing Oscillation and Turn Regression
1. In Round 13, the search reached Turn 8 before failing on Golisopod's Grassy Terrain heal (`89/100` vs `88/100`).
2. `inferSpreads` generated a new guess with adjusted spreads for Rillaboom, Tyranitar, and Salamence to fix the Turn 8 exchange.
3. In Round 14, this new guess broke Turn 3 / Turn 4:
   `reproduces through turn 3 - wanted |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|83/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p2a: Rillaboom|84/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`
4. The search regressed 5 full turns (from Turn 8 back to Turn 3).
5. Fallback on Turn 4 intervened:
   `fallback on turn 4: Rillaboom hp 17 - reproduces it`
6. Forcing Rillaboom's HP to 17 SP (192 HP) fixed the Turn 4 percentage, but an HP of 192 changes all subsequent recoil calculations:
   - On Turn 2: Wood Hammer recoil on Tyranitar produces `70/100` instead of `71/100`.
   - On Turn 10: Wood Hammer critical hit recoil produces `69/100` instead of `70/100`.
7. Each local fix at Turn 4 broke Turn 2, Turn 8, or Turn 10, causing the search to oscillate continuously between early-turn and late-turn percentage mismatches.

### Root Cause 5: Search Stalling and Restart Escalation
1. In `infer.mjs`:
   ```javascript
   const at = [diff?.turn ?? -1, diff?.index ?? -1];
   if (!deepest || past(at, deepest)) deepest = at;
   else stalled++;
   ```
2. Because Round 13 reached Turn 8, `deepest` was set to Turn 8.
3. When subsequent rounds failed at Turn 3, Turn 4, or Turn 6, `past(at, deepest)` returned `false`, incrementing `stalled` every single round.
4. After 16 stalled rounds, the initial search terminated with `stalled`.
5. `inferSpreads` escalated to sequential restarts:
   - **Restart 1 (`start: 'usage'`)**: Restarted from Smogon usage priors. Stalled at the same Turn 4 / Turn 8 circular constraints.
   - **Restart 2 (`wide: true`)**: Ran with 4x fallback rebuilds (`spend.rebuilds = FALLBACK_TOTAL * 4`). Fallback spent hundreds of rebuilds mutating Rillaboom and Politoed, exhausted `fallbackTotal`, and stalled again.
   - **Restart 3 (`widest: true`)**: Ran with `budgetScale = 4` across all 3 start strategies (`default`, `usage`, `wide`), multiplying search probe limits and guess tries.
6. In spectator view, each round of `evidencePass` across 8 active Pokémon with tens of millions of candidate spreads required between 30 and 125 seconds per round.
7. Across all restarts, the search ran **157 rounds, 528 rebuilds, 971,629 probes**, and **15,361.7 seconds (~4.27 hours)**.
8. When `unchanged` candidate bounds exhausted all budget levels (`++level >= budgets.length`), the search terminated with `stop = 'searches'`, recording `GAVE UP`.
