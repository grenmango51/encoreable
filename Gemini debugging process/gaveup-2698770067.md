## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Floor Percentage Masking
In Channel 0 (spectator view), exact HP values are completely withheld from both sides.
Every protocol line reports integer percentages:
$$\text{pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
Because max HP for every Pokémon is an unobserved hidden variable ($base + SP + 75$), each percentage point spans a variable range:
- Swampert ($base = 100, SP \in [0, 32]$): $\text{maxHP} \in [175, 207]$; 1% spans $1.75 \dots 2.07$ HP.
- Annihilape ($base = 110, SP \in [0, 32]$): $\text{maxHP} \in [185, 217]$; 1% spans $1.85 \dots 2.17$ HP.
- Archaludon ($base = 90, SP \in [0, 32]$): $\text{maxHP} \in [165, 197]$; 1% spans $1.65 \dots 1.97$ HP.
- Maushold ($base = 74, SP \in [0, 32]$): $\text{maxHP} \in [149, 181]$; 1% spans $1.49 \dots 1.81$ HP.
- Charizard ($base = 78, SP \in [0, 32]$): $\text{maxHP} \in [153, 185]$; 1% spans $1.53 \dots 1.85$ HP.

Every damage roll involves four simultaneous unknowns:
$$\text{Damage} = \left\lfloor \left( \left\lfloor \frac{2 \times 50 / 5 + 2}{50} \times \text{Power} \times \frac{\text{Atk}}{\text{Def}} \right\rfloor + 2 \right) \times \text{Modifier} \times \frac{\text{Roll}}{100} \right\rfloor$$
Coupled with the defender's unknown max HP, this produces a severely underdetermined nonlinear system.

$$\text{pct}_{\text{heal}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HP} + \lfloor \text{maxHP}/16 \rfloor}{\text{maxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
Any slight variation in incoming damage on previous turns cascades into a heal mismatch:
- Round 6: `reproduces through turn 2 - wanted |-heal|p1a: Archaludon|95/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p1b: Swampert|76/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`.
- Round 9: `reproduces through turn 4 - wanted |-heal|p1a: Archaludon|90/100|[from] item: Leftovers, got |-heal|p1a: Archaludon|89/100|[from] item: Leftovers`.
- Round 10: `reproduces through turn 4 - wanted |-heal|p1b: Swampert|83/100|[from] Grassy Terrain, got |-heal|p1b: Swampert|82/100|[from] Grassy Terrain`.
- Round 14: `reproduces through turn 5 - wanted |-heal|p1a: Archaludon|42/100|[from] item: Leftovers, got |-heal|p1a: Archaludon|41/100|[from] item: Leftovers`.

### Root Cause 4: Turn 4–7 Switch Cascades and Desynchronization
On Turn 2, Rillaboom's Eject Button forced Pelipper back in at 1 HP.
On Turn 3, Drought changed weather to Sun; Charizard killed Swampert; Pelipper entered and reset weather to Rain.
On Turn 4, Archaludon used Electro Shot in Rain (firing immediately without charging turn), boosted +1 SpA, and damaged Maushold.
On Turn 5, Charizard switched in, resetting weather to Sun; Rillaboom used Fake Out on Charizard; Maushold used Follow Me; Archaludon killed Maushold with Dragon Pulse.
On Turn 6, Whimsicott Encored Rillaboom; Charizard used Heat Wave in Sun; Rillaboom fainted; Archaludon boosted +1 Def from Stamina and fired Dragon Pulse back at Charizard.

Every turn involves weather shifts (Rain $\leftrightarrow$ Sun), ability triggers (Drizzle, Drought, Eject Button, Stamina, Prankster, Friend Guard), and multi-target spread damage.
In Round 15–16 (widest start):
- The search managed to reach Turn 6, but halted at Turn 7:
  `reproduces through turn 6 - wanted |-damage|p1b: Swampert|146/177, got |-damage|p1b: Swampert|145/177`
- Fallback perturbed Swampert and Maushold stats across 32 rebuilds:
  - Perturbing `Swampert atk 15` broke Turn 1 Wave Crash (`wanted 25/100, got 30/100`).
  - Perturbing `Swampert atk 32` broke Turn 7 Recoil (`wanted 20/100y, got 22/100`).
  - Perturbing `Swampert def 32` broke Turn 1 Wave Crash.
  - Perturbing `Swampert spe 32` broke Turn 3 Grassy Terrain heal.
  - Perturbing `Maushold spe 0` inverted turn order on Turn 6 (`wanted Beat Up, got Heat Wave`).

Because changing any stat to satisfy Turn 7 broke Turn 1, Turn 3, or Turn 6, the search was trapped in an irreducible cyclic contradiction.

### Root Cause 5: Joint Guessing Failure in `choose`
When `reconstruct` encounters diffs, the engine calls `choose(0, cond)` to evaluate candidate spread combinations:
1. `choose` runs `evidencePass({ exact: true })` over active combatants.
2. It attempts to find a simultaneous exact HP assignment matching every spectator line across all turns.
3. Due to the coupling of:
   - Wave Crash damage + recoil
   - Sitrus Berry heal + color threshold
   - 4 Beat Up hits
   - Weather Ball damage
   - Grassy Terrain healing (Turns 2–6)
   - Leftovers healing (Turns 4, 6)
   - Heat Wave spread damage
   - Electro Shot damage
   `paths(pre)` consistently returns `null`.
4. The search exhausts `GUESS_TRIES` ($32 \times S \times W$), triggering the `guessTries` limit.
5. As a consequence, `exact = null` and no dice pins are handed on to guide downstream simulation.

### Root Cause 6: The Fallback Exhaustion Loop
Without dice pins, `fallback(t, diff)` is invoked to test single-stat boundary and middle perturbations:
- In `default` start:
  - Round 4: fallback on Turn 1 exhausts 8 rebuilds without getting past the line (`fallbackRebuilds` hit).
  - Round 5: fallback on Turn 1 exhausts 8 rebuilds again.
  - After 8 rounds failing to advance `deepest`, search gives up and restarts from `usage`.
- In `usage` start:
  - Round 4: fallback finds `Annihilape def 32` passes Turn 1 in 6 rebuilds.
  - Advances to Turn 2, Turn 3, Turn 4.
  - Round 10: diffs on Turn 1 again; fallback exhausts 8 rebuilds.
  - Round 12: diffs on Turn 2 Rillaboom damage; fallback exhausts 8 rebuilds.
  - Round 14: diffs on Turn 4 Swampert recoil; fallback exhausts 2 rebuilds.
  - Search stalls after Round 20 and restarts with `wide`.
- In `wide` start:
  - Stalls at Turn 1; exhausts fallback rebuilds across 8 searches.
  - Search restarts with `widest` (all limits $4\times$ wider).
- In `widest` start:
  - Reaches Turn 6/7, but exhausts 32 rebuilds on Turn 7 Swampert/Maushold fallback.
  - Stalls on Round 16–20 and gives up completely.
