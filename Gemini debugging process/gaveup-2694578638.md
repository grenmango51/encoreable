## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Untargeted Spread Move Resolution via `getRandomTarget`
In Showdown, `Heat Wave` has target `allAdjacentFoes`. In double battles:
1. `reconstruct.mjs` recognizes `allAdjacentFoes` as non-choosable (`CHOOSABLE_TARGETS.has('allAdjacentFoes') === false`), omitting any target suffix and issuing simply `move 1 mega`.
2. When Showdown executes an untargeted spread move, it calls `this.battle.getRandomTarget(pokemon, move)` to select the primary target to display in the protocol line `|move|<pokemon>|<move>|<target>|[spread] ...`.
3. `getRandomTarget` draws directly from Showdown's pseudo-random number generator (`this.prng.sample(foes)`).
4. If preceding rolls in Turn 1 (Aerodactyl's Rock Slide accuracy on Delphox and Sneasler, Rock Slide damage rolls on both slots, and Rock Slide flinch roll on Sneasler) deviate by even a single PRNG step or roll value due to candidate stat combinations, the PRNG stream shifts.
5. Consequently, Delphox's Heat Wave resolves with:
   $$\text{Wanted: } \texttt{|move|p1a: Delphox|Heat Wave|p2b: Farigiraf|[spread] p2a,p2b}$$
   $$\text{Got: } \texttt{|move|p1a: Delphox|Heat Wave|p2a: Aerodactyl|[spread] p2a,p2b}$$
   Halting the rebuild immediately at Turn 1 index 11 (`1.11`).

### Root Cause 2: Cross-Turn Dependency and Rebuild Deadlock
When fallback triggers on Turn 1:
- Probing single stats (e.g. `Aerodactyl hp 0`, `Aerodactyl def 32`, or `Delphox def 32`) alters the damage roll calculation, which happens to shift the PRNG draw on Turn 1 and temporarily pass line `1.11`.
- However, as soon as Turn 1 passes, the rebuild advances to Turn 2:
  `round 7 (search 4): reproduces through turn 1 - wanted |-damage|p1a: Delphox|18/100, got |-damage|p1a: Delphox|15/100` (or `14/100`, `16/100`).

  `fallback on turn 1: Delphox spa 32 - stops at 1.13: wanted |-damage|p2a: Aerodactyl|76/100, got 79/100`.

- **The Deadlock**:
  - Fixing Turn 2 requires candidate guesses that break Turn 1 (`1.11`).
  - Fallback on Turn 2 cannot even evaluate candidate variations because the simulation crashes/diverges before Turn 2 begins!

### Root Cause 3: Ineffectiveness of 1D Fallback Probes on Coupled PRNG Streams

- In this battle, the simulation requires simultaneously satisfying:
  1. Aerodactyl Rock Slide damage roll on Delphox producing percentage `18/100`.

  3. Delphox surviving with enough HP and appropriate PRNG state to draw `p2b: Farigiraf` as the target of Heat Wave.
  4. Delphox Heat Wave damage roll on Aerodactyl producing percentage `76/100`.
  5. Delphox Heat Wave damage roll on Farigiraf producing percentage `63/100`.
