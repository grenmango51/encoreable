## 6. Comparison: Single-View vs. Spectator Inference

### Why `p2@p1` (Channel 1) and `p1@p2` (Channel 2) Passed
1. **4-Pokémon Single-Side `choose()` Never Exhausts `guessTries`**:
   - In `p2@p1` or `p1@p2`, one side's 6 Pokémon are fixed (`known[s] = true`), so `seq` in `choose()` contains **at most 4 unknown Pokémon** instead of 8!
