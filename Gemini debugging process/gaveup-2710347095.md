## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why All Views Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The `planSegment` + `recoverChargeTargets` Blind Spot on Encored Two-Turn Moves (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
In `reconstruct.mjs`, rebuilding a turn with a two-turn charging move (`Solar Beam`, `Electro Shot`, `Sky Attack`, etc.) relies on two steps working together:
1. **`recoverChargeTargets(plans)` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 208–225)**: Scans every `action` in `plans[k].actions[side]` where `!action.target && action.needsTarget`. When it finds the matching `|move|...|target|[from] lockedmove` on a later turn `later`, it copies `action.target = match.target` and sets **`action.landsOn = plans.indexOf(later)`**.
2. **`carriedInto(plans, t)` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 228–234 & 2523–2549)**: When Turn `t` fails (`4.10`) and neither Turn `t`'s dice (`resolveTurn`) nor Turn `t`'s own hidden choices (`variants[t]`) can fix it, `reconstructOnce` asks `carriedInto(plans, t)` which earlier turns `k < t` hold choices that land on turn `t` (`a?.landsOn === t`), and walks `variants[k]`!

**What broke on Turn 3 (`k = 3`) in this match**:
1. On Turn 3, `p1a: Whimsicott` used `Encore` on `p2b: Archaludon`, emitting `|-start|p2b: Archaludon|Encore` *before* `|move|p2b: Archaludon|Electro Shot||[still]`.
2. In `planSegment(segments[3], dex)` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 170–177):
   ```js
   if (kind === '-start' && /Encore$/.test(parts[3] || '')) {
     encored.add(parts[2]);

   }
   ```

3. Consequently, **`recoverChargeTargets(plans)` never saw Turn 3 `p2b: Archaludon`**, so:
   - `plans[3].actions.p2[1]` never recovered `target: 'p1b: Charizard'`.
   - **`landsOn: 4` was never set on `plans[3]`!**
4. When `reconstructOnce` played Turn 3 (`choiceFor`, lines 525–544), `action === undefined` caused `choiceFor` to treat `p2b: Archaludon`'s Turn 3 action as an invisible choice with width 8 (`move 1 +1`, `move 1 +2`, `move 1 -1`, `move 2`, `move 3 +1`, `move 3 +2`, `move 3 -1`, `move 4`).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why All Three Views (`p2@p1`, `p1@p2`, `both@spectator`) Gave Up
Even in `p2@p1` (P1 known) and `p1@p2` (P2 known), `reconstructOnce` runs the exact same `planSegment` $\to$ `recoverChargeTargets` $\to$ `carriedInto` pipeline. Because `p2b: Archaludon` was Encored on Turn 3 before its charging move (`Electro Shot||[still]`), `plans[3].actions.p2[1]` was discarded as `undefined` in all three views, preventing `recoverChargeTargets` from attaching `landsOn: 4` and preventing `carriedInto(plans, 4)` from revisiting `variants[3]`. Thus all three views froze at `4.10` (`searches` exhaustion) even when Stat Points were completely known.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Track `landsOn` on Charging Turns Even When the Actor Was Encored (`planSegment` / `recoverChargeTargets`)**:
   In `planSegment` (`reconstruct.mjs` lines 177–193), skipping `if (encored.has(parts[2])) continue;` is intended to avoid treating the executed Encored move as the player's *chosen* move (since they may have chosen another move and been forced into the Encored one). However, when the executed Encored move is a two-turn charge move (`|move|...|Electro Shot||[still]`) whose `lockedmove` discharge appears on a later turn `later`, `landsOn` **must still be recorded on turn `k`** (e.g., via a separate `charges` list or a flag on `plans[k]` marking `{ side, slot, landsOn }` without fixing `action.moveid`), so that `carriedInto(plans, t)` knows turn `k`'s `variants[k]` can change where turn `t`'s `[from] lockedmove` fires!
2. **Note on Encore + `OverrideAction` Mechanics (`sim/battle-actions.ts`)**:
   In Showdown, if `Archaludon` had chosen `move 1 +2` (`Electro Shot`) on Turn 3, `Encore`'s `onOverrideAction` returns `undefined` (because `move.id === this.effectState.move`) and preserves `targetLoc = 2` (`p1b: Charizard`). Thus trying `variants[3] = 1` (`move 1 +2`) immediately reproduces both Turn 3 and Turn 4 (`4.10`). Making `carriedInto(plans, 4)` include Turn 3 directly resolves this entire class of Encored charge-move stalls across all three views.
