## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Aborted

The failure on `both@spectator` is a two-stage interaction between a **Turn 8 speed-order / invisible-choice tie** and a **Turn 5 HP-ambiguity abort inside `fallback()`** that prevented `fallback()` from ever probing a single stat on Turn 8.

### A. Why Turn 8 Stalled at `8.0` (`Grimmsnarl` vs. `Incineroar` & `Venusaur`'s `Protect`)
1. **Default Invisible Substitution `Protect` (`+4` Priority)**:
   Because `p2a: Venusaur` emitted no `|move|` or `|cant|` line in Turn 8, `planSegment()` (`reconstruct.mjs:107`) left `p2a` with no action (`!action`). `choiceFor()` (`reconstruct.mjs:525–544`) builds invisible-choice variants in movelist order (`selfSwitchLast`), placing `Venusaur`'s slot-1 move **`Protect` (`+4` priority)** at `variant = 0`. Thus `v = 0` immediately printed `|move|p2a: Venusaur|Protect|p2a: Venusaur` at index `0` (`score = [0, 2, 2]`).
2. **Why `variants[8] = 1, 2, 3` Tied at `score = [0, 2, 2]`**:
   When `reconstruct` tried `Venusaur`'s other moves (`Sleep Powder`, `Sludge Bomb`, `Leaf Storm`, all `0` priority), **`p1a: Incineroar` (`Spe 82` in Start 1, `Spe 80` in Start 2) still outsped `p2b: Grimmsnarl` (`Spe SP = 0` $\to$ `Spe 72`)** and acted at `8.0` instead of `Grimmsnarl`! Because `|move|p1a: Incineroar|...` also has `score = [0, 2, 2]` (only `''` and `'move'` match `|move|p2b: Grimmsnarl|Spirit Break|p1a: Incineroar`), `consider()` (`reconstruct.mjs:2405`, strict `ahead`) never replaced `variant = 0`, leaving `diffs[0]` on `|move|p2a: Venusaur|Protect|p2a: Venusaur`.
3. **Why Evidence Hooks Learned Nothing & `Grimmsnarl`'s `Spe` Never Moved**:
   - All `Sassy` usage spreads for `Grimmsnarl` in `usage-spreads.json` have `Spe SP = 0` (`Spe = 72`), whereas the mimic battle rolled `randomSpread(rng, 66)` for `Grimmsnarl` (5 of 7 seen Pokémon were random), allowing `Grimmsnarl` to reach up to `Spe 100` (`Spe SP >= 9` outspeeds `0`-Spe `Incineroar` `80`; `Spe SP >= 11` outspeeds `2`-Spe `Incineroar` `82`).
   - Because `8.0` is the very first line of Turn 8, `evidencePass` stopped before `8.0` and `speed.mjs` never observed `Grimmsnarl` moving before `Incineroar`. Thus `picks` remained `unchanged` across Rounds 12–16 (`search 1..4`).

### B. Why `fallback` on Turn 8 Aborted at `5.16` Without Probing a Single Stat
Normally, `fallback()` (`infer.mjs:761–786`, `order && disputed.size`) exists specifically for this scenario—pushing `Grimmsnarl`'s and `Incineroar`'s Speeds apart when an invisible `Protect` masks a speed order. **However, in all 6 starts, `fallback` never reached line `761` because of a pre-check abort at line `617`:**

1. **The `122/187` vs. `123/187` (`5.16`) HP-Precision Split on `Incineroar`**:
   Both Start 1 (`17/0/32/0/14/2`) and Start 2 (`17/0/20/0/14/0`) placed `Incineroar` at **`hp = 17` SP (`MaxHP = 187`)**. At `MaxHP = 187`:

   - Turn 5's Grassy Terrain upkeep heals $\lfloor 187 / 16 \rfloor = +11$ HP:
     - $111 + 11 = \mathbf{122/187}$ ($65.24\% \to \mathbf{65/100}$)
     - $112 + 11 = \mathbf{123/187}$ ($65.78\% \to \mathbf{65/100}$)
   - Turn 5's poison tick at **`5.16`** deals $\lfloor 187 / 8 \rfloor = -23$ HP:
     - $122 - 23 = \mathbf{99/187}$ ($52.94\% \to \mathbf{52/100}$, **`wanted`**!)
     - $123 - 23 = \mathbf{100/187}$ ($53.48\% \to \mathbf{53/100}$, **`got`**!)
   Notice that **both `111/187` and `112/187` round identically to `59/100` and `65/100` on Channel 0**, differing only at `5.16` (`52/100` vs. `53/100`).
2. **How `Rounds 12–16` Passed `5.16`, While `fallback` Failed at `5.16`**:

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Grimmsnarl`'s exact Speed SP (and `Venusaur`'s exact Speed/HP/bulk) are known constants, so `Grimmsnarl` immediately outspeeds `Incineroar` once `Incineroar`'s Speed SP is narrowed, and `fallback` never loses `p2`'s exact HPs (`|split|` shows Channel 2 exact integers).
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Incineroar`'s exact `MaxHP`, `Def/SpD`, and `Spe` are fixed constants, and Channel 1 prints `Incineroar`'s exact `hp/maxhp` integers on Turn 5 (`59/100`, `65/100`, `5.16` `psn`), completely eliminating the `122` vs. `123` percentage ambiguity even when `fallback()` runs with `exact: null`! Thus `fallback()` reaches Turn 8 cleanly and pushes `Grimmsnarl`'s `spe` above known `Incineroar`'s `Spe`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/infer.mjs` & `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`)
1. **Pass `proven.exact` and `built.variants` Into `fallback` Pre-Checks (`infer.mjs:492, 499, 604`)**:
   In `fallback()`, `rebuild(start, budget)` runs with `exact: null` and `handed = handOn(built, null)` (stripping `built.variants` and `proven.exact`). Whenever an earlier turn had a non-zero `variant` (such as an invisible move on a paralyzed/fainted slot), `base` calls `forget(k)`, drops Turn 5's pins, and hits a spectator-only percentage ambiguity (`122/187` vs. `123/187` both printing `65/100`) that `built` had already solved via `exact`! Handing `exact` (or `proven.exact`) and `built.variants` to `fallback`'s `rebuild` for turns `< t` prevents `fallback` from losing already-verified earlier turns.
2. **Gate `fallback` Abort on `dice` (`maxBacktracks > 0`), Not `base` (`maxBacktracks: 0`) (`infer.mjs:614`)**:
   `base` runs with `maxBacktracks: 0` (even at `S = 4`). Even when `dice = await rebuild(start, budgets[1])` (`maxBacktracks: 2 * S`) has the backtracking budget to resolve an earlier turn's HP ambiguity and reach Turn `t`, line `614` checks `if (reach(base)[0] < t)` instead of `reach(dice)[0] < t`, throwing away `dice`'s progress, aborting Turn `t`'s `fallback`, and permanently blacklisting Turn `t` in `spend.fruitless`.
