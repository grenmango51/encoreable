### Root Cause 2: Turn 4 Invisible Choice PRNG Desynchronization
On Turn 4, Charizard was KOed by Sneasler's Dire Claw before using its move:
1. In the ground truth mimic battle, Charizard's AI queued `move heatwave mega, move wideguard`.
   - `Heat Wave` has target `allAdjacentFoes`. In Showdown, moves that do not target a specific slot (`allAdjacentFoes`, `allySide`, `self`) omit the target location string.
   - When a move lacks a target string, Showdown's `BattleQueue.resolveAction` and `Battle.getTarget` invoke `Battle.getRandomTarget` -> `Side.randomFoe` -> `battle.prng.sample(2)` to assign a nominal target during action scheduling and speed resolution.
   - On Turn 4, this internal targeting consumed **4 random draws** (`random(2)`).
2. In `reconstruct.mjs`, when reconstructing an invisible move for a fainted Pokémon, variant 0 selected Charizard's first move: `Hurricane` (`move hurricane +1 mega`).
   - `Hurricane` has target `normal` (a choosable target), so `reconstruct` appended an explicit target string (`+1`).
   - Because an explicit target was provided, Showdown's `getRandomTarget` was **not called**.
3. As a result, the PRNG draw stream in `reconstruct` entered Turn 5 with a **4-draw offset** relative to the ground truth battle.

### Root Cause 3: Turn 5 Secondary Effect Mismatch and the Fallback Rebuild Trap
On Turn 5, Sneasler attacked Pelipper with Dire Claw:
1. In the ground truth battle:
   - Dire Claw's 30% status chance rolled on Draw 56: `random(100) = 43 >= 30` (no proc).
   - Poison Touch's 30% poison chance rolled on Draw 57: `random(10) = 7 >= 3` (no proc).
   - Pelipper took damage and remained un-statused.
2. In `reconstruct`:
   - Due to the 4-draw offset, Dire Claw's status check consumed Draw 49 instead of Draw 56.
   - Draw 49 had value `random(100) = 14 < 30`, triggering Dire Claw's secondary effect.
   - Draw 50 rolled status: `random(3) = 0` (poison).
   - Reconstruct emitted `|-status|p2b: Pelipper|psn`.
3. Reconstruct immediately failed at Turn 5, line 8:
   - `expected: |move|p1a: Salamence|Hyper Voice|p2a: Venusaur|[spread] `
   - `actual: |-status|p2b: Pelipper|psn`
4. The inference loop interpreted this mismatch as a Stat Point failure and entered `fallback(seen, start)` on Turn 5:
   - Fallback ran 8 rebuilds modifying the Speed, HP, Attack, Defense, and Special Attack of Venusaur, Pelipper, Salamence, and Sneasler.
   - Because the poison proc was caused by the PRNG draw sequence rather than Stat Points, **no Stat Point combination could avoid the status roll**.
   - Fallback reported: `fallback on turn 5: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds` and added `fallbackRebuilds` to limits.

### Root Cause 4: Multi-Restart Stall Cascade
Because Turn 5 could not be bypassed under the real seed:
1. Budget level 4 (`resample: 1`) periodically sampled a new random seed that avoided the Turn 5 poison roll, allowing individual exploratory searches to reach Turn 9 or 10.
