## 6. Why `both@spectator` (and all views) Stalled and Gave Up (Detailed Breakdown)

### A. The Spectator Channel (Channel 0) Information Deprivation
1. On Channel 0, every switch-in and damage line is rendered as an integer percentage (`100/100`, `54/100`, `77/100`, `17/100`, `50/100g`, `49/100`, `64/100`).

3. In `firstGuesses()`, every unknown Pokémon falls back to `defaultSpread(dex, set, undefined)`, which defaults HP Stat Points to **32** for all Pokémon.

### B. The Revival Blessing Odd/Even Max HP Discrepancy (`49/100` vs `50/100y`)
On Turn 4, Pawmot casts `Revival Blessing` to revive Floette-Eternal:
1. Showdown implements Revival Blessing as:
   $$\text{Revived HP} = \lfloor \text{Max HP} / 2 \rfloor$$
2. The displayed health percentage is:
   $$\text{Display} = \lfloor \text{Revived HP} \times 100 / \text{Max HP} \rfloor$$
3. Mathematical behavior under integer parity:
   - **If Max HP is Even ($2k$)**:
     $$\text{Revived HP} = k \implies \lfloor k \times 100 / 2k \rfloor = 50\% \implies \mathbf{50/100y}$$
   - **If Max HP is Odd ($2k + 1$)**:
     $$\text{Revived HP} = k \implies \lfloor k \times 100 / (2k + 1) \rfloor = \lfloor 50 - \frac{50}{2k+1} \rfloor = \mathbf{49\%} \implies \mathbf{49/100}$$
4. In the ground truth, Floette-Eternal has planted HP SP of 10:
   $$\text{Max HP} = 74 + 10 + 75 = 159 \quad (\text{Odd!})$$
   $$\text{Revived HP} = \lfloor 159 / 2 \rfloor = 79 \implies \lfloor 7900 / 159 \rfloor = 49\% \implies \mathbf{49/100}$$
5. Under `defaultSpread`, Floette is assigned HP SP 32:
   $$\text{Max HP} = 74 + 32 + 75 = 181 \quad (\text{Odd, but 181 gives } \lfloor 9000/181 \rfloor = 49\%)$$
   However, during single-stat probing in Round 2, the engine tried various standard spreads where Floette's nature or base combinations resulted in an even HP (e.g. HP SP 25 $\rightarrow$ 174, HP SP 31 $\rightarrow$ 180, or level rounding):
   `round 2, 0.2s: reproduces through turn 3 - wanted |-heal|p2: Floette|49/100|[from] move: Revival Blessing, got |-heal|p2: Floette|50/100y|[from] move: Revival Blessing`
