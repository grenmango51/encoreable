## 6. Why `both@spectator` Failed (78 Rounds, 595 Rebuilds, 171,884 Probes, Searches Limit Hit)

### A. The Mechanics of the Turn 5 Hyper Voice Mismatch
On Turn 5, Salamence uses Hyper Voice, a spread sound-based move:
$$\text{Damage}_{\text{spread}} = \left\lfloor \text{BaseDamage} \times 0.75 \right\rfloor$$
Hyper Voice targets `p2b: Pawmot` with spread flag `[spread] p2a,p2b`:
```
line 5.8: |move|p1a: Salamence|Hyper Voice|p2b: Pawmot|[spread] p2a,p2b
line 5.9: |-damage|p2a: Delphox|0 fnt
line 5.10: |-crit|p2b: Pawmot
line 5.11: |-damage|p2b: Pawmot|...
```

In Pokémon Showdown's battle engine, when a spread move executes:
1. It loops through active targets in order (`p2a`, then `p2b`).
2. For each target, it calculates hit, damage, and checks if the target faints (`faint()`).
3. If the primary target or first defender faints, the `|-damage|...|0 fnt` line and subsequent faint handling emit immediately.
4. If `p2a: Delphox` survives with even **1 HP**, Showdown emits `|-damage|p2a: Delphox|<remaining>/100`, does not emit `0 fnt`, and proceeds immediately to `p2b: Pawmot`, emitting `|-crit|p2b: Pawmot`!

This produced the exact diff that stalled the search across dozens of rounds:
```
wanted |-damage|p2a: Delphox|0 fnt, got |-crit|p2b: Pawmot
```

## 7. Conclusion

Match `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2699891620` gave up after **4,397.2s** because the inference engine encountered an irreconcilable multi-turn damage and KO threshold bottleneck on **Turn 5**.

On Turn 5, P1 Salamence fired a spread Hyper Voice targeting P2 Pawmot with Delphox as the secondary spread target. In the ground truth battle, Delphox took fatal damage and fainted at line 5.9 (`|-damage|p2a: Delphox|0 fnt`), preceding Pawmot's damage and crit lines. However, all candidate stat point assignments that accurately reproduced Turn 1 Nasty Plot/Tailwind speeds and Turn 2 damage percentages into Delphox (`52/100`) and Sinistcha (`22/100`) left Delphox surviving Turn 5 with minimal HP. When Delphox survives, Showdown emits survival damage rather than a faint line, causing the simulation to mismatch at line 5.9 (`wanted |-damage|p2a: Delphox|0 fnt, got |-crit|p2b: Pawmot`).

Because speed adjustments broke Turn 1 move order and SpA/SpD adjustments broke Turn 2 damage lines, fallback grid sweeps repeatedly exhausted 128 rebuilds across all starts. The deadlock was so severe that even single-player views (`p1@p2` at 3,843s and `p2@p1` at 4,397s) failed to bridge the gap, leading all three views to terminate with `stop = "searches"` and limits `["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches"]`.
