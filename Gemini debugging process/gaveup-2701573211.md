## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. Blind Spot in `evidence.mjs`: `carriedChange` Drops `Metal Burst` When Neither Side Is Exact
In `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` (`carriedChange`, lines 1683–1703), carried-damage moves (`Counter`, `Mirror Coat`, `Metal Burst`, `Comeuppance`) are only hooked in two cases:
1. `U.exact` is `true` (user's exact damage dealt/taken is printed — true in `p1@p2` Channel 2); or
2. `T.exact` is `true` (target's exact HP is printed — true in `p2@p1` Channel 1).

In **`both@spectator` (Channel 0)**, neither `Bastiodon` (`U.exact = false`) nor `Meganium` (`T.exact = false`) has exact HP printed. `carriedChange` therefore logs `'its damage was carried over from an earlier hit, so it was not used'` and falls back to a generic `band('down')` check on `35/100 brn`! Consequently:
- `evidencePass` **never links** `Metal Burst`'s damage (`1.5 × d`) to `Weather Ball`'s damage `d` or to `Bastiodon`'s `MaxHP` (`47/100`).
- In Round 2, `evidencePass` happily returns **`proof.exact` (`3 turns exact`)** for the Round 2 guess (`Meganium 16/0/0/17/0/2`, `Bastiodon 7/0/2/0/7/0`), leaving `picks` **unchanged** (`unchanged === true`) and forcing `fallback` to solve `2.7` on its own.

### B. The 2-Stat Coupling Trap at Turn 2 (`2.7: |-damage|p1b: Meganium|35/100 brn`)
From Round 2 onward, every start converged onto the same central guess for `Meganium` and `Bastiodon`:
- `Meganium`: `16/0/0/17/0/2` (`hp = 16`, `MaxHP = 171`, `burn = 10`, pre-`2.7` HP = `161`, `SpA = 198`)
- `Bastiodon`: `7/0/2/0/7/0` (`hp = 7`, `MaxHP = 142`, `SpD = 181`)

Under these stats, `Weather Ball` has damage ladder `[63, 64, 64, 66, 66, 67, 67, 69, 69, 70, 70, 72, 72, 73, 73, 75]`. The **only** roll producing `47/100` (`67` or `68` HP out of `142`) at `2.5` is **`d = 75`**, which forces `Metal Burst = 112` and drops `Meganium` to `161 - 112 = 49 / 171` (**`28/100 brn`**; trace lines 9, 23: `wanted 60/171 brn, got 49/171 brn`).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Bastiodon` (`139 HP, 183 SpD`) is fixed and exact, and Channel 2 prints exact `hp/maxhp` for `Bastiodon` (`66/139`, revealing `d = 73` exactly!). Thus `carriedChange` in `evidence.mjs` line 1688 takes the `U.exact` branch, handing `amountChange` the exact `109` Metal Burst damage so `Meganium` is immediately narrowed to spreads where `187 - 11 - 109 = 67` (`35/100`), bypassing `fallback` entirely.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Meganium` (`187 HP, 210 SpA`) is fixed and Channel 1 prints `176/187` after burn and `67/187` after `Metal Burst` (`109` exact damage!). `carriedChange` (`evidence.mjs` line 1692) takes the `T.exact` branch (`dealtChange`), enumerating every `Weather Ball` damage `d` where $\lfloor 1.5 \times d \rfloor = 109$ (`d = 73`!) and narrowing `Bastiodon` directly to `(hp, spd)` pairs that take `73` damage and print `47/100`.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs`, `infer.mjs`)
1. **Extend `carriedChange` (`evidence.mjs:1683`) to Two-Sided Spectator Views**:
   When neither `U.exact` nor `T.exact` holds, `carriedChange` currently abandons `Metal Burst` / `Counter` / `Mirror Coat` / `Comeuppance` to `band('down')`, discarding the `1.5×` / `2.0×` link to `T.lastDealt`. Even in Channel 0, `U.lastDealt` (from `T`'s preceding hit on `U`) holds the exact candidate damage table `d` per `(A, D)` pair, which directly bounds `T`'s post-hit HP via `1.5 × d` / `2.0 × d` (just as `drainChange` does for `Draining Kiss` / `Giga Drain`).
2. **Sample Spot Pairs *Before* Exhaustive 1D Sweeps in `fallback` (`infer.mjs:826–845`)**:
   In Start 6 (`128` rebuilds), `fallback` already knew the extreme endpoints (`Meganium hp 32 -> 34/100`, `Bastiodon hp 0 -> 32/100`) by rebuild #11, yet spent rebuilds #49–#128 testing every single intermediate 1D integer (`Meganium hp 1..31`, `Bastiodon hp 1..25`, `Bastiodon spd 1..27`) before ever reaching the pair search on line 833! Trying pairs of the `moved` stats' endpoints/best values (`spots(a) × spots(b)`) **before** (or interleaved with) the exhaustive 1D sweep would have resolved `(Meganium hp 32, Bastiodon hp 0)` in ~25 rebuilds instead of timing out at 128.
3. **Skip `halve` on Stats Whose Ends Only Break Earlier Turns (`infer.mjs:813`)**:
   `halve` spent **22 rebuilds** in Start 6 binary-searching `Scovillain`'s `atk, def, spa, spd, spe` between `'early'` (`1.13` / `1.0`) and `'same'`, even though `Scovillain` did not move the failing line `2.7` at all (`d.moved === false`, and `1.13` only tripped because `Scovillain` was at `66/66` SP!). Restricting `halve` to stats where `'early'` comes from the failing turn itself (or after `moved` pairs are tried) prevents off-target SP redistribution from eating `fallback` budget.
4. **Canonicalize `markOf(diff)` Across `exact` vs. Percentage Views (`infer.mjs:426`)**:
   Keying `spend.fruitless` on `diff.expected` (`35/100 brn` vs. `60/171 brn`) caused every start to run `fallback` **twice** on the exact same Turn 2 line (`240` duplicate rebuilds). Normalizing HP tokens in `markOf(diff)` to `(turn, index, lineKind, actor)` (or percentage) prevents `fallback` from re-running an already-exhausted line after `evidencePass` hands back `exact`.
5. **Preserve `usage` Start's Exact Bulk When `aimFor` Only Eliminates Non-Contiguous Mod-16 Burn Gaps (`infer.mjs:1026`)**:
   In Starts 2 & 5, `Meganium` started at `hp = 32` (`#1 Modest`), yet Round 1's `choose()` put `aimFor(kn, prev)` ahead of `prev`, shifting `hp` to `16` because burn `94/100` poked holes (`[5..11]`, `[21..28]`) in `[0, 32]`. Keeping `prev` (or testing `prev` before `aimFor` when `start === 'usage'`) would preserve `Meganium hp = 32` across Round 1.
6. **Note on `mimic.mjs` Two-Turn Move Target Reading (`mimic.mjs:106`)**:
   `readIntents()` only reads `|move|` lines and ignores `|-anim|` targets, causing `Mega Sol` `Solar Beam` (and similar single-turn `|-prepare|`+`|-anim|` moves) to lose its real target and diverge on Turn 1.
