## 6. Root Mechanical Cause of the Divergence

1. **Turn 3 Encore on Milotic**:
   - On Turn 3, `p2b: Whimsicott` uses `Encore` on `p1b: Milotic` (`move encore +2`).
   - Milotic had used `Protect` on Turn 2.
   - Milotic receives the Encore volatile condition (`-start|p1b: Milotic|Encore`).
   - Encore disables Milotic's other three moves (`Muddy Water`, `Hypnosis`, `Coil`), marking them `disabled: true` with `disabledSource: 'Encore'`.

2. **Turn 5 Imprison by Indeedee**:
   - On Turn 5, `p2a: Indeedee` uses `Imprison` (`move imprison`).
   - Indeedee's moveset is `Expanding Force`, `Imprison`, `Trick Room`, `Protect`.
   - In Showdown's `data/moves.ts` (`imprison.condition.onFoeDisableMove`), Imprison checks all moves known by the Imprison user and calls `pokemon.disableMove(moveSlot.id, true)` on opposing Pokémon.
   - Because Indeedee knows `Protect`, Milotic's `Protect` is disabled with `isHidden = true` (`moveSlot.disabled = 'hidden'`).
   - Consequently, all four of Milotic's moves are simultaneously disabled:
     - `Muddy Water`: disabled by Encore
     - `Hypnosis`: disabled by Encore
     - `Coil`: disabled by Encore
     - `Protect`: disabled by Imprison

3. **Turn 6 Move Request and Choice Resolution in `playMimic`**:
   - In `sim/pokemon.ts` (`Pokemon.prototype.getMoves`):
     When computing available moves for Milotic, each move's `disabled` property is checked.
     For `Protect`, `disabled === 'hidden'` evaluates to `!restrictData` (`true` in doubles).
     Because all 4 moves are disabled, `hasValidMove` evaluates to `false`, and `pokemon.getMoves()` returns an empty array `[]`.
   - `MimicPlayer` submits the turn choices:
     - Arcanine: `Head Smash` targeting slot 2 (`move 2 2`)
     - Milotic: `Protect` (`move 4`)
     Raw submitted string: `>p1 move 2 2, move 4`.

     - Arcanine's choice is registered as `move headsmash +2`.
     - Milotic's choice is processed: `moves = pokemon.getMoves()`.
     - Because `moves.length === 0`, lines 699–709 execute:
