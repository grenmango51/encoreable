## 5. Detailed Breakdown: Why `p1@p2` Gave Up While `both@spectator` Passed

In `all.jsonl`, `p1@p2` consumed 18,377.7 seconds across 34 rounds and 448 rebuilds before giving up with `stop: "searches"`, leaving Sinistcha completely unresolved (`seen: false`, 136,663,185 spreads). Meanwhile, `both@spectator` passed in 8,297.9 seconds (106 rounds, 452 rebuilds).

The investigation revealed the exact mechanical factors that caused this failure:

### A. The Double-Invisible Choice & Combinatorial Explosion on Turn 2
On Turn 2:
1. `p1b: Floette-Eternal` rolled full paralysis (`|cant|p1b: Floette|par`), emitting no `|move|` line.
2. `p2b: Volcarona` flinched from Incineroar's Fake Out (`|cant|p2b: Volcarona|flinch`), emitting no `|move|` line.
3. In `reconstruct.mjs`, when a Pokémon faints, flinches, or is fully paralyzed, its slot has no action recorded in `plan.actions`. The simulator marks it as an **invisible choice**:
   - Floette: 4 usable moves (`Protect` [1], `Calm Mind` [1], `Moonblast` [3 targets], `Dazzling Gleam` [1]) = 6 options.
   - Volcarona: 4 usable moves (`Tailwind` [1], `Struggle Bug` [1], `Overheat` [3 targets], `Rage Powder` [1]) = 6 options.
   - Incineroar (`Fake Out` targeting foes) = 3 options.
   - Raichu (`Zap Cannon` targeting foes) = 3 options.
   - Combined turn variant width: $3 \times 3 \times 6 \times 6 = \mathbf{324\text{ variants}}$!
4. The engine pins `MAX_VARIANTS = 256`. Because $324 > 256$, the search cannot even walk the full variant space if indexed in the upper tail, and flags `variants` in the limits.

### B. The Asymmetric Speed & Move Priority Deadlock
In the ground-truth battle:
- Volcarona selected **`Rage Powder`** (priority +2).
- Action order: Incineroar (`Fake Out`, priority +3) $\rightarrow$ Volcarona flinches at priority +2 $\rightarrow$ Raichu (`Zap Cannon`, priority 0) $\rightarrow$ Floette paralyzed at priority 0.

In `reconstruct.mjs`:
- The rebuild loop always starts at `variant = 0`.
- Under Variant 0, Volcarona selects `options[0]`: `Tailwind` (priority 0).
- Floette selects `options[0]`: `Protect` (priority +4).
- When Volcarona's move has priority 0:
  - In `p1@p2`, **Player 2's sets are known and fixed**.
  - Raichu-Mega-Y has **190 Speed**. Volcarona has **157 Speed**.
  - Because 190 > 157, Raichu **always** acts before Volcarona at priority 0!
  - The simulator executes Raichu's `Zap Cannon` before Volcarona acts.
  - But the observed log demands `|cant|p2b: Volcarona|flinch` at index 3 before `|move|p2a: Raichu|Zap Cannon|p1b: Floette`!
  - Reconstruct immediately fails at index 3:
    `wanted |cant|p2b: Volcarona|flinch, got |move|p2a: Raichu|Zap Cannon|p1b: Floette`

### C. Fallback Impotence on Known-Side Turn Order
When reconstruct encounters a divergence, `fallback(seen, start, ...)` attempts to find an inferred Pokémon whose Stat Points could resolve the order mismatch:
- In `p1@p2`, `known = [false, true]`. Only Player 1's Pokémon (`p1:0` to `p1:5`) are in `unknown`.
- Raichu and Volcarona belong to Player 2 (the known side). Their base stats, natures, and planted Stat Points are fixed constants.
- No change to Player 1's Stat Points can alter the speed order between Raichu (190) and Volcarona (157)!
- `fallback` tests variations of P1's Pokémon (Floette and Incineroar), finds that neither can affect the order between two P2 Pokémon, logs:
  `fallback on turn 2: |cant|p2b: Volcarona|flinch - nothing gets past the line in 2 rebuilds`
  and returns `null`.

### D. Probe Budget Exhaustion on Dead-End Variant 0
In `reconstruct.mjs`:
1. Line 2409 runs `resolveTurn(2)` on `entering` (Variant 0).
2. Because Variant 0 cannot legally advance past index 3, `resolveTurn` runs through its chance pairs, wide dice, and candidate permutations attempting to reconcile the impossible line.
3. This consumes the entire probe allocation for Turn 2 (`probes >= budget`).
4. When `rewalk()` subsequently inspects other variants, `spent[t]` has already reached `maxProbes`.

### E. Backtrack Traps into Pre-Turn Ineffective RNG

### F. Lock-Out of Post-Turn 1 Protocol & `seen: false` on Sinistcha
Because every single rebuild in `p1@p2` failed on Turn 2:
- `built.inputLog` only ever verified through Turn 1.
- In Turn 1, Player 1 only fielded Basculegion (which fainted) and Floette-Eternal. Incineroar entered on upkeep.

- Sinistcha's entire spread space remained untouched at **136,663,185 spreads** (100%).

### G. Why `p1@p2` Hit `stop: "searches"` after 34 Rounds
In `infer.mjs`:
```js

  if (++level >= budgets.length) { stop = 'searches'; break; }
} else {
  level = floor;

```
1. Because `unchanged` remained true every round, `level` incremented on every pass: Level 0 $\rightarrow$ Level 1 $\rightarrow$ Level 2 $\rightarrow$ Level 3 $\rightarrow$ Level 4.
2. At Level 4 (`budgets.length = 4`), the round loop aborted with `stop = 'searches'`.
3. The engine invoked `restarts()`:
   - `start = 'usage'`: Rebuilt again; stalled on Turn 2; `unchanged = true`; hit `stop = 'searches'`.
   - `start = 'wide'`: 4x fallback rebuilds; stalled on Turn 2; hit `stop = 'searches'`.
   - `start = 'widest'`: `budgetScale = 4` (16,000 probes, 24 backtracks).
4. Under `widest`, each rebuild took up to 10–15 minutes because 24 backtracks were executed per rebuild, exploring millions of states across Turn 1 and Turn 2.
5. In total, 34 rounds and 448 rebuilds ran, consuming **4,246,810 probes** and **18,377.7 seconds** (5.10 hours), until `widest` also exhausted all budget levels and permanently gave up.

### H. Why `both@spectator` Passed
In `both@spectator`, **Player 2 was also unobserved and unknown** (`known = [false, false]`):
1. Raichu and Volcarona were in `unknown`, rather than fixed constants.
2. Turn 1 featured Basculegion outspeeding Raichu-Mega-Y. In `both@spectator`, `orderSpeeds` dynamically linked Basculegion's speed to Raichu's speed.

6. Across 106 rounds and 452 rebuilds (8,297.9s), the search navigated the joint parameter space, eventually hitting a start where Variant 31 was solved, Turn 2 reproduced, and the entire 10-turn battle reproduced to completion (`stop: "complete"`).
