## 6. Spectator Search Pathology (`both@spectator`)

### Root Cause 1: Universal Integer Percentage Truncation Across Both Sides
In Channel 0 (spectator view), neither player's exact HP is visible. All damage and healing events appear only as integer percentages:
$$\text{pct} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{currentHP}}{\text{maxHP}} \times 100 \right\rfloor$$
Because max HP for every Pokémon on both sides is an unobserved variable ($base + SP + 75 \in [base+75, base+107]$), each integer percentage represents a discrete bucket of $1.5 \dots 2.3$ actual HP. Every single damage exchange couples 4 simultaneous unknowns: Attacker offensive stat, Defender defensive stat, Defender max HP, and the RNG damage roll (85% to 100%).

### Root Cause 2: The Turn 1/Turn 2 Multi-Hit Spread / Target Disruption Knot
On Turn 1:
- P2 Sylveon uses Moonblast on P1 Archaludon: `wanted |-damage|p1b: Archaludon|45/100`.
- In early rounds (e.g. Round 1), candidate guesses assign low SpD/HP to Archaludon or high SpA to Sylveon, causing Archaludon to take 77% damage (`got |-damage|p1b: Archaludon|23/100`).

On Turn 2:
- P1 Sneasler uses Rock Slide on Charizard and Incineroar: `wanted |-damage|p2a: Charizard|25/100`.
- If Sneasler is given high Atk, Charizard faints to 0 HP (`got |-damage|p2a: Charizard|0 fnt` as observed in Round 73!).
- When Charizard faints, Charizard cannot use Heat Wave.
- In rebuilds where Charizard survives, Charizard-Mega-Y uses Heat Wave (spread move). In ground truth, Heat Wave's primary target is `p1b: Archaludon`, dealing lethal damage (`0 fnt`), while Sneasler takes `17/100`.
- If Archaludon fainted on Turn 1 due to high Moonblast damage, Archaludon is not active at the time of Charizard's attack. Under `MimicPlayer.aim()`, Charizard re-targets the only remaining foe (`p1a: Sneasler`), producing the protocol mismatch:
  `wanted |move|p2a: Charizard|Heat Wave|p1b: Archaludon|[spread] p1a, got |move|p2a: Charizard|Heat Wave|p1a: Sneasler|[spread] p1a`.
- This single mismatch immediately halts Turn 2 simulation verification across Rounds 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 22, etc.

### Root Cause 3: Cross-Turn Tightrope on Sylveon's Special Attack
Sylveon is involved in attacks across 5 distinct turns:
1. Turn 1: Moonblast on Archaludon (`wanted 45/100`).
2. Turn 5: Moonblast on Pelipper (`wanted 19/100 slp`).
3. Turn 6: Quick Attack on Sneasler (`wanted 0 fnt`).
4. Turn 7: Moonblast on Pelipper (`wanted 0 fnt`).
5. Turn 8: Moonblast on Basculegion (`wanted 16/100`).

Increasing Sylveon's SpA or lowering Pelipper's SpD to satisfy the heavy Turn 5 Moonblast damage (`wanted 19/100`) increases Moonblast damage on Turn 1 against Archaludon, dropping Archaludon to `23/100` or `37/100` (which triggers the Turn 2 Heat Wave target failure). Conversely, lowering Sylveon's SpA to satisfy Turn 1 causes Turn 5 Moonblast to leave Pelipper with too much HP (`got 25/100` or `28/100` instead of `wanted 19/100`). The search is locked in a direct multi-turn trade-off between Turn 1 and Turn 5.

### Root Cause 4: The 5-Round Search Tier Escalation and Cyclic Mixing Trap
When the search stalls on Turn 2, `fallback` tests single-stat perturbations on Archaludon, Sneasler, Charizard, and Sylveon:
- Perturbing `Archaludon hp 0`, `atk 32`, `spa 32`, `spd 1`: stops at 1.7 (`wanted 45/100, got 36/100` or `37/100`).
- Perturbing `Archaludon hp 32`, `spd 32`: stops at 2.15 (Heat Wave target mismatch).
- Perturbing `Sneasler hp 1`, `hp 32`, `def 32`: stops at 2.15.
- Perturbing `Sneasler atk 0`: stops at 1.5 (`wanted Sylveon 75/100, got 81/100`).
- Perturbing `Sneasler atk 32`: stops at 2.14 (`wanted Charizard 25/100, got 11/100`).
- Perturbing `Charizard hp 30`, `atk 32`, `def 3`: stops at 2.14 (`wanted Charizard 25/100, got 12/100`).
- Perturbing `Charizard def 32`: stops at 2.15 (Heat Wave target mismatch).

Because single-stat perturbations cannot simultaneously satisfy the joint multi-turn constraints, `fallback` reports:
`fallback on turn 2: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds`.

The search then escalates through search budgets:
- **Search 1, 2, 3**: Fails on Turn 2 Heat Wave target.
- **Search 4 (Level 4 budget)**: Deep backtracking pushes past Turn 2 and reproduces through Turn 6, but diffs on Pelipper HP:
  `reproduces through turn 6 - wanted |-damage|p1a: Pelipper|19/100, got |-damage|p1a: Pelipper|25/100`.
- The engine records evidence, hands on 17 dice, and mixes the guess with the previous candidate:
  `back to a guess already tried; mixing it with the last one: Sylveon from the new, Incineroar from the last`.
- The mixed guess immediately breaks the Turn 2 Heat Wave line again!
- The search falls all the way back to Turn 1/2, stalling across Rounds 1, 2, 3, escalating to Search 4, reaching Turn 6, diffing on Pelipper `25/100`, and repeating this exact 5-round loop (Rounds 16, 21, 26, 31, 36, 41, 46, 51, 56, 61, 66, 71).

### Root Cause 5: Budget Exhaustion and Stalled Verdict
Because `deepest` never permanently progresses past Turn 6 to complete the match (Turn 9), the search accumulates stalls and progresses through all search restart phases:
1. **Default start**: Stalls on Turn 2 / Turn 6 after 22 rounds.
2. **Usage start**: Re-initializes from usage-weighted spreads; diffs on Charizard `0 fnt` and stalls again (Rounds 1–10).
3. **Wide start**: Reruns with $4\times$ fallback rebuilds and joint guess tries; stalls again (Rounds 1–22).
4. **Widest start**: Reruns from each start with every limit 4 times wider (128 fallback rebuilds, expanded probes, backtracks); runs through 71 rounds in the final cycle before exhausting search limits.
