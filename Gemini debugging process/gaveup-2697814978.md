### B. Mathematical Proof of the Poison Percentage Quantization Bottleneck
The core reason why candidate guesses and Smogon usage priors failed across every search phase is mathematical quantization:
Let $H$ be the maximum HP of Delphox ($H = 150 + \text{SP}_{\text{hp}}$, where $\text{SP}_{\text{hp}} \in [0, 32]$).
Poison damage is $P = \lfloor H / 8 \rfloor$.
We require the existence of an integer damage value $D_1$ such that:
$$\left\lceil \frac{H - D_1}{H} \times 100 \right\rceil = 79 \quad \text{AND} \quad \left\lceil \frac{H - D_1 - \lfloor H / 8 \rfloor}{H} \times 100 \right\rceil = 66$$

#### Why the Default Smogon Spread ($\text{SP}_{\text{hp}} = 0, H = 150$) Is Mathematically Impossible:
- If $H = 150$, poison damage is $P = \lfloor 150 / 8 \rfloor = 18$ HP.
