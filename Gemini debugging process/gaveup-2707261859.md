### 3. Invisible Choice Branching on Turn 3
On Turn 3, because Golisopod was fully paralyzed, no action executed for P1b in the replay log.
- In `reconstruct.mjs:choiceFor` (lines 520–538):
  ```javascript
  if (!action) {
    notes.push(`${sideId} slot ${i + 1} made an invisible choice - substituted a legal move`);
    state.invisible = true;
  ```

- Because Golisopod's choice was invisible, `reconstruct` introduced a variant digit over 7 legal move/target combinations (Iron Head, Leech Life, Sucker Punch into slots +1 or +2, and Protect).
- If a probe failed the 25% paralysis check, Golisopod executed the substituted move. Choosing Priority +1 Sucker Punch inverted turn order against Sneasler's Rock Slide, altering subsequent PRNG consumption and desynchronizing the entire RNG stream for Turn 3 and Turn 4.

### 4. Overlapping Speed Domains Without Rain

### 5. Fallback Rebuild Deadlock and Fruitless Blacklisting
When candidate guesses diverged on Turn 3, `infer.mjs:fallback` attempted stat modifications across active Pokémon:
1. In round 3, fallback attempted setting `Golisopod spe 32, Sneasler spe 0`.
2. Under the 66 Stat Point cap, reallocating Stat Points broke Turn 1 Raichu damage (`got 43/100`).
3. Setting `Golisopod spe 0, Sneasler spe 32` broke Turn 2 Sneasler damage (`got 43/100`).
4. Fallback exhausted its budget:
   ```
   fallback on turn 3: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds
   ```
5. When fallback failed to pass the line, line 484 of `infer.mjs` executed:
   ```javascript
   searched.set(mark, 2); spend.fruitless.add(mark);
   ```
   **The Turn 3 diff mark (`3|0||cant|p1b: Golisopod|par`) was permanently added to `spend.fruitless`.**
6. From that point forward, whenever any subsequent round stopped at Turn 3 paralysis, fallback was immediately rejected (`spend.fruitless.has(mark)` returned true).

### 6. Stalling Cascade Across Search Restarts
With Turn 3 permanently marked fruitless:
- Every round that failed at Turn 3 or Turn 4 could not trigger fallback.
- Because candidate guesses could not advance past Turn 3/4, `at` never surpassed `deepest`, incrementing `stalled` on each round.
- Upon reaching `stalled >= maxRounds` (32 rounds), the default start terminated with `stop: "stalled"`.
- The engine initiated restarts:
  1. `start: 'usage'`: generated spreads from usage priors, stopped at the same Turn 3/4 barrier, stalled after 32 rounds.
  2. `start: 'wide'` (4× fallback rebuilds and joint guess tries): hit the same diff marks and probe limits, stalled.
  3. `start: 'widest'` (4× widened search budget): ran default, usage, and wide sub-searches with budgetScale = 4, accumulating millions of probes without breaking the Turn 3–5 dice/order bottleneck.
- Total accumulated work across all restarts reached **291 rounds, 703 rebuilds, and 2,330,512 probes** over **13,647.8 seconds** (3.79 hours).
- The final inference terminated with `stop: "stalled"`, leaving P1 Sneasler completely unseen (`seen: false`, 136.6M spreads) and leaving P1 Archaludon (136.6M), P2 Golisopod (136.6M), P2 Archaludon (130.5M), and P2 Raichu (115.8M) unresolved.
