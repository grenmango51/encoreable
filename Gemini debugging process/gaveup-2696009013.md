## 7. Multi-View Failure & Architectural Takeaways

### Why All Three Views (`p1@p2`, `p2@p1`, `both@spectator`) Failed Together
Unlike `2706101187` (where knowing either side's true spreads resolved the spectator HP-parity trap cleanly), **all three views of `2696009013` failed with `stop = "searches"`**. Because `p1@p2` has Player 2's exact ground-truth Stat Points and `p2@p1` has Player 1's exact ground-truth Stat Points, the `3.5` stall (`wanted |move|p1b: Kingambit|Kowtow Cleave|p2a: Dragapult, got |move|p1b: Kingambit|Kowtow Cleave|p2b: Pawmot`) is **not** a Stat-Point search failure — it is a **Turn 3 choice/target reconstruction failure** triggered in the sequence `Maushold switch` (`3.0`) $\to$ `Dragapult Phantom Force` (`3.1`–`3.2`) $\to$ `Pawmot Revival Blessing` (`3.3`–`3.4`) $\to$ `Kingambit Kowtow Cleave` (`3.5`).
