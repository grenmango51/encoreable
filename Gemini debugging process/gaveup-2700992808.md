## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why Spectator Inference Gave Up

### 5.1 The Focus Sash Protocol Divergence Trap
1. In Pokémon Showdown's battle engine, when a Pokémon with `Focus Sash` is hit by an attack that would otherwise knock it out from 100% HP:
   - The engine triggers `item: Focus Sash`.
   - It emits: `|-enditem|<target>|Focus Sash` followed by `|-damage|<target>|1/100` (or `1/xxx`).
2. If the attack leaves the Pokémon with a non-zero HP total (even 1 HP or 1%), Focus Sash does **not** trigger, and **no `|-enditem|` line is emitted**.
3. In `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2700992808`:
   - `p1a` Sneasler took damage on Turn 7 down to **1/100**.
   - Because its actual planted stats had **30 Def SP** (Def 110), the damage fell just short of lethal, leaving Sneasler alive with 1 HP naturally. The replay protocol contained only `|-damage|p1a: Sneasler|1/100`.
   - Every candidate guess in inference initialized Sneasler with 0 Def SP (Def 80). In all these guesses, the incoming damage was lethal, triggering Focus Sash and producing the extra `|-enditem|` line.
   - Reconstruct immediately rejected the turn as a protocol mismatch.

### 5.2 Mimic Drift: Basculegion's Survival Alters the Field
1. In the original human game, Kingambit's Sucker Punch knocked out Basculegion on Turn 1.
2. In the mimic battle, because Kingambit was assigned a random spread with only **6 Atk SP** (Atk 177), Sucker Punch failed to KO Basculegion.
3. This survival caused the mimic battle to drift: Basculegion remained active on the field through Turn 8.
4. On Turn 7, the incoming attack that targeted Sneasler originated from a high-power physical attacker (Basculegion or Sneasler), making the damage roll exceptionally sensitive to Sneasler's defensive bulk.

### 5.3 The Joint HP/Def Optimization Barrier
To survive the incoming hit on Turn 7 without popping Focus Sash, Sneasler required a massive combined defensive investment:
$$\text{Damage}(\text{Attacker Atk}, \text{Sneasler Def}, \text{Sneasler HP}) < \text{Sneasler HP}$$
In the ground truth:
- Sneasler had **16 HP SP** (HP = 171) and **30 Def SP** (Def = 110).
- Total defensive SP allocated: $16 + 30 = \mathbf{46\text{ SP}}$ (out of 66 total).

In inference fallback:
- `fallback` in `infer.mjs` systematically mutates **only one stat dimension at a time** (testing extreme bounds: stat = 0, stat = 32, stat = median).
- When mutating `Sneasler def 32` while holding HP at 0 (HP = 155):
  - In Phase 1 (default guesses), the attacker (Basculegion) was guessed with 32 Atk SP. With 32 Atk, Basculegion's attack still dealt $\ge 155$ damage, popping Focus Sash (`plays out the same`).
- When mutating `Sneasler hp 32` while holding Def at 0 (Def = 80):
  - The attack easily dealt $\ge 187$ damage, popping Focus Sash.
- Because single-stat mutations could not bridge the coupled two-dimensional manifold (requiring simultaneously high HP and high Def against high Atk), every fallback attempt in Phase 1 failed:
  ```
  fallback on turn 7: nothing gets past the line in 8 rebuilds
  ```

1. In the cluster sweep, the engine cycled through multiple budget levels (`default`, `usage`, `wide`, `widest`).
2. In perspective `p1@p2` (where P2 was known and P1 was hidden):

## 7. Search Limits Analysis & Conclusion

### Limits Hit Analysis
The cluster sweep run reached `limits: ["backtracks", "fallbackRebuilds", "probes", "searches"]`:

1. **`searches`**:
   - Primary stop condition. In `infer.mjs`, when candidate bounds make no progress across rounds and fallback fails to advance past Turn 7, the search level increments until `++level >= budgets.length`, terminating the engine with `stop = 'searches'`.
2. **`fallbackRebuilds`**:
   - Triggered when individual fallback attempts exhausted their allocated rebuild budgets (8 rebuilds in `default`, 32 in `wide`, 128 in `widest`) without finding any single-stat modification that eliminated the unwanted `|-enditem|...|Focus Sash` line.
3. **`probes`**:
   - Triggered in `reconstruct.mjs` as backtracking searched through move branches and roll variations on Turns 1–7, accumulating **762,220 total probes**.
4. **`backtracks`**:
   - Triggered in `reconstruct.mjs` when exploring damage rolls for Turn 1 Sucker Punch and Turn 7 physical attacks.

### Diagnostic Conclusion
Match `mimic:gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3-2700992808` exemplifies the **Focus Sash protocol artifact coupled with a multi-stat bulk threshold**:
1. In competitive Pokémon, Focus Sash holders almost universally run maximum speed and offensive investment with 0 bulk (`0 HP / 0 Def / 0 SpD`).
2. In this mimic battle, the mimic generator assigned P1's Sneasler an anomalous random spread featuring **16 HP and 30 Def** (Level 50 stats: 171 HP, 110 Def).
