## 7. Summary & Root Cause Synthesis

1. **Replay Complexity & Multi-Weather Setup**:
   - Game 3 featured complex weather and terrain interactions: Grassy Terrain (Turn 2, 6, 8), Sandstorm (Turns 2–6), Grassy Seed defense boost, Quiver Dance stat stages, and multiple double-switch pivots via Parting Shot and U-turn.

2. **Why Single-View Inference Succeeded**:
   - In `p2@p1` and `p1@p2`, fixing one side's exact stats decoupled the damage equations and turn/weather orders into independent 1-variable linear bounds. Both single views solved cleanly in under 30 seconds with zero fallback rebuilds.

3. **Why Spectator View Stalled and Gave Up**:
   - Spectator Channel 0 provides only rounded integer percentages out of 100.
