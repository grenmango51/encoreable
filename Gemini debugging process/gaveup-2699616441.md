- Inference cycles through 4 restart budget tiers: `default`, `usage`, `wide`, and `widest`.
- Within each tier, candidate assignments are guessed and verified forward in the simulator.
- When spread candidate bounds remain unchanged across consecutive cycles (`newRounds === 0` and bounds do not contract), the engine increments `level` (`level = 0, 1, 2, 3`).
- When all 4 tiers fail to find an advance and candidate bounds remain invariant, the search gives up cleanly, returning:
  $$\text{stop} = \text{"searches"}$$

### Why `p2@p1` and `both@spectator` Failed
1. **The Root Mechanism: Missing `allAdjacentFoes` in `CHOOSABLE_TARGETS`**:
   In `scripts/lib/reconstruct.mjs`:
   ```javascript
   const CHOOSABLE_TARGETS = new Set(['normal', 'any', 'adjacentFoe', 'adjacentAlly', 'adjacentAllyOrSelf']);
   ```
   Blizzard has target `allAdjacentFoes`. Because `allAdjacentFoes` is not included in `CHOOSABLE_TARGETS`:
   - `needsTarget` evaluates to `false`.
   - The reconstructed move choice emitted to Showdown is `move blizzard` without a specific target slot index.
   - In Showdown, when a move is executed without a chosen target, Showdown calls `battle.getRandomTarget(pokemon, move)`.
   - `getRandomTarget` calls `this.sample(targets)`, which **consumes a PRNG die** to select uniformly between `p1a: Indeedee` and `p1b: Sneasler`.
2. **The Protocol Divergence on Turn 2**:
   - Original replay log:
     `|move|p2a: Froslass|Blizzard|p1a: Indeedee|[spread] p1a,p1b`
   - Candidate simulation output:
     `|move|p2a: Froslass|Blizzard|p1b: Sneasler|[spread] p1a,p1b`
   Whenever Showdown randomly selected `p1b: Sneasler` as the primary spread target, the protocol lines diverged immediately.
3. **PRNG Die Phase Shift**:
   Consuming an extra die via `getRandomTarget` desynchronized the entire PRNG sequence downstream. Damage rolls, accuracy rolls, critical hits, and secondary effects (such as Dire Claw's poison/paralysis) became completely misaligned with the recorded replay for all subsequent turns.
4. **Why Fallback Could Not Fix the Divergence**:
   `fallback()` mutates Stat Points (HP, Atk, Def, SpA, SpD, Spe). Stat point variations cannot alter the outcome of `getRandomTarget` unless an earlier speed tie is inverted. In round 14, setting `Kingambit spe 24` coincidentally inverted a Turn 1 speed check, changing the number of dice rolled before Turn 2 and temporarily matching `p1a: Indeedee`. However, this alignment broke down again at Turn 5 and Turn 8.
