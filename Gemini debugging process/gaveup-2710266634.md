### A. Single-Line `|-crit|` Mismatch on a Lethal Overkill Hit (`5.8–5.9`)
At `5.8`, `p2a: Pawmot` sits on **`1/100` HP** (`1` HP remaining after `Focus Sash`). `Basculegion`'s `Last Respects` (`150` BP, `Adaptability` `2.0×`, `Life Orb` `1.3×`) trivially overkills `Pawmot` (`0 fnt`) on every damage roll, **whether it lands a critical hit or not**. Consequently:
- Stat Points have **zero** effect on whether `Last Respects` crits or changes the displayed `0 fnt` outcome at `5.9–5.10`.
- `reconstruct` should normally resolve `|-crit|p2a: Pawmot` via `resolveTurn()` flipping the `randomChance(1, 24)` crit die to `0`. Yet across **all 4 budget levels** (`search 1` `maxProbes=150`, `search 2` `600`, `search 3` `4000`, `search 4` `4000` with `resample: 1`, and `4×` wider in Starts 4–6 up to `maxProbes=16,000` with `pins: null`), `reconstruct` terminated at `5.9` in only **2.2s–4.3s** (far below the `16,000` probe cap) without advancing past `5.9`.
- Crucially, `slowest_gave_up.json` shows **`p1@p2` and `p2@p1` also gave up** with `stop: "searches"` and `limits: ["probes", "searches"]`. Even when one side's Stat Points and exact HP integers are 100% known, `reconstruct` cannot get past the Turn 5 `|-crit|` failure.

Reproducing Turn 5 of `2710266634` in isolation on a cluster machine with `--trace` on `reconstruct` will show why `resolveTurn(5)` fails to flip `Last Respects`'s `randomChance(1, 24)` crit die to `0` even with `maxProbes = 16,000` and `pins = null`.
