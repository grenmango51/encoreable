## 5. Root Cause Analysis: Why `both@spectator` Stalled & Why Fallback Rebuilds Cycled

### A. The Turn 3 Recoil / Turn 4 Rough Skin Off-by-1 HP Trap (`38` vs. `39` Recoil)
At Turn 4 `4.6`, `evidencePass` reads the cutoff line `|-damage|p2a: Basculegion|67/100|[from] ability: Rough Skin|[of] p1b: Garchomp` (`atCut === true` in `scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` line 2473–2475). - Because `Charizard` (`78` base HP, max `185` HP at `hp = 32`) was at `63/100` before `Wave Crash`, its pre-KO HP is at most $\lfloor 0.6399 \times 185 \rfloor = 118$, capping `Wave Crash` recoil at $\text{round}(118 \times 33 / 100) = \mathbf{39}$ HP.
- Under `39` recoil and `24` Rough Skin:
  - `Basculegion hp = 0` (`MaxHP = 195`): $195 - 39 = 156$ (`80/100`) $\xrightarrow{-24} 132 / 195 = \mathbf{67/100}$ (`PASS`).
  - `Basculegion hp = 1` (`MaxHP = 196`): $196 - 39 = 157$ (`80/100`) $\xrightarrow{-24} 133 / 196 = \mathbf{67/100}$ (`PASS`).

## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, P2 Known)**: `Basculegion`'s exact `MaxHP = 195` (`hp = 0`) and exact HP integers (`156/195` after Turn 3 recoil, `132/195` after Turn 4 Rough Skin) are observed directly. Knowing Turn 3 recoil was exactly `39` HP (`195 -> 156`) immediately forces `Charizard`'s Turn 3 `63/100` HP to `116..118`, preventing `aimFor` from picking `Charizard hp = 29` (`MaxHP = 182`, `38` recoil) and resolving Turn 4 cleanly.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, P1 Known)**: `Charizard`'s exact `MaxHP = 185` (`hp = 32`) and exact `117/185` HP after `Close Combat` are fixed constants printed directly in Channel 1. Turn 3's `Wave Crash` recoil is therefore unambiguously `39` HP, immediately narrowing `Basculegion` to `156/195` or `157/196` and passing `4.6` (`67/100`) on the first attempt.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` & `infer.mjs`)
1. **Include Recoil / Drain Targets in `rec.lent` (`evidence.mjs`)**:
   In `evidence.mjs`, `totalChange` (`Wave Crash` recoil) and `drainChange` read the victim's HP (`via = { rec: V, dim: 'hp' }`), yet `rec.lent` is only populated by `storedStats` writes (`askWrites` / `transformInto`). Recording `V.id` in `S.lent` when `totalChange` or `drainChange` couples two Pokémon would place fainted Turn 3 victim `Charizard` in `Basculegion`'s `lender` tier inside Turn 4 `fallback` (`infer.mjs` line 577), allowing `fallback` to bump `Charizard hp` from `29` to `32` (`39` recoil) instead of cycling 128 times on the remaining four active Pokémon.
2. **Propagate Post-Cutoff `wholePaths()` Constraints Back Through `totalChange` (`evidence.mjs`)**:
   `totalChange.after()` (`evidence.mjs` line 2167) only runs once when the recoil line itself is settled (Turn 3 `80/100`), before Turn 4 `4.6` (`67/100` Rough Skin) prunes `recoil = 38` in `wholePaths()` at `finish()`. Re-filtering `totals` / `V.chain` (or adding an explicit HP-tie between recoil user and victim in `wholePaths`) would immediately narrow `Charizard.hp` from `[26, 32]` to `[30, 32]`, preventing `aimFor` from choosing `Charizard hp = 29` and eliminating the combinatorial `guessTries` blowup in `choose()`.
3. **Allow `probe()` to Re-Search Earlier Dice When a Single-Stat Change Hits the Same Turn Line Under Pinned Earlier Rolls (`infer.mjs` line 686)**:
   In `fallback`'s `probe()`, `again = await rebuild(chosen, budgets[1], { given: null })` only runs when `reach(b)[0] < t`. Even at `4.6` (`reach(b)[0] === t`), `handed` still held Turn 3's `68`-damage `Close Combat` roll fixed, preventing any earlier-turn roll adjustment during `probe`.
