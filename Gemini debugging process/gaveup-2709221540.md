## 6. Root Cause Analysis: Why `p1@p2` Gave Up

### Root Cause 1: The Tailwind vs Choice Scarf Speed Bottleneck
1. On Turn 3, Salamence sets Tailwind, doubling P1's speeds for 4 turns.
2. In `p1@p2`, Player 2 is the observer. Player 2's Rotom-Heat has fully known stats:
   - Nature: Timid
   - Planted SP: `{ hp: 5, atk: 0, def: 0, spa: 32, spd: 0, spe: 29 }`
   - Raw Speed: $\lfloor (86 + 29 + 20) \times 1.1 \rfloor = 148$
   - Choice Scarf Speed: $148 \times 1.5 = \mathbf{222}$.
3. On Turn 4, Rillaboom must move before Rotom-Heat. To achieve this under Tailwind:
   $$2 \times (85 + \text{SP}_{\text{spe}} + 20) > 222 \implies 105 + \text{SP}_{\text{spe}} \ge 112 \implies \mathbf{\text{SP}_{\text{spe}} \ge 7}$$
4. In `infer.mjs`, candidate guesses for Adamant Rillaboom originate from Smogon usage priors (`scripts/fixtures/usage-spreads.json`):
   - Prior #1: `32/32/0/0/2/0` ($\text{Spe} = 0$, Tailwind Spe = 210)
   - Prior #2: `32/32/0/0/0/2` ($\text{Spe} = 2$, Tailwind Spe = 214)
5. **Both top usage spreads have Spe $\le 2$**. When tested, Rotom moves first (`|move|p2b: Rotom|Volt Switch|p1b: Rillaboom`), causing an immediate move-order divergence on Turn 4 in every standard guess round.

### Root Cause 2: The Asymmetric Rigidity Trap
Why did fallback easily fix this in `both@spectator`, but stall and exhaust budgets in `p1@p2`?
- In `both@spectator`, Rotom's HP and Def were free variables. Setting Rillaboom's `spe: 32` left enough flexibility across both sides to satisfy spectator percentages.

### Architectural Recommendations
1. **Speed-Aware Prior Seeding under Tailwind**:
   - In `infer.mjs`, when a team is under active Tailwind and opposes a known Choice Scarf user, candidate guesses should filter or elevate usage spreads with sufficient Speed investment to clear the known opponent's Speed threshold ($\text{Spe} > \text{Scarf Spe} / 2$).

3. **Early Cutoff on Asymmetric Rigid Exact-HP Divergence**:
   - When an inferred attacker faces exact integer HP targets on the observer's side and fails across multiple rounds with identical damage discrepancies, fallback should prioritize the attacker's primary offensive stat range before cycling through non-impactful defensive dimensions.
