## 7. Single-View vs. Spectator Contrast & Architectural Takeaways

### Why `p2@p1` and `p1@p2` Passed Cleanly
- **In `p1@p2` (Channel 2, `p2` Known)**: `Basculegion-F`'s exact `MaxHP` and `Def` are fixed ground-truth constants, and Channel 2 prints exact `hp/maxhp` integers for P2. Only `Rillaboom`'s `Atk` is unknown on the `1.4` exchange, so `fallback` varies `Rillaboom.atk` directly along a 1D `[0..32]` search against the true `Basculegion-F` bulk (or avoids `replayWith` false-`same` on `atk = 0` when `Basculegion` has nonzero bulk) and immediately hits the exact `1..4` HP `1/100` target.
- **In `p2@p1` (Channel 1, `p1` Known)**: `Rillaboom`'s true `Atk` is a fixed constant, so `fallback` only needs to adjust `Basculegion-F`'s `(hp, def)` (with `Basculegion def` moving `got` off `Focus Sash` to trigger `moved` and 2-stat `(hp, def)` pairs in `fallback`), resolving `1.4` cleanly without needing a cross-side `(Rillaboom atk, Basculegion hp/def)` joint move.

### Architectural Takeaways (`scripts/lib/inference/evidence.mjs` & `infer.mjs`)
1. **Enforce Negative Survival-Item Evidence at `cutoff` in `evidence.mjs` (`provenLines` / `awaited`)**:
   When the replay emits `|-damage|X|p/100` directly at `prefix.cutoffAt` while the scaffold emits `|-enditem|X|Focus Sash` (or Sturdy/Endure/Focus Band) first, `provenLines` (`evidence.mjs` line 1109) returns `null` because `seen.from === prefix.cutoffAt`. As a result, `moveChange` treats Focus-Sash-triggering hits as consistent with `|-damage|X|p/100`, keeping all lethal spreads alive and preventing `evidence` from driving the guess out of the Focus Sash region. When `awaited` is active at `cutoffAt` (meaning the replay explicitly lacked the survival announcement), `moveChange` should require `ev.lines` to contain no survival announcement (`ev.lines.length === 0` or matching the replay's lines).
2. **Guard `replayWith` `same` Check Against Unpinned Failing-Turn Dice (`infer.mjs` line 677)**:
   Just as seen in `2706101187`, `probe()` skips `rebuild()` whenever `replayWith(built.inputLog, ...)` matches `plain` on a single arbitrary unpinned roll on the failing turn `t`. Here, `built.inputLog`'s high damage roll caused `Rillaboom atk 0`, `Rillaboom atk 16`, and `Basculegion def 32` to still trigger `Focus Sash` (`same = true`), skipping `rebuild()` and leaving `moved = []` so Starts 3, 4, and 6 exited `fallback` in 19 rebuilds without ever scanning `Rillaboom atk` or `(Rillaboom atk, Basculegion def)` pairs.
3. **Avoid Stripping `hp` to `0` on `closestSpread` Equal-Cost Ties (`knowledge.mjs` lines 672–679)**:
   Because `closestSpread` iterates `(hp, def, spd)` keys from `hp = 0` upward with strict `<`, probing `Basculegion def 32` on a 66/66-SP guess stripped `hp` from `2` (or `32`) down to `0` (`MaxHP = 195`) instead of shedding `spa` or `spe`. At `MaxHP = 195`, the 16-roll `Grassy Glide` ladder skipped `1/100` (`2/100` at `190`, `Focus Sash` at `195`).
